**Úlfr Kveld** — "the evening wolf," the wolf of twilight — is a Goliath
**barbarian with the heart of a poet and a voice as pure as gold**. He is learning
that words can be as powerful a weapon as his axe: in battle he hurls damaging
verses at his enemies, or lifts his allies with encouraging ones and tales of
their glory. Think a Nordic Bruce Banner — noble of soul, but with a quick temper
and an appearance that unsettles those who don't know him.

## Origins & Name

Úlfr was found as an infant at the edge of a village near the [[War Rock Mountains]] —
a huge, grey-skinned baby taken in by a local family. **Kveld** was the family name
of his adopted mother, a renowned poet; **Wolf** was a childhood nickname that stuck
until few remembered whatever his birth parents had called him. His true name, and
where he came from, are a mystery even to him. He carries his past quietly and is
guarded about his origins.

## Persona

A collector of stories more than a teller of his own. As he once corrected a
companion: *"Not my stories. These are the stories I collect, sir."* He is drawn to
the poetry and verses his adopted mother tried to teach him, and by night carves
runes and kenning-verses (see [[Kennings]]) into a quarterstaff he is slowly shaping
into a bardic focus. His relationship with his own rage is central to who he is — he
is reluctant to let it flow uncontrolled.

## Objects & Weapons

- **Last Word** — Úlfr's greataxe, named for "the argument that ends all arguments."
  He has never explained the name.
- **Quarterstaff / [[Nithing Pole]]** — a carved wooden staff he is transforming into
  a níðstöng, carving runes and kennings into it each night; intended to become his
  bardic spellcasting focus.
- **A worn notebook of verse** — filled with poetry, which he has learned to channel
  into a supernatural ability to undermine his enemies.
- **[[Leith Chantos's Legacy]]** — the sentient +1 greataxe he claimed in Leith's
  forge (Session 6); it communicates through emotion and is sworn to protect the
  family of its bearer. Úlfr still wields Last Word until he trusts the new blade.
