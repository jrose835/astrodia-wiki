The **Dark Lords** are **fallen angels** who rebelled against the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]** and were cast out of the [[Sanctuary in the Sky]].

## The Rebellion

Shortly after the **Solemn** created [[Astrodia]] and before he departed, a group of **higher angels** — just below the **[[Agents]]** in the order of divinity — rose up against the Pantheon. A war followed, and the rebels were **cast down and fell to earth**. Since then, even in the age of the higher beings before the **[[Severance]]**, they have **sown discord among the mortal races**.

## The Morning Star

The first and greatest of the Dark Lords, who **led the rebellion**, is known as the **Morning Star**. Some claim to have seen his physical presence walking the world. Beneath him is a whole host of **nefarious beings** — not quite demons or devils, but malevolent entities that spread discord. The faithful pray to keep their influence out.

## Session 12 — The Temple of Venus (September 23, 2026)

### The Scriptures

The entry-hall scriptures at the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] set out the hierarchy: the **Solemn** created the world and the **[[Agents]]** to govern it, then receded; **below the Agents were the angels.** Some of those angels — the Dark Lords — rebelled and fell, the first of them **the Morning Star.** **What caused the rebellion is disputed**; scholars of scripture argue about it.

### Ill Will Toward Venus

The temple's priest said the Dark Lords hold ill will toward **[[Venus]]**, **the Morning Star most of all** — Venus was one of the hands they felt had **overreached** — though he knows of no personal feud. Since the fall, the Dark Lords have tried to **reclaim enough power to ascend again.**

### The Mural and the Meteor

![[Pasted image 20260923220651.png]]

The temple's basement holds its prize artwork: **the Solemn and his host against the Morning Star and his armies**, at the moment the rebels were **cast down to earth.** [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] judged it **a couple of centuries old**, unsigned, and brought from **the South** — from cultures that revere the higher beings — rather than painted locally.

[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] sensed that something in it didn't belong, and [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] found it: a small **flaming meteor** falling in the middle of the scene, a **deliberate addition**. Asked about it, [[Nephthys]] said only that the battle *"was even before my time."*

### She Who Fell Before the First Fall

The priest explained that a **fringe sect** within the church believes the meteor represents **the cause of the Morning Star's rebellion**: a being — in the version [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] had heard, **an angel, a woman** — was **exiled from the [[Sanctuary in the Sky]]** for something that angered the gods, and her exile **spurred the Morning Star to rebel.** The Morning Star's was the first fall; hers came before it. She is known only by the pseudonym **[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]**. The priest does not personally believe it. *(This is one sect's answer to the disputed cause of the rebellion, not established doctrine.)*

> **Note — an uneasy echo:** the party has named itself **[[The Fallen]]**. Whether that resonance with the fallen angels is coincidence or foreshadowing is unresolved.
