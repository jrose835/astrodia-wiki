## Session 6 — The Temple on the Route (May 31, 2026)

There is a **temple to Nephthys** located in a **higher-being magic hotspot** near the party's planned route to the [[White Forest]]. The temple is **deeply significant to [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes]]**, whose own temple was **attacked by [[Lady Nectaria]]'s vampires**.

As [[The Fallen]] travel toward the White Forest (by way of the [[Quandrix College]] campus), they plan to **stop at this temple**. If Lady Nectaria's attackers remain in the area, the party means to find them.

Cross-references: [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes]], [[Lady Nectaria]], [[White Forest]], [[S.A.T.O.]].

## Session 7 — The Route Confirmed & Drolta's Past (July 15, 2026)

[[The Fallen]] committed to the temple as their next major destination — a **~6-day overland trek from [[Quandrix College]]**, ostensibly a pilgrimage. [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] shared more of her history: she was a **high priestess** here until a **gruesome vampire attack** ([[Lady Nectaria]]'s kind) killed many of the faithful while **searching for something**. She left, tasked with uncovering what happened, and hasn't returned since — the chalice and the new magic now feel eerily *familiar* to her.

Drolta also offered to **ask Nephthys for information about [[The Whispering One]]** — [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s newly-named patron.
