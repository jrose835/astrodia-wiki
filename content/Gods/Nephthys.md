The Phoenix Queen. A higher being and [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]'s patron. **She can take human form**, and the statue outside her temple shows her as a woman. Per Drolta (Session 13): *"She tends to be a bit elusive, except to those she's chosen... Just like the Phoenix, she burns bright and quick. But she always comes back."* Her temple is the **[[Temple of Nephthys]]**. Her male counterpart is the **[[Phoenix King]]**, whose worshippers, the Fire Kings, live on [[Fire King Island]].

## Session 12 — Communion Beneath the Temple of Venus (September 23, 2026)

In the basement of the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]], [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] sensed that something in the temple's mural of the war against the [[Darklords|Dark Lords]] didn't belong — a small **flaming meteor**, later tied to **[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]**. She **meditated and reached out to Nephthys**, who answered:

> *"I do not know what this is trying to invoke. The battle between the Dark Lords and the Solemn Pantheon was even before my time. There are still many mysteries about it that the higher beings are not aware of."*

Drolta then asked about **a portal to another world, and beings coming through it** (the party had just learned that the [[Great Cataclysm]] was caused by such a portal). Nephthys was silent for a moment.

> *"I cannot speak of this freely."*
> *"...But continue on your path."*
> *"Of course, my child."*

Two takeaways: the Dark Lords' war **came before Nephthys's time**, so even the higher beings don't know all of it; and she **knows something about the rift that she will not share openly.**

## Session 13 — The Temple (October 7, 2026)

The party reached the **[[Temple of Nephthys]]** at last, on the day a child, **[[Solenne]]**, was anointed its new high priestess. Learned there:

- **The Phoenixes' relics.** [[Fire King Island]] houses relics of **both the Phoenix King and the Phoenix Queen**, kept for **when the Phoenixes are reborn** (per [[Petra]]).
- **Robes of Nephthys** are among the relics powerful enough to **break the contracts that bind the undead**, and may be what [[Lady Nectaria]]'s vampires were after. Some are still on Fire King Island (per [[Nephthara]], via Drolta).
- **The ashes** Drolta guarded were untouched in the attack.
- **Venus and the Phoenixes:** there's *"some good faith"* between [[Venus]] and the Phoenixes, since the Phoenix re-creates itself every cycle (per [[Ysmene]]).
- **The orders are divided.** Women aren't privy to the higher rites on Fire King Island because of the division between Nephthys's order and the Phoenix King's (per Petra).
- **Her protection on Drolta's mind lapsed** during the anointing (a failed Wisdom save), and at sunset Nectaria's way into Drolta's mind was **thrown open.**

Solenne's vow: *"I want to see her rise again from the ashes."*

## Session 14 — The Dress, and the Test (October 21, 2026)

- **Her dress.** The relic [[Lady Nectaria]] wants is **the dress Nephthys wore in her human form**, said to **break chains and destroy unjust hierarchies**, including a vampire's bond to its **sire**. It is kept in the Fire Kings' temple on **[[Fire King Island]]**.
- **"A test of love."** Nectaria told Drolta privately that her siege of the temple was a test, and that **this has happened before**: Nephthys has been **tested** like this.
- ***"Nephthys failed the test the first time."*** Drolta doesn't know whether that means the first attack on the temple, or something from **before Nephthys became what she is**, when she still wore the dress.
- **"The Phoenix Queen knows more than she has shared,"** Nectaria insisted, even with Drolta. Drolta is holding true, but wants to **dig into Nephthys's history.**
- **Nectaria's contempt.** Her parting words through the temple door: ***"And once again, your goddess did nothing."***
- **Her silence.** Per [[Nephthara]], the temple has *"prayed for years to a voice that has not spoken back."* Until she chose Drolta, Nephthys had not made herself known to any of them **in centuries, if not more.** Nephthara hopes she will speak to [[Solenne]] too.
