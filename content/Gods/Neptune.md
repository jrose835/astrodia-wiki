The [[Agents|Agent]] of Life and god of nature within the [[Solemn Pantheon]].

## Followers

**[[Leif Kalanchoe]]** — Leif's clan, the Unending Sky Clan, honors Neptune through their actions. Leif shared at the campfire that his faith is practiced through deeds rather than formal worship.

## Session 12 — Identified by Illustration (September 23, 2026)

In the library beneath the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]], the party confirmed from the book illustrations that the **water-themed Agent is Neptune** — the Agent Leif's clan honors.
