**Mars** is the **[[Agents|Agent]] of Force**, one of the nine canonical Agents of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]**. The **[[Endymion Empire]]** **enforces the Agent's worship.**

## The Empire's Agent

In the library beneath the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] (Session 12), the party read that the **Endymion Empire enforces worship of Mars.** Pentamarone, by contrast, worships **[[Venus]]** — so the two neighboring powers have **two different patron Agents.**

Nothing else about the Agent's depiction (including gender), clergy, or temples has yet been established.
