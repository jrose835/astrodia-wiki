The **God of Dogmatika** is the new deity worshiped by the [[Nation]] in [[Dogmatika]] — **not a part of the [[Solemn Pantheon]]**, and in the words of a Pentamarone priest (Session 12), the head of *"a new pantheon"* whose church **rejects the Solemn Pantheon** outright. Its saints, marked with [[Stigmatas]], perform miracles in its name.

## Session 12 — The Priest of Venus's Account (September 23, 2026)

[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] asked the priest at the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] about people he saw back home carrying **banners showing no Agent he recognized.** The priest identified them as the **Church of Dogmatika** in the [[Closed Land]]:

- They claim **a new pantheon and deity separate from the Solemn**, **reject the [[Solemn Pantheon]]**, and say they have **saints who perform miracles.**
- They have grown **exponentially over the last couple of decades.**
- His guess is that they were **founded about a century or a bit more ago** — which *"might coincide"* with the [[Great Cataclysm]]. *"In times of great peril, people are always looking for hope."*

**Party theory (speculation, not established):** having just learned that the Great Cataclysm was caused by **a portal to another world** through which otherworldly magic and beings came, someone whispered to the party afterward — *what if Dogmatika's new god and saints came through the rift too?*
