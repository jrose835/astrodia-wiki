Pluto is the [[Agents|Agent]] of Death in the [[Solemn Pantheon]].

## Followers

Clerics of Pluto hunt necromancers and undead—those who seek to extend life past their mortal time.

**[[Aren Greycloak]]** - A cleric who found faith after hearing a sermon about Pluto while wandering the [[Endymion Empire]]. She was granted divine power, including the ability to detect undead via her pendant.

## Worship

Pluto is not widely worshipped in the crafting city of **[[Pentamarone]]**. Asked in Session 11 whether there was a temple to him anywhere in the city, the [[WitchCrafters]] said no — the city's faith runs almost entirely to **[[Venus]]**, the Agent of Creation. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] has yet to find anywhere in the north to pay her respects.

## Session 12 — Enemies of Pluto (September 23, 2026)

In the library beneath the **[[Temple of Venus]]**, [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] read about **Pluto's enemies.** It was mostly **necromancers** and other things she already knew, with one exception: **[[The Prophecy Destroyer]]** *(as heard — unconfirmed)*, thought to have been **an enemy of Pluto** in some unknown capacity.

### Aren's Fear — a Spoil Against Death

*(Party speculation.)* After the [[WitchCrafters]] concluded that the [[Golden Chalice]] is "sinful" because it violates [[Venus]]'s domain, the party wondered whether **each [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoil]] mocks a different Agent.** [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] suggested the **white gown** might be a mockery of a **burial shroud** — a Spoil against Pluto — and the **lance** was floated as either [[Mars]] or Pluto.

**Aren became very concerned** that a Spoil might rebuke **her god**, and about what a rebuke of death would look like: **unnatural, eternal life.** That pointed the party at **[[Madame Verre]]** — suspiciously long-lived, and dressed in white. *(Aren later learned privately that Verre is **not undead**; her condition was an accident of eternal-youth research, and she is still aging. Whether she carries a Spoil is unknown.)*

## Doctrine

Pluto's doctrine is compatible with the faith of [[Nephthys]] and the Phoenix worship, as both oppose unnatural undeath.
