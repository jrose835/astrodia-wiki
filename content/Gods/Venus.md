**Venus** is the **[[Agents|Agent]] of Creation**, one of the Hands of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]**. She governs the making of new things — new ideas, invention, and *anything in which something arises from nothing*. She is widely revered, and often prayed to by **entrepreneurs and inventors** seeking to bring a new product or work into being.

## The Three Orbs

Venus is depicted with **three orbs** — her **muses**, through which she channels her divine abilities. These orbs are said to sometimes **present themselves to mortals as a form of divine inspiration**.

## Connection to Red

**[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]** has a **recurring dream** of three spheres — **purple, blue, and red** — twisting around the moon, with animals gathered beneath. [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]] apprentices (Session 3) and later the [[Lathen]] priest (Session 7) both identified the orbs as **Venus's three orbs**. Combined with Red's separate **dreams of the moon** (see [[The Moon]]), this marks him as someone the divine may be reaching out to.

## A Temple in Pentamarone (Session 10)

While the rest of the party shopped the [[Pentamarone]] market, **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** kept an ear out for anything religious and overheard a passerby praising *"the service at the Temple of Venus yesterday"* — establishing an **active Temple of Venus in [[Pentamarone]]**, somewhere outside the market circle.

Aren intends to pay her respects there. Given [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s unexplained connection to Venus's three orbs — and the fact that he woke that same morning from a dream of **something grand and then the same thing in ruins** — the party has not yet visited it. **Open thread.** *(Visited in Session 12 — see below and [[Temple of Venus]].)*

**Session 11 confirmed the scale of it.** Asked by [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] whether there was a temple to **[[Pluto]]** anywhere in the city, the [[WitchCrafters]] said no — *"most of the people here worship **Venus**, so there's a temple to Venus."* **Venus is Pentamarone's principal faith**, which is a fitting patron for a city that exists entirely to make new things: she is the [[Agents|Agent]] of Creation, and Pentamarone is a city of crafters.

## Session 12 — The Temple of Venus (September 23, 2026)

### The Chalice Judged "Sinful"

The [[WitchCrafters]]' report on the [[Golden Chalice]] concluded that it **generates something from nothing** — no components, no ambient arcane magic, no siphoning from elsewhere. [[Genni]]'s verdict:

> *"And I think that is what makes it **sinful**. From our best ability, we believe that this object violates the laws of nature — and specifically, quite appropriately, the domain of **the Agent of Creation.**"*

The WitchCrafters themselves mostly honor Venus, understanding their crafts as channeling the Agent of Creation. This finding set up the party's theory that **each [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoil]] may mock a different Agent** (see [[Agents#Party Theory — One Spoil per Agent?|Agents]]).

### The Visit

[[The Fallen]] went to the **[[Temple of Venus]]** to learn whether Venus has enemies who might build something to mock her. The statue out front shows Venus with her **three orbs**, the **muses of creation** — matching the statue on the roof of [[Morgana's Tower]]. The entry-hall scriptures say Venus lets those who pray to her **thrive and manifest what they desire** in their lives.

### The Priest's Account

- Venus is the Agent of Creation, one of the hands of the Solemn. She watches over **all artistic expression and the making of goods and services**, and works **through her three muses**, which usually appear as **spheres orbiting her.**
- **No documented ill will between the Agents** — they respect each other's domains.
- **The [[Darklords|Dark Lords]]** do hold ill will toward her, **the Morning Star most of all.** Venus was one of the hands they felt had **overreached**, but he knows of no personal feud.

### Red's Dream, Read Again

[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] described his dream — three spheres (**purple, blue, and red**) orbiting **[[The Moon]]** while animals sat staring at it. The priest's reading: the Moon may be the **protector of the Agents**, and perhaps the divine is **inspiring Red to create something with animals.** *"I love animals."*
