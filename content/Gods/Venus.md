**Venus** is the **[[Agents|Agent]] of Creation**, one of the Hands of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]**. She governs the making of new things — new ideas, invention, and *anything in which something arises from nothing*. She is widely revered, and often prayed to by **entrepreneurs and inventors** seeking to bring a new product or work into being.

## The Three Orbs

Venus is depicted with **three orbs** — her **muses**, through which she channels her divine abilities. These orbs are said to sometimes **present themselves to mortals as a form of divine inspiration**.

## Connection to Red

**[[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]]** has a **recurring dream** of three spheres — **purple, blue, and red** — twisting around the moon, with animals gathered beneath. [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]] apprentices (Session 3) and later the [[Lathen]] priest (Session 7) both identified the orbs as **Venus's three orbs**. Combined with Red's separate **dreams of the moon** (see [[The Moon]]), this marks him as someone the divine may be reaching out to.
