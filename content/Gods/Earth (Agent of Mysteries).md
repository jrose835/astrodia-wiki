**Earth** is the **[[Agents|Agent]] of Mysteries**, one of the nine canonical Agents of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]**. She is depicted as a **young woman with wings**; her faith embraces the great **mysteries of the world** — including the open question of *which* being is the right one to devote one's life to. Her temples welcome worshippers of any higher power.

## The Temple in Lathen

The small town of **[[Lathen]]** has a temple dedicated to Earth on its eastern side, marked by a **winged statue** over the door ([[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] recognized her on sight). Its priest was warm and open, inviting the outsiders of [[The Fallen]] to pray to whatever power they served, since his goddess "is also open and accepting of the great mysteries of the world."

In **Session 7**, this priest delivered the party's fullest account yet of the cosmology:

- The **[[Solemn Pantheon]]** and the **nine canonical [[Agents]]** (some argue ten).
- The disputed tenth, **[[The Moon]]**, protector of the Agents — and his suggestion that she may be reaching out to **[[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]]**.
- **[[Venus]]** and the meaning of her three orbs.
- The **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]** — fallen angels who rebelled against the Pantheon.

He knew nothing of the [[White Forest]] beyond its curse, which he believed (on faith) was stopped by divine intervention.
