The **Agents** are the lesser gods of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]** — the "Hands" of the **Solemn**, the all-powerful creator who shaped [[Astrodia]] and has since departed the realm, leaving the Agents to govern his domains. Most religious folk of Astrodia choose one Agent as their primary deity.

## The Nine Canonical Agents

Per the priest of [[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]] at [[Lathen]] (Session 7), there are **nine canonical Agents** — though some argue for a tenth (see [[The Moon]]):

| Agent | Domain |
|---|---|
| **Mercury** | Wisdom |
| **[[Venus]]** | Creation |
| **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]]** | Mystery |
| **[[Mars]]** | Force |
| **Jupiter** | Miracles |
| **Saturn** | Judgment |
| **[[Uranus]]** | Entropy |
| **[[Neptune]]** | Life |
| **[[Pluto]]** | Death |

## The Disputed Tenth

**[[The Moon]]** is held by some to be a tenth Agent. Rather than governing a mortal domain, she is believed to **protect the Agents themselves**, and to reach out to worthy mortals to aid her.

The priest at the [[Temple of Venus]] in [[Pentamarone]] (Session 12) gave the same account independently: the Moon is *thought* to be a tenth Agent, though that's disputed, and may instead be **the protector of the Agents** — the one who *"makes sure that everything stays as it should,"* including, *"ideally,"* protection from the [[Darklords|Dark Lords]].

## The Fallen Angels

Below the Agents in the hierarchy of divinity were a class of **angels**, some of whom rebelled against the Pantheon and were cast down — the **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]**.

## The Statues at Morgana's Tower (Session 9)

The roof of **[[Morgana's Tower]]** in [[Freedale]] holds **nine statues of winged figures** arranged around a teleportation circle — **one per canonical Agent**, with **[[The Moon]] conspicuously absent.** This is independent corroboration of the nine-Agent count from a witch's private shrine, not a temple's doctrine.

Before each statue sits a **basin with a lit flame**, and this is where it gets strange:

- **Five flames burn a dark blue**; **four burn as ordinary fire.**
- [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s Arcana read confirmed the flames are **magical and tied to their specific Agent**, and that the blue-flame group **shares something** the others do not — but not *what*.
- **Saturn** — the Agent whose temple stands in that very town — sits **front and center**, and his flame is **not blue.**

[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] identified four of the nine by sight: **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]]** (seen at [[Lathen]]), **Saturn** (the Freedale temple), **[[Venus]]** (her three orbs), and **[[Pluto]]**. Aren stopped mid-dungeon to **pray at Pluto's statue.** The remaining five are Agents the party has not encountered. *(In Session 12, temple-library illustrations at the [[Temple of Venus]] let the party put faces to two more — [[Uranus]] and [[Neptune]] — though nobody has yet matched those to specific statues on the roof.)*

**Open question:** what distinguishes the blue five from the ordinary four — and whether the flames **change** as the campaign progresses. The party intends to check on return visits.

## Session 12 — The Temple of Venus (September 23, 2026)

[[The Fallen]] visited the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] specifically to ask whether Venus has enemies who might build something to mock her. What they learned about the Agents as a group:

- **No documented ill will between the Agents.** The priest said they respect each other's domains, and the temple library's books held **no record of any conflict between Agents.** The Agents' known enemies are the [[Darklords|Dark Lords]], who felt some of the hands had overreached.
- **The religions of the two neighboring powers differ.** The **[[Endymion Empire]] enforces worship of [[Mars]], the Agent of Force**; **Pentamarone worships [[Venus]].**
- **Two more Agents identified by illustration.** The creepy figure with **blue hair, red eyes, and yellow beneath them** is **[[Uranus]], the Agent of Entropy.** Tommy noted he has *"dropped a blue-haired guy a couple of times"* that the party hasn't picked up on. The water-themed Agent was confirmed as **[[Neptune]]**, whom [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s clan honors.

### Party Theory — One Spoil per Agent?

*(Speculation, not established.)* The [[WitchCrafters]]' report concluded that the [[Golden Chalice]] is "sinful" because it generates something from nothing — a violation of **[[Venus]]'s domain** of Creation. Back at the inn, [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] led a round of religion checks and the party wondered whether **each of the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]] mocks a different Agent** (chalice → Venus is established; book → Mercury?, lance → [[Mars]] or [[Pluto]]?, gown → Pluto?). The count doesn't match: **nine Agents, only four known Spoils.**

The party also asked whether the **five blue flames** at [[Morgana's Tower]] (above) might mark **the Agents targeted by Spoils.** Unresolved.
