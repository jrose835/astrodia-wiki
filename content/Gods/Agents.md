The **Agents** are the lesser gods of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]** — the "Hands" of the **Solemn**, the all-powerful creator who shaped [[Astrodia]] and has since departed the realm, leaving the Agents to govern his domains. Most religious folk of Astrodia choose one Agent as their primary deity.

## The Nine Canonical Agents

Per the priest of [[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]] at [[Lathen]] (Session 7), there are **nine canonical Agents** — though some argue for a tenth (see [[The Moon]]):

| Agent | Domain |
|---|---|
| **Mercury** | Wisdom |
| **[[Venus]]** | Creation |
| **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]]** | Mystery |
| **Mars** | Force |
| **Jupiter** | Miracles |
| **Saturn** | Judgment |
| **Uranus** | Entropy |
| **[[Neptune]]** | Life |
| **[[Pluto]]** | Death |

## The Disputed Tenth

**[[The Moon]]** is held by some to be a tenth Agent. Rather than governing a mortal domain, she is believed to **protect the Agents themselves**, and to reach out to worthy mortals to aid her.

## The Fallen Angels

Below the Agents in the hierarchy of divinity were a class of **angels**, some of whom rebelled against the Pantheon and were cast down — the **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]**.

## The Statues at Morgana's Tower (Session 9)

The roof of **[[Morgana's Tower]]** in [[Freedale]] holds **nine statues of winged figures** arranged around a teleportation circle — **one per canonical Agent**, with **[[The Moon]] conspicuously absent.** This is independent corroboration of the nine-Agent count from a witch's private shrine, not a temple's doctrine.

Before each statue sits a **basin with a lit flame**, and this is where it gets strange:

- **Five flames burn a dark blue**; **four burn as ordinary fire.**
- [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s Arcana read confirmed the flames are **magical and tied to their specific Agent**, and that the blue-flame group **shares something** the others do not — but not *what*.
- **Saturn** — the Agent whose temple stands in that very town — sits **front and center**, and his flame is **not blue.**

[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] identified four of the nine by sight: **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]]** (seen at [[Lathen]]), **Saturn** (the Freedale temple), **[[Venus]]** (her three orbs), and **[[Pluto]]**. Aren stopped mid-dungeon to **pray at Pluto's statue.** The remaining five are Agents the party has not encountered.

**Open question:** what distinguishes the blue five from the ordinary four — and whether the flames **change** as the campaign progresses. The party intends to check on return visits.
