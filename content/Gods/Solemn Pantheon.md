Collection of gods the people of Astrodia choose most often to worship. Led by an all-powerful creator, simply referred to as the Solemn, the lesser gods, called the [[Agents]], are the gods with specialized domains most religious folks choose from as a primary deity. These gods as well as a collection of angels are said to reside in the [[Sanctuary in the Sky]].

## Session 12 — Scripture at the Temple of Venus (September 23, 2026)

The books in the entry hall of the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] give the temple's account of the cosmology:

- The **Solemn** created the world, made the **[[Agents]]** to govern its domains, and then **receded into the ether** — a deist creator, like a clock-winder who sets the world going and steps back.
- **Below the Agents were the angels.** Some of them, the **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]**, rebelled and fell; the first was **the Morning Star.** What caused the rebellion is **disputed** (one fringe sect credits [[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]).

The priest added that there is **no documented ill will between the Agents**; they respect each other's domains.

The temple's basement mural shows **the Solemn and his host** casting down the Morning Star's armies. *(An observation from the table, not established: the Solemn there is a heavily built man with a long white beard in pale robes — close to the man [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] saw attacked in her Session 10 vision. See [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]].)*

### Rival Faith

The priest identified the **Church of [[Dogmatika]]** as claiming a new deity **separate from the Solemn** and **rejecting the Solemn Pantheon** outright. See [[God of Dogmatika]].
