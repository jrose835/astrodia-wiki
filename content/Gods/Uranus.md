**Uranus** is the **[[Agents|Agent]] of Entropy**, one of the nine canonical Agents of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]**. Uranus is depicted as a **creepy figure with blue hair, red eyes, and yellow beneath them.**

## Depiction

In the library beneath the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] (Session 12), the party identified Uranus from a book's illustrations: **blue hair, red eyes, and yellow beneath the eyes.**

## Tommy's Hint

At the table, Tommy noted that he has *"dropped a blue-haired guy a couple of times"* that the party hasn't picked up on. Who or where that figure was is **unknown** — worth going back through earlier recaps for any blue-haired figure.
