**The Moon** is the disputed **tenth [[Agents|Agent]]** of the **[[Solemn Pantheon]]**. Some theologians argue she is a true Agent; the canonical count holds at nine. Either way, the faithful believe she is a **sentient being with power much like the Agents** — but with a different purpose.

## The Protector of the Agents

Where the nine canonical Agents **govern** their respective domains over the mortal realm, the Moon's role is to **protect the Agents themselves**. She is said to **reach out to mortals she deems worthy** to aid her in whatever act of protection she is undertaking — choosing a "lucky few" to serve that end.

## Connection to Red

**[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]** has been having **recurring dreams of the moon** — and, in a related dream, of **[[Venus]]'s three orbs** arranged **in front of the moon**. He came to [[Lathen]] partly chasing these dreams as "things started happening." The Lathen priest's read: the **Moon may be reaching out to Red** as one of her chosen. The priest promised to research the Moon's worship (there may be a dedicated temple somewhere) and report back.

## Session 12 — The Priest of Venus (September 23, 2026)

At the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]], Red told his dream again: three spheres (**purple, blue, and red**) orbiting **the moon** while animals sat staring at it. The priest's account of the Moon matched the Lathen priest's:

- She is **thought to be a tenth Agent**, though that's **disputed.**
- She may instead be **the protector of the Agents** — the one who *"makes sure that everything stays as it should."* That includes protection from the [[Darklords|Dark Lords]], *"ideally."*

His reading of the dream itself: perhaps the divine is **inspiring Red to create something with animals.**
