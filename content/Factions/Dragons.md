Play an important role in Astrodia. They are considered some of the most powerful beings in existence, almost to that of the gods, but they are rarely seen and rarely interact with mortals. 

## Dragon Worship in Caelwyrm (Session 12, September 23, 2026)

The [[WitchCrafters]]' research into the [[Golden Chalice]] turned up stories from **dragon-worshipping clans** in [[Caelwyrm]]:

- One clan **worshipped a white dragon**, and used a **ritual object that could conjure whatever the dragon required.**
- The object was **fought over by rival dragon-worshipping clans** in **the Great Ravine** — itself thought to have been carved by dragons in ancient times, and where [[Lucian Cugg]] found the chalice.
- Its last record **disappeared a few centuries ago.**

It is **unconfirmed** that this object is the chalice.

**Did dragons make it?** *(Possibility, not established.)* The guild concluded the chalice makes **something from nothing**, violating the domain of [[Venus]], the Agent of Creation. Asked who could make such a thing, the answer was a god, possibly — or, **given the history, the dragons.**
