**The Fallen** is the name the party finally settled on for their adventuring group, decided at the close of Session 6.

They were recruited by the [[S.A.T.O.]] to serve as the group's **outside adventuring arm** — the agents who can move and act where the students embedded inside the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] cannot.

## Current Goal

Take the [[Golden Chalice]] to the [[White Forest]] — away from the Academy and toward answers about the [[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]] and the forbidden research that cost [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] her standing.

**As of Session 10:** the teleport **missed.** [[Archibald]]'s circle put the party not at the [[Nephthys]] temple but at a crossroads in **[[Endymion Empire]] woodland**, hours north of **[[Pentamarone]]** — which they then walked into, and where they remain. Roughly **15 days** remain before the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] can seize the chalice.

**At the end of Session 10 they were paralyzed in the courtyard of the [[WitchCrafters]] guild hall**, facing **[[Madame Verre]]**, after discovering what the [[Golden Chalice]] does by drinking from it — and detonating a conjured horse in the guild library. *(Superseded: see **As of Session 12** below.)*

The White Forest is no longer only a destination. Using the tower's scrying pool, **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] found her missing mother alive and bound to a tree inside it**, beside a second captive — so the party is walking toward a **rescue**.

### What the Tower Gave Them

- **[[Guardian Command Amulet]]** (worn by Asha) — commands the tower's shield guardian, and proves how the constructs posted across [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]] work
- **Teleportation circle runes** for the tower roof, and a **scrying pool** usable once a day
- **Spell components up to 500 gp**, three 3rd-level **spell scrolls**, unidentified **potions**, and a **lightning-resistant cloak**
- **[[Morgana]]'s papers** — and the names **[[Yssa]]**, **[[Verelda]]**, and **[[Dia]]**

### What Pentamarone Gave Them (Session 10)

- **The [[Golden Chalice]]'s function** — drink from it and it conjures what you think of. Living copies are inert; **objects may be real** (they now hold **a second chalice**). Everyone who drank *"feels a little weird."*
- **The nondetection is the chalice's own** and cannot be dispelled — per **[[Genni]]** of the [[WitchCrafters]]
- **Four Sinful Spoils named** by [[Tobin]] — **spear, book, chalice, dress** — and the Academy believes **the Seeker already has one**
- **[[Rellyona]]** — a name and a death for the [[Magistus]] who founded [[Pentamarone]]; Asha carries her **unread biography** *(read by Session 11, contents not yet shared)*
- An **[[Alchemy Jug]]** ([[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]), a **[[Clockwork Amulet]]** ([[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]), glassware, two histories, a dagger, and a bear-skin cloak nobody has picked up *(Úlfr collected it in Session 11: [[Bear Cloak]])*

**Immediately ahead** *(as of Session 10)*: whatever [[Madame Verre]] wants. Behind that, still unresolved: [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]'s homecoming at the vampire-sacked [[Nephthys]] temple, and the rescue in the [[White Forest]].

### As of Session 12 (September 23, 2026)

See [[Session 12 Recap — Something from Nothing]].

- **The chalice is back in their hands.** [[Madame Verre]] heard the [[WitchCrafters]]' report and **declined to use it**. She honored the contract and **returned it**, and she still won't say what she wanted it for. The party's own ruling: **no one drinks from it except in extreme emergencies**, because repeated use breeds grandeur, superiority, and impulses that aren't the drinker's own.
- **What they now know about it:** indestructible, all schools of magic at once, undispellable, and it **makes something from nothing**, which is why the guild calls it sinful against [[Venus]]. Its **nondetection covers whoever holds it**, so the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] can't scry the carrier.
- **The repossession clock is about 10 days.**
- **New ties:** an open offer of **supplies for the White Forest** from the WitchCrafters, a standing invitation from Verre to **come back later**, and a contact in **[[The Sundered Watch]]**, anti-Empire resistance fighters on [[Aleister the Invoker]]'s trail.
- **New questions out** to the [[S.A.T.O.]]: see [[S.A.T.O.#Session 12 Assignments (September 23, 2026)|Session 12 Assignments]].
- **Next destination is undecided:** the **[[Nephthys]] temple** (the original plan, still unvisited) or **straight to the [[White Forest]]** for [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s mother. Verre expects them to go to the forest "by way of a temple or two."
