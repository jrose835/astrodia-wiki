The **Magistus** are the legendary spellcasters credited with **stopping the [[Great Cataclysm]]** roughly **150 years ago** *(see [[Great Cataclysm#Timeline Note|Timeline Note]])* — the surge of magical energy that corrupted and nearly destroyed everything in its path. They are among the most powerful mortal spellcasters ever recorded, and their history is tangled in contradiction.

## The Legend

- Accounts disagree on their **number**: some histories say **three**, others **four**. The reconciling version (per [[Quintilius]]): **four set out, but only three returned** — the source of the confusion.
- After stopping the Cataclysm, the Magistus **went their separate ways**.
- The green **[[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]]** runs along the very line of the Cataclysm the Magistus supposedly *stopped* — a coincidence the [[S.A.T.O.]] and [[The Fallen]] find deeply suspicious. *(Revealed in Session 12: the Cataclysm was a **portal to another world**, the green magic leaked in through it, and **the Magistus sealed the rift**. See below.)*

## Session 10 — A Name and a Death (August 26, 2026)

[[The Fallen]] stumbled into [[Pentamarone]] and put a **name** to its founder for the first time: **[[Rellyona]]**, identified by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] off the spine of ***The Biography of Rellyona*** in a market bookstall (which Asha bought, and has **not yet read**).

**[[Genni]] of the [[WitchCrafters]] confirmed she is dead** — *"the town was founded by one of the Magistus. She's no longer with us."* The party had been half-hoping the surviving Magistus lived here; that hope is closed. **Emperor Endymion remains the only Magistus known to be alive.**

A dockside sailor also passed along the rumor that **Rellyona and the Emperor "go way back"** and that the city's independence rests on an **old truce** honored since her death — the friendliest of the three explanations in circulation (see [[Pentamarone]]). If true, the "falling-out" reading of the Magistus's split may need revising: they may have parted on terms, not in anger.

## Session 12 — They Sealed the Rift (September 23, 2026)

**The truce, per Verre.** [[Genni]] told [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] the version she has from [[Madame Verre]]: **[[Rellyona]] and the Emperor** went their separate ways after stopping the [[Great Cataclysm]] but kept a **deep mutual respect**, and the Emperor has **never encroached on [[Pentamarone]], to honor her legacy.** This supports the "parted on terms" reading above.

**The slip.** At the fireworks festival, [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] asked Verre whether the Cataclysm's rift, a **portal to another world** opened by mortals, was ever healed:

> *"Oh, yes, the Magistus did what they had to do to **seal the thergy**."*
>
> *(Tommy's Session 13 correction: the word was "thergy", as heard and not yet spelled, not "rift". He flagged it as important.)*
> *"Ooh. I don't think I was supposed to say that."*

So the Magistus did not merely stop a surge of energy; they **closed a door.** What "did what they had to do" involved, and whether it relates to the member who **never returned**, is unknown. The portal material is **kept within the party at Verre's request.**

**Speculation (Aren's, unconfirmed):** Verre knows all this because she was **a disciple of one of the Magistus**, most likely **[[Rellyona]]**.

## Why They Matter

The Magistus sit at the intersection of every current mystery: the [[Great Cataclysm]], the [[The Fourth Magic|green magic]] that now secretly powers civilization, the anomalous magic at [[Pentamarone]], and the ambitions of the [[Endymion Empire]]. Because Emperor Endymion is untouchably powerful, **no one dares ask questions** about the Magistus openly.
