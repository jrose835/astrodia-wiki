The **WitchCrafters** are a **female guild of witches** based in **[[Pentamarone]]**, a walled artisan city on the [[Endymion Empire]]–[[Artmegía|Artmegia]] border. They combine spellcraft with artisanry, producing much of the **art and crafting supplies traded across the continent**, and they effectively **run the city.**

Their sigil — a **hammer, shears, and a needle** — flies over Pentamarone's gates and over their own walled guild compound inside it.

## Mission / Purpose

The guild's craft is exactly that — *crafting*: magically-made goods, art, and supplies distributed far beyond Pentamarone. What sets them apart is where they do it: **Pentamarone is the only place within [[Endymion Empire]] borders that is not under the Empire's jurisdiction**, despite the Empire's otherwise strict control over all magic (registries, approved schools, [[Keystones]]).

Their reputation travels. Per a trader at the Pentamarone docks:

> *"A little too artsy fartsy for my taste, but they make good stuff. Quality. Especially anything made by a WitchCrafter herself — it'll last your lifetime, if not more."*

## Session 11 — The Contract and the Research (September 9, 2026)

[[Madame Verre]] released the paralyzed party, heard them out, and **proposed a trade**: the guild's full effort at identifying the [[Golden Chalice]] in exchange for **her own undisclosed use of it**, the chalice returned afterward, **housing and meals**, and a **guarantee of no harm.** [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] had the party's warning about the Academy written into it; **[[Marka]]** drafted the document and **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** read it before anyone signed. Verre's estimate was **one week**, less with help.

### The Four Lines of Inquiry

Verre assigned every master in the guild, in under a minute:

| Team | Question |
|---|---|
| **[[Edel]] & [[Schmietta]]** | What the chalice is physically **made of** — metal, set jewels — and whether either can be **dated** to an era or place in [[Astrodia]] |
| **[[Haine]] & [[Pittore]]** | What the **conjured objects** are constructed of, and whether a copy **fully replicates** its original |
| **[[Seed]] & [[Ceramia]]** | The **living copies** — what they're made of, whether they're truly alive, and what animates them. *(Test subjects: the cats.)* |
| **[[Genni]] & [[Marka]]** | **Records** — any account of the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]], or of any chalice that manifests physical copies |

[[The Fallen]] embedded themselves across all four: [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] at Schmietta's forge, [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] with Haine and Pittore, [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] with Seed and Ceramia, and [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] in the books. **Two days of work cut Verre's seven-day estimate to three days remaining**, with no findings released yet.

### Their Method

This is the guild's real distinguishing feature, and Verre stated it plainly:

> *"Everyone thinks they rely too much on divination. As soon as your Identify spell doesn't work, everyone just assumes it's impossible. But this is **grassroots magic** — getting to the core, to the essence of things, without having to rely on a pre-existing spell."*

Where the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] casts a spell at a problem and declares it unsolvable when the spell fails, the WitchCrafters **take the object apart.** Nobody at the table had seen anyone do that before.

### What They Told the Party Over Drinks

![[Pasted image 20260909204641.png]]

- **On [[Madame Verre]]:** she has been a child *"for a very long time"*; not the mind of a child but *"the smugness of one"*; wise beyond her years; bored by everything except whatever is currently interesting; **and she keeps her word.**
- **On [[Genni]]:** she came alone from near the **Citadel**, won't discuss it, and is **not** a spy.
- **On the [[White Forest]]:** they know the curse (it threatened Pentamarone before it receded) and know a **coven** lived there, but nothing of its fate. One recalled a witch near the forest who worked with **[[Rellyona]]** — i.e. **[[Morgana]]**, whom Session 10 placed alongside **Verre** instead.
- **On [[Endymion Empire|Empire]] law outside the city:** all unregistered magic is illegal and requires **[[Keystones|power stones]]**; registration is possible; **whether a warlock can use patron magic at all under that system, nobody knows.**
- **On the city:** nothing to worry about. Shops, restaurants, temples, nature. A temple to **[[Venus]]**, none to **[[Pluto]]**.

## The Fireworks Festival

A newer [[Pentamarone]] tradition, falling **at the end of the week** as of Session 11: **every guild in the city competes** to stage the most breathtaking firework show. No limits on materials, spells, or method.

**The winning guild runs the city.**

**The WitchCrafters have won every year since its inception**, which is why they run it. Asked whether that comes down to magic or effort: *"Magic is effort, as I'm sure you know."* Asked whether they fear the competition: *"No one's ever really provided any."*

They invited [[The Fallen]] to enter. **[[Marka]]** was cagey about the guild's own plans, and let one thing slip about whatever Verre intends with the chalice:

> *"If it goes how we want it to go, it'll be **a beautiful unveiling.**"*

*(The festival was held in Session 12. Verre ultimately **declined to use the chalice**; whatever "unveiling" Marka meant remains unexplained.)*

## Session 12 — The Report and the Festival (September 23, 2026)

### Fireworks Prep and Tenure

While the rest of the party was in the alley with [[Aleister the Invoker|Aleister]], [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] were put to work on the **fireworks** — an informal tour, each witch showing off her part: **[[Schmietta]]** treating and pressing the gunpowder, **[[Pittore]]** explaining the chemistry behind the colors.

Every master was open about why she came to [[Pentamarone]] **except [[Genni]]**. The shared story: traditional schools of magic felt **too strict and not artistic enough.**

- **[[Haine]]** is the most senior — directly under [[Madame Verre]] for **about a decade.** Several of the others knew the guild by reputation long before joining.
- **[[Genni]]** is second most senior.
- **[[Ceramia]]** is the newest — really a young girl (*"unlike her boss"*) who *"showed a lot of promise."*

### Faith

The masters practice to varying degrees, but **most honor [[Venus]]**, because they understand their crafts as **channeling the Agent of Creation.** [[Marka]] asked Aren what had brought her to the group (*"a holy mission"* against the undead — *"We love a girl with a mission"*); the guild knows of no undead trouble in the city, only tall tales of **haunted ruins or a haunted mansion** nearby, and promised to keep an ear out. **[[Seed]]** asked Asha about the copy of her mother.

### Offers

- **Supplies for the [[White Forest]]**, if the party is set on going.
- *"Kind of crazy how it just happened that y'all came across this seemingly very powerful item. So, maybe it's fate."*

### The Truce, Per Verre

Relayed by [[Genni]]: [[Rellyona]] and the Emperor parted after the [[Great Cataclysm]] with deep mutual respect, and the Emperor has **never encroached on the city, to honor her legacy.** See [[Pentamarone]].

### The Report

On the fourth night Genni presented the findings in Verre's office. Headlines — full detail on [[Golden Chalice]]:

| Section | Team | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| **The chalice itself** | [[Edel]] & [[Schmietta]] (with Úlfr) | **Indestructible**; gold **too pure** to be of this earth — *"the idea of the pure essence of gold"*; age undeterminable |
| **History** | [[Genni]] & [[Marka]] (with Aren, Asha) | Best match: a **white-dragon-worshipping clan's** ritual object from the Great Ravine of [[Caelwyrm]], lost a few centuries ago |
| **The magic** | the whole guild | Undispellable; **all schools at once**; **nondetection covers the holder** |
| **The copies** | [[Haine]] & [[Pittore]] (with Leif); [[Seed]] & [[Ceramia]] (with Drolta) | Objects: perfect, non-magical duplicates. Living things: hollow husks. **The cats were put down** |
| **The cost** | — | Repeated use breeds **grandeur, superiority, a god complex** — cumulative, scaled to the request |
| **Something from nothing** | — | No components, no ambient magic, no siphoning (unlike the [[Alchemy Jug]]). A violation of **[[Venus]]'s** domain — *"that is what makes it sinful"* |

### Verre Withdraws

Genni argued the chalice **shouldn't be used** for Verre's goal. **Verre agreed** — *"I think the risk is too great. And I don't want what we're working on to be tainted"* — and **returned it**, honoring the contract without exercising her right. The goal remains undisclosed. She also revealed she **knows [[Simone]]'s sand-map spell**; the guild will look for the other Spoils with it.

### The Festival

The festival went ahead that night. The **WitchCrafters' show** played as an animated sequence, and the guild was out mingling with the town. **The competition's results weren't covered.** Verre, in a very good mood, **invited the party back** for when *"maybe we'll be done with our project."*

## Structure & Access

- **Open classes** are taught to the townsfolk of Pentamarone in each master's respective craft. Show **sufficient skill** and you can begin studying under a master witch — *"but not all are capable of getting to the level of skill that the witches require."*
- **The guild market runs once a month**, and is the only place to buy genuinely **magic-infused** goods. Everything in the city's ordinary market circle is the work of **novices and junior members** — beautifully made, entirely mundane. *(Next guild market: roughly two weeks after Session 10.)*
- **Audiences with [[Madame Verre]] are effectively unavailable.** Commoners deal with other guild members. The party's persuasion attempt to be taken to her **failed**; she came to them instead.

## The Mystery

Simone's map of [[The Fourth Magic|the fourth magic]] shows an **anomalous spike of green magic at Pentamarone** — one of only two spikes that don't sit at a major capital. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] speculated the WitchCrafters may have **learned to harness the fourth magic** — perhaps as leverage to keep the Empire at bay. **[[The Fallen]] spent two days in the guild's own city, walked through its guild hall, and never asked.**

## What They Want

The single largest unknown about this guild is now its head's motive. Overheard by the party as [[Haine]] argued against the deal:

> *"Are you sure this is a good idea? You don't really know these people, or this chalice."*
> *"I have a good feeling about this group. And this chalice. **It's the best lead we've got.**"*

**A lead toward what has not been stated to anyone.** Verre will say only that she has **one goal**, that the chalice may help her reach it, and that she will hand the chalice back once she has. *(Session 12: she **handed it back unused** — the risk too great, and she didn't want the project *"tainted"* — and still won't say what the goal is, *"until I'm certain of a few other things."*)*

## Ties Worth Watching

- **[[Morgana]] — and [[Rellyona]].** A [[Freedale]] refugee said Morgana **worked with [[Madame Verre]]**; the guild's own masters say she worked with **[[Rellyona]]**, Verre's dead predecessor. Both accounts passed insight. Morgana also steered fleeing Freedale families toward Pentamarone as a **safe haven**, implying a long-established relationship with the guild.
- **Two child-shaped masters.** [[Madame Verre]] and [[Ceramia]] both appear to be small girls. Ceramia acts like one; Verre very much does not. Nobody in the guild questions either. *(Resolved in Session 12: Ceramia **really is** a young girl; Verre's youth is an **experimental accident** — see [[Madame Verre]].)*
- **They know about [[The Fourth Magic]] now.** [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] told Verre. Her answer about the guild's own magic was expertly noncommittal — natural magic, *"whether that be arcane, whatever it may be"* — and [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s insight said she was meeting him exactly where he stood and no further.
- **The Empire's symbol inside the guild.** [[Genni]]'s **staff** and the **safe under her desk** both bear the symbol of the magic the [[Endymion Empire]] uses. The party's benign reading: this is where the guild stores the **[[Keystones]]** its members need to cast legally outside the city. The less benign reading is that the guild's independence is a **front**.
- **On the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]]**, Genni was diplomatic — mutual respect, magic as academic study versus creative expression, *"some say they have radical overreach, but they've also held out against the Endymion Empire."* On the Empire itself she said only: ***"Yes. But I would know."*** She left before anyone could ask what she meant.
- **Glass as an arcane focus.** [[Madame Verre]] channels through glass, which bears directly on [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s recovered **glass orb** and on [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s plans for the [[Nithing Pole]].
