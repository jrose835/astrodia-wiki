The **WitchCrafters** are a **female guild of witches** based in **[[Pentamarone]]**, a small artisan town on the [[Endymion Empire]]–[[Artmegía|Artmegia]] border. They combine spellcraft with artisanry, producing much of the **art and crafting supplies traded across the continent**.

## Mission / Purpose

The guild's craft is exactly that — *crafting*: magically-made goods, art, and supplies distributed far beyond Pentamarone. What sets them apart is where they do it: **Pentamarone is the only place within [[Endymion Empire]] borders that is not under the Empire's jurisdiction**, despite the Empire's otherwise strict control over all magic (registries, approved schools, [[Keystones]]).

## The Mystery

Simone's map of [[The Fourth Magic|the fourth magic]] shows an **anomalous spike of green magic at Pentamarone** — one of only two spikes that don't sit at a major capital. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] speculated the WitchCrafters may have **learned to harness the fourth magic** — perhaps as leverage to keep the Empire at bay. Pentamarone was reportedly **founded by one of the [[Magistus]]**, tying the guild's independence to the very spellcasters who stopped the [[Great Cataclysm]].
