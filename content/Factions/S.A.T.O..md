The **S.A.T.O. — Students Against Tyrannical Overreach** (jokingly shortened to "**Satyrs**") is a clandestine group of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] students drawn from the same cohort. Once classmates, they are now scattered across the various colleges, and they reunited in secret after [[Asha Nyvani Korré]] was **expelled** for researching a forbidden artifact tied to the [[White Forest]].

## Mission

They view Asha's expulsion as an antithetical **abuse of academic freedom**. Their goal is twofold:

- Discover *why* the topic is forbidden — what the Academy is hiding about the [[White Forest]] artifact and the [[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]].
- Clear Asha's name and restore her standing.

The Satyrs are the **inside operatives** — students embedded across the colleges (and even, in [[Tobin]]'s case, undercover in Academy administration). Because they cannot move freely without drawing the Academy's attention, they recruited an **outside adventuring arm**: [[The Fallen]], already Asha's allies and proven capable.

## The Safehouse

To bring the party in without being tracked, the S.A.T.O. drugged them with **sleeping wine** — not out of malice, but so the group could travel undetected. The party woke, unrestrained and with all gear intact, in the basement of [[Myrna Nor]]'s family vacation beach house in [[Lathen]], which now serves as the group's safehouse. Members can return there via teleport runes.
