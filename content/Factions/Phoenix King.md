## The Fire Kings

The **Fire Kings** are the **male worshippers of the Phoenix King**, and they live on **[[Fire King Island]]**. Their order is separate from the female order of **[[Nephthys]]**, the Phoenix Queen. Per [[Petra]] (Session 13), **women are not privy to the higher rites or secrets** on the island because of that division. *(A name heard at the table as "Garunix" came up in that conversation, possibly the Phoenix King's own; unconfirmed.)*

## Session 13 — Relics for the Rebirth (October 7, 2026)

- **The island keeps relics of both the Phoenix King and Queen**, held **for when the Phoenixes are reborn**, so the items will be waiting for them. What they are isn't known.
- After [[Lady Nectaria]]'s attack on the [[Temple of Nephthys]], the Fire Kings **sent one priestess**, Petra, to help rebuild, and **raised security on the island**. There has been little contact between the island and the temple since.
- Some of these relics, including **robes of Nephthys**, may be able to **break the contracts that bind the undead** (per [[Nephthara]]).
