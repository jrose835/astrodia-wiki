The **Sundered Watch** *(name as heard at the table — unconfirmed)* is a small adventuring party of **resistance fighters against the [[Endymion Empire]]** who move from town to town. [[The Fallen]] first met them in Session 11, cornering **[[Aleister the Invoker]]** in a back alley of [[Pentamarone]], and fought [[Cocytus]] alongside them. They introduced themselves by name in Session 12.

> *"We're a group that we call the **Sundered Watch**. We're a group of resistance fighters against the Endymion Empire. So we kind of go from town to town."*

## Mission

- **Resisting the [[Endymion Empire]]**, working town to town. Nothing more about their aims, operations, or allies has been established.
- **Right now: hunting [[Aleister the Invoker]].** They have been on his trail **for weeks**, and after patching up in [[Pentamarone]] they plan to **get back on it.**

## Their Grievance with Aleister

Their account, told in full in Session 12:

- They met Aleister in a town **far to the north**, where he offered to help them explore a set of **ruins** rumored to hold a magical artifact.
- Underground they were **overrun by undead**. Aleister summoned one of his creatures to fight them off.
- While the fighting went on, he **walked straight to the artifact and took it**: **a sword**. He decided it *"wasn't going to be able to help him,"* **tossed it aside, and fled.**
- His summon killed the undead and then turned on them. **Two of their friends died.** The survivors only made it out because the creature *"got bored of us."*

The location of the ruins has not been identified. [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] made sure [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] heard about the **undead-infested ruins**, as a possible lead for her mission.

## Relations with The Fallen

After the fight, the Watch **apologized for dragging the party into it.** The party gave them **the name of their inn** and said they'd be in town at least the week. Both sides agreed adventurers should stick together.

### Session 13

**They never came by.** The party had told them where they were staying, but the Watch never called at the inn before the party left [[Pentamarone]]. *"I guess they just didn't like us."* [[Genni]], who was once the Emperor's right-hand assistant, said that if they sought her out she *"may be able to answer some questions."*
