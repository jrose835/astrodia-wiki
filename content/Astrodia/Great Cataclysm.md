A surge of magical energy across [[Astrodia]] that corrupted and nearly destroyed everything in its path, around 100 years ago

## Session 6 — The Map of Magic

Simone's enchanted magic-map (see [[Types of Magic]]) revealed that the newly discovered [[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]] (green sand) traces the **exact path of the cataclysm**: from the **northwest of the [[Endymion Empire]]**, **southeast through the [[Terget Forest|Purodetapo Forest]]**, down to the **Prana Mountains**. This fourth magic now secretly **powers every major civilization in Astrodia** — yet no one in power is talking about it. Why the cataclysm's path and this unknown magic coincide, and why the magic also appears in hotspots *off* that path, remain open mysteries.

## Session 7 — The Magistus

The party recalled that the Cataclysm was **stopped by the [[Magistus]]** — a group of spellcasters (three by some accounts, four by others, with only three returning). Only one is known to still live: **Emperor Endymion** of the [[Endymion Empire]]. That the green fourth magic runs along the very line the Magistus supposedly *stopped* — and now powers the Empire and the anomalous town of [[Pentamarone]] (founded by another Magistus) — has made [[The Fallen]] deeply suspicious of the official history.
