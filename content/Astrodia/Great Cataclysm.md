A surge of magical energy across [[Astrodia]] that corrupted and nearly destroyed everything in its path, about **150 years ago** *(see [[Great Cataclysm#Timeline Note|Timeline Note]])*.

*(Revealed in Session 12: it was **caused by mortals opening a portal to another world**. See [[Great Cataclysm#Session 12 — It Was a Door (September 23, 2026)|below]].)*

## Session 6 — The Map of Magic

Simone's enchanted magic-map (see [[Types of Magic]]) revealed that the newly discovered [[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]] (green sand) traces the **exact path of the cataclysm**: from the **northwest of the [[Endymion Empire]]**, **southeast through the [[Purodetapo Forest]]**, down to the **Prana Mountains**. This fourth magic now secretly **powers every major civilization in Astrodia** — yet no one in power is talking about it. Why the cataclysm's path and this unknown magic coincide, and why the magic also appears in hotspots *off* that path, remain open mysteries.

## Session 7 — The Magistus

The party recalled that the Cataclysm was **stopped by the [[Magistus]]** — a group of spellcasters (three by some accounts, four by others, with only three returning). Only one is known to still live: **Emperor Endymion** of the [[Endymion Empire]]. That the green fourth magic runs along the very line the Magistus supposedly *stopped* — and now powers the Empire and the anomalous town of [[Pentamarone]] (founded by another Magistus) — has made [[The Fallen]] deeply suspicious of the official history. *(Revealed in Session 12: the Magistus **sealed the rift** the Cataclysm came through — see below.)*

## Session 12 — It Was a Door (September 23, 2026)

In a private audience, [[Madame Verre]] told [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] something she asked him **not** to share. He shared it with [[The Fallen]] behind a closed door, and **it is kept within the party at Verre's request**. Verre warned it could *"really impact some people"* if it got out.

> **The Great Cataclysm was caused by a group of people opening a portal to another world.**

- The rift let **magic that isn't of this world** into Astrodia: **[[The Fourth Magic]]**, which runs along the Cataclysm's path because it *leaked in* through the rift. To Verre's knowledge it is **still present** across Astrodia.
- **Other things came through with it.** Leif's patron, **[[The Whispering One|Sorath]]**, is probably one of them.
- **Who opened the portal is lost to time.**

At the fireworks festival Leif asked the follow-ups:

- **Origin:** the **northern mountain range of what is now the [[Endymion Empire]]**, from where it **traveled southeast** across Astrodia, matching the green line on [[Simone]]'s map.
- **Was the rift sealed?** *"Oh, yes, the [[Magistus]] did what they had to do to seal the **thergy**."* *(Tommy's Session 13 correction: her word was "thergy", as heard and not yet spelled, not "rift", and he flagged it as important. What a "thergy" is, is unknown.)* She pursed her lips: *"Ooh. I don't think I was supposed to say that."*

**At the [[Temple of Venus]]**, [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] asked her patron **[[Nephthys]]** about a portal to another world and beings coming through it. After a silence:

> *"I cannot speak of this freely."*
> *"...But continue on your path."*

**Party speculation (unconfirmed):** that [[Dogmatika]]'s new god and saints, which the temple priest guessed were founded about a century or a bit more ago, might also have come through the rift.

**Still unknown:** who opened the portal, exactly where, and whether the seal holds.
