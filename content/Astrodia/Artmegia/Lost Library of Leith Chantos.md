The final library built by [[Leith Chantos]], located approximately 4 days northeast of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]], built into a mountainside.

Like the other libraries Leith Chantos built across [[Astrodia]], this one likely contains the buried remains of his murdered family members. It is believed to house the **[[Nath Loom]]** — his spellbook of poisons and antidotes.

## Layout (explored so far)

1. **Cage Room** — Entry room with a warg cage. [[Powell]]'s goblin mercenaries were camped here.
2. **Old Barracks** — 4 beds and empty storage lockers. Iron portcullis with winch blocks the corridor north.
3. **Bear's Cavern** — Natural cave with a stream, accessible through a hole in the wall. A brown bear lived here alongside skeletal remains bearing a magical amulet (Divination magic).
4. **Supply Room** — Crates of wooden slats, leather, and parchment. Three hobgoblins were searching here. Contains a **secret door** leading to the circular library.
5. **Circular Library** — Magnificent multi-level room filled with thousands of books. One book displayed in a **glass case** (likely the [[Nath Loom]]). Balcony circles the upper level. Goblins present inside, trying to make a fire. Accessible via the barracks portcullis or the supply room secret door.

## Current Status

The party is taking a short rest in the supply room (Session 4). [[Powell]] and potentially more mercenaries are still unaccounted for. The goblins in the circular library are unaware of the party's presence.
