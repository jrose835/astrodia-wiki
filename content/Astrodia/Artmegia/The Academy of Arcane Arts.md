The most prestigious school for magic in [[Astrodia]], located in [[Artmegía]]. The main campus is called **the Acropolis**.

Teleportation circles exist at the Academy, costing 50 gold per group of 8 to use.

## Colleges

**[[Silverquill college]]** - Focuses on words, encompassing literature (including fiction, essays, drama, and poetry), languages, linguistics, oratory, rhetoric, and dramatic performance.

**[[Witherbloom College]]** - Teaches necromancy. Located on a satellite campus to the south of the Acropolis. [[Aren Greycloak]] has set out to investigate and possibly destroy this college. **[[Professor Lang]]** is based here and may be able to help with rare diseases.

**[[Lorehold College]]** - Focuses on history, archaeology, and anthropology. Its campus is [[Pillardrop]]. Where [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] studied before being expelled for researching forbidden knowledge related to the [[White Forest]].

**Prismari College** - One of the five colleges. Little detailed yet.

**[[Quandrix College]]** - One of the five colleges. Its campus is the one closest to the [[White Forest]].

The Academy follows a five-college structure: Silverquill, [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]], Prismari, [[Witherbloom College|Witherbloom]], and [[Quandrix College|Quandrix]].

## Magical Item Buyback Program

The Academy has posted an official decree across [[Artmegía]] offering gold compensation for any magical items brought to the main campus. [[Lucian Cugg]] is attempting to sell the [[Golden Chalice]] through this program.

### Arcane Reclamation Initiative

The formal office handling item submissions. Staffed by **[[Ms. Maris]]** and junior apprentices such as **[[Tobin]]**. Three-step process: initial Identify assessment, hazard/curse evaluation, and scholarly review. Submitters must sign an enchanted disclosure form (reveals true name if a false one is attempted). The Initiative appears to have a watch list of specific items — see [[Golden Chalice]] for details.

## The Biblioplex

The grand central library of the Academy. Majestic marble archways, tomes for miles. Houses a **Snarl** (tangled essence of pure magic) in the [[Hall of Oracles]], which serves as a power source for the university. Staffed by **cogwork archivists** — magical constructs that guide visitors to texts.

- **1st Floor:** Open to public and first-years
- **2nd Floor:** Restricted to upper-years and professors
- **3rd Floor:** Prohibited

An **Oracle of the Academy** exists somewhere within — supposedly the most powerful being at the school.

## Campus Locations

- **[[Bow's End Tavern]]** — Popular on-campus tavern. Owned by **[[Tulk]]** ("the Bulk"), a full orc. Known for Dwarven Ales and more exotic options.
- **[[University Inn]]** — On-campus lodging, 5 gold/night.
- **[[Fire Jolt Café]]** — Most popular campus café, owned by **[[Alina Tanglewood]]**. Known for permanent illusion décor and the 10-gold Fire Jolt Latte (grants fire resistance for 1 minute).
- **[[Rose Stage]]** — Performance space on campus.

## Access

Students receive an ID that allows them to use the Academy's teleportation circles. Expelled students like [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] have their IDs revoked.

## Session 6 — The Central Mystery (May 31, 2026)

[[Simone]]'s enchanted map of magic exposed a profound anomaly: **none of the three known magics spike at the Academy.**

- **Arcane** magic spreads uniformly everywhere, yet does **not** concentrate at the Academy despite the **Snarl** and the **[[Biblioplex]]**.
- **Higher-being** magic is concentrated where expected across [[Astrodia]] — but shows **nothing** at the Academy, strange given it was reputedly founded by [[Dragons]] and giants.
- **Divine** magic shows only a **tiny, recently-appeared speck** — traced to the [[Golden Chalice]] the party brought back two days prior.

So **what actually powers the Academy is unknown.** This is now one of the campaign's central mysteries — see [[Types of Magic]] and [[The Fourth Magic]].

It was also confirmed that the Academy **suppressed [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s forbidden research** into the [[White Forest]], expelling her — the injustice that gave rise to the [[S.A.T.O.]]. The chalice was registered with the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]], placing the party on the Academy's watch list.
