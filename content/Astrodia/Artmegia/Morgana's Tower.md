The **wizard's tower** at the edge of **[[Freedale]]** — home of the witch **[[Morgana]]**, abandoned for roughly two decades and locally believed to be **haunted**. [[The Fallen]] broke in at the close of Session 8 and cleared it in Session 9, finding Morgana's body, her correspondence, and a working scrying pool.

It is now effectively **a party asset**: they hold the guardian's [[Guardian Command Amulet|command amulet]], they have copied the roof's teleportation runes, and they can return whenever they like.

## Layout

**Five levels above ground plus a basement.** A single spiral staircase connects all of them; the ground-floor front door is locked and unyielding, which is why the party's first entry was a rope to the **third-floor balcony**.

| Level | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Basement** | Morgana's **forge** (where she built her constructs), a warren of small rooms, and the chamber holding **her body.** The door was **barred from the outside** |
| **1st floor** | Entry hall, foyer, bathroom, closet, pantry, sitting room — and **the guardian** on patrol |
| **2nd floor** | Ring corridor around a sealed inner chamber, patrolled by **floating orbs**; the chamber holds the **scrying pool** |
| **3rd floor** | Bedroom (writing table, chest) and dining room/kitchenette; the **balcony** the party entered by |
| **4th floor** | **Library/study** (south) and **storage room** (north), plus a fire-trapped hall leading to Morgana's **workroom** |
| **Roof** | Open to the sky: a **teleportation circle** and **nine statues of the [[Agents]]** |

## Defenses

- **The shield guardian** (1st floor) — the **third** of the constructs Morgana built with [[Verelda]]. Answers to its [[Guardian Command Amulet|command amulet]], which the party now holds. **Currently stood down.**
- **The orb corridor** (2nd floor) — orbs radiating strong **evocation**, drifting counter-clockwise at walking pace. Anything they sense takes a **lightning bolt**. Passable by moving *with* them; [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] crossed wearing a **lightning-resistant cloak** found upstairs. **Still active.**
- **The fire statues** (4th floor) — twin statues firing crossing **cones of fire** at anything on the floor between them (a rolled barrel was reduced to ash in one trigger). Bypassable along the ceiling. **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] permanently disarmed them** by scratching off the small glyph etched into each statue's neck.

## Notable Features

### The Scrying Pool (2nd floor)

The **font** Morgana mentions in her journal — *"I've watched them through the font for the better part of a decade."* A pool of water radiating strong **divination** magic.

Morgana built it so **anyone proficient in Arcana can use it once per day, free of charge**, effectively casting **Scrying**. Targets make a Wisdom save, with a large penalty if the caster knows them well or possesses something of theirs. This is how [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] located her mother.

### The Roof — Nine Agents and a Circle

A **permanent teleportation circle**, whose runes the party has copied.

Ringing it, **nine winged statues** — one per canonical [[Agents|Agent]], with the disputed tenth ([[The Moon]]) absent. Each has a **basin with a lit flame**; **five burn dark blue**, four burn ordinary fire. The flames are **magical and tied to their specific Agent**, and the blue-flame group shares *something* the others don't — unknown what. Identified so far: **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]]**, **Saturn** (front and center, *not* blue), **[[Venus]]**, and **[[Pluto]]**.

### The Workroom (4th floor)

Behind the fire trap: a hanging **orrery** of celestial bodies and orbits, a work table of notes (where the copy of the letter to [[Verelda]] was found), **potions**, and **symbols drawn on the floor in chalk**.

The chalk radiates **transmutation**; the symbols are **abjuration — protection.** [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] worked out it's an **infusion circle**: something placed at the center gets imbued with abjuration magic. Morgana was warding something in her final months.

### The Component Store (4th floor)

Morgana's storage room, full of jars and boxes of **spell components** — feathers, coins, dust, string. The party can source **any material component up to 500 gp of value** here. It paid for itself immediately, covering the Identify cast on the guardian amulet.

### The Foyer Mail (1st floor)

A pile of **dusty, never-collected mail**, including [[Verelda]]'s reply letter under the **seal of the Academy** — undelivered for twenty years while Morgana lay in the basement.

## The Basement, and What Was In It

The basement door was **barred from the outside with planks** — someone wanted something to stay in. Beyond it, [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] sensed **necromancy**, and the party fought **two ghouls** (one already wounded by something they never identified) and then **a ghoul and a ghast**.

In the last room: **[[Morgana]]'s body** — dead about twenty years, but with flesh **eaten within the last few days** by the ghouls. Her eyes had been **gouged out before death** and her face covered with a **mask**; her corpse carries the marks of a prolonged **magical battle**. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] spoke last rites over her. The [[Guardian Command Amulet]] was in her pocket.

*(How the ghouls got into a barred basement, and what wounded one of them, are unresolved. Aren re-barred the door on the way out.)*
