The most popular café on the campus of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]]. Owned and managed by **[[Alina Tanglewood]]**, an elf woman.

## Interior

Clean and well-lit, with walls decorated by **permanent illusion magic** — animated cartoonish scenes including a frog wizard stirring a cauldron, dancing lightning bolts with faces, and a raven in a cravat sipping coffee. Features a back lounge area.

## Menu

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plain coffee/tea | 1 copper | Standard |
| Specialty drinks | 2 copper | Various |
| Fire Jolt Latte | 10 gold | Grants **fire resistance for 1 minute** |

## Events

- **Session 4**: The party met [[Myrna Nor]] here to discuss the [[Leith Chantos]] quest. Students held a frog race in the back lounge that went wrong when the frogs were enchanted with a transmutation substance and grew into **Giant Frogs**. The party defeated them. [[Murgaxor]] (expelled Bullywug student) suspected of being behind the incident.
