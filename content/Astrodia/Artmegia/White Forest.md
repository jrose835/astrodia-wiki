A region in [[Artmegía]]

Years ago, a curse began to spread from the White Forest, thought to be the home of a coven of witches, into the Kingdom, threatening [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] and its students. It is unclear why, but before the curse could do any real damage to the Kingdom, it retreated back into the Forest. Some believe the Forest is safe now, while others believe the curse could return at any moment and threaten Artmegia’s way of life again, but all who have gone to investigate haven’t returned.

## Session 6 — A Hotspot of Divine Magic (May 31, 2026)

[[Simone]]'s enchanted map of magic revealed a **HUGE spike of divine magic** centered on the White Forest — the **largest concentration in all of [[Astrodia]]**. This makes the Forest central to the mysteries the [[S.A.T.O.]] is investigating.

- **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] was expelled** from [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]] for researching a **forbidden artifact tied to this Forest** — the antithetical abuse of academic freedom that prompted the founding of the S.A.T.O.
- The White Forest is **[[The Fallen]]'s next destination**: they plan to bring the [[Golden Chalice]] here, away from [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] and toward answers.
- It appears tied to **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]** — the woman in black and red who has appeared in the dreams of Asha and [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]].

Cross-references: [[The Fourth Magic]], [[S.A.T.O.]].

## Session 7 — What Is Known of the Curse (July 15, 2026)

Piecing together accounts from [[Myrna Nor|Myrna]], [[Quintilius]], the butler [[Archibald]], and the [[Lathen]] priest, [[The Fallen]] assembled the fuller picture:

- The forest is named for its **ethereal, unnatural white trees** — clearly touched by magic (probably **divine**, per Simone's map).
- Decades ago, a **curse** spread from the forest as **cursed vines and thorns** that corrupted the land and **killed or corrupted anyone who touched them**. [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|The Academy]] and even the [[Endymion Empire]] sent people; all failed.
- After ~**10 years and roughly 50% progress into the continent**, the vines **suddenly receded on their own** — no one knows what stopped them.
- Even before the curse it was strange, with a persistent rumor of a **coven of witches** living within.
- **Quintilius's warning:** a cursed place that "almost destroyed the continent," now holding the **largest store of divine power in the world**.
- The Lathen priest knew nothing beyond the curse, and believed on faith it was ended by **divine intervention**; [[Archibald]] suggested finding a **scholar who studied the forest before the Academy's research ban**.
