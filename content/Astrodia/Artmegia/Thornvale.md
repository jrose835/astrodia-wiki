A town in [[Artmegía]], located about a day and a half's walk west of the party's starting village and east of the Acropolis. Larger than the starting village with a bustling market, though not as large as [[Bayhaven]].

## Notable Locations

**The Copper Cauldron** — A potion shop run by a former [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] student who studied potions. Sells healing potions, greater healing potions, climbing potions, antitoxins (30 gold each), and alchemist's fire.

**The Silver Tankard** — A tavern where the party met [[Lucian Cugg]] and took on the escort job.

**Quest Board** — Posted jobs including an Academy decree offering gold for magical items, escort work, odd jobs, and a wolf hunt.

## Session Events

### Session 1
The party arrived here en route to the Acropolis. They shopped at the Copper Cauldron, checked the quest board, and met [[Lucian Cugg]] at the Silver Tankard before heading north to recover the [[Golden Chalice]] from bandits and goblins.
