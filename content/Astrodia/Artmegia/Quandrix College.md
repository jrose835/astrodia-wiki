One of the five colleges of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] — the **college of numeromancy**, where students study patterns, fractals, and the fundamental mathematics of nature and magic (the Academy's "STEM" wing). Its campus is a striking place of geometric walkways, ascending ramps, cube-shaped fountains, aqueducts of solid water, and gardens of spiraling, fractalized plants and animals.

Its campus is the one **closest to the [[White Forest]]** and the [[Endymion Empire]] border, reachable by **teleportation circle**. Because of this, it is [[The Fallen]]'s stepping-off point toward the forest with the [[Golden Chalice]].

## Session 7 — Stepping-Off Point (July 15, 2026)

[[Archibald]] teleported [[The Fallen]] here using **publicly available circles**, so they'd arrive looking like ordinary travelers (though teleporting still risks alerting the Academy). [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] used **Disguise Self** to avoid recognition. The party did **not** linger — they stepped out and immediately set off on the **~6-day overland journey to the [[Nephthys]] temple**. [[Simone]] provided the campus runes so they can return.
