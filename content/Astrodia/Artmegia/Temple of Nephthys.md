The **Temple of Nephthys** is the temple of **[[Nephthys]]**, the Phoenix Queen, where **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** served as **high priestess** until **[[Lady Nectaria]]**'s vampires attacked it about **five years ago**. It is a Gothic cathedral of spires and stained glass, with a **great phoenix in the rose window**, built into the side of a **mountain or large hill**. Part of the interior runs back into the rock. It stands near the edge of the **[[White Forest]]**, in a **higher-being (red) magic hotspot** on [[Simone]]'s map of magic. That hotspot is the reason [[The Fallen]] first marked it as a destination in Session 6.

A **statue of Nephthys in human form**, a woman rather than a phoenix, stands out front.

## History

- **The attack (about five years ago).** Vampires led by [[Lady Nectaria]] attacked the temple **searching for something.** Many of the faithful died, including children in Drolta's care. Drolta destroyed the attackers with holy flame, nearly died, and was remade by Nephthys as a **dhampir**. She then left on the mission Nephthys gave her. *(Revealed in Session 14: Nectaria got in because **a five-year-old girl invited her.** See below.)*
- **Rebuilding.** Locals helped rebuild. The Fire Kings of [[Fire King Island]] sent a priestess, **[[Petra]]**, who stayed. A visiting priest was paid out of temple funds to lay a **protective spell** over the temple *(heard as a "hallow" spell)*. **There has been no money to recast it, and nobody knows whether it still holds.** There have been no attacks since.
- **A new congregation.** Many longtime worshippers left after the attack, and the temple rebuilt with new people.
- **A new high priestess.** **[[Solenne]]**, an orphan taken in about two years after Drolta left, was anointed in **Session 13**, the day The Fallen arrived.

## Layout *(Session 13)*

- A long nave of pews. A **hidden door to the vault** is just inside the entrance, to the right. It is locked, and the key is kept in a chest in the priestesses' quarters behind a kitchen. The vault turned out to be **much smaller than the party expected.**
- An **inner sanctum**, holy ground **open only to those permitted** (Session 14).
- **Two further doors lead deeper into the mountainside.** [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] judged it unlikely the attackers came in that way.
- **Graves** can be seen from a window.
- The temple offers **lodging to travelers**, apparently including people fleeing the [[Endymion Empire]].

## The Vault

It holds the **belongings of those who died in the attack**. [[Ysmene]] offered to let Drolta and the party look through them. In **Session 14** it served as a **holding cell for Drolta**, knocked out and tied up so [[Lady Nectaria]] couldn't use her. Among the belongings, [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] found **a little girl's journal** (see below).

## What the Attackers Wanted *(Sessions 13–14)*

Per **Nephthara**, as relayed by Drolta:

- The undead are **bound under some sort of contract**, and certain **relics** carry enough power to **break those bonds.** That includes **robes of Nephthys**, which may be **what the vampires were looking for.**
- **The ashes Drolta used to guard were untouched** in the attack.
- **The vampires must have been let in.** They can't enter a sanctuary uninvited.

**Session 14:** the relic is **the dress Nephthys wore in her human form**, said to **break chains and destroy unjust hierarchies**, including a vampire's bond to its sire. It is kept on **[[Fire King Island]]**, not here, and Nectaria knows it.

## Who Let Them In *(Session 14)*

The journal from the vault answered it. **A five-year-old girl**, the daughter of a **mason** who worked at the temple and a child **Drolta loved like her own**, was befriended over several days by **a woman with white hair**: Nectaria. The woman learned her name, gave her a flower, asked about the prettiest parts of the temple, and mentioned that she could only come in **if somebody invited her.** On the last night, at the gate while the guards were busy unloading, she asked to see the windows from the inside:

> *"Of course you can. Come on in."*

**The girl died in the attack.** *(Her name was heard as "Santa"; unconfirmed.)*

## Session 13 — Homecoming (October 7, 2026)

[[The Fallen]] teleported in from [[Pentamarone]], using Drolta's **plume** as the associated object, and arrived at midday as the **anointing of Solenne** was finishing. Drolta walked in partway through the rite and was recognized. The party acquired the vault key by **Suggestion** and returned it unused, and Drolta reconnected with her sisters. At sunset **[[Lady Nectaria]]** appeared on the grounds with **Fiendish Icons** and then reinforcements. See [[Session 13 Recap — Homecoming]].

## Session 14 — The Siege (October 21, 2026)

The priestesses fought for the temple from its doorway while The Fallen lost the fight outside to Nectaria and **two vampire spawn**. One of them, **Damien**, was destroyed. Everyone retreated inside. **Churches are sacred ground**, so the vampires had to stop at the open door.

Nectaria demanded **[[Solenne]]** by midnight. The plan was for everyone to hole up in the vault. Instead, once the party realized Nectaria could **take over Drolta**, they knocked Drolta out, **locked her in the vault alone**, held the nave, and **doused every door in holy water.** At midnight, with the bells ringing and Nectaria rapping on the door, **[[Petra]]** walked to the door in a trance to invite her in. The party stopped her. Nectaria withdrew: *"I'll always find my way in."*

**Afterward:** [[Nephthara]] plans to have a local priest **recast the Sanctuary spell** *(the protective spell heard in Session 13 as "hallow")*, which should keep vampires out **even with an invitation.** The temple is on **high alert.** Petra has left. The priestesses gave the party **four potions of healing.** See [[Session 14 Recap — Invitation Only (Shillelagh her down)]].
