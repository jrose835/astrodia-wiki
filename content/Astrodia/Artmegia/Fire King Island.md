 Not technically a part of the Kingdom of [[Artmegía]] but they have a good relationship as the island is largely inhabited by those that continue to venerate the Phoenix King, a Higher Being, and some temples to the Phoenixes can be found around Artmegia proper.


The island is home to the **Fire Kings**, the **male worshippers of the [[Phoenix King]]**. It is a **red (higher-being) hotspot** on [[Simone]]'s map of magic.

## Session 13 — What Petra Said (October 7, 2026)

[[Petra]], a priestess the island sent to the [[Temple of Nephthys]] after [[Lady Nectaria]]'s attack, told [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]:

- The island houses **relics of high importance belonging to both the Phoenix King and the Phoenix Queen**, kept **for when the Phoenixes are reborn**. She doesn't know what they are.
- **Security was increased** after the attack on the temple, but it has been years.
- She doesn't think the [[Great Cataclysm]] affected the island's temple.
- **Women are not privy to the higher rites** there, because of the division between Nephthys's order and the Phoenix King's.

Per [[Nephthara]], some of those relics, including **robes of [[Nephthys]]**, have the power to **break the contracts that bind the undead**, and may be what vampires are hunting. **The island has not been attacked.** Drolta named it as a possible next destination for learning more about the Cataclysm.

## Session 14 — Nephthys's Dress Is Here (October 21, 2026)

What [[Lady Nectaria]]'s vampires actually want is **on this island**, in the Fire Kings' temple: **the dress [[Nephthys]] wore in her human form.** It is said to **break chains and destroy unjust hierarchies**, including the bond that chains a vampire to its **sire.** **Nectaria knows it isn't at the [[Temple of Nephthys]].** Drolta asked [[Petra]] to make sure it is protected.

Petra, it turned out, had been **seeded by Nectaria** and tried to let her into the temple at midnight. She has left the temple and says she **won't return to the island** anytime soon, for fear of bringing the vampires with her. See [[Session 14 Recap — Invitation Only (Shillelagh her down)]].
