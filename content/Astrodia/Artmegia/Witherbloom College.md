A satellite campus of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]], located to the south of the Acropolis. Teaches necromancy.

[[Aren Greycloak]] has set out to investigate and possibly destroy this college.

## Faculty

**[[Professor Valentine]]** — Dean of Witherbloom. A **full vampire** and sangromancer (receives visions from blood he drinks). Has denounced the higher vampires, which has made him a target for vampire assassins. Serves as mentor to **[[Aurora Luna Wynterstarr]]**. Potential ally for vampiric research.

**[[Dean Lassette]]** — Witherbloom's top healer and herbalist. Rumored to have once cured a dragon of a secret illness. Potentially relevant to [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s sister's illness.

**[[Professor Lang]]** — Recommended by a potion seller in [[Thornvale]] as someone who may be able to help with [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif's]] sister's mysterious illness. Also does cross-teaching with [[Quandrix]] on artificial life forms.

## Access

The potion seller in [[Thornvale]] noted that teleportation would be much quicker than walking to Witherbloom from the main campus.

## Session 7 — A Deferred Detour (July 15, 2026)

Witherbloom rose again as a destination: [[Rosie]] noted that vampires are the **only higher beings still active** and possibly the only witnesses to the **[[Severance]]**, making the vampire dean **[[Professor Valentine]]** a rare (if dangerous) source. [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] means to visit him, and [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] pointedly floated destroying him instead. [[The Fallen]] ultimately prioritized the [[Nephthys]] temple and [[White Forest]] via [[Quandrix College]], **deferring the Witherbloom trip** for later.
