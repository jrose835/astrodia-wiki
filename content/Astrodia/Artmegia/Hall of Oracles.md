A chamber within the **[[Biblioplex]]**, the grand central library of **[[The Academy of Arcane Arts]]**. At its centre sits a **Snarl** — a knotted, tangled essence of pure magic that serves as the **power source for the entire university** and affects magic around it in unpredictable ways.

[[The Fallen]] walked through it in **Session 3** and did not linger.

## The Oracle

Separately, and never explained, [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] has told the party that an **Oracle of the Academy** exists somewhere within the school — **supposedly the most powerful being there**, and not remotely approachable.

Whether the Oracle *is* the Snarl in this hall, lives beside it, or is something else entirely is unknown.

## Session 11 — The Repo Man Lead (September 9, 2026)

[[Tobin]], reporting from inside the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] on a **99**, could not say what the Academy's **"repo man"** actually is — only this:

> *"Don't know exactly what repo man is. Some kind of experiment academy is working on. **Oracle may be involved.**"*

The party's read: **the repo man may not be a man.** The table's leading theory is the Academy's **cogwork constructs**, powered or directed by whatever sits in this hall.

That was the best answer a near-perfect roll could produce, which the party took as a warning in itself. **[[Tobin]]'s standing assignment is now the Oracle**: *send all you can.*
