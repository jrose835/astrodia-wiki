The campus of **[[Lorehold College]]**, one of the five colleges of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]].

## Description

A **desert canyon city** of stark mesas and steep hills, located **northeast of the main campus** (the Acropolis). Pillardrop is built from and around the **excavated ruins of an ancient, unknown settlement** — fitting for Lorehold's focus on history and archaeology. The city is a mix of **carved cliff-halls** hewn into the canyon walls, **new construction**, and **precarious bridges** that pass alongside **enormous ancient stone statue-faces**.

It is reached by **teleportation circle**.

## Historical Context

Towering **star arches** and rocky peaks testify to the powerful magical entities that once dwelled here — energy once harnessed by the [[Agents]] who inhabited the land. The original builders of the ruins remain unknown, and uncovering their identity is part of Lorehold's ongoing work.
