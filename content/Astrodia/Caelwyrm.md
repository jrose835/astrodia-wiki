A part of the [[Closed Land]] continent.

Several sects of those living in the south of the Closed Land are dedicated to preserving or restoring the influence of the [[Primordial Beings]], and a conflict is brewing within many of the tribes of how exactly to go about that.

## Notable Locations

**The Great Ravine** — A massive ravine thought to have been carved by [[Dragons]] in ancient times. [[Lucian Cugg]] explored an excavated temple here and found the [[Golden Chalice]].

## Session 12 — The Chalice's Clan (September 23, 2026)

Researching the [[Golden Chalice]]'s history, [[Genni]] and [[Marka]] of the [[WitchCrafters]] searched for chalice references tied to Caelwyrm and found stories from the region's **dragon-worshipping clans**:

- One clan **worshipped a white dragon** and kept a **ritual object that could conjure whatever the dragon required.**
- **Rival dragon-worshipping clans fought over it** in the region of **the Great Ravine** — where [[Lucian Cugg]] later found the chalice.
- The last record of it **disappeared a few centuries ago.**

**Unconfirmed** that this object is the chalice, but it is the guild's best match. See [[Dragons]].
