A part of the [[Closed Land]] continent.

Several sects of those living in the south of the Closed Land are dedicated to preserving or restoring the influence of the [[Primordial Beings]], and a conflict is brewing within many of the tribes of how exactly to go about that.

## Notable Locations

**The Great Ravine** — A massive ravine thought to have been carved by [[Dragons]] in ancient times. [[Lucian Cugg]] explored an excavated temple here and found the [[Golden Chalice]].
