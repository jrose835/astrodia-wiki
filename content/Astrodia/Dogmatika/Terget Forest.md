A forest located in the [[Dogmatika]] Nation within the [[Closed Land]].

## Settlements

**Longleaf Holler** (also called **Awen** by locals) - A small village on the outskirts of the Terget Forest where [[Harlan "Red" Reed]] was born and raised. The villagers are mostly farmers who believe the land gives back what you put into it.

## Related Locations

- **Purodetapo Forest** - Located south of the Terget Forest, home to [[Leif Kalanchoe]] and the Unending Sky Clan of orc druids. It lies directly in the path of the [[Great Cataclysm]] and the [[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]]; it is where [[The Whispering One]] first reached Leif, and where **[[Howl]]** brought him **[[Leif's Glass Orb|the glass orb]]** he uses as a focus.

  **Two powers have taken an interest in it in as many sessions.** [[Madame Verre]] went still at the name of it (Session 11), and **[[Aleister the Invoker]]** — told that Leif found Howl there — announced *"I'll have to make a visit there."* Leif's parents, grandparents, and three younger sisters live in that forest.

## Recent Events

A mysterious cavalry in dark armor has been scouting the forest edges, warning villagers to watch for "the next saint."
