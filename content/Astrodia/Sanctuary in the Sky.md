The **Sanctuary in the Sky** is the home of the [[Solemn Pantheon]] and the [[Agents]], along with the angels who serve beneath them. It is the sanctuary from which the rebel angels, the [[Darklords|Dark Lords]], were cast down to earth.

## The Exile of She Who Fell (Session 12)

According to a **fringe sect** within the church of the [[Solemn Pantheon]] — described by the priest at the [[Temple of Venus]] in [[Pentamarone]] on September 23, 2026 — **a being was exiled from the Sanctuary in the Sky before the war with the Dark Lords**, and that exile is what **spurred the Morning Star to rebel.** In the version [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] had heard, she was **an angel, a woman**, who did something that **angered the gods so much she was exiled to earth.** Nobody knows her name, what she did, or whether she existed; she goes by the pseudonym **[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]**. The priest does not personally believe it.

The sect slips her into artworks as a **flaming meteor** — as in the mural beneath the Temple of Venus.
