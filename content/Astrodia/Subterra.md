A legendary **subterranean civilization** said to dwell deep beneath [[Astrodia]]. Long the stuff of legend, the Subterra are dismissed by most surface-dwellers as too secluded and remote to be worth bothering with.

## Discovery

During the exploration of the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]] (Session 6, May 31, 2026), the party found that the tunnel **beyond Una's grave** — past the spider-pit chasm — descended **deep underground**. [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] recalled (a Nat 20 history check) old legends of the Subterra, an underground people most consider too isolated to matter.

The party agreed that following the tunnel would be a **full expedition for another day** and left it undisturbed for now.

## Status

A **future expedition.** The deep tunnel beyond Una's grave in the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]] is the known route to them.

## First Appeared

Session 6 (recalled in legend by [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]).
