The **Purodetapo Forest** is home to [[Leif Kalanchoe]] and the **Unending Sky Clan** of orc druids. It lies **south of the [[Terget Forest]]** and **just north of the [[War Rock Mountains]]**. Life there isn't always easy (even the vegetation has teeth), and the forest sits **directly in the path of the [[Great Cataclysm]]** and of [[The Fourth Magic|the fourth magic]] that runs along it.

## Significance

- **Leif's home.** His parents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins, and three younger sisters live there. His sister's mysterious illness is the reason he gives for leaving.
- **On the Cataclysm's path.** On [[Simone]]'s magic map, the green fourth-magic line runs from the northwest of the [[Endymion Empire]] **southeast through the Purodetapo**. Leif began hearing voices after meditating in the forest (see [[The Orb]]).
- **Where Leif's patron first reached him**, and where **[[Howl]]** was found with an injured wing. She later brought him **[[Leif's Glass Orb|the glass orb]]** he uses as a focus.

## Session 11: Two Powers Take Notice (September 9, 2026)

- **[[Madame Verre]] went still** at the name of the forest when Leif explained where the orb came from: *"The Purodetapo Forest, you say."* She reversed herself on housing the party on the spot.
- **[[Aleister the Invoker]]**, told that Leif found Howl there, said: *"How interesting. The Purodetapo Forest, you say. I'll have to make a visit there."*

## Session 12: Sorath Probably Lives Here (September 23, 2026)

Per [[Madame Verre]], via Leif, Leif's patron **[[The Whispering One|Sorath]]** is a being **not of this world** that probably came through the [[Great Cataclysm]]'s rift and is **probably living in the Purodetapo Forest.** [[Aleister the Invoker]] **has fought Sorath before**, and he is the man who said he would visit. The [[The Sundered Watch|Sundered Watch]] mean to get back on his trail, but where he went after [[Pentamarone]] is not established.
