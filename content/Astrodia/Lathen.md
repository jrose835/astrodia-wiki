A small coastal **port city** and Academy **spring-break destination** where **[[Myrna Nor]]**'s family owns a **vacation beach house**. For roughly two weeks a year it's overrun by college students; the rest of the year it's a quiet town of locals just getting by.

## Description

The beach house holds a **wine cellar** stocked with expensive vintage from Myrna's parents' **vineyard south of [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]]**. Its **basement** currently serves as the **safehouse of the [[S.A.T.O.]]** ("the Satyrs"). The household is run by the improbably capable butler **[[Archibald]]**.

The town proper (explored in Session 7) has three rough quarters:
- A lax **town gate** with bored guards.
- A **nightlife/shanty district** — student rentals and taverns; the trashier quarter.
- A **business/port district** — warehouses and cargo staging where ships offload goods bound inland (the Nor family's supply-chain trade runs through here).
- A small **temple to [[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth, the Agent of Mysteries]]** on the eastern side, and a local **apothecary**.

## Session 6 — The Safehouse

After being drugged with a sleeping draught (disguised as a celebratory wine) in Myrna's [[Pillardrop]] dorm, the party — now calling themselves **[[The Fallen]]** — woke unrestrained in the basement here. The draught was a means to **travel undetected**, since [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] watches its people. Here the S.A.T.O. revealed themselves, demonstrated [[Simone]]'s map of magic, and laid out the plan to take the [[Golden Chalice]] to the [[White Forest]].

The party was given **teleportation runes** to return to the Lathen safehouse anytime.

## Session 7 — The Beach House & the Town

The party rested at the beach house, met the butler **[[Archibald]]**, and — over dinner — [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] named [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s patron **[[The Whispering One]]**. The next day they explored the town and visited its **temple to the [[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Agent of Mysteries]]**, where a priest laid out the [[Solemn Pantheon]], the disputed tenth Agent (**[[The Moon]]**), and the **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]**.
