**Pentamarone** is a walled **artisan crafting city** on the river that runs the border between **[[Artmegía|Artmegia]]** and the **[[Endymion Empire]]** — home of the **[[WitchCrafters]]**, and, in the words of its own gate guards, *"the last stronghold against the encroaching Endymion Empire."*

[[The Fallen]] arrived here **by accident in Session 10**, when [[Archibald]]'s teleport to the [[Nephthys]] temple went wrong and dropped them at a crossroads in unfamiliar woods several hours' walk to the north.

*(Earlier notes described it as a "small village" — that was Simone's map talking. It is a proper city: walled, gated, sprawling, on a navigable river, with a market circle, a docks district, and a guild compound of its own.)*

## The WitchCrafters

Pentamarone is home to the **[[WitchCrafters]]** — a female guild of witches who blend spellcraft with artisanry, producing much of the **art and crafting supplies traded across the continent**. They effectively **run the city**, and their head is **[[Madame Verre]]**.

## An Anomaly on the Map

Simone's enchanted map of magic shows the green **[[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]]** concentrated at every major seat of power (the Citadel, La Maison, the head church of [[Dogmatika]], [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]]) — plus **two anomalies** that don't fit. One is over a desert on the Lost Continent; the **other is Pentamarone**. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] suspects the [[WitchCrafters]] may have **learned to harness the fourth magic**, perhaps to resist the Empire.

> **Still uninvestigated.** [[The Fallen]] spent two in-game days in the city and did not ask a single person about the green magic.

## Independence from the Empire

Pentamarone is **the only place inside [[Endymion Empire]] borders that is not under the Empire's jurisdiction** — a striking exception given the Empire's otherwise total control of magic. It was **founded by [[Rellyona]]**, one of the [[Magistus]] — while another Magistus, **Emperor Endymion**, rules the Empire itself.

### Why the Empire Leaves It Alone — Three Rumors

A sailor at the docks gave [[The Fallen]] three mutually exclusive explanations, with no way yet to tell which is true:

1. **A truce.** [[Rellyona]] and **the Emperor went way back**; they made an agreement before she died, and he has honored it since.
2. **A facade.** The city is **secretly working with the Empire**, and its independence is theater.
3. **Fear.** The WitchCrafters are powerful enough to **hold their own**, and the Empire won't risk provoking them.

The townsfolk themselves don't dramatize it. Asked what would happen if Empire soldiers came close, a market glassblower said simply: *"They never do. It's just kind of an understood thing that this is an area free of their dominion."*

**Outside** the city's borders, the rules snap back: casting **unauthorized magic without [[Keystones|keystone]] authority** in Empire territory means the army makes it your problem.

## A Refugee City

The gate guards' first question to armed strangers is *"Are you escaping their reign, as many others who have entered the city have?"* — and they mean it as hospitality. **The city is openly welcoming to refugees**, including those who fled [[Artmegía|Artmegia]] during the **[[White Forest]] curse.** [[Morgana]] kept Pentamarone on a list of **safe havens** she gave to fleeing families out of [[Freedale]].

## Places in the City

- **The Gate** — open, lightly guarded, no artillery on the walls. The guards are welcoming and unimpressed by lies.
- **The Market Circle** — the heart of the city, and startlingly colorful after the sterility of the Academy: everything handmade, patchwork, and locally crafted. Stalls for **glassware, pottery, textiles and clothing, blacksmithing, gemstones, paintings, and books.** *Important caveat:* the market vendors are **novice artisans and junior guild members**; nothing here is magically infused.
  ![[Pasted image 20260826195548.png]]
- **The [[WitchCrafters]] Guild Hall** — walled off from the rest of the city, flying the city's crafting sigil. Entry hall, receptionist ([[Dolly]]), workshops, a **library/study** ([[Genni]]'s), and a **central courtyard garden.**
  ![[Pasted image 20260826211841.png]]
- **The docks** — on a river that runs down to the bay and out to the [[Expanse]]. Busy with traders; WitchCrafter goods are sought after worldwide.
- **The SS Nap Time** — the dockside inn: three long-grounded ships (or one three-decked one, depending who's describing it) converted into an inn and tavern. **1 gp a room.** No outside beverages, officially. *(Name coined by the party and adopted as canon.)*
- **A Temple of [[Venus]]** — overheard by [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] in the market. Located somewhere outside the market circle; the party has **not visited it.**
- **A large central inn** — passed over in favor of the docks.

## Craft, Magic, and the Guild Market

- Genuinely **magic-infused goods come only from the WitchCrafters themselves**, sold at a **guild market held once a month** — roughly **two weeks out** as of Session 10.
- The guild **teaches open classes to the townsfolk**; show sufficient skill and you can study under a master witch. Very few reach that level.
- A **firework festival** falls a couple of days after the party's arrival.
- **Glass is a viable arcane focus here** — [[Madame Verre]]'s own staff is glass.
