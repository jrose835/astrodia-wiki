**Pentamarone** is a small **artisan crafting town** on the river that runs the border between **[[Artmegía|Artmegia]]** and the **[[Endymion Empire]]**. Though modest in size, it holds outsized significance — and has become a target for [[The Fallen]]'s investigation into [[The Fourth Magic|the green magic]].

## The WitchCrafters

Pentamarone is home to the **[[WitchCrafters]]** — a female guild of witches who blend spellcraft with artisanry, producing much of the **art and crafting supplies traded across the continent**.

## An Anomaly on the Map

Simone's enchanted map of magic shows the green **[[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]]** concentrated at every major seat of power (the Citadel, La Maison, the head church of [[Dogmatika]], [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]]) — plus **two anomalies** that don't fit. One is over a desert on the Lost Continent; the **other is Pentamarone**, supposedly *"just a little village."* Why a small border town carries that magic is an open question. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] suspects the [[WitchCrafters]] may have **learned to harness the fourth magic**, perhaps to resist the Empire.

## Independence from the Empire

Uniquely, Pentamarone is **the only place inside [[Endymion Empire]] borders that is not under the Empire's jurisdiction** — a striking exception given the Empire's otherwise total control of magic. It was reportedly **founded by one of the [[Magistus]]**, the spellcasters who stopped the [[Great Cataclysm]] — while another Magistus, **Emperor Endymion**, rules the Empire itself. This hints at a **falling-out among the Magistus**.

## Getting There

Per [[Archibald]], the best approach is **by ship up the river** on the Empire–Artmegia border. Reaching it means crossing into contested territory; getting *back out* is harder, as Artmegia keeps strong borders and mostly admits only crisis refugees.
