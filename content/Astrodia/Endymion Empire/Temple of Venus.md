The **Temple of Venus** is a temple of **[[Venus]]**, the [[Agents|Agent]] of Creation, in the crafting city of **[[Pentamarone]]** — a city whose faith runs almost entirely to Venus. [[The Fallen]] visited it in Session 12 to learn whether Venus has enemies who might build something to mock her, and came away with the fullest account yet of the [[Darklords|Dark Lords]]' rebellion, the religions of the neighboring powers, and a fringe legend: **[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]**.

## The Statue

Out front stands a statue of Venus with her **three orbs**, the **muses of creation** — matching the Venus statue on the roof of **[[Morgana's Tower]]**. [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] has been seeing those orbs in his dreams.

## The Entry Hall

The entry hall holds books of scripture, which [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] and [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] read *(the investigation roll survived disadvantage)*:

- The **Solemn** created the world, made the **[[Agents]]** to govern its domains, and then **receded into the ether** (see [[Solemn Pantheon]]).
- Below the Agents were the **angels.** Some of them, the **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]**, rebelled and fell; the first was **the Morning Star.**
- **What caused the rebellion is disputed.**
- Venus lets those who pray to her **thrive and manifest what they desire.**

## The Priest's Office

A commoner pointed the party to the priest's office. The priest (unnamed) was happy to talk, and the party presented themselves as **"students of life"** doing anthropology. Nobody joined the church. His account:

- **Venus** watches over **all artistic expression and the making of goods and services**, working **through her three muses**, usually shown as spheres orbiting her.
- **No documented ill will between the Agents.** The **Dark Lords**, the Morning Star most of all, do hold ill will toward Venus as one of the hands they felt had overreached.
- **[[The Moon]]** is thought to be a disputed tenth Agent, or the **protector of the Agents** who *"makes sure that everything stays as it should."* On Red's dream of three spheres orbiting the moon, he suggested the divine may be inspiring Red to **create something with animals.**
- The **Church of [[Dogmatika]]** claims a new deity separate from the Solemn, rejects the Pantheon, and has miracle-working saints. It has grown exponentially in recent decades and was founded about a century or more ago, possibly around the [[Great Cataclysm]]. See [[God of Dogmatika]].
- Back in the office after the party saw the mural, he explained the meteor: the sect belief in **[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]**, which he does not share.

## The Basement Mural

![[Pasted image 20260923220651.png]]

At the priest's recommendation, the party went down to the basement to see the temple's **prize artwork**: **the Solemn and his host against the Morning Star and his armies**, at the moment the rebels were **cast down to earth.**

- **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] (History):** old, probably **a couple of centuries**, well preserved, and **unsigned**. It **wasn't painted here** but brought from **the South**, in a style from cultures that revere the **higher beings** (the oceanic kingdoms, [[Fire King Island]], the [[Phoenix King|Phoenix]] worshippers).
- **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] (Religion)** sensed something in the depiction didn't belong, and **Red** found it: a small **flaming meteor** in the middle of the scene, a **deliberate addition.** The sect behind it identifies it as She Who Fell.
- Drolta **communed with [[Nephthys]]** here, who said the battle was *"even before my time"* and that she *"cannot speak of this freely"* about a portal to another world.

## The Library and Classroom

Downstairs is a **Sunday-school classroom and library.** It had **no books on She Who Fell**, but:

- **Aren** read about **[[Pluto]]'s enemies**, and found **[[The Prophecy Destroyer]]** *(as heard)*.
- The **[[Endymion Empire]] enforces worship of [[Mars]]**, the Agent of Force; Pentamarone worships Venus.
- Illustrations identified **[[Uranus]]** (blue hair, red eyes, yellow beneath) and confirmed **[[Neptune]]** as the water-themed Agent.
- **No record of any conflict between Agents.**
