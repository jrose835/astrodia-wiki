All magic is strictly monitored within the borders of the [[Empire]]. Spellcasters can only learn certain types of approved magic at government run schools. Those with inherit magical capabilities are placed on registries and prevented from using magic except at pre-approved locations and times. Magical gems known as [[Keystones]] are distributed by the Empire’s government to local leaders to allow for the casting of approved spells to benefit the towns and cities by those not trained in magic. (Think of it like a magical welfare system). Some view this level of control as tyrannical and reprehensible, while others belief the harsh laws and support from the government have only made life better. However, the Empire is still somewhat reeling from a recent failed attempt to annex [[Marseille]].

## Emperor Endymion, the Magistus

The Empire is ruled from the **Citadel** by **Emperor Endymion** — revealed in Session 7 to be one of the **[[Magistus]]**, the spellcasters who stopped the **[[Great Cataclysm]]**, and the **only Magistus known to still be alive**. He is currently marching an army to **unify the continent**, and is considered possibly the **most powerful spellcaster in the world** — which is why no one dares ask questions about the Magistus, or about how the green **[[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]]** now powers his Empire from the Citadel.

Notably, **[[Pentamarone]]** — founded by *another* Magistus, **[[Rellyona]]** — sits inside the Empire's borders yet **outside its jurisdiction**. Whether that reflects a falling-out among the Magistus or an **old truce between Rellyona and the Emperor** is exactly what the party can't yet determine (see [[Pentamarone]] for the three competing rumors). *(Session 12: [[Madame Verre]]'s account, via [[Genni]], is the truce — the two parted with deep mutual respect, and the Emperor has never encroached on the city to honor Rellyona's legacy.)*

## Session 10 — First Sighting on the Ground (August 26, 2026)

[[The Fallen]] were dumped by a misfired teleport into Empire woodland north of [[Pentamarone]] and got their first look at the Empire's forces in the field.

- **The banner: a sword crossed with an axe.** [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] identified it; nobody else at the table recognized it.
- A **column marching south** from the north, roughly 30–45 minutes' march behind the party — and then, at a crossroads, it **split east and west** and stopped heading south entirely. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] read the maneuver as a **pincer.** Around what is unknown.
- Pentamarone's residents were unbothered: **routine patrols**, they say, which *"know not to get close to the city."*
- Confirmed by a trader at the docks: outside a sanctioned town's borders, being caught casting **unauthorized magic without [[Keystones|keystone]] authority** means the army makes it your problem.
- Unsettling detail from inside Pentamarone: the **safe in [[Genni]]'s study** and **Genni's own staff** both bear **the symbol of the magic the Empire uses.** The benign reading is guild-held keystones; the other reading is that the Empire's reach inside the "free" city is further than advertised.

## Session 12 — Religion, Resistance, and the Cataclysm's Origin (September 23, 2026)

- **The Empire enforces worship of [[Mars]], the Agent of Force** — found by [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] in the [[Temple of Venus]] library. Neighboring [[Pentamarone]] worships **[[Venus]]**. The two powers have two different patron Agents.
- **Organized resistance exists.** The adventurers who cornered [[Aleister the Invoker|Aleister]] in Pentamarone call themselves **[[The Sundered Watch]]** *(name as heard — unconfirmed)*: *"a group of resistance fighters against the Endymion Empire. So we kind of go from town to town."* They first met Aleister in a town **far to the north**, and lost two friends in **undead-infested ruins** there.
- **The [[Great Cataclysm]] began here.** Per [[Madame Verre]], it started in the **northern mountain range of what is now the Endymion Empire** and **travelled southeast** across Astrodia — matching [[The Fourth Magic|the green line]] on Simone's map. She also let slip that the Cataclysm was a **portal to another world**, and that **the [[Magistus]] sealed the *thergy*** *(Verre's word, as heard; first recorded as "rift" and corrected by Tommy in Session 13)*. *(She didn't say which of them; the Emperor is one of the Magistus.)*
- **Genni's account of the truce** — see above and [[Pentamarone]].

## Session 13 — The Emperor's Former Right Hand (October 7, 2026)

- **[[Genni]] was the Emperor's right-hand assistant.** [[Haine]] revealed it, and Genni confirmed the outline: raised in the Empire, she believed in the Emperor's work *"for the good of people and magic"* until *"some lines were crossed,"* and she fled. She doesn't know whether he knows where she is: *"I have a feeling. Just 'cause he knows a lot of things."* *"To his credit,"* he has kept his agreement never to touch [[Pentamarone]].
- **A power stone for the road.** Genni gave the party an empty **[[Keystones|power stone]]**, which in Empire territory works **like a passport**. See [[WitchCrafters' Parting Gifts]].
- **Exod's Cross** *(as heard)*, the northernmost and largest city of [[Artmegía|Artmegia]], is the crossing **everyone passes through between Artmegia and the Empire.**
- **Correction to the Session 12 entry above:** per Tommy, Verre said the Magistus sealed **the *thergy*** *(as heard)*, not "the rift."
- The [[Temple of Nephthys]] takes in travelers, apparently including people **fleeing the Empire** *(partly heard)*.
