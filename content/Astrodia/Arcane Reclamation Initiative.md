The [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy of Arcane Arts]] office that **registers, evaluates, and reclaims powerful magical artifacts** brought to campus. The formal desk is staffed by **[[Ms. Maris]]** and junior apprentices such as **[[Tobin]]**. Submissions go through a three-step process (initial Identify assessment, hazard/curse evaluation, scholarly review), and submitters must sign an enchanted disclosure form that reveals a false name if one is attempted. The Initiative maintains a **watch list** of specific items it has been told to look out for. *(During the Session 6 reveal it was also referred to as the "Arcane Reclamation Project.")*

## The Golden Chalice

The party submitted the [[Golden Chalice]] here in Session 3 ([[Ms. Maris]] offered 50 gold, which they declined). Because they signed the disclosure form, the chalice technically belongs to the Initiative, and the party has a **30-day deadline** before a "repo man" is sent to collect it (the clock is still ticking).

In Session 6 the party learned that **Tobin had registered the chalice** with the Initiative, so the Academy now **knows the party holds one** of its watch-listed artifacts. However, the **magically binding agreement** prevents repossession until the deadline expires.

## The Watch List — "the Sinful Spoils"

In Session 3, [[Tobin]] revealed the chalice matched one of several specific items the Initiative was told to watch for — which he could only half-remember as **"the Something Spoils."** In Session 6 the full name was revealed: **"the Sinful Spoils,"** pursued by the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]. The party suspects the Initiative's true aim is to gather up the Spoils and keep them out of the Seeker's reach. Three other item types remain on the watch list; the chalice was the first to come through.
