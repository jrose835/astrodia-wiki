**Marseille** is one of the last independent powers on [[Valedrune]]. Along with the Kingdom of [[Artmegía]] and the small city of [[Pentamarone]], it is one of the only holdouts left after the [[Endymion Empire]] swallowed nearly all of the continent's once-independent city-states. The Empire is **still reeling from a recent failed attempt to annex it.**

## What Is Known

- **A holdout against the Empire.** Per Tommy's pre-campaign lore post (see [[Starting Places]]), Marseille resisted annexation, and the failure still weighs on the Empire.
- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] wants to go there.** After her private audience with [[Madame Verre]] in Session 12, she said she wants to visit (heard at the table as "Marcio"): *"I know interesting ways of magic there."* It may hold something useful against her undead problem. She wouldn't say more.
- **[[Myrna Nor]] has been asked for its history**, via the [[Sending Stones]] in Session 12. No answer yet.
- **Possibly home to the Grand Spellbook Tower.** [[Marka]] of the [[WitchCrafters]] said in Session 11 that she studied at the Grand Spellbook Tower in "Marcell" *(as heard)*. It's likely the same city, but that hasn't been confirmed at the table.
