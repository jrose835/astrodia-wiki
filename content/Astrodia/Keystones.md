**Keystones** are magical gems **distributed by the [[Endymion Empire]]'s government** to local leaders, allowing **approved spells** to be cast for the benefit of towns and cities by people with no formal magical training. Think of it as a **magical welfare system** — and, simultaneously, as the mechanism by which the Empire keeps every legal act of magic inside its borders licensed and accounted for.

They are one leg of the Empire's control of magic, alongside **registries** for the innately magical and **government-run schools** teaching only approved disciplines. See [[Endymion Empire]].

## Enforcement

Per a trader at the [[Pentamarone]] docks (Session 10): outside a sanctioned town's borders, if the army catches you **casting unauthorized magic without keystone authority**, you are in serious trouble.

## The Symbol

Keystone magic carries a **distinct symbol** — recognized by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] in Session 10 as *"the symbol of the magic the Endymion Empire uses."*

She found it in an unexpected place: **on a safe under [[Genni]]'s desk** inside the [[WitchCrafters]] guild hall in [[Pentamarone]] — the one city the Empire does *not* rule — and **on Genni's own staff.**

- **Benign reading:** the guild holds keystones so its witches can cast legally when they travel **outside** Pentamarone's borders, and keeps them in a safe.
- **Less benign reading:** the Empire's reach inside the "free" city runs deeper than the city admits.
