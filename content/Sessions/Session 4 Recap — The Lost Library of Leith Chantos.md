## The Fire Jolt Café
![[Pasted image 20260419195020.png]]
The party headed to the **[[Fire Jolt Café]]**, the most popular café on campus. The interior is clean and well-lit, with walls decorated by **permanent illusion magic** — animated cartoonish scenes including a frog wizard stirring a cauldron, dancing lightning bolts with faces, and a raven in a cravat sipping coffee. The café is owned and managed by **[[Alina Tanglewood]]**, an elf woman.

- **Menu**: Plain coffee/tea = 1 copper; specialty drinks = 2 copper; **Fire Jolt Latte** = 10 gold (grants fire resistance for 1 minute)
- Three party members ordered plain coffee for 3 copper total.

### Meeting with Myrna Nor

**[[Myrna Nor]]** was found in a curtained private study room. She's a **4th-year [[Lorehold]] student** researching a thesis on [[Leith Chantos]].

Myrna noticed [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] and asked if he was a **Goliath or descendant of giants**. Úlfr deflected, saying he didn't know what that was and preferred not to be asked. Myrna mentioned she'd heard giants have some connection to the university, from the campus tour.

#### The History of Leith Chantos

Myrna provided the full backstory:

- **~500 years ago**, a kingdom was established under the **[[War Rock Mountains]]**, led by an orc priest named **Wound** with a figurehead ruler named **Uruth**. They were a warmongering tribe.
- Within that kingdom lived an **orc wizard named Naf**, unhappy with the kingdom's direction. He **fled to [[Artmegía]]** and settled in a small village with his wife and children, farming and studying arcane magic.
- Eventually, Naf felt guilty for abandoning his people. He had skilled crafters forge a **sentient axe** that could communicate with its wielder, hoping to use it to reconcile differences with the kingdom's rulers.
- He returned to the War Rock Mountains, leading to a **years-long power struggle between Naf, Uruth, and Wound**.
- Eventually, **communications from his family ceased**. Naf fled home to find **his family had been poisoned** — killed by a poison only Wound knew how to brew. Wound had sent spies to identify and murder Naf's family.
- Devastated, Naf donned **black robes of mourning**, forsook his birth name, and became known as **Leith Chantos**.
- He abandoned the power struggle and spent years **researching poisons and their antidotes**, compiling them into a book called the **[[Nath Loom]]** — a spellbook containing hundreds of poisons and their antidotes.
- Leith Chantos **traveled around Astrodia building libraries**, and within these libraries, he **buried his murdered family members**.
- He built a **final library**, after which he departed to parts unknown.

#### The Quest

- Myrna believes she has located the **final library** — about a **4-day trek to the northeast**, in the mountains.
- She wants the party to find the library, explore it, confirm the **[[Nath Loom]]** is there, and return it to her.
- **Complication**: Myrna had a research partner named **[[Powell]]**, a skilled wizard. They parted ways, and **Powell hired mercenaries** and set out to claim the book herself. Powell has about a **one-day head start**.

## Frog Racing & the Giant Frog Incident

### The Race
![[Pasted image 20260419195443.png]]
While at the café, **[[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]]** heard frog croaks from the back lounge. Students had set up a **frog racing track** with chalk lanes and four unusual **glowing frogs** (red, blue, violet, orange) dressed in tiny costumes — a cape, suspenders, a bow tie, and a top hat.

The party bet on the **suspenders frog**:

- **Red** cast **Speak with Animals** to coach the frog, promising it flies if it won
- **Drolta** bared her fangs to intimidate an opposing frog, causing it to retreat
- **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** intimidated another frog with help
- The **suspenders frog won**, earning the party **30 gold**

### Giant Frog Combat

Just as winnings were about to be collected, all four frogs' eyes turned red and they began **growing into Giant Frogs**, shredding their costumes. Students fled.

**Key moments:**

- **Úlfr** held back initially, laughing: *"Where I come from, we eat frogs, not the other way around."*
- **Red** cast **Animal Friendship** on the suspenders frog (Frog C), making it docile — *"Don't hit this one!"*
- **Frog A** crit **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** for 7 damage and grappled her
- **Frog D swallowed [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] whole** — she was blinded and restrained inside its stomach
- **Leif** cast **Ice Knife** at Frog D, slicing its stomach open and freeing Drolta
- **Úlfr** raged and used **Cleave** — hit Frog B and one-shot **Frog A** (15 damage), which shrank back to normal size
- **Drolta** (while grappled earlier) bit the frog's tongue as a vampiric bite, healing herself for 4 HP — **Red saw her do it**: *"What the fuck? What tarnation?"*
- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** finished Frog B with **Poison Spray**
- All frogs defeated; they shrank back to normal frog size

### Post-Combat Investigation

- **Detect Magic** revealed a **faint transmutation aura** on the frogs
- The frogs had been belching **black, oily substance** — the **same oil found on the mimic** from the [[Biblioplex]]
- The oil **smells slightly sweet**, suggesting something was added to encourage the frogs to ingest it
- **Asha** rolled a **25 on her history/arcana check** (nat 20 + 5) and recalled a student named **[[Murgaxor]]** — a **Bullywug** (frog-like humanoid) who was **expelled from the university years ago**. The frog connection makes him a prime suspect behind both incidents.

**Red adopted the friendly suspenders frog as a pet.**

[[Alina Tanglewood|Alina]] demanded an explanation. With the students vouching for the party and a successful persuasion check, she accepted they weren't responsible and took the students who brought the frogs for questioning.

## The [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]]
![[Pasted image 20260419204757.png]]
After 4 days of travel, the party arrived at the base of a mountain. The library entrance is **built into a hillside** — brush had been cleared, footprints led up to the door, and it was already **open**. Asha identified **goblinoid boot prints** among the tracks — [[Powell]]'s mercenaries.

### Room 1: The Cage Room

**Red** Wild Shaped into a **panther** (+7 Stealth, Darkvision) and scouted ahead (Stealth: 23). He found:

- A door ajar in the north wall
- A hole dug into the east wall
- **4 goblin warriors** — one on watch, others resting
- A **Warg** sleeping in an open cage

**Combat highlights:**

- The party achieved **surprise** — all enemies had disadvantage on initiative
- **Úlfr** raged, took an opportunity attack (4 damage after resistance) to reach the cage, and **slammed the cage door shut, trapping the Warg**. Then one-shot a goblin with his great axe.
- **Red (panther)** dealt 9 damage to a goblin with Rend
- **Drolta** finished a goblin with **Chill Touch** and summoned her pact weapon — "The Broom of the Phoenix Queen"
- The remaining two goblins **fled deeper into the dungeon** — one into a cave, one down a hallway
- **Úlfr** chased one and **crit** with his great axe, killing it as it turned to mock [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] for missing
- **Asha** reacted to the Warg's escape attempt using her **mother's notebook** — rolled a d6 and subtracted it from the Warg's strength check, keeping it trapped
- **Úlfr** piled rubble on the cage for good measure
- **Úlfr gave his first Bardic Inspiration** before the fight: *"The wolf that hesitates feeds no one. Go."*

**Loot: 18 gold pieces** from the goblins (4d6)

### The Barracks

Red scouted an **old barracks** with 4 beds and storage lockers (all empty). A corridor north was blocked by an **iron portcullis** with a **winch** on the far side. **Asha** used **Mage Hand** to reach through the bars and raise the portcullis.

### The Circular Library (First Glimpse)

Through a cracked door, Red spotted a **magnificent circular library filled with thousands of books**, including **one book displayed in a glass case** (likely the [[Nath Loom]]). A balcony circles the upper level.

He heard **goblin voices** inside: *"Hurry up"* and *"I'm getting cold. Let's make a fire."*

The party decided to **explore the other route first** and come back to the library.

### The Bear's Cavern

Through the hole in the wall, a winding tunnel led to a **large natural cavern** with a stream cutting through it. A **large brown bear** rose from the stream, dropping a fish, and roared.

**[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** cast **Speak with Animals** and negotiated passage — the bear agreed to let them through as long as they **did not touch his things**.

A party member spotted **skeletal remains** in the stream with a **glinting magical amulet** around its neck. Leif tried to persuade the bear to let them take it. The persuasion check wasn't enough, and a follow-up luck roll came up **1** — the worst possible result. The bear attacked.

**Combat highlights:**

- **Red (panther)** dealt early damage with Rend
- **Úlfr** hit the bear with his great axe and activated **"Fires Burn"** — a new ability gained from a dream. His axe caught fire, dealing an additional d10 fire damage (14 total). *This surprised everyone.*
- **Asha** nearly killed the bear with Fire Bolt but was one damage short
- **Drolta** dealt the finishing blow

**Loot:**

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Magical Amulet** | Divination magic (same type Asha detected before); needs Identify spell. Given to Asha. |
| **Brown Bear Pelt** | Could make a great cape |
| **Bear Skull & Meat** | Harvested |

### Mystery: Leif's Magic

After the bear fight, **Red** noticed something odd about how **Leif** casts **Starry Wisp** — it looked different from other druids he'd seen cast the same spell. Red asked Leif to cast it at a rock to confirm. **Asha** and later **Drolta** also noticed something off (each received private DM messages). Leif insists it looks the same as always. **Something is wrong or unusual about Leif's magic**, but no one can explain what.

### Room 4: The Supply Room — Hobgoblin Combat

Red scouted north into a **square chamber filled with crates** (wooden slats, leather, parchment). **Three hobgoblins** were searching the crates. Red stumbled on an unstable box, alerting them.

**This was the hardest fight of the session.** The hobgoblins have **Martial Advantage** — extra 2d6 damage when an ally is within 5 feet of their target.

**Key moments:**

- **Hobgoblin B** hit Drolta for **11 damage** (4 + 7 from Martial Advantage), dropping her to 6 HP
- **Hobgoblin C** hit Úlfr for **18 damage**, dropping him to 4 HP
- **Asha** killed Hobgoblin B with Fire Bolt — *"you did save y'all"*
- **Red (panther)** crit Hobgoblin C for 10 damage
- **Aren** healed Úlfr with **Cure Wounds** for 7 HP
- The last hobgoblin **disengaged and ran to a secret door** leading to the circular library, knocking on it before **Asha** killed him with another Fire Bolt
- A goblin on the other side said *"What was that?"* — another responded *"Must have been the wind. Come help us with the fire."* The library goblins remain unaware.

### Short Rest

The party took a **short rest** in the supply room. A long rest would risk letting **[[Powell]]** escape with the [[Nath Loom]].
