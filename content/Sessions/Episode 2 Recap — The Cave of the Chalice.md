## Morning at the Village

The party woke up in the small no-name village from last session. Sleeping arrangements were the same: guys in one room, [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] and [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] in another, [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] solo.

- **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] investigated** whether anyone snuck into the guys' room overnight. Rolled terribly (again) and became *more* convinced that Drolta snuck in to see [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]. He put a hand on Leif's shoulder and shook his head in disappointment. This is now a **two-session streak** of Úlfr misreading this situation.
- **Leif** mentioned feeling "a bit weird" from the night before — quieter and less enthusiastic than usual.
- **Aren and Drolta** had a brief private conversation. Aren apologized for dragging Drolta into this group, but acknowledged safety in numbers and a connection to the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] through the party. Drolta offered to kill Aren if her curse ever takes over, and Aren responded: *"You can try."* Aren expressed understanding of being burdened by unwanted curses and promised to help.
- **Asha** woke up feeling great (she slept alone and had no issues). She walked around the village looking for signs of her mother — rolled an 18 investigation. No signs of her mother, but she noticed **the town was preparing for some kind of festival or marketplace** (stalls being set up).

## Travel: Village → Thornvale

Asha took the lead navigating. The next major settlement is **[[Thornvale]]**, about a day and a half's walk west. Tommy offered the option to double-time it and arrive before nightfall but at the cost of a level of exhaustion. The party chose to travel at normal pace and camp one night on the road.

### Úlfr's Road Knowledge

Úlfr arrived in the region a couple weeks ago and did some exploring around [[Bayhaven|Bay Haven]]. He shared with the group:
- There **used to be a major goblin presence** on [[Artmegía]]'s roads and highways
- Around the time of the **"forest curse"** (a significant event Úlfr has heard about but doesn't understand), the goblins disappeared
- Roads are now **relatively safe** — occasional bandits, but commonly traveled enough that a group with protection should be fine
- Úlfr advised the group to be ready to camp out and set watches

### Camp Setup

The party found a passable campsite ([[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] rolled a nature check — decent but not luxurious ground).

- **Úlfr and Aren** stayed at camp. Úlfr used his giant strength to bend trees and drape tarps, creating **two makeshift tents** — one for the women, one for the men. He pointed and said: *"For you. Leif, for us. You will go that way."* (Still operating under his misguided theory about Drolta and Leif.)
- **Red, Drolta, Leif, and Asha** went hunting/foraging. Red and Drolta succeeded on survival checks and brought back deer and vegetables. Leif struggled (open clearing isn't his terrain) but foraged mushrooms instead. Asha served as guide.

### Campfire Conversations

The group gathered around the fire for stories and got to know each other better.

**Úlfr asked for stories.** He told the group: *"Life is a story. Everything we do is a story."*

- **Red** told the story of a student from the Academy who years ago tried to enter the "old forest" near his village. Red got the student drunk at a party so he'd forget about going in, potentially saving his life. Red hopes to find the student at the Academy someday.
- **Úlfr** told the story of **Thyra Halfhand**, a warrior from his people's legends who lost half her hand yet became one of the bravest fighters — using a shield strapped to her arm instead of a sword after losing the ability to grip one. He told it from a book he carries, reading the story aloud. Performance check: 12. Well-received, told in a nice three-act structure. Red complimented him: *"You have the best stories, man."* Úlfr corrected: *"Not my stories. These are the stories I collect, sir."* He closed his little book.
- **Aren** told a story about the **Phoenix Queen** — rising from the ashes. She revealed she was a **high priestess at the Phoenix Queen's temple** and that she and the Queen still have a relationship. She hopes to learn from the Queen's story and "arise from the ashes" herself, in a way that feels one with nature.
- **Red shared his recurring dream:** Running with a pack of animals under a full moon (on all fours this time). The animals all stopped and stared up at the moon, where he saw **three spheres — purple, blue, and red** — making strange twists and turns around it. He asked the group if anyone could interpret it. Asha took interest but couldn't fully decipher it (Tommy gave her a private DM message with partial info).
- **Leif** was shy about sharing stories, saying his experiences felt like "memories, not stories." Úlfr encouraged him: *"You're young yet. Once you travel around, you'll make some stories."* Leif mentioned having an **uncle who is a traveler** and tells wonderful stories when he returns for winter.

### Religion Discussion

A group conversation about faith emerged:

- **Leif** follows **[[Neptune]]** — the "Agent of Life" and god of nature. His clan honors Neptune through their actions.
- **Asha** learned (and explained to the group) that in the **[[Solemn Pantheon|Solemn and Beyond]]** cosmology, the **[[Agents]]** are like "the hands of God" — each managing a domain and ensuring things go as they should. Believing in one Agent essentially means believing in all of them.
- **Úlfr** realized he's technically religious by that definition.
- **Aren** stated her belief that the gods exist and hold sway over life, and it's important to recognize when they act through others.
- **Úlfr** noted: *"That's wise."*

### Asha's Discovery

Before bed, Asha searched her **mother's notebook** (prompted by Red's dream about the spheres). Rolled a Nat 20 on investigation. She found:
- A **triangle of orbs** drawn in the notebook, colored **red, blue, and purple**
- The word **"Venus"** written next to it

This connects to Red's dream but the full meaning is unknown.

### Leif and Drolta's Private Talk

Leif pulled Drolta aside (away from earshot of others but within Aren's line of sight — Aren was watching body language).

- Leif clarified he wasn't romantically interested — he was worried about **her fangs**
- Drolta teased him, circling him and pinching his cheeks: *"You are so close"*
- She revealed she's a **dhampir** — not a full vampire, "something in between"
- That explains why she can walk in sunlight
- Leif apologized for exposing his neck/shoulder area the night before (now realizing why that was a bad idea)
- Drolta said it wouldn't be a problem anymore and asked Leif to **keep it between them** for now
- They agreed to let the guys keep their wrong assumptions
- Drolta called Leif "little one" and patted his head before walking away

**Meanwhile, Úlfr and Asha** were watching from the fire:
- Úlfr rolled a **5 on perception** and gestured suggestively toward Leif and Drolta, thoroughly convinced of a romance
- Asha (who is book-smart but perhaps not street-smart) didn't fully grasp what Úlfr was implying
- Úlfr gave up trying to explain and looked down at his staff in silence

### Night Watch & Rest

- **Watch rotation:** Úlfr and Asha took first watch; Aren took second
- During first watch, Úlfr sat by the fire **carving something into his quarterstaff**
- Asha sat with him since she was already rested and had the information from her mother's notebook on her mind
- **Drolta snuck out** and communed with her Phoenix Queen (private DM conversation)
- **Wisdom saves before bed:** Everyone passed. Uneventful night.

## Arrival at Thornvale
![[Pasted image 20260308200754.png]]
After another half-day of travel, the party arrived at **Thornvale** — a proper town, much bigger than the previous village, with a bustling market, though not as large as Bay Haven.

Asha told the group to split up and meet back in 20 minutes.

### Shopping: The Copper Cauldron (Potion Shop)

**Available inventory:**
- Potions of Healing
- Potions of Greater Healing
- Potion of Climbing
- Antitoxins (30 gold each)
- Alchemist's Fire

Red looked at prices and left (too expensive). **Leif** spoke with the shopkeeper — a former Academy student who studied potions:
- Leif explained he's looking for a rare potion to heal a "specific type of disease" that normal healing potions haven't helped
- The shopkeeper recommended **[[Professor Lang]]** at **[[Witherbloom College]]** (a satellite campus to the south, not the main campus)
- The shopkeeper noted that teleportation would be much quicker than walking to Witherbloom
- Asha overheard this conversation from the background

### The Quest Board

The group reconvened at the quest board. Available postings:

| Job | Details | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Carpenter needed | Fix a leaky roof | ~2 gold |
| Escort wanted | Travel escort to the Academy | 10 gold |
| Wolf hunt | Someone's livestock being killed | A few gold |
| **Magical Items Wanted** | Official Academy decree — bring any magical item to the main campus for gold compensation (like a "buyback program") | Varies |
| Misc. odd jobs | Various mundane tasks | Small amounts |

- **Red tried to fix the roof** — rolled poorly with carpenter's tools. The fancy artisanal tiles were beyond his expertise. He refused to accept payment for a job not done.
- The party decided to take the **escort job** and headed to the **Silver Tankard** tavern to meet the contact.

### The Silver Tankard — Meeting [[Lucian Cugg]]
![[Pasted image 20260308202504.png]]
The party found a man with his head down at a table in the back corner. **Red** approached.

**Lucian Cugg** — an explorer and former Academy student (studied there briefly but left because he didn't like academia). He's been exploring the world, particularly interested in **higher beings** and ancient history.

**His story:**
- He was in **[[Caelwyrm]]**, exploring the **Great Ravine** — thought to have been carved by dragons in ancient times
- He found a **[[Golden Chalice|golden chalice]]** with apparent magical properties in an excavated temple
- He hired two women to escort him to the Academy to sell it
- The escorts — **[[Vonet]]** (orc woman) and **[[Dilly]]** (human woman) — **double-crossed him** a couple miles outside Thornvale, stole the chalice, and fled north
- This happened **yesterday**

**Party response:**
- Red offered to track them down. *"I don't like thievery. People work hard enough as it is."*
- Úlfr approved: *"A job is a job."*
- The party agreed to a **communal gold fund** for group expenses
- Úlfr intimidated Lucian (rolled 19) when Lucian questioned whether Úlfr was really a fighter: *"I said I don't like to fight. Doesn't mean I'm not a fighter."*
- **Asha rolled insight** on Lucian — he seems genuine about what happened, but she suspects **he's understating how valuable the chalice actually is**

## The Cave Dungeon

### Tracking the Bandits

The party, with Lucian in tow, traveled about 2 hours north to where Lucian was ambushed. Red and Leif tracked footprints heading north (Red almost followed the wrong trail but Leif caught it at the last second).

Leif sent **Howl** (his owl) ahead to scout — the trail led to **a cave a couple miles north**. The party decided to pursue immediately, hoping to catch the bandits resting overnight.

### Cave Exploration

**Lighting situation:**
- Asha, Drolta, and Leif have natural darkvision (Leif has 120 ft)
- Aren cast **Light** on her mace (20 ft bright / 20 ft dim)
- Red had torches; Úlfr had a lamp

**Room 1 — Spider Den:**
![[Pasted image 20260308205830.png]]
- Ceiling covered in webs; a human-sized boot on the floor
- Red identified them as **wolf spider webs** (nature check)
- Úlfr spotted **two wolf spiders on the ceiling** (perception check)
- **Aren grabbed and cast Sacred Flame** before anyone could stop her — both Úlfr and Leif tried to restrain her (contested strength) but she rolled a Nat 20 and broke free
- Turned out the spiders were **already dead** — riddled with crossbow bolts. Aren's sacred flame was a moot point but dramatic.

**Navigating deeper:**
- Red spotted a **trip wire** that would have released boulders onto Leif (perception check saved the group)
- The party found a **bizarrely flat section of cave wall** — Asha investigated (19) and found a **hidden lever** for a secret door
- Leif's owl scouted and found the two bandits further in the cave, hiding and looking north at something — they appeared to be scoping out the goblin lair

### Confronting the Bandits

The party climbed down a 30-foot cliff (Drolta used spider climb; everyone else used ropes). They set up an ambush position. Leif called Howl back to lure the bandits.
![[Pasted image 20260308212220.png]]

**Combat — Bandits (2 enemies):**
- Drolta opened with Eldritch Blast (3 damage) and demanded: *"We're missing a chalice. You wouldn't happen to know where one is?"*
- Asha smacked Bandit B (the human, already limping and missing a boot) with her quarterstaff
- Red hit Bandit A with Poison Spray (6 damage)
- Leif hit Bandit A with Starry Wisp — she fell unconscious
- **Úlfr raged** and declared: *"They thought us prey. Let us teach them what prey looks like."* He attempted to grapple Bandit B but she escaped

**After combat:**
- The conscious bandit (Dilly) surrendered and revealed they **sold the chalice to goblins** in the cave for 50 gold. They had been planning to **steal it back** from the goblins to sell again at the Academy for even more money.
- The party took the **50 gold**
- Debate over what to do with the bandits — Drolta knocked them both out (non-lethally). Red stole one of Dilly's remaining boots.
- The party left them unconscious and moved on to find the goblins

### The Goblin Lair

The party pulled the hidden lever and opened the secret stone door, revealing:
- Two connected rooms with a **makeshift forge** stoked to white-hot heat
- **Four goblin warriors** and a **goblin leader** wearing topaz jewelry, holding a leash attached to a **giant centipede**
- The leader shouted: *"Kill the intruders!"*

**Combat — Goblins (5 enemies + giant centipede):**
![[Pasted image 20260308214812.png]]
Key moments:
- **Red (Nat 20 initiative)** went first
- **Leif** cast **Fairy Fire** — affected 4 of the 5 goblins (giving advantage on attacks against them)
- **Drolta** used her Hexblade pact weapon (Phoenix Queen's feather — bladed claws) and Hex, shredding a goblin
- **Úlfr raged** and used his greataxe to **bisect one goblin**, then used **Cleave** to severely wound another on the backswing
- **Asha** max-damage firebolt charred a goblin to death instantly
- **Aren** healed Red (who had taken 6 damage from a goblin arrow) with Healing Word, then contributed Sacred Flame damage
- **Leif** finished off the last goblin and the centipede with a **life-draining vine spell** — vines grabbed the enemies, sucked the life from them, and bloomed flowers around Drolta to heal her. *"I didn't know you had that in you,"* someone remarked. Leif's ring glowed during the casting.
- **Howl (the owl) was killed** by a bandit scimitar in a later fight — Leif can re-summon her but was sad about it

### The Chalice

The party found the chalice in the forge — **completely unmelted** despite the white-hot fire. The goblins had been trying to smelt it down for gold but it was indestructible.

**Drolta cast Detect Magic + Arcana check:**
- The chalice is **definitely magical**
- Its magic is **all types at once** — it doesn't fit neatly into any single school of magic
- This is very unusual and powerful

**Lucian** was surprised it hadn't melted. He didn't know what it does either.

**Party debate:**
- Drolta was nervous about giving it back to Lucian, sensing its power
- Asha pointed out that neither the goblins nor the bandits knew what they had
- The party decided to **hold the chalice themselves** until they reach the Academy, with Lucian accompanying them
- Lucian agreed (after some persuasion/intimidation) — he wants the gold from the Academy buyback program and to learn the chalice's history
- He acknowledged the party would get a cut of whatever the Academy pays
