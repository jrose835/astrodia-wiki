## Arrival at the Acropolis

The party arrived at the **[[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy of Arcane Arts]]** in late afternoon with a couple hours of sunlight remaining. The campus is massive and bustling — hundreds of students in colored uniforms, a diverse mix of species, mostly early-to-late 20s in age. The party stuck out as obvious non-students.

- **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** rolled a 20 on stealth and hid under a cloak, blending in while everyone was distracted by the group of misfits she was traveling with.
- **[[Lucian Cugg|Lucian]]** was eager to get the chalice appraised and led the group toward the administrative building — about a 20-30 minute walk.

### The Academy's College System

Asha filled the party in on the campus structure. After their first year (black robes), students sort into specialized colleges:

## The Arcane Reclamation Initiative

The party found the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] desk staffed by **[[Ms. Maris]]**. She explained a three-step process: initial assessment (Identify spell), hazard evaluation (curse check), and scholarly review. Everyone had to sign a disclosure form before proceeding.

- **Asha tried to sign a fake name** but the enchanted pen and paper revealed her true name. She rolled poorly on deception. Fortunately, Ms. Maris didn't recognize her — but someone in the party noticed (DM sent a private message).
- **[[Tobin]]**, a junior mage apprentice, attempted the Identify spell twice and failed both times. Ms. Maris cast it herself and discovered the [[Golden Chalice|chalice]] has a **non-detection spell** on it, blocking all divination.

### The Offer

Ms. Maris offered **50 gold** for the unidentified [[Golden Chalice|chalice]]. The party declined, opting to wait for the non-detection spell to wear off (30 days). **However**, the signed disclosure means the chalice technically belongs to the Initiative now. The party has **30 days** to return it or face a "repo man."

- **Lucian** agreed to the plan and said he'd find work in the meantime. The party needs a way to stay in contact with him (nobody has the Sending spell yet).
- As they walked away, [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] overheard **Tobin whisper** to Ms. Maris: *"Wasn't that a chalice? Wasn't that one of the—"* before she cut him off. Red immediately confronted them. Ms. Maris deflected, claiming Tobin was just an eager student. The party saw through her (Insight checks) but couldn't press further.

## The Bow's End Tavern
![[Pasted image 20260330194758.png]]
The party headed to the **[[Bow's End Tavern]]**, a cozy establishment with mahogany floors, black leather furniture, and 20-foot ceilings. Bartender is **[[Tulk]]** ("the Bulk"), a full orc who owns the place.

Everyone ordered **Dwarven Ales** (the menu had fancier options like "Succubus's Kiss" and "Wyburn's Death Punch," but the party kept it simple).
![[Pasted image 20260330195536.png]]
### Úlfr's Drinking Game with Perrin

[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] cornered a first-year student named **[[Perrin]]** and challenged him to drinks. Key intel gathered:

- Perrin wants to study medicine at [[Witherbloom College|Witherbloom]]
- He entered the Academy through a standard application process (essay, letters of rec, interview) — Úlfr got bored hearing about it
- He has **no knowledge of Dispel Magic** — "Give me a week, dude"
- He's heard of Tobin but doesn't know him well

### Drolta Meets Aurora

[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] spotted a woman playing lute in the corner — wearing **Witherbloom robes**. Something felt familiar. Drolta approached and they discovered a mutual connection: **[[Aurora Luna Wynterstarr|Aurora]] is a born dampier** (vampiric lineage from her father, whom she never knew). This is the first time either has met a dampier of the other type (born vs. turned).
![[Pasted image 20260330195528.png]]
Key information from Aurora:

- **[[Professor Valentine]]** is a **full vampire** and one of the **deans of Witherbloom**. He's a sangromancer (receives visions from blood he drinks), has denounced the higher vampires, and serves as Aurora's mentor.
- Aurora's mother hid information about her father for safety — minimizing any connection the vampire could use to contact her.
- Aurora confirmed vampire assassins still occasionally target Valentine for defecting.

**[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] followed them outside**, sensing the dampier presence with her pendant and assuming Drolta was in danger. Tense moment, but Drolta explained the situation and it resolved peacefully. The group exchanged contact info (Aurora can send messages via the Sending spell; the party cannot yet reciprocate).

Aurora also suggested the **[[Biblioplex]]** (campus library) as the best place to find someone who knows Dispel Magic.

### Asha's Intel from Tobin

Tobin arrived at the tavern as promised. After a **botched intimidation** by Úlfr (who grappled the poor kid after rolling terribly and Tobin tried to flee), **Asha** smoothed things over and got him talking:

- The [[Golden Chalice|chalice]] matches one of **several specific items** the Initiative was told to watch for. They're called **"the Something Spoils"** (Tobin couldn't remember the full name).
- It's the **first item** matching those descriptions to come through.
- The initiative is aware of one chalice and **3 other types of items** on their watch list.
- Only people within the Initiative would know this information.
- **Ms. Maris acted weird** the rest of the day after the party left.

Asha also recognized Tobin recognized **her** — he knew about her academic trouble. She tried to deflect with a deception check (failed). Tobin said he should probably keep his distance but shared the info out of gratitude for being rescued from the grapple.

The party stayed at the **[[University Inn]]** (5 gold/night). To save money, everyone crammed into one room, resulting in **disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws** for nighttime effects.

**Úlfr and Drolta both failed.**

He woke up **screaming "[[Kári]]!"** in the middle of the night. When the party stirred, he growled at them and refused to speak. He went back to sleep visibly shaken.

> **"Justice for Leith Chantos! Justice for Leith Chantos!"**

### What the Party Knows

- **Úlfr recognized the name** — [[Leith Chantos]] has history connected to the **[[War Rock Mountains]]** (Goliath territory).
- **Erin** (highest history roll) recalled that Leith Chantos is a **well-known criminal figure** — somewhere between a Jesse James and a vigilante. Popular belief says he did bad things, but Myrna insists he was trying to protect his family and do the right thing.
- She claims to have **a way to clear his name** and is willing to **pay big gold** for adventurers to help.
- She'll be at the **[[Fire Jolt Café]]** later with posters, trying to recruit.

The party agreed to circle back to her after handling other business.

## The Biblioplex (Grand Library)
![[Pasted image 20260330194541.png]]
The party entered the **[[Biblioplex]]** — the grand central library of the Academy. Majestic marble archways, tomes stacked for miles, magical energy everywhere.

### Floor Access

- **1st Floor:** Open to public and first-years
- **2nd Floor:** Restricted to upper-years and professors (permission required)
- **3rd Floor:** Prohibited — this is where Asha was caught previously
![[Pasted image 20260330211258.png]]

![[Pasted image 20260330211556.png]]
### The Snarl

In the [[Hall of Oracles]], the party found a **Snarl** — a knotted, tangled essence of pure magic that serves as a power source for the university and affects magic in unpredictable ways. Asha also mentioned the **Oracle of the Academy** — supposedly the most powerful being at the school, but not exactly approachable.
![[Pasted image 20260330211946.png]]
### The Cogwork Archivists

The library is staffed by **cogwork archivists** — magical constructs that handle bookkeeping and can guide visitors to relevant texts. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] used one to find **medical texts on maladies of the mind** (for his sister's illness). Drolta used one to find **texts on vampiric lineages and ancestries**.

### Combat: The Mimic

A chest attacked **[[Rosie|Rosimyffenbip]]**, a gnome LARPer who opened it looking for costumes. *(Later revealed in Session 6 to be **[[Rosie]]** of the [[S.A.T.O.]].)* Initiative was rolled and the party engaged.

**Post-combat investigation:** Red found a **black, oily substance** on the mimic — clearly not natural. It had been **tampered with**. Professor **[[Mavinda Sharpbeak]]** (an owlin [[Silverquill college|Silverquill]] professor) arrived, dismissed it as "leather conditioner," but the party's Insight checks confirmed she was hiding something — putting on a front that nothing bad could happen at the school.

**Reward:** Each party member received **1 platinum piece** from Professor Sharpbeak. Possibly a bribe.

- The **runic transportation circles** at the base of staircases can teleport students to upper floors — but 2nd floor access requires faculty permission.
