## Confronting Powell

The party tracked down **[[Powell]]**, [[Myrna Nor]]'s former research partner and rival. She had reached the [[Nath Loom]] first and was prepared to defend it.

The party **defeated Powell** in combat and claimed the **[[Nath Loom]]** — the spellbook of poisons and antidotes compiled by [[Leith Chantos]] over a lifetime of grief and study.
