## Madame Verre

The paralysis dropped and the conversation started over, civilly, in the guild's foyer.

Introductions were brief and evasive on both sides. She has run the guild and the city **since it was handed down to her from Madame Rellyona** — *"very efficiently, I'd say"* — which is the first time anyone has said out loud that **[[Madame Verre]] personally succeeded [[Rellyona]]**, the [[Magistus]] who founded [[Pentamarone]] and who has been dead for years. She looks about eleven.

[[Genni]] was bewildered: she'd had no idea the chalice was this interesting, and Verre cut her off — *"Genni did such a bad job of explaining how interesting this chalice was. I'd love to get a better look at it."*

**[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s insight** read her as **genuinely curious and carrying no ill intent** — hard to read, but not hostile. That was enough for the party.

Before they could move, a door on the floor above banged open and a woman in a wide hat came out — **Haine** — and scolded her boss in front of six armed strangers.

> *"Madame, what is the meaning of this? You're supposed to be in a meeting right now."*

Verre told her to go **gather all of the other master WitchCrafters** instead.

[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] asked the only question that mattered: **are we your prisoners?**

> *"Oh, God, no, never. It was the only way to get your attention."*
> *"Efficient, I'd say."*
> *"It was very interesting, to say the least."*

## The Deal

Up in her study, the team told Verra that the chalice came from someone who got it out of **[[Caelwyrm]]**, and that **[[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]]** intends to repossess it.

Asked about **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|the Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]**, she said she didn't know much, and **Úlfr's second insight check backed her up** — with a telling shade to it: it isn't that she's hiding it, it's that *the subject had never been interesting enough to her before now.*

Then she made an offer.

> *"I'd like to propose a little bit of a trade. Given the recounting of what occurred in the library, it seems like y'all are relatively new to the powers of this chalice. What if I can help you with that — and in return, you let me use the chalice how I please."*

**The terms, as negotiated:**

- She does **not** claim ownership. *"I have no intention to use something that does not belong to me."*
- She has **one specific goal** the chalice may help her achieve. **She will not say what it is.** Once it's done, she returns the chalice.
- She and her guild will **do their best** to determine what the chalice actually is — origin, function, and why it matters. The party's own participation is expected to be *"imperative."*
- **A week**, by her calculations — *"which are usually correct"* — and less with help.
- **No harm** comes to any of them. Guaranteed.

Úlfr warned her, twice, that carrying this thing means the Academy will hunt whoever holds it.

> *"I am not worried about such people."*

### Get It in Writing

**[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] asked for the warning in writing** — an acknowledgment that the party had disclosed the danger, and no liability for it. Verre *liked* that. *"That's fair, and it makes it quite interesting."* (Table verdict: *"A cleric and a lawyer."*)

She whispered to Genni, who ran out and came back with **Marka** — the guild's calligrapher and its expert in documentation of every kind — who set up at a table and began drafting the contract with a self-writing quill.

Then Úlfr, sensing an opening, asked whether the guild would **put them up** while they worked.

Verre laughed at him. *"No."*

Marka, deadpan, asked whether she should add a clause requiring housing or the deal was off. **Yes**, said Úlfr. *"Food and lodging."* Verre rolled her eyes. *"Whatever, fine."*

**The persuasion check that sealed it was a natural 20 from [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** — and it worked for a reason nobody expected. Mid-argument, Verre's eye caught the **glass orb mounted on Leif's staff.**

> *"Interesting. What is that?"*

Leif explained it's his spellcasting focus, that he's *"technically a warlock, although I'm coming to terms with it,"* and that **his owl Howl brought him the orb in the [[Terget Forest|Purodetapo Forest]].** 

Verre went very still.

> *"The Purodetapo Forest, you say."*
> *"Marka. Give them whatever they want."*

Housing and meals, at an inn of their choosing, added to the contract without further discussion. And to Leif: *"We should have a chat later. I think there's something very special about that."*

Leif looked at his owl. *"You been holding back on us?"*

**[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] read the contract before anyone signed it** — *"we got burned with the fine print at the Academy"* — and her investigation came back clean. **Everyone signed.**

## Drinks with the WitchCrafters

![[Pasted image 20260909204641.png]]

**Seed, Edel, Schmietta, and Marka** took the party up on a celebratory drink. **Ceramia** declined — and this is where the party learned she is also, apparently, a small girl, but one who **behaves like an actual child**, unlike her boss. *"I don't think I can drink yet."*

**Haine** pulled Verre aside instead, in a whisper the party half-caught:

> *"Are you sure this is a good idea? You don't really know these people, or this chalice."*
> *"I have a good feeling about this group. And this chalice. It's the best lead we've got."*

**The best lead she's got — for what, still unstated.**

They found a dive bar. Red offered the [[Alchemy Jug]] and was overruled: **drinks were on the guild.** Drolta ordered a bottle of red. Aren ordered a cheap beer. The waitress recognized the WitchCrafters immediately and asked whether this had to do with **the fireworks festival at the end of the week.** Marka said no — *"but if it goes how we want it to go, it'll be a beautiful unveiling."*

### The Fireworks Festival

A newer tradition: every guild in [[Pentamarone]] competes to put on the most breathtaking show. **The winning guild runs the city.** No limits on materials, spells, or method.

**The WitchCrafters have won every year since its inception.** Asked whether that's down to magic or effort: *"Magic is effort, as I'm sure you know."* Asked whether they fear the competition: *"No one's ever really provided any."*

They cheerfully invited the party to enter.

### Their Stories

- **Marka** — obsessed with **penmanship as magic**. Her thesis: it isn't only what a spell says, it's how it's *written*; calligraphy itself imbues a specific kind of magic. She studied at the **Grand Spellbook Tower in Marcell** *(name as heard — unconfirmed)* and left because it didn't suit her.
- **Schmietta** — from a small crafting village in the **[[Endymion Empire]]**, famous for its forges. **Their army came.** She got out, arrived here with nothing, worked up through apprenticeships, and now makes very good weapons.
- **Edel** — *"I always just liked pretty things."* Gems, magic stones. Comfortable background, good teachers, studied what she wanted, got very good at it, and **Verre found her.**
- **Seed** — the **only WitchCrafter who studies living things**: botany, horticulture. She came from **a line of druids, in a forest up north**, and left after *"some bad things happened in the forest"* she would not describe. She didn't think a guild of crafters would have a place for someone who works with living things. **Verre carved one out for her.** She keeps the garden.

Ulfr's assessment of Seed, gesturing at Leif: *"This big green guy here likes plants too."*

### What They Said About Madame Verre

The party asked the obvious question, and got a shrug that was itself informative.

> *"We kind of just don't question it at this point. She's been a child for a very long time."*
> *"She doesn't have the mind of a child. She has the smugness of one."*
> *"Wise beyond her years, let's say."*

They confirmed she is **easily bored** — meetings, mostly, day after day — and **intensely drawn to anything interesting.** None of them had ever seen her paralyze anyone before; the party's reception was, by guild standards, extraordinary. And on whether she'd abandon the party once bored: she might drift off and get distracted, **but she keeps her word.**

Asha tried, later and separately, to get the anti-aging secret out of her directly. The persuasion **failed.**

> *"You know, I like to be a girl's girl. But it's too good. I gotta keep it a secret."*

### What They Said About Genni

Asha asked why the guild's librarian carries **the Endymion Empire's symbol** on her staff *(and on the safe under her desk — Session 10).*

They all looked at each other.

> *"Genni doesn't really like to talk about her past. We think she came from somewhere pretty close to the Citadel. She has no family here, to our knowledge. She came here all on her own."*

Is she a spy? They were entirely comfortable: **no.** She's been nothing but good to them. She's just anxious and timid and slow to open up.

### What They Said About the White Forest

They know **the curse** — it threatened Pentamarone before it receded — and they know **a coven lived in the forest**, but none of them ever spoke to it and none know what became of it. Then one of them offered a detail that reframes a Session 10 rumor:

> *"I think I've heard there was a witch in a town near the forest who sometimes worked with **Rellyona**, if I remember correctly."*

That witch is **[[Morgana]]**. Last session, a [[Freedale]] refugee remembered Morgana working with **[[Madame Verre]]**. Tonight the guild remembers her working with **[[Rellyona]]**, Verre's predecessor. **An insight check at the table said they're telling the truth as they know it.** Either the two accounts are of different collaborations — or the distinction between Verre and Rellyona is thinner than it looks.

### What They Said About Empire Law

Practical and unwelcome, if the party leaves town in the wrong direction:

- Outside Pentamarone's jurisdiction, **[[Endymion Empire|Empire]] law applies. Any unregistered magic is illegal.**
- Legal casting requires their **power stones** — the [[Keystones]] the party already knows about.
- **Registration or approval** is possible; you're then issued a supply of stones.
- Whether a **warlock** can cast anything at all through a patron under that regime — *"I don't really know."* Even registered.

Otherwise: a lovely town, nice people, shops, restaurants, temples, and nature outside the walls. **Nothing to worry about.**

Aren asked whether there was a temple to **[[Pluto]]** in the city. There is not. **Most of Pentamarone worships [[Venus]]**, and there is a temple to her.

## The Montage

The next stretch of the session ran as a **montage**: each day, every PC contributed a skill check to their team's line of research, and successes **shaved time off Verre's seven-day estimate.**

**Day one** was a rout in the party's favor — Leif's **natural 20 Perception** comparing the two chalices, Úlfr's **Athletics with advantage** at the forge, Aren's **Insight** sorting useful sources from noise in the archives, Drolta's **Investigation** on the living copies, and Asha's **History**, boosted by a **Portent** die. *(Asha's portent rolls: a 20 and a 5. The 20 replaced a natural 1; the 5 stayed in her pocket.)*

> **Two and a half days of work in one day.** Seven days became four and a half.

**Day two** knocked off another half-day on top of a normal day's progress. **Down to three days of work remaining.**

Verre was delighted, and unusually forthcoming about *why*:

> *"Everyone thinks they rely too much on divination. As soon as your Identify spell doesn't work, everyone just assumes it's impossible. But this is **grassroots magic**. We're really getting to the core, to the essence of things, without having to rely on a pre-existing spell."*

She is treating the chalice not as a puzzle to be solved by a spell, but as a thing to be **taken apart and studied.** Nobody at this table has seen anyone do that before.

*(No findings were released. Tommy is holding the results for a single report at the end of the process.)*

### And Then Úlfr Told Verre About the Fourth Magic

Separately, Úlfr went to ask Verre about the **basis of the magic the WitchCrafters use** — arcane, divine, or something else — having spent time at the Academy with people researching exactly that question.

Morning of the third working day. The party is walking to the guild hall together when **Howl**, perched on Leif's shoulder, snaps her head around, starts screaming, and **takes off down a back alley.**

The party split:

- **Chasing the owl:** Leif, Red, Úlfr, Drolta *(and Bama, Red's familar)*
- **Staying with the chalice:** Aren and Asha — on the theory that a bird bolting is exactly what a diversion looks like

![[Pasted image 20260909223115.png]]

What they found in the alley was **a man cornered by an adventuring party** — a captain with a **pistol**, a mage, a swashbuckler, and a druid.

Howl flew straight to him. **Leif's animal handling read her reaction not as fear but as rage.** She is *furious* at this man.

The confrontation already in progress:

> *"You think you can escape us after what you did?"*
> *"I don't know what you're talking about. I did what I came to do. I helped to the best of my abilities, and then left."*
> *"You left us to die. We only made it out of there because the thing that you summoned got bored of us — after it killed two of our friends."*

Their account: he agreed to help them explore **a set of ruins**. At the end of the dungeon he **took the loot they were after**, they were ambushed by whatever haunted the temple, he **summoned something** to fight it off, and then he left. The summon killed the haunts, then turned on them.

His apology was sincere and entirely useless. *"I'm still getting used to working with magic in this way. I can't control everything that I summon."*

Then the captain gave the party his name.

> *"This motherfucker is **Aleister the Invoker.**"*

### Aleister the Invoker

**Úlfr rolled a natural 20 on History**, and this is a name his people tell stories about.

**Aleister the Invoker** is known across the world — half legend, half folk tale — as a man who can **summon beings from outside Astrodia**: things that cannot be explained and are found nowhere in nature. His reputation is genuinely **split**. Some tell it as the story of a hero. Some don't.

> *"His reputation precedes him. This is a well-known man. I'm not sure if he's good or bad — I guess it depends on the circumstance."*

And then he looked at Howl properly, and Howl **shrank** — feathers hunched, glancing at the ground, making herself small.

> *"Oh. I do know what this is, then."*
> *"What is it?"*
> *"...What's your name?"*
> *"Leif Kalanchoe. This is Howl. I found her back home in the Purodetapo Forest — she had an injured wing."*
> *"How interesting. The Purodetapo Forest, you say. I'll have to make a visit there."*

**That was the wrong thing to say to Leif.** He demanded answers first; Aleister declined and started walking.

Leif threw his hand forward to cast **Eldritch Blast** —

— and nothing happened, because **his arcane focus is sitting in Madame Verre's study.**

### Cocytus

A vast thing of **ice and running water**, blue light moving through its body like blood. Nobody in the alley — including the adventurers hunting Aleister — had ever seen it before. *"I've never seen this. What the fuck?"*

The critical discovery came within the first round, and it came from the enemy: the bandit **mage's Ray of Frost** landed, and part of Cocytus's body **froze solid**. **Cold damage halves its movement.** For the rest of the fight, every frost effect on the board was a tempo win — and **Leif's Ice Knife** became the party's best weapon precisely because it wasn't a warlock spell.

**What the party did:**

- **Red** cast **Moonbeam** on Aleister (8 radiant) and then wild shaped into a **brown bear** — large enough to ignore the beast's prone rider.
- **Drolta** used her **spider climb** to scale the wall for line of sight and landed a **Guiding Bolt** on Aleister through three-quarters cover.
- **Bama** was dispatched to fetch **Aren and Asha** — and Leif's staff.
- **Úlfr** raged, threw hand axes, and hit Cocytus with **Dissonant Whispers** — it failed the save, took 3d6 psychic, and was forced to flee across the alley.
- **Leif** fought the entire encounter on **druid spells only** (a ruling from Tommy: his druidic magic predates the pact and is untouched by the missing focus), plus **thrown daggers**, plus **magic stones**. Ice Knife did the heavy lifting. One of them also clipped Red for 5.

**Aleister never engaged.** He watched from the roof, cast **Fly** on himself, and left.

> *"I just seem to make a mess of everything I do. I guess I'm still not ready."*

**He escaped clean.** Nobody could reach him. The party's only remaining problem was the thing he left behind.

### Drolta Goes Down

Cocytus dropped down onto the lower roof and hit **Drolta and Red** with two slams. Drolta took **28** and went down — **the first death save of the campaign.**

She **failed the first one.**

**Úlfr burned his last spell slot on Healing Word** (8 hp) and brought her back up. She spent her next turn getting to her feet and going right back after it: *"it fucked me up, boy."*

Cocytus then **engulfed Red** — 20+ damage, knocked out of bear form, **restrained and suffocating** inside the elemental's body. He broke free on a Strength check, described as a brown bear reading a river the way it reads a salmon run, and immediately **wild shaped into a lion.**

### The Kill

The party ground it down between them — axes, claws, rapiers, rocks, ice — and the finishing blow went to **the swashbuckler**, who ran up the back of Red's lion form, **launched off it, and buried her rapier in the beast's eye.**

Cocytus fell.

**End of session.**
