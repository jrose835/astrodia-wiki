## Where We Left Off

Picking up in the basement safehouse beneath [[Myrna Nor|Myrna's]] beach house in [[Lathen]], the party — **[[The Fallen]]** — sat with the fallout of the previous session: [[Simone]]'s enchanted-sand [[The Fourth Magic|map of magic]] had exposed **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] as a Warlock**, not the druid he'd claimed. The green (fourth-magic) sand had crawled toward him just as the divine sand crawled toward the [[Golden Chalice]]. The [[S.A.T.O.]] ("the Satyrs") had also confirmed the chalice is an **extremely powerful divine artifact**, that the Academy is hunting a figure called the **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]**, and that a fourth, undisclosed magic tied to the **[[Great Cataclysm]]** now secretly powers every major civilization.

---

## A Warlock Among Us

With the secret out, the party turned to Leif with concern rather than judgment. **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]**, herself a warlock, reassured him — a patron isn't necessarily a curse. *"I appreciate my patron... it's not always bad. It depends on your relationship with them."*

Leif described his own patron plainly: he **hears whispers**, occasionally, **in a language he cannot understand**, echoing in his head. That is all he knows. The party wondered if it could be *healed*:

- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** (a cleric) doubted any cleric could undo a **warlock pact** — *"I'm not sure any cleric can heal a divine deal."*
- Notably, Aren — who despises anything that perverts the boundary between life and death — had **no objection to Leif being a warlock**: *"Leif is fully living right now. He's not broken."* Her enmity is reserved for the undead.

Leif and **[[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]]** shared a quiet reconciliation — Red held no grudge over the deception. *"Still staff bros."*

## Myrna's Beach House
![[Pasted image 20260715202511.png]]

Myrna led everyone up out of the basement into the main floor of what turned out to be her family's **summer vacation home** — a genuine mansion. Myrna waved it off as *"my little family's getaway,"* while her friends quietly clued the party in: **Myrna is the wealthy exception** among them, and they've learned to let her live in the assumption that everyone has that kind of money.

**Red gained a new companion:** through a ritual, he bound a **raven familiar named Bama**. Bama and Leif's owl **Howl** took to each other immediately.

## Meet Archibald

The Nor family's butler, **[[Archibald]]**, introduced himself and set about preparing dinner for twelve on short notice — with unsettling ease. Úlfr's read on him (Insight 14): a perfectly competent butler, but *more* than a butler — a **fixer/bodyguard** who quietly provides "additional services" to the family.

The party soon learned why:

- **Archibald can cast Teleport** — a high-level wizard spell (confirmed by an Arcana check). He is the one who ferries the S.A.T.O. between their campuses. He deflected questions about where a "butler" learns such magic.
- He serves the meal using **Unseen Servant**, plates floating themselves back to the kitchen (Leif recognized the spell).
- He **eavesdrops as a matter of professional duty** — *"It's my job to know what takes place under this roof."* On the [[White Forest]], he offered the most useful lead of the night: there was **research into the forest's history before the Academy banned the subject**, and the party should **find a scholar or professor who studied it prior to the restriction**.
- He is **discreet about the Nor family** but vouched for Myrna: *"a good soul, with a strong will and a good head on her shoulders."*

*(For the record — [[Archibald]]'s menu: potato-and-leek stew, an iceberg wedge salad with blue-cheese cream, panko-crusted seared halibut, and mini molten chocolate lava cakes.)*

## The Locked Door


During the pre-dinner rest, curiosity got the better of **Drolta, Red, and Leif**, who tried to break into a **locked room upstairs**. Drolta's lockpicking failed; Red then tried to *dismantle* the lock with his carpenter's tools — and struck the metal so hard it rang a **deafening TANG through the whole house**.

Myrna and the S.A.T.O. came running. Red's cover story (Deception at disadvantage) fell flat, and Myrna was genuinely hurt: *"I know I just drugged you, but this is way not cool... you could have just asked me."* She refused to open it — it's her **father's away-from-home study** — and shut the matter down. Simone gently advised the party to stay on Myrna's good side (the perks of her friendship — and her mansion — outweigh the costs). At dinner, **Drolta apologized** (Persuasion with advantage, backed by Red and Aren) and Myrna let it go.

## Dinner and the Map of Magic

Over [[Archibald]]'s courses, **[[Tobin]]** raised a question about Simone's map. The green fourth-magic concentrates at every major seat of power — the **Citadel** of the [[Endymion Empire]], La Maison of the arts college, the head church of [[Dogmatika]], the Academy — but **two anomalies** stood out:

1. A spot over a **desert on the Lost Continent**.
2. A spike at **[[Pentamarone]]** — supposedly *"just a little village"* on the border of [[Artmegía|Artmegia]] and the Endymion Empire.

**Aren** (a native of the Empire) knew Pentamarone: a small **artisan crafting town**, home to the **[[WitchCrafters]]** — a female guild of witches who produce much of the art and crafting supplies traded across the continent. Crucially, it is **the only place inside Empire borders not under the Empire's jurisdiction**. Aren speculated the witches may have **learned to harness the fourth magic to resist the Empire**.

---

## The Magistus

Someone recalled that Pentamarone was **founded by one of the Magistus** — and the table's collective history (Red rolling best) surfaced the legend:

- The **[[Magistus]]** are the spellcasters who **stopped the [[Great Cataclysm]]**.
- Accounts conflict on their number: some say three, some four. Quintilius's version reconciles it — **four went out, but only three returned.**
- **Only one Magistus is known to still be alive today: Emperor Endymion himself** — currently marching an army to unify the continent, and possibly **the most powerful spellcaster in the world**. The other two returners **vanished shortly after coming back**.

The implications rippled outward: one Magistus rules the Empire from the Citadel, while **another founded Pentamarone — the one town the Empire *doesn't* control** — hinting at a **falling-out among the Magistus**. And the green cataclysm-magic runs along the very line the Magistus supposedly *stopped*. Úlfr connected the threads aloud: the students want to know where the green magic came from, and here is an **unexplained pocket of it in a town founded by a Cataclysm-stopper.** Someone should go look.

## The Whispering One

At the head of the table, **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** rose to deliver on the quest Leif had given him — to **name his patron**. He spoke of his people's reverence for names and stories, and of the old belief in a **time before names**, when the gods had not yet carved the world into things that could be spoken of, and there were older powers — vast, and loud, and quiet.

> *"I think we should call your patron the Whispering One."*

The table loved it. **[[The Whispering One]]** — a working name until the entity's true name is known, a way to begin *understanding* it rather than merely being spoken to. The party toasted: *"To the Whispering One."* Drolta offered to **ask her own patron, [[Nephthys]], for more information** — no guarantees, but worth a try. Leif was moved and grateful.

## A Night's Rest, Then the Town of Lathen


The S.A.T.O. teleported home for the night (Archibald casting), leaving the party the run of the safehouse. After a **long rest**, the party set out to explore **[[Lathen]]** — a small **port city** to the south, better known as a **spring-break destination** for Academy students than for anything else. Two weeks a year it's overrun; the rest of the year the locals just get by.

- A town **gate** with bored, lax guards who waved the party through once they name-dropped Myrna.
- A **shanty/nightlife district** to the "left" — rentals and taverns where the college crowd comes to drink; clearly the trashier quarter.
- A **business/port district** to the "right" — warehouses and cargo staging where ships offload goods bound inland. This is the part of town with a purpose beyond tourism.

## The Temple of the Agent of Mysteries
![[Pasted image 20260715211147.png]]
Lathen's small temple is dedicated to **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth, the Agent of Mysteries]]** — a winged young woman whose statue crowns the doorway (Aren recognized her at once). A sermon had just let out; the friendly **priest** welcomed the outsiders to worship whatever higher power they served, since his goddess embraces the great mysteries — including *which* being is the right one to devote oneself to. This turned into the session's great cosmology download:

**The Solemn Pantheon.** The world was shaped by a singular higher being, the **[[Solemn Pantheon|Solemn]]**, who has since departed, leaving his **[[Agents]]** to govern his domains. There are **nine canonical Agents** (some argue ten):

| Agent | Domain |
|---|---|
| **Mercury** | Wisdom |
| **Venus** | Creation |
| **Earth** | Mystery |
| **Mars** | Force |
| **Jupiter** | Miracles |
| **Saturn** | Judgment |
| **Uranus** | Entropy |
| **[[Neptune]]** | Life |
| **[[Pluto]]** | Death |

**The disputed tenth — [[The Moon]].** Some theologians hold that **the Moon** is a sentient power on par with the Agents, but whose purpose is to **protect the Agents** rather than govern mortals. She is said to **reach out to those she deems worthy** to aid her in that protection.

**The Dark Lords.** Shortly after creation, a group of **higher angels** (just below the Agents in power) **rebelled against the Pantheon**, were cast down, and **fell to earth**, sowing discord among mortals ever since — even before the [[Severance]]. The first and their leader is the **[[Darklords|Morning Star]]**; some claim to have seen his physical form. *(An uneasy echo: the party calls itself [[The Fallen]].)*

On the **White Forest**, the priest knew nothing beyond the curse, and believed — on faith alone — that it was stopped by **divine intervention**. He was aware of no divine or Pantheon connection to the forest itself.

**Red drew a private thread out of the sermon.** He described a recurring dream — **three orbs (purple, blue, red)** hanging before the moon, with the animals he knew "betwixt and bewitched" — which the priest identified as **Venus's three orbs**, the muses through which she channels divine inspiration. Red then admitted he's also been **dreaming of the moon**, and things had "started happening." The priest's read: **the Moon may be reaching out to Red** as one of her "lucky few." He promised to research the Moon's worship and get back to him. Red dropped a silver in the collection basket.

## The Curse of the White Forest

Across the day's conversations, the party assembled what's known of the **[[White Forest]]**:

- Named for its **ethereal white trees** — unnatural, clearly touched by magic (probably **divine**, per Simone's map).
- Decades ago, a **curse** spread from the forest as **cursed vines and thorns** that corrupted the land and **killed or corrupted anyone who touched them**. The Academy and even the [[Endymion Empire]] sent people to stop it; all failed.
- After roughly **ten years and ~50% progress into the continent**, the vines **suddenly receded on their own** — no one knows what stopped them, or why.
- Even before the curse it was a **strange place**, with a persistent rumor of a **coven of witches** living within.
- **Quintilius's warning:** it's a cursed place that "almost destroyed the continent," and now holds the **largest store of divine power in the world**. He cheerfully offered to give a good eulogy at the party's funeral.

## Drolta's Temple, and the Vampires Who Attacked It

Discussing the planned stop at a **[[Nephthys]] temple** on the edge of the White Forest, **Drolta** shared her past: she was a **high priestess** there. She left after a **gruesome attack** in which **vampiric beings** ([[Lady Nectaria]]'s kind) killed many of the faithful **searching for something** — and she has been **tasked with, and is searching for, the truth of what happened**. She hasn't returned since; the memories are too painful, but the chalice and the new magic feel *familiar* to her.

This sparked a broader realization (Rosie leading): **vampires are the only higher beings still active in the world** — likely **among the oldest living beings**, and quite possibly **the only ones who actually remember the [[Severance]]**, which mortals do not understand. There is a **vampire professor at [[Witherbloom College]] — [[Professor Valentine]]** — who could be a source, if she can be approached without being killed by (or killing) the party. Aren, unsurprisingly, floated simply eliminating her.

## The Plan Forward

By session's end the party had a layered plan:

1. **Local temple first** — done (the Agent of Mysteries temple in Lathen), for general divine grounding.
2. **Teleport to the [[Quandrix College|Quandrix]] campus** (Simone provided the runes; Archibald casts, using **publicly available circles** so the party arrives looking like ordinary travelers). Then **immediately leave campus** to avoid Academy eyes.
3. **Travel overland ~6 days to the [[Nephthys]] temple** on the edge of the White Forest — ostensibly a pilgrimage, really to investigate Drolta's tragedy and the divine energy.
4. **Side trip to [[Pentamarone]]** — via **ship up the river** (Archibald's suggestion; Myrna may arrange passage through her father's shipping business) — to investigate the anomalous green magic and the WitchCrafters, crossing into contested Empire border territory.

Chosen **Quandrix over Witherbloom**: Drolta still means to visit Professor Valentine, but the group prioritized the forest mystery and Nephthys temple for now.

**S.A.T.O. tasks (the "Dumbledore's Army" on the inside):**
- **Tobin** will dig Academy records for **how many "Sinful Spoils" there are and what they are**.
- **Simone** will trace **who donated the star-tablet Asha translated** (the artifact that got her expelled) — a potential lead to the White Forest.
- Contact maintained via the **[[Sending Stones]]** (once per day).

**The repo clock:** the Arcane Reclamation Initiative can't repossess the chalice for about **20 more days** (~10 of 30 used), so the party isn't yet actively hunted — but teleporting will still flag their location to the Academy.
