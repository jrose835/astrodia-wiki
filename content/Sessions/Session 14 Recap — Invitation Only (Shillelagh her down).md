## The Fight Goes Badly

**[[Solenne]]** tried to run out and join the fight. Nephthara **grabbed her and held her back.** On a later turn Solenne fired from the doorway, over her elders' heads, at a spawn clawing Drolta: ***"Hands off our high priestess!"***

### The Spawn

The two **vampire spawn** went for the party's front line. Tommy labelled them **A** and **B**.

- **One clawed at Drolta.** Her **Mirror Image** flickered and took the hit. Over the fight, her duplicates soaked several attacks before the spawn started getting through.
- **B dashed at [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]**, the biggest thing it could see (*"they want that blood"*), clawed him for 8, and **grappled** him. It climbed onto his back and went for his neck, and Leif **passed the Constitution save** (DC 14). *("That's okay, because I took War Caster.")*
- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** hit B with **Sacred Flame**, and **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** ran out and hit it with **Fire Bolt.**

### Úlfr Breaks Free

Last session the table had believed nothing could break Nectaria's charm. Tommy had looked it up since: **damage dealt to the charmed creature or its allies by the vampire's side ends it.** Úlfr had just watched a spawn claw Leif. **The charm broke.**

He **raged again**, flanked B, and hit it with his axe and **Fires Burn** for **19.**

Úlfr also got a ruling on **Battle Cry**: it **isn't a bonus action**. It happens whenever he rages. **Up to five creatures** get a d6, including Úlfr himself, but **no one can hold more than one die at a time.** Since Leif had already spent his, Úlfr and Leif both got fresh dice.

Leif fired **Eldritch Blast** at Nectaria with advantage from [[Howl]]'s **Help**, and missed. He then broke B's grapple (DC 13).

### Nectaria Feeds

**Drolta** went after Nectaria herself, who had been *"talking shit in my ear."* She missed. She spent her **Lucky** points and **still missed.** Nectaria dodged and cackled.

Nectaria answered by turning on **[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s lion**, *"the only thing that I think has damaged her."* She used a **Grave Strike** (bludgeoning plus necrotic, 15) and **grappled** the lion, which she was strong enough to do. Then she **bit it**. Red failed the save and took **17 necrotic**, and **his maximum hit points dropped by 17.**

That became the theme of the night. **Every vampire bite drains maximum HP**, and **a character whose maximum drops to zero is dead, permanently.**

Red tore free (16 against 14) and ran for the temple. The opportunity attack knocked him **out of lion form** (13 damage, 7 bludgeoning and 6 necrotic). Nephthara healed him for 11 from the doorway. **Ysmene ran out, grabbed Asha's hand, and pulled her toward the door.**

**Spawn A** turned on **Úlfr**: two claws (rage resistance took the edge off), a **grapple**, and a **bite** that he failed to resist. That was 4 piercing after resistance and **12 necrotic**, and **his maximum HP dropped by 12.**

> *"I will bring you the death you seek."* *(as heard)*
