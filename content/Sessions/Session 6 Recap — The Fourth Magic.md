## A New Day in the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]]

With [[Powell]] routed and the [[Nath Loom]] secured, the party took a **long rest** inside the now-cleared library. Everyone began the day at full health and resources. Rather than immediately teleport back to campus, they decided to finish exploring the rooms they hadn't yet reached.

Two doors on their level gave off faint **cave sounds** — dripping water, skittering — that fell dead silent whenever the party knocked or listened, as if something behind them was getting into position. The dust on the handles confirmed Powell's crew had never opened them. One door was locked; with no rogue (or thieves' tools) in the party, they left the cave doors alone **for now** and turned to the unlocked rooms.

---

## The Forge & The Sentient Axe

Behind one unlocked door lay a **cold forge** — anvil, hammers, tongs, and a striking **double-bladed axe** resting on the anvil. [[Detect Magic]] (cast as a ritual by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]) revealed **enchantment magic**.

When [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] grasped it, the axe communicated not in words but in **emotion** — much like the orb creature, though Drolta determined the two were *different entities* with different magic. Later identification revealed it to be:

- A **+1 greataxe** with **sentience** (Int, Wis, Cha) — it "speaks" by transmitting feelings.
- A genuinely **good-aligned** weapon with a special purpose: **to protect the family of its bearer** (chosen/found family included).
- It had not been touched since [[Leith Chantos]] himself, centuries ago, and **does not know where he is**.
- Its given name is "**Leith Chantos's Legacy**," though Úlfr means to rename it. *(Note: this is NOT the famous double-headed axe from the fresco that Leith forged to stop the warlord — just another of his magical creations.)*

Úlfr claimed the axe but is still wielding his old greataxe until he trusts the new one.

## The Spider Pit & Una's Grave
![[Pasted image 20260531211052.png]]
The eastern cave door opened onto a **chasm room**: a wide ledge, two small platforms hanging on chains over the void, and a far ledge bearing a **headstone**. Lurking on webs below were **two giant spiders and a swarm of spiders**.

- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s spider phobia** triggered instantly — she broke free of an attempted grapple (Nat 20) and opened with Sacred Flame before initiative was even rolled.
- The party's **summoned wolf companion was killed** by the giant spiders.
- [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] missed a platform jump and fell into the webs, then went on to **eradicate the entire spider swarm in a single flaming-claw strike** ("a thousand blades"). Leif's [[Entangle]] locked down the rest.
- Spiders defeated, the party crossed via acrobatics and ropes to reach the grave.

The headstone read, in Orcish: **"Una, beloved wife."** A platinum ring rested on it. The party agreed **not** to take it — Una was Leith's poisoned wife. (Úlfr eyed it and grabbed it unseen.)

Beyond the grave, a tunnel descended **deep underground**. Úlfr recalled legends of a subterranean civilization called the **Subterra** — dismissed by most as too secluded to bother with. The party agreed this was a full expedition for another day and left it alone.

## The Reveal: The S.A.T.O.
![[Pasted image 20260531222702.png]]
The party woke in chairs in an unfamiliar room — **the basement of Myrna's vacation beach house in Lathen** — unrestrained, all gear intact. The wine was a **sleeping draught used to travel undetected**, since the Academy is watching its people. Asha had stayed awake the whole time.

A group of students stepped forward — old friends from the same Academy cohort, now scattered across the colleges. The party realized they'd **already met all but Simone back in Session 3** at the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]], never knowing these students were connected:

- **[[Myrna Nor]]** ([[Lorehold College|Lorehold]]) — the one who hired them for [[Leith Chantos]]'s library
- **[[Quintilius]]** ([[Prismari]]) — the drama-guild recruiter who **chatted with [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** in the [[Biblioplex]]
- **[[Simone]]** — the brains/most advanced mage; was working in the [[Biblioplex]] that first day (the only Satyr the party hadn't actually spoken to before)
- **[[Tobin]]** — *the appraiser the party once grilled at the cafe*, secretly undercover in Academy administration
- **[[Rosie]]** ([[Lorehold College|Lorehold]]) — the gnome LARPer **rescued from the tampered mimic** in the [[Biblioplex]] (real name Rosimyffenbip)
- **[[Ersmatok Rostch]]** ([[Witherbloom College|Witherbloom]]) — the orc plant-enthusiast **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] befriended** in the [[Biblioplex]]; showed a showman's streak ("I am my own fan... but I believe in telling a good story")

They call themselves the **S.A.T.O. — Students Against Tyrannical Overreach** ("Satyrs"). They banded together after **Asha was expelled** for researching a forbidden topic — an artifact from the **[[White Forest]]** — which they consider an antithetical abuse of academic freedom. Their mission: discover *why* the topic is forbidden and clear Asha's name. They recruited the party (already Asha's allies and clearly competent) as the **outside adventuring arm** the students-on-the-inside can't be. The Nath Loom quest was both a real need and a **test**.

## The Map of Magic

Simone demonstrated her great discovery using **enchanted sand poured onto a map of [[Astrodia]]**, each color a type of magic flowing across the world:

1. **White — Arcane magic.** Spread uniformly everywhere, yet oddly **does NOT spike at the Academy** despite the Snarl and Biblioplex.
![[Pasted image 20260531224110.png]]
2. **Red — Higher-being magic.** Concentrated where expected (Southern Kingdoms, Oceanic Alliance, Fire King Islands, War Rock Mountains) — but **still nothing at the Academy**, which is strange given the Academy was founded by dragons/giants.
3. **Yellow — Divine magic** (of the Solemn Pantheon). A **huge spike in the White Forest**, plus a **tiny new speck at the Academy that only appeared recently** — which the party realized is **the [[Golden Chalice]] they brought back two days ago**.
4. **Green — A fourth, undisclosed magic.** Newer than arcane, it traces a **line matching the path of the [[Great Cataclysm]]** (NW Endymion → SE through the Purodetapo Forest → Prana Mountains). This unknown magic **currently powers every major civilization in Astrodia** — and **no one is talking about it.**
![[Pasted image 20260531224531.png]]

**Simone's hypothesis:** Arcane magic may **not be native to Astrodia**. Before the [[Severance]], there are no records of mortal wizards casting spells — all magic came from divine or higher sources. *After* the Severance, mortals could suddenly harness arcane magic on their own.

The chalice tested as **divine magic** and **extremely powerful**.

## The Sinful Spoils
![[Pasted image 20260601072222.png]]

**Tobin** revealed an Academy record: they're hunting someone called the **"Seeker of the Sinful Spoils"** — depicted as a woman in **black and red**, matching **Asha's dream figure**. The party theorizes the **artifacts themselves are the Sinful Spoils**, and the Academy wants to keep them from this Seeker. Tobin had registered the chalice with the Academy's **Arcane Reclamation Initiative**, so they now know the party holds one — but a magically binding agreement means the Academy **can't repossess it until the deadline expires** (clock still ticking).

## Leif's Secret

When the green (fourth-magic) sand spilled, grains **crawled toward [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** — just as the divine sand crawled toward the chalice. Under questioning, Leif's secret surfaced:

- He is secretly a **Warlock** (with a Druidic background) — Simone proved it by commanding him to cast **Eldritch Blast**, which worked.
- He's been **hearing voices** he can't understand — in the **same language** as the creature trapped in the **orb**.
- He's from the **Purodetapo Forest**, directly in the Cataclysm's path. The voices began recently, after meditating in the forest — he fears he may have unknowingly made a **pact** or contacted something. His grandmother taught him magic; he's told almost no one.
- Drolta (a fellow warlock) offered to help him, but warned she **can't come between him and his patron** if one exists.

The party urged Leif to **commune** with the voices to learn the truth.

## The Plan

The S.A.T.O. and party agreed the next move is to **take the Golden Chalice to the [[White Forest]]** — away from the Academy, and toward answers about the new magic and the forbidden research. En route, they intend to stop at a **temple to [[Nephthys]]** (a higher-being hotspot near their path) — deeply significant to **Drolta**, whose temple was attacked by [[Lady Nectaria]]'s vampires. If those attackers remain in the area, the party means to find them.

The closest campus to the forest is **Quandrix** (reachable by teleportation circle).
