## Breakfast and a Plan

The party woke at the inn and held a meeting over a hearty breakfast of bangers and mash (Pentamarone feeds people who work in the fields). [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] laid out the options:

1. Go to the **[[White Forest]]** to rescue [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s mother. *"We've probably been scared of doing that for a while, but maybe we're ready."*
2. Go to the **Temple of [[Nephthys]]**, the original destination, possibly on the way to the forest.
3. Keep chasing **[[The Fourth Magic]]**, perhaps by getting back to the [[S.A.T.O.]], which would mean heading back toward the Academy.
4. Join **[[The Sundered Watch]]** against the [[Endymion Empire]].

Most of the table leaned toward the original plan. Nobody could quite remember *why* the temple had been the plan. The answer, dug out of the Session 6 notes, was the map of magic: the temple sits in a **higher-being (red) hotspot** near the White Forest, and Drolta has a personal connection to it.

### Word from the Satyrs

Tommy let the party roll all of its outstanding [[S.A.T.O.]] tasks at once and assign the results where they liked. Five d100s came up **30, 51, 14, 98, and 17**. Tommy said only the 98 would get an answer.

The 98 came down to two candidates: **White Forest lore** (any magic that would stop someone entering the forest) or **[[Simone]]'s star tablet**, the [[White Forest]] artifact that got Asha expelled. A d4 settled it: evens for the tablet. It came up even.

Over breakfast, a [[Sending Stones|sending stone]] buzzed:

> ***"Professor Astellar, Lorehold Professor, taking a sabbatical at Quandrix College this semester. She donated the tablet."***

**[[Professor Astellar]]** *(spelled A-S-T-E-L-L-A-R by Tommy)*. It's the first result Simone's assignment has ever produced. The party had started to suspect it was one of the secretly impossible tasks.

The other four questions (conservation of magic, the history of [[Marseille]], White Forest lore, and [[Hall of Oracles|the Oracle]]) came back with nothing.

### The Map

Tommy put up the regional map: the party in **[[Pentamarone]]**, the **[[Temple of Nephthys]]** marked in red, **[[Quandrix College]]** in green, and in blue a city called **Exod's Cross** *(as heard)*, the northernmost and largest city in [[Artmegía|Artmegia]] and the crossing everyone uses between Artmegia and the Empire.

### Genni's Power Stone

Genni went last.

> *"I don't know if this will be helpful, but since we all are slowly heading down south... if you ever come back this way, you'll need one of these."*

It is a **black circular stone bearing the triangle symbol**, one of the **[[Keystones|power stones]]** the [[Endymion Empire]] uses to manage magic. This one is **empty.**

- **Any spell can be cast into it.** Loading it takes the spell's normal casting time. It **holds one spell at a time**.
- **Anyone can then use the stored spell**, including someone who can't cast it. It's a reusable spell scroll. (The party's first idea: load **Healing Word** and hand it to someone with no healing.)
- Once the stored spell is used, the stone can be loaded again.
- In Empire territory it works as a **kind of passport**. Carrying one shows you're allowed to be there and have the means to cast legally.

> *"These things have kind of a terrifying or uncomfortable history for me, but they are extremely useful. And you'll need one if you're getting into the Empire's territory at all."*

### Genni's Story

The party pushed her (*"We're friends now, aren't we? You can share"*), and a **Persuasion check with Guidance (16)** got halfway there. Genni didn't answer. **Haine**, standing next to her, took her hand and answered for her:

> *"Genni's just very humble. She doesn't really like to talk about her history. But she's not just a little librarian. She gave up a lot to be here, given that she was, at one point, **the right-hand assistant of the current Emperor.**"*

When someone pressed for more, **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** cut in: *"Not all stories are meant to be told at this time."* Genni thanked her, and then said this much on her own:

> *"I was raised in the Empire, and for the longest time, I believed in what the Emperor was doing. I thought it was for the good of people and magic. But some lines were crossed that made me have to rethink a lot of things, and ultimately, I can't support the current Empire. So I fled and left my whole life behind."*

- **Is she safe here?** *"I think so."* Madame Verre protects her and the whole town, and *"to his credit, the Emperor has stayed to his agreement"* and never touched Pentamarone.
- **Does the Emperor know she's here?** *"I don't know. I have a feeling. Just 'cause he knows a lot of things."*
- **The Sundered Watch?** The party mentioned they had met resistance fighters. *"If they sought me out, I may be able to answer some questions, but I try to just keep that part of my life behind me."*

This explains her Session 10 line about the Empire, *"Yes. But I would know,"* and her Session 11 answer that she is *"not, technically,"* related to the Emperor.

## The Lab

The party asked the guild for the fastest way to the temple. The answer was the long way: **south across the river into Artmegian territory, then through Exod's Cross**, a few days on foot.

**Red rolled a natural 20 on Perception** and caught the witches trading looks: *should we just tell them? should we show them?* They had **a faster way** and weren't offering it. Red pushed (*"We're tired of walking"*). His first Persuasion attempt didn't get there, so Úlfr gave him Bardic Inspiration (*"You got this, Red"*), and it was **just enough.**

> *"Well, why don't we just take them to the lab?"*

**Genni** objected that Madame Verre had specifically said not to tell the party what they were working on. **[[Pittore]]** pointed out that the party already knew they were working on *something*, so they could use the circle without being told what. The guild agreed.

> *"Just don't, you know, while you're in there, don't go poking around."*

On the walk, Leif fell in beside Genni, partly to cheer her up after making her talk about her past, and showed her **the creature in [[The Orb]].** She had never seen anything like it. *"It even speaks."* It started talking in the language only Leif recognizes.

He told her where it came from, the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], and pointed out that **the robes he wears are [[Leith Chantos]]'s** and that Úlfr's axe is Leith's too. Genni's reaction: *"Wow. > *"Could you tell her that it speaks in the same insane language as my patron?"*

## The Temple of Nephthys

![[Pasted image 20261007201531.png]]

They came out on the road just below the **[[Temple of Nephthys]]**, a cathedral of spires and stained glass with a **great phoenix in the rose window**, built into the side of a **mountain or large hill**. It looks **rebuilt**. By Tommy's notes, the attack was **about five years ago**.

Úlfr asked Drolta how she felt, seeing it for the first time since that day.

> *"I'm feeling... a lot of things, I guess. Feeling is different now. But more than anything, I just want to get inside."*

She hoped they would find only practitioners inside, and given the rebuilding, no surprises.

**The statue out front is Nephthys in human form, a woman.** Drolta explained that the Phoenix Queen can take a human shape:

> *"She tends to be a bit elusive, except to those she's chosen... Just like the Phoenix, she burns bright and quick. But she always comes back."*

### After Church

A crowd was milling outside in Sunday best. It had just let out of a service. Drolta wanted to talk to them directly, since eavesdropping on her own sisters felt wrong. Úlfr wandered off to admire the statue and listened anyway (**Perception 14**):

> *"It was such a lovely ceremony. I think she's gonna make a great high priestess."*
> *"And she's so young, but she really looks ready for the task."*
> *"The last one, she left after the attack, which is understandable. No one's really heard from her since."*

Úlfr walked over to Drolta: *"I think they're talking about you."* *(At the table: "Not my replacement.")*

Drolta chose not to disguise herself, reasoning that people might be more forthcoming if they knew who she was. The crowd were ordinary worshippers, not temple staff. They explained that the **public anointing of a new high priestess** had just finished and the private part was happening inside.

- The new high priestess is **[[Solenne]]** *(heard at the table as "Celine" or "Selin"; spelling from the map token)*: very young, *"just out of childhood,"* who **arrived as an orphan** and was **raised by the temple** for the past couple of years.
- Drolta told them she had been a practitioner here **about five years ago, before the attack**, and that **she was there that night.** *"You survived? A miracle."* *"It was. And it was quite memorable."*

They thought her connection might get her into the private ceremony. Drolta went to try the door.

Úlfr stepped **behind a bush**, cast **Disguise Self**, and came out as **one of the commoners, a little old lady**, later introduced as **"Gertrude."** Leif and Red took a while to work out who she was.

## The Anointing

![[Pasted image 20261007203133.png]]

Drolta opened the door on the ceremony she had once gone through herself. **Nephthara**, the elder priestess, was placing the headdress on **Solenne**, who is plainly a child. **[[Ysmene]]** *(heard as "Yasmin"; spelling from the map token)*, in **blue**, stood beside them. All three were in ceremonial dress. Some of the faces in the pews were familiar and some weren't.

It hit Drolta hard. Tommy called for a **Wisdom saving throw**, and **she failed.** Seeing the ceremony caused a **lapse in the mental fortification she has grown used to**, and **a voice came into her head** *(delivered privately to Yemko; at the table: "Oh, fuck, I knew it")*. The little old lady beside her asked, *"You all right?"*

She kept walking, slowly, past the final pew. The song ended. Nephthara turned to the congregation:

> *"And with that, we anoint our new—"*
> *"...Oh, my God. Drolta."*

The whole temple turned around. People came up to talk to her while Nephthara stood frozen. **Ysmene abandoned her post** and ran to hug her:

> *"Oh, Drolta, it's been so long. I can't believe you're here."*
> *"Me neither. But it's great to see you."*
> *"Have you been well? Are you safe? Unharmed?"*
> *"I have been protected by Nephthys. And that's all I can ask for."*

Nephthara recovered: *"Ysmene. Please return to your post. We have to finish this."* Ysmene went back, and Nephthara finished the rite:

> *"...We now welcome and proclaim Solenne the next high priestess, to usher in the next age of Nephthys."*

## The Key

![[Pasted image 20261007210231.png]]

While the congregation crowded the new high priestess, Drolta whispered to Úlfr:

> *"There is a door to a vault near the door we just came in, to the right of it. That might be a great place to start."*

*"On it."* The old lady wandered over to light candles. Úlfr gave himself **Bardic Inspiration** and still couldn't find the door on Investigation. Then he took out **[[Edel]]'s Clear-Sight Lens**, which pulsed and pointed: **the outline of a hidden door**, right beside him. **It was locked.**

He sent Drolta a **Message** cantrip: *"I found the door. It's locked."* Drolta told him the key would be with one of the priestesses, and asked that nobody beat them up for it. *"Although Nephthara is old, she is quite spry."*

Leif had an idea:

> *"I have the Suggestion spell. ...It's time that I lean into being a proper warlock."*

Drolta confirmed by Message that **Nephthara** was the likeliest keyholder, and the party went to work:

- **Red** tried to separate Solenne from Nephthara (*"High priestess! Will you come bless me?"*). His Persuasion roll was poor. Nephthara corrected her pupil: in her position she **cannot show favoritism** and must acknowledge each person as their own spirit. Solenne promised to bless him after she'd attended to the people already in front of her.
- **Leif** leaned in behind Nephthara and said, very confidently: ***"I need the key to the door by the temple entrance. Can you give it to me?"*** She **failed the Wisdom save.** Her eyes glazed over. *"Yes, I can show you where the door is, and where the key is stored."*
- Solenne, left alone with the crowd, called after her: *"Nephthara?"* Leif, passing: *"Just be firm with them."* *"Uh, okay. Who are you?"*
- **Red** kept the distraction going: *"I need a blessing! I need a blessing!"*

Nephthara led Leif through a kitchen in the back, then into a bedroom, dug through a chest, and handed him **the key.** Leif sent her back to help Solenne. *"I feel kind of like a dick. Don't tell Drolta."*

### Ysmene

Meanwhile Ysmene came back to Drolta, quieter now.

Drolta told her that Nephthys had been with her through all of it, *"even closer than before,"* and that she had been **tasked with finding out what really happened here, and why.** Had the temple learned anything since?

- Ysmene had tried to push that night out of her memory.
- **Locals helped rebuild** after the attack and Drolta's departure.
- A visiting priest came, and the temple **paid a great deal from its funds for a protective spell over the temple** *(heard as a "hallow" spell)*. Then, leaning in: ***they haven't had the money to cast it again, so they don't know whether it's still holding.*** There have been **no attacks since**, to their knowledge.
- They were focused on rebuilding and **never got to the root cause.** Many longtime worshippers didn't feel safe and **left**. The temple had to build a **new congregation**, and today was the fruit of that.
- **Solenne** came to their door about **two years after Drolta left**, *"much like you did,"* and Ysmene was quick to put her on Drolta's path.

Drolta asked whether Ysmene was really all right with laying this on a child. Ysmene's answer was halting and seemed tied up with how hurt the temple was when Drolta left. Drolta let it go: *"That's neither here nor there."*

- **Anything missing or out of place?** **The ashes Drolta used to guard** were **untouched**, apparently unaffected by the attack. *"We think they were looking for something."*
- **The Fire Kings.** Ysmene hasn't had much contact with the **Fire Kings**, the **male worshippers of the [[Phoenix King]]** who live on **[[Fire King Island]]**. Perhaps attackers went after their temple too. Soon after the attack they **sent one priestess to help rebuild**, and she pointed her out: **[[Petra]]**. There has been little communication since.
- **[[Venus]]?** She hasn't thought about the Agents in a long time. Venus governs creation, and there is **some good faith between Venus and the Phoenixes**, since the Phoenix re-creates itself every cycle.
- **The meteor** from the [[Temple of Venus]] mural? Her religion check turned up nothing. *"It doesn't really jump out to me in any way."*

Then she offered the reason for the vault:

> *"If you're trying to get to the bottom of this, some of the belongings of those who were not so lucky the night of the attack, we held on to them and kept them in the vault. I don't know if anything in there would be of help, but I might be able to get you in there."*

And finally, with a look that said she **knew** something:

> *"I did what I could to save a lot of people that night. And you put up a good fight, and it's a miracle that you're still here. But I know I wasn't able to stop everything. When you left, [Nephthara] told us that you were going out on the mission that Nephthys gave you. She didn't give us much details. But I never knew if that was the truth, or if you felt shame or embarrassment for... what you are. I hope you know that we hold no ill will. At least, I don't. And I'm sorry if what I did pushed you into this uncomfortable spot between life and death."*

*(The name in that line was heard as "Ashara"; it is most likely Nephthara.)*

> *"You did the best you could, as we all did. I appreciate you."*

Ysmene couldn't speak for the temple now that it has a new high priestess, but **Drolta and her comrades are welcome here**, and she'll help however she can.

### Petra and Fire King Island

While they waited, Drolta talked to **[[Petra]]**, the priestess the Fire Kings sent. Petra had heard about the attack back on the island, came to help rebuild the temple physically and spiritually and to recruit worshippers, **fell in love with the place**, and stayed. She only goes home for short visits.

Drolta asked about anomalies on **[[Fire King Island]]**: strange visitors, spikes of energy, out-of-place relics.

- The island houses **relics of high importance belonging to both the Phoenix King and the Phoenix Queen.** They are kept there **for when the Phoenixes are reborn**, so the items will be ready and waiting for them. **Petra doesn't know what the items are.**
- She doesn't think the **[[Great Cataclysm]]** affected the island's temple.
- After the attack here, **security on the island was increased**, but it has been years and she isn't sure it's as strong now.
- As a woman, she is **not privy to the higher rites or secrets** on the island, because of the division between the orders of Nephthys and the Phoenix King *(a name was heard here as "Garunix", possibly the Phoenix King's own name; unconfirmed)*. That division is part of why she feels better here.

Drolta's summary to the party: **Fire King Island may be a good place to learn more about the Great Cataclysm.**

**Aren had a bad feeling** *(Tommy called it divine intuition)* and touched her pendant again. This time **it pinged, from outside.**

Through the window, about **a hundred feet away**, a **woman stood watching the temple with glowing red eyes.**

![[Pasted image 20261007221227.png]]

> *"There's an undead out there."*

Aren and Úlfr went outside, careful not to invite anything in.

Then Drolta felt a **pain in her head.** The locked door she has felt knocking and tapping for years **was thrown open**, and she saw ***the woman who turned her.*** **[[Lady Nectaria]].**

Nectaria spoke in a hushed whisper Drolta shouldn't have been able to hear from that distance:

> *"Brave of you to return to where your Phoenix Queen once failed you."*

## The Fight

![[Pasted image 20261007224122.png]]

**Roll initiative.**

**Six "Fiendish Icons"** pulled themselves out of the earth: squat, gargoyle-like figures that chip and crumble like stone or clay when struck. **Red's Arcana check** found they **aren't undead.** They behave more like **constructs.** They hit with **two slams**, can **dash**, and when surrounded or nearly destroyed they set off a **fiery eruption** (a Dexterity save for everyone within 5 feet).

**What the party did:**

- **Red** opened with **Moonbeam** on an icon. It passed the save, and the radiant did nothing special. He then wild shaped into a **lion** and **roared** to warn the temple. Later he leaped across the field and raked **Lady Nectaria twice for 9.** She scoffed: *that's nothing.*
- **Aren** summoned **Spiritual Weapon** (a sword) and rolled **max damage** on Icon E, which began to crumble like a statue. The sword finished E later (*"a pile of rocks"*) and broke another apart afterward. Her mace never found an icon's armor. She healed herself with **Cure Wounds**, then spent her **Human inspiration** on a **Guiding Bolt** at Nectaria that missed (15).
- **Asha** ran inside to warn the priestesses (*"There's a vampire thing going on!"*), hit the icons that followed her in with **Shatter**, and used **Shield** to turn away one of two slams.
- **Drolta** cast **Mirror Image** (*"Now which one is the real Drolta?"*) and drew her **plume**, whose dancing blades cut down an icon that was already badly hurt. She moved her **Hex** to another, then went after Nectaria herself and landed a plume strike with Hex damage. *(She tried to Hex and Inflict Wounds on the same turn. Tommy ruled that two leveled spells in one turn isn't allowed, and she took it back.)*
- **Howl** harassed the icons with the **Help** action, then flew over to hover above Nectaria.
- **Úlfr** raged and debuted a new ability, **Battle Cry**: as he rages, he **chants a verse**, and **every player character gets a d6** to add to one damage roll. He then hit Icon D with a hand axe for **18**, nearly destroying it. He threw another axe at Nectaria later. She tilted her head, and it missed.
- **Leif** stepped forward and fired **Eldritch Blast** at Nectaria. She tilted her head and it went past her ear. He fought the icons with **magic stones** until his next turn.

**Icon D**, nearly dead, went off in a **fiery eruption**. Leif, Drolta, and Úlfr (with **Danger Sense**) all passed and took 3 fire each. **Three icons dashed into the temple.**

### Aren's Bell

Later, **Icon A** erupted next to **Aren and Red's lion**, and both failed the save. Aren used her reaction on a **new feature tied to her backstory**: a d20 roll on a table of effects *(the feature's name was unclear on the recording)*. It came up **11.**

A sound rang out of her like a **church bell tolling**, like the bells of Notre Dame, followed by a flash of light. **Aren and the icon were both stunned.** Neither had ever seen it happen before. (Both still took the 7 fire.) Red used his reaction to say *"what in tarnation."*

### Leif and the Echo

Leif cast **Dissonant Whispers**, a spell from his pact, at **Lady Nectaria.** She was ready for it:

> *"You can't touch me. You are nothing."*

She **made the save.** But behind Leif's words came **an echo in the language he can't understand, in a voice that wasn't his.** That echo is what hurt her (7 psychic). Her head snapped back. She looked at him with fury (*how dare you*) and with **real interest.** No one should be able to do that to her. *"Yes, I have made a new enemy."*

He followed with **Mind Spike**, adding his Battle Cry d6 for **22**. She deflected the first attack and **the echo got through again.** She was angrier than before. *"Huzzah."* A later Eldritch Blast with advantage hit for very little.

### Nectaria Takes Úlfr

Nectaria answered by **walking over to Úlfr without taking her eyes off Leif.** She leaned up and **whispered in his ear.** Úlfr **failed the Wisdom save** and was **charmed.** Without breaking eye contact with Leif, she told him:

> *"That is how you fuck with someone's mind."*

Charmed, Úlfr couldn't attack her, and the icons inside counted as friendly to her, so he **turned to walk away.** She took the **opportunity attack** with her teeth and **bit Úlfr's neck.** He passed the Constitution save (**18**). **Her fangs couldn't get through his Goliath hide.** She pulled back and **spat blood.** It didn't break the charm.

Still charmed, Úlfr spent his turn on a **Healing Word** for a wounded ally (at 19 of 31).

### Inside the Temple

The priestesses fought for their temple. **Solenne** ran straight at the icons that had gotten in (Nephthara shouted *"Solenne, no, don't!"*) and fought them with radiant flame and **Fire Bolt**. When an icon attacked her, **Nephthara spoke words of power** and it **burned up in holy light** into nothing. *"Thank you."* **Ysmene** joined in as well.

### Reinforcements

With most of the icons broken, Nectaria was finished with them:

> *"I knew those fiendish icons weren't going to be able to get the job done. I need to send in a bit more of the artillery."*
> ***"Now is the time."***

Things came out of **the woods behind her.**

![[Pasted image 20261007232802.png]]

From the table: *"Those are spa—"* *(confirmed in Session 14: two vampire spawn)*. *"Holy fuck."*

Aren got the undead she wanted, and she is furious about it. Leif and Drolta are nearly out of spell slots. Úlfr is still charmed.

**End of session, mid-combat.**
