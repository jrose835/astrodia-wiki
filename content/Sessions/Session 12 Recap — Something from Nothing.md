## The Report

![[Pasted image 20260923204843.png]]

Everyone gathered in Verre's office, WitchCrafters on one side and [[The Fallen]] on the other, with **the chalice sitting on Verre's desk.** Genni opened by thanking everyone and was about to disclose the guild's funding sources when Verre told her to *get on with it.*

### I. The Chalice Itself *(Edel & Schmietta, with Úlfr)*

- **Indestructible.** No physical or magical damage left a dent or a crack.
- **Pure gold, *too* pure.** Schmietta's words: it is as if someone **crafted it out of the *idea* of the pure essence of gold**, not mined gold. There are no imperfections or impurities at all. It is *"too pure to be made of this earth."*
- **Age undeterminable.** It shows no wear, so it could be new, or it could be **prehistoric** for all anyone can tell.

### II. History *(Genni & Marka, with Aren and Asha)*

The party knew the chalice came out of **[[Caelwyrm]]**, so the team searched for chalice references tied to that region and found stories from **dragon-worshipping clans**:

- One clan **worshipped a white dragon** and used a ritual object that could **conjure whatever the dragon required.**
- The object was **fought over by rival dragon-worshipping clans** in the region of **the Great Ravine**, where [[Lucian Cugg]] found the chalice.
- The last record of it **disappeared a few centuries ago.**

It is unconfirmed that this is the same object, but it's the best match they have.

### III. The Magic On It *(the whole guild)*

- **Every dispel failed.** The magic can't be separated from the object.
- **The nondetection extends to whoever holds it.** Scrying on the holder returns nothing, *as if they were not on this plane of existence.* If the holder stands near someone else being scried, the image shows that person with **empty space** where the holder should be. **Carrying the chalice makes you invisible to divination.**
- **The magic is every school at once.** When they tried to dispel it, they couldn't tell whether the dispel failed or whether the magic was **replaced so fast it was imperceptible.** Tommy's analogy: *you can't tell if you're failing to flip the light switch, or if it's flipping back on instantly, and the light switch is all of magic.*

The party's immediate reaction: **if the Academy is trying to track them by scrying, it can't.**

### IV. The Copies *(Haine & Pittore with Leif; Seed & Ceramia with Drolta)*

**Objects:**
- **100% physical copies**, made of the same material, **down to the imperfections.**
- **No magical properties carry over.** Leif's duplicate chalice is only gold.
- The copy **can reproduce details the drinker doesn't know about, as long as the drinker has shared physical space or interaction with the original.** Genni's example: if someone tears a page out of a book you've seen, then hands it back without telling you, your copy will be missing that exact page.
- **Things the drinker has never encountered can't be made.** This is why Leif couldn't conjure the repo man.
- A copy **would pass physical inspection as the real chalice.**
- **A copy can be enchanted afterward.** Asha's idea: make a mundane duplicate and then imbue it with magic of your choosing. Genni confirmed it would work.

**Living things:**
- **Physically real, but hollow.** They feel no pain and **don't bleed.** They can be injured, and **injuries that would kill or maim the original destroy them.**
- **No self-preservation, emotion, or social response.** They are inert husks shaped like the creature.
- **Pieces separated from the body evaporate**, and **a dead copy leaves no trace.** The horse had evaporated by the time anyone got to the library. *(Tommy compared it to a dead Titan in* Attack on Titan.*)*
- **The cats were put down.** The guild explained, apologetically, that they aren't technically alive. **Aren was visibly upset** and got even quieter than usual.
- To **Asha**, directly: *the copy is not your mother, has no connection to your mother, and feels nothing.*

Úlfr needed one clarification:

> *"So you're telling me, if I punch a real horse, it would explode like that?"*
> *"You're a strong man, Úlfr."*

### V. The Cost

This is what "you feel a little weird" meant.

> **Repeated use of the chalice shapes the drinker's psyche.**

- It produces **unnatural feelings of grandeur and a drive for power, a growing god complex**, and an **unnatural sense of superiority.**
- It prompts **thoughts and impulses the user would not normally have.** WitchCrafters who drank during testing documented these feelings themselves.
- **The effects are cumulative** and scale with **the size of the request**. Asking for more, or for something bigger, costs more.
- **It varies by person.** The same request can affect two people at different speeds and intensities.
- **Reversible?** Maybe. Their best guess is that it fades with time **if the chalice goes unused**, but they can't guarantee it.
- **Overuse** could lead to **involuntary actions, possibly a full psychotic break.**

The party's ruling: **no one uses the chalice except in extreme emergencies.** Someone asked, *"Like needing horses?"*

*(Tommy confirmed that the players he messaged privately about how their characters feel after drinking still feel that way.)*

### VI. Something from Nothing

Genni's summary is the most important finding of the night:

> **The chalice generates something from nothing.**

Ordinary spells transmute material components or channel ambient arcane magic through verbal and somatic components. **The chalice does neither.** It doesn't draw on arcane magic and needs no components. Under every known rule of magic, that is impossible. The table called it a violation of **equivalent exchange.**

As a contrast, Genni pointed at **Red's [[Alchemy Jug]]**. It *seems* to make liquid from nothing, but it actually **siphons microscopic amounts of that liquid from all across the world**. Each cup of beer is less than a drop taken from a thousand cans elsewhere. The chalice doesn't siphon from anywhere.

> *"And I think that is what makes it **sinful**. From our best ability, we believe that this object violates the laws of nature — and specifically, quite appropriately, the domain of **the Agent of Creation.**"*

**[[Venus]].** Who could make such a thing? A god, possibly, or given the history, **the dragons.**

### Verre Withdraws Her Claim

Genni had one more point to make before Verre gave her judgment:

> *"Given the effects the chalice has on its users and the nature of it, I don't think it should be used for what you want to use it for."*

Verre thought about it and agreed.

> *"I think the risk is too great. And I don't want what we're working on to be tainted by whatever magic this thing has."*

**She returned the chalice** and honored the contract, simply not exercising her right to use it. **She still won't say what the goal was**, *"until I'm certain of a few other things."*

Verre cut in: *"That won't be necessary. **I know the spell.**"* The guild will look into it, though the nondetection will make it hard if the other Spoils share it.

> **The [[Great Cataclysm]] was caused by a group of people opening a portal to another world.**

The party's conclusion: **[[The Fourth Magic]] is otherworldly magic.** It runs along the Cataclysm's path because it *leaked in* through the rift. Aren suspects Verre knows because she was **a disciple of one of the [[Magistus]]**, most likely [[Rellyona]].

## The Temple of Venus

![[Pasted image 20260923220002.png]]

The party took Red's suggestion ("Who feels like going to get religion today?") and visited the **[[Temple of Venus]]**. The goal was to learn whether Venus has enemies who might build something to mock her.

The statue out front matches the one on the roof of [[Morgana's Tower]]: Venus with her **three orbs**, the **muses of creation**. Red has been seeing those orbs in his dreams.

### The Scriptures

Red and Aren read the books in the entry hall *(the investigation roll survived, of all things, disadvantage)*:

- The **Solemn** created the world, made the **[[Agents]]** to govern its domains, and then **receded into the ether**, like the deist clock-winder.
- Below the Agents were the **angels**. Some of them, the **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]**, rebelled and fell. The first was **the Morning Star**.
- **What caused the rebellion is disputed**; scholars of scripture argue about it.
- Venus lets those who pray to her **thrive and manifest what they desire** in their lives.

### The Priest

A commoner pointed them to the priest's office. He was happy to talk.

- **Venus** is the Agent of Creation, one of the hands of the Solemn. She watches over **all artistic expression and the making of goods and services**, and works **through her three muses**, which usually appear as spheres orbiting her.
- **There is no documented ill will between the Agents.** They respect each other's domains.
- **The Dark Lords** do hold ill will toward her, the Morning Star most of all. Venus was one of the hands they felt had overreached, but he knows of no personal feud.

**Red asked about his dream** of the three spheres (**purple, blue, and red**) orbiting **the moon** while animals sat staring at it. The priest's reading:

- **[[The Moon]]** is thought to be **a tenth Agent**, though that's disputed. She may instead be **the protector of the Agents**, the one who *"makes sure that everything stays as it should."* That includes protection from the Dark Lords, *"ideally."*
- Perhaps the divine is **inspiring Red to create something with animals.** *"That certainly is [something that resonates]. I love animals."*

**Red then mentioned the people he saw back home** with banners showing no Agent he recognized. The priest identified them as the **Church of [[Dogmatika]]** in the [[Closed Land]]:

- They claim **a new pantheon and deity separate from the Solemn**, **reject the Solemn Pantheon**, and say they have **saints who perform miracles.**
- They have grown exponentially over **the last couple of decades.** His guess is that they were **founded about a century or a bit more ago.**
- *Around the time of the Great Cataclysm?* *"It might coincide with it... In times of great peril, people are always looking for hope."*

A theory was whispered to the party afterward: **what if Dogmatika's new god and saints are things that came through the rift too?**

The party presented themselves as **"students of life"** doing anthropology. Nobody joined the church.

### The Mural

![[Pasted image 20260923220651.png]]

In the basement, at the priest's recommendation, is the temple's prize artwork. It depicts **the Solemn and his host against the Morning Star and his armies** at the moment the rebels were **cast down to earth**. Ever since, the Dark Lords have tried to reclaim enough power to ascend again.

- **Asha (History):** the mural is **old, probably a couple of centuries**, and well preserved. It **wasn't painted here**; it was brought from elsewhere. The style suggests **the South**, from cultures that revere the **higher beings** (the oceanic kingdoms, [[Fire King Island]], the [[Phoenix King|Phoenix]] worshippers). It isn't the work of plain Pantheon worshippers, and it's unsigned.
- **Drolta (Religion)** knew the story and sensed that **something in the depiction doesn't belong to it.** **Red** found it: a small **flaming meteor falling in the middle of the scene**, clearly a **deliberate addition**, not an artistic flourish.
- In the timeline (creation → the war with the Dark Lords → millennia → the Severance → millennia → the Cataclysm), **this event predates everything.**

**Drolta meditated and reached out to [[Nephthys]].**

> *"I do not know what this is trying to invoke. The battle between the Dark Lords and the Solemn Pantheon was even before my time. There are still many mysteries about it that the higher beings are not aware of."*

Then she asked about **a portal to another world, and beings coming through it.** Nephthys was silent for a moment.

> *"I cannot speak of this freely."*
> *"...But continue on your path."*
> *"Of course, my child."*

### She Who Fell Before the First Fall

Back in his office, the priest explained the meteor:

> A **sect** within the church believes it represents **the cause of the Morning Star's rebellion**, and its members slip that belief into artworks like this one. In their account, **a being was exiled from the [[Sanctuary in the Sky]]** before the war, and that exile is what **spurred the Morning Star to rebel.** Nobody knows her name or whether she existed. She goes by a pseudonym:
>
> ***[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]].***

He doesn't personally believe it. **Aren passed a check and had heard of it before.** One or two believers turn up in many of the churches she's visited. It's a fringe belief with a quiet following. In their version she was **an angel, a woman**, who did something that **angered the gods so much she was exiled to earth**, and the Morning Star's rebellion came *in response.* What she did and what became of her are unknown. The Morning Star's fall was the first fall; hers came before it.

Someone asked aloud what everyone was thinking: ***what if she's the Seeker of the Sinful Spoils?*** A fallen woman who makes things that mock the gods.

### The Library

Downstairs, a Sunday-school classroom and library had **no books on her**. *"It's a bit of a fringe theory."* (Aren: *"You don't need to have evidence to have faith."*)

- **Aren read about [[Pluto]]'s enemies.** It was mostly necromancers and other things she already knew, with one exception: **[[The Prophecy Destroyer|the "Prophecy Destroyer"]]** *(as heard)*, thought to have been **an enemy of Pluto** in some unknown capacity.
- **The religion of the Empire:** the [[Endymion Empire]] **enforces worship of [[Mars]], the Agent of Force.** Pentamarone worships **[[Venus]]**. The two neighboring powers have two different patron Agents.
- **Two more Agents identified from the book's illustrations.** The creepy figure with **blue hair, red eyes, and yellow beneath them** is **[[Uranus]], the Agent of Entropy.** *(Tommy noted he has "dropped a blue-haired guy a couple of times" that the party hasn't picked up on.)* The water-themed Agent was confirmed as **[[Neptune]]**, whom Leif's clan worships.
- **No record of any conflict between Agents.**

- **Is the leftover green magic from the original leak?** Yes. To her knowledge it is still present across Astrodia.

Her farewell:

> *"You and your group have found yourself in the crosshairs of a number of very interesting happenings in Astrodia at this point, I will say."*

She expects them to leave soon, probably for the White Forest "by way of a temple or two," and invited them to **come back later**, when *"maybe we'll be done with our project."* She also warned them:

> *"Keep that chalice safe. Very powerful artifact. If word of its power were ever to get out, more powerful people would probably be after you."*

She asked how long they have before the Academy comes for it. After some checking of recaps at the table, **the repo clock is about 10 days.**

**End of session.**
