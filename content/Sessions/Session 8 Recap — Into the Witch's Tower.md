## Housekeeping — The Training Wheels Come Off

Before play, DM Tommy called a table check-in. Everyone's enjoying the campaign and eager to head north. Two structural notes for the road ahead:

- **The campaign wiki is live.** Jim updates the knowledge base (player pages, NPCs, locations) after every session and publishes it, so it grows over time — the party now has a reference to consult between games.
- **"Training wheels off."** The campaign isn't changing, just widening in scope. Tommy will **minimize backseat-driving** (no more obvious hints), so players are responsible for knowing their own abilities and remembering to use them. Everyone is now **Level 3** with a subclass.

### New Mechanic — Satyr Rolls (S.A.T.O. tasks)

With the students back at campus and the party heading north, [[The Fallen]] can now deploy the **[[S.A.T.O.]]** as remote **"hirelings."** The rules:

- Each **in-game day**, any student assigned a task rolls a **d100** against a hidden target number.
- Difficulty sets the starting target and the rate it **drops each day** (easy tasks might start near 50 and fall by 10/day; hard ones start ~90–95 and crawl down by ~2/day). Some tasks may be **secretly impossible** — the number never drops, and the party will eventually have to reassign that student.
- Tasks must fit each student's **specialty**, and the party can only assign work via the **[[Sending Stones]]** — **one student per day**, and using the stone for a task **spends the day's single use**.
- Crucially, tasks are only things the party **could do themselves** — this offloads background legwork without removing player agency.

Standing assignments (from the end of Session 7):
- **[[Tobin]]** — investigate **what and how many** the "[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]]" are.
- **[[Simone]]** — trace **who donated the star-tablet** [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] translated (the artifact that got her expelled). *(Tommy hinted the party may already hold the answers to these in visions and books they've been sent.)*

The six recruitable students and their wheelhouses: **Simone** (numeromancy / library), **Tobin** (administration / investigative journalism), **Rosie** (invention & tinkering), **Quintilius** (performance & dead languages), **Myrna** (history & lore), and **Ersmatok** (biology & plants).

## Reintroductions — Who We Are Now

At Level 3, the table went around and re-declared characters, secrets, and who-knows-what:

- **[[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]]** — druid, now **Circle of the Moon**; expect a lot more wild shape.
- **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** — a **Warlock who has accepted it**, **Pact of the Chain**, with his familiar **Howl**. Squishy. *(The table joked that "coming out as a warlock" was its own kind of coming out.)*
- **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** — the Goliath skald delivered his own introduction aloud:

> *"They named me Úlfr Kveld, the Evening Wolf. And for most of my life I believed my hands were the only true thing about me. I was raised in a house that prized verse, and I threw the gift back in their faces — certain that a creature shaped like me had no business shaping words. But grief has a way of teaching what pride refuses to learn. I have come to understand that the strength in my arms and the verses of my throat are not two selves at war, but a single fire that can either burn or warm. So I carry both now, without shame. I will break a shield-wall when I must, and I will make a song of it after — for the axe speaks the last word, but the poet decides what is remembered."*

- **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** — former **High Priestess of [[Nephthys]]** (the Phoenix Queen, both Destroyer and Reviver); orphaned young, she found family and purpose at the temple until it was attacked by **vampires**, who killed nearly everyone — including a child she loved. She nearly died and was brought back **between life and death** as a **dhampir**, tasked by Nephthys to learn why the temple was attacked and what the vampires wanted. **Only [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] and [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] know she is a dhampir** — notably, **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] still does not.** As a **celestial-patron warlock**, she now has healing on tap.
- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** — cleric on a patron's mission to deal with the **undead**; blunt, uninterested in small talk, and — as the party would soon learn the hard way — she **really hates spiders**. Leaning ever more into radiant/light magic.

## Ambush in the Woods
![[Pasted image 20260722203648.png]]

On the very first stretch out of the Quandrix forest, [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] — traveling with a small aviary now (his raven **Bama**, Leif's owl **Howl**, and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s hawk **Astra**) and having cast **Speak with Animals** — caught a **bird-call that was wrong**: a person mimicking a signal to someone ahead. He sent Bama to scout, and the raven found a man in the trees. It was an ambush.

Combat broke out against a five-strong bandit crew — **the Eyes** (their archer/lookout, barely a fighter), **the Slicer** and **the Cutthroat** (dagger duelists), a hulking **Muscle**, and eventually their **Boss**, called in as reinforcement. Highlights:

- **[[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] wild-shaped into a giant spider** — and it worked *too* well. **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]**, viscerally repulsed, spent much of the fight hurling **Sacred Flame at her own ally**, muttering *"abominations should not be tolerated."* Red's giant-spider **poison bites finished off both the Eyes and the Slicer**.
- **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** deliberately **held his rage in check** at first, taking hits in stride and swinging **[[Leith Chantos's Legacy]]** (his new sentient greataxe, which has a **cleave**), before finally letting the rage loose on the Muscle.
- **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** dropped the Eyes and Cutthroat with **Tasha's Hideous Laughter** and chipped away with **Eldritch Blast**; **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** worked **Inflict Wounds, Hex, and Mind Sliver**; **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** rained **Fire Bolt** and sent Astra diving in.
- **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** used a **Portent** roll to turn one of Úlfr's misses into a hit; he thanked her with **Bardic Inspiration**. Úlfr also spent charges of **[[Snorri's notebook]]** to shave damage off incoming attacks.
- The **Muscle** fell to Úlfr and Drolta. The **Boss**, badly outmatched, took the **Disengage** and **fled into the woods** — and **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] noticed he didn't run randomly**: he made straight for a particular spot, as if toward someone waiting.

The party chose not to pursue. **Loot:** a greataxe (Muscle), a longbow (the Eyes), and four daggers (Cutthroat & Slicer) — to be sold in town.

## Camp, and the Dreams

The party made camp in the trees. With the Satyr rolls now ticking each day for Tobin and Simone, the night brought **dream rolls** — and several rough ones (Leif, Asha, and Drolta took **exhaustion**; Red rolled a **Nat 20** for inspiration; the dreams themselves carried weight):

- **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s dreams** braided together several recurring visions. A **woman in black and red** — winged, with **talons for hands and wings of talons** — holding **the [[Golden Chalice|chalice]]**. And a *different* woman, robed **all in white and godly**, **drinking from the chalice** while holding **a book, a lance, and a white gown/cloak.** Tallying the objects — **chalice, lance, book, dress** — the party now believes these are the **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]]** (or several of them). Notably, **neither woman matches the Academy's wanted poster** of the Seeker.
- **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s dream** — his usual nightmare where everything **turns yellow** and he hears **[[The Whispering One]]** speaking in its unknowable voice. This time the voice came **from Howl**, and the **owl's eyes went completely yellow**. Leif fears the Whispering One may be trying to **take (or speak through) his familiar.**
- **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]'s dream** — someone she **deeply loved** at the [[Nephthys]] temple, now with **blood-red eyes and fangs.** She fears **vampires may still be active** at the temple they're approaching, and that this homecoming will be *"a little rough."*

The party relayed the Sinful Spoils theory to **[[Tobin]]** via the [[Sending Stones]] — and promptly ran into the stones' **25-word limit**, forcing a telegram-style message (*"...spoils are: dress, lance, book, chalice. Tobin, keep eye out for..."*). Tobin replied: *"Will do — will continue to check what the Academy knows."*

### On-the-road conversations

## Freedale — A Town the Curse Nearly Took
![[Pasted image 20260722222237.png]]

Two and a half days on, the party reached **[[Freedale]]** — an older town (~2 centuries) on the western edge of [[Artmegía]]'s civilization, and **one of the places hit hardest by the [[White Forest]] curse.** Only about **20% of the population** has returned; buildings sag with holed roofs and boarded windows, and the few residents look worn.

At a **chicken-and-beer tavern** full of **orcs** (Freedale has become an accidental **fishing town**, drawing orcs up from the [[Expanse]] to chase a prized **freshwater mackerel**), a friendly barmaid — herself a former worshipper at Drolta's temple — laid out the town's history and the **clearest firsthand account of the curse yet**:

- **Before the vines came**, strange omens: **children vanished** for stretches of time and then **returned unharmed**, and the **trees around town began turning white**.
- When the **cursed vines** came within sight of Freedale, everyone who could **fled**; the few who stayed to wait it out or fight **mostly died**.
- The vines eventually **receded on their own** (no one knows why); returnees spent **years** clearing the decayed, ivy-and-thorn-choked ruins.
- The **[[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] has told them nothing** and offered no real help — a familiar grievance.

The party scoped the town: a **smithy** (to offload the bandit loot), a **stable** (six horses; **75 gp to buy, 25 gp/week to rent**; secured at night by a single flimsy padlock), the **General Goose Store** (fishing and general goods), and a nicer **private residence** with a sunroom. **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] repeatedly floated stealing the horses** — even donning a **Disguise Self** as a shifty-looking stranger — but the rest of the party, unwilling to rob a town still crawling back from ruin, talked him down. They'd buy or rent in the morning; the horses aren't going anywhere.

## The Witch's Tower

The tavern-keeper explained the **wizard's tower** at the town's edge: it belonged to a witch named **[[Morgana]]**, who — **before the curse fully reached Freedale — went into the [[White Forest]] to fight whatever was coming**, and **never returned.** Rumor holds that when the vines swallowed the town, they **carried her body back and left it at the tower** — a cruel joke, if true. Most locals give the place a wide berth; they say it's **haunted.**

That was all the invitation the party needed. Approaching, **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** used her pendant to **Detect Undead** and froze: a **strong undead presence, somewhere beneath the ground under the tower.** *"There's something unnatural beneath the earth here."*

The front door was locked and unyielding. Spotting a **third-floor balcony**, **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** (dhampir-nimble) scaled the wall with ease and dropped a rope. **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** hauled himself and **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** up — but the **rope snapped**, leaving **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] and [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] stranded below.** Through the balcony's glass doors the climbers glimpsed a **dining room and bedroom**; Úlfr **shattered the glass with his axe** and the party pushed inside.
