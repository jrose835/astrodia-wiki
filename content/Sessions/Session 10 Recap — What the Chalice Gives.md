## Somewhere Else Entirely

The party steps out of the circle with everything they carried — onto a **dirt crossroads in unfamiliar woods.** No temple. No town. No Archibald, who called the circle and went straight home to [[Myrna Nor|Myrna]].

> *"This is definitely not where my temple is."* — [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]

Whether [[Archibald]] botched the casting or something else redirected them is unresolved. The table's leading theories were *divine misdirection* and *demonic misdirection*, in that order.

**First question asked, before anything else: what color are the trees?** Regular. Not white. Relief all around.

[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s investigation — soil, flora, the shape of the woods, and a certain amount of literal dirt-tasting — returned a firm negative: **this is not [[Artmegía|Artmegia]].** No civilization visible in any direction. [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] confirmed it wasn't his home forest either.

### The Army from the North

Red sent **Bama** up for a bird's-eye sweep, and the eagle found something a few minutes' flight north:

> **An army on the march**, under a banner of **a sword crossed with an axe.**

[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s history check came back with nothing whatsoever. **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] recognized it instantly: the [[Endymion Empire]].**

Which answered where they were, and immediately raised a worse question. The column was roughly **30–45 minutes of marching away** and heading south — the same direction the party needed to go. Consensus: **travel south, parallel to the road, off it.**

**Red's survival roll carried the whole party** — keeping the slower members on pace and branches out of everyone's faces — and after a couple of hours the treeline gave way to **crop rows and farmhouses.**

Bama went up again for a second look, and this is the detail worth remembering:

> At the crossroads the party had passed earlier, the Endymion column **went south, then split — half east, half west.** They are no longer following the road south at all.

[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]], who knows something about military formations, called it: **a pincer.** Two fronts. Against what, nobody could say.

## Pentamarone


Cresting the last hill past the farmland, the party looked down on a **walled city on the banks of a river** — and on its banners, a sigil that was very much not a sword and axe:

![[Pasted image 20260826194137.png]]

A **hammer, shears, and a needle.** *"Well, this one seems to be of crafting."*

The party put it together on the spot: they had stumbled into **[[Pentamarone]]** — the border town flagged on [[Simone]]'s map as one of only two anomalous **green-magic** spikes outside a major capital, the town **founded by one of the [[Magistus]]**, and the one place inside [[Endymion Empire]] borders the Empire does not rule.

The farmers in the fields waved to each other and went about their day. The gates were open. No artillery on the walls, a couple of guards at the entrance.

![[Pasted image 20260826195254.png]]

### The Gate

The lead guard — a woman, arms crossed, entirely unbothered — asked the obvious question of six armed strangers walking up a road out of Empire territory:

> *"Are you escaping their reign, as many others who have entered the city have?"*

Ulfr, constitutionally unable to let a question go unanswered, **lied.** The deception roll was abysmal even after a **Bardic Inspiration** die went in on top of it, and the guard's insight barely needed to work for it:

> *"I can just tell that y'all are not from around here, and y'all are not escaping the Endymion Empire. This town is open to everybody. I'm just curious why you feel the need to lie and be deceptive."*

[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] stepped up and used his full Goliath height to try a different angle — *"I think you all have bigger problems than us, based on what we've seen on the road"* — which did not help either. In the end **Red talked them in honestly**: they'd seen armed forces on the move, they were behind them, and all anyone wanted was a night off the hard ground. The second guard shrugged. The first stepped aside.

> *"This is the city of Pentamarone. Home of the WitchCrafters Guild, and the last stronghold against the encroaching Endymion Empire. Enjoy your time in Pentamarone."*

Ulfr's read, once they were out of earshot: *"These guards don't seem too concerned with safety. Their own city... I don't think we'll be safe here if that army comes. We protect ourselves."*

## The Market

![[Pasted image 20260826195548.png]]

Walking into the market circle was, per the DM, **the shot in the Wizard of Oz where the film turns to color.** Everything handmade, everything patchwork, everything vividly colored — **nothing like the sterility of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]].** Gemstones, clothing, pottery, glassware, blacksmithing, paintings, books.

### Leif and the Glassblower

[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] went to the glassware stall and found a young woman selling dishware and goblets — *"mostly decorative,"* she admitted, *"obviously inspired by Madame Verre."*

Asked whether glass can serve as a **spellcasting focus**, she was emphatic: **yes.** All of **Madame Verre's** work is magical, **her staff is glass**, and she uses it as her arcane focus — *"given that she's actually a powerful witch."* Verre **heads the [[WitchCrafters]] guild** and effectively runs the city.

Then the conversation turned, and turned into the session's best piece of luck.

She is **a refugee from [[Freedale]].** She fled with her family as a child **during the curse.** And when the party mentioned the witch's tower:

> *"You went into the witch's tower? Morgana's? And you escaped? Is it haunted, like everyone said?"*
> *"...A little bit."*

Her family came to Pentamarone **because [[Morgana]] told them to.** Morgana was prolific and well-connected among **covens and guilds**, kept a list of safe havens — and, per the glassblower, **had worked with Madame Verre in the past** on something she couldn't name. The party told her Morgana was dead. *"A great witch."*

**Leif bought a plate, cutlery, and a glass goblet for 3 gold** — the woman's *first sale.* [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] quietly cast **Detect Magic** over it to be sure nothing evil had followed them: **nothing.** Ambient WitchCrafter magic saturates the whole market, but the set itself is pure craft, *"hard work and dedication."*

Leif also produced **the glass orb** recovered from Morgana's tower. She couldn't read anything on it — *"remarkably smooth, very nice handcrafted work,"* nothing more. **Madame Verre would know**, she said, but audiences with Verre are nearly impossible; commoners deal with other guild members instead.

Warned about the army on the road, she shrugged it off. **Routine patrols. They never come close.**

> *"It's just kind of an understood thing that this is an area free of their dominion."*

### The Other Stalls

- **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] — the tailor.** Commissioned the **bear skin** into a cloak, with the **head left intact to rest on his shoulder.** **2 gold 5 silver**, ready the next day. *(Still uncollected — see Open Threads.)*
- **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] — the book stall.** Her investigation turned up a title that stopped her: ***The Biography of Rellyona*** — and a history check named her. **Rellyona is one of the [[Magistus]]: the woman believed to have founded this town.** Asha bought it along with a history of **the Greater Endymion Empire**, haggling **30 silver down to 15.**
- **Asha — the blacksmith.** Ornate but entirely mundane stock. The smith explained the market's economics: **these are novice artisans and junior guild members.** Genuinely magic-infused work comes only from the **WitchCrafters themselves**, at a market they hold **once a month** — **about two weeks out.** The guild also **teaches open classes to townsfolk**, and sufficient skill earns access to the masters. He also talked Asha out of a chest plate (*"as a wizard — I'm just guessing you're a wizard — armor makes casting pretty difficult"*) and sold her an **ornate curved dagger for 2 gold.** Parting note: a **firework festival in a couple of days.**
- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] — listening.** Kept an ear out for anything religious and caught a passerby praising *"the service at the Temple of Venus yesterday."* **There is a temple to [[Venus]] in this city.**

### Red and the Jug

[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] went to the pottery stall asking after **brewing supplies** — no farm, no equipment, no beer since he left home. The potter liked the cut of his jib, reached under the stall, and set down a ceramic jug.

> *"There's not much magic that goes around here, it's pretty novice — but I did get help from one of the WitchCrafters with this. I've been saving it for somebody I think is gonna appreciate it."*

Red's arcana named it: an **Alchemy Jug.** Speak a liquid from its list and the jug produces it — including **four gallons of beer.** The asking price was **50 gold**, and Red did not hesitate for a full second.

He sat down, said **"beer,"** and has not paid for a drink since. *(A competing table proposal to test the word "mayonnaise" remains, as of this writing, untested.)*

### Housekeeping

The long-unclaimed **[[Clockwork Amulet]]** in the party inventory finally found an owner: **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] took it** — forgo the attack roll, treat the d20 as a flat 10. Given his dice this session, an extremely well-timed acquisition.

## The SS Nap Time

Late afternoon. Asking around for lodging turned up two options — a large inn in the center of town, and one down by the docks. The party picked the docks, on the theory that it would be cheaper and full of **sailors** (and thus of **stories**, which is [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s entire operating principle).

What they found was **three ships that hadn't moved in a very long time** — or one three-decked ship, depending on who was describing it — **converted into an inn and tavern.** The party's verdict was "gentrified boats." The table christened it **the SS Nap Time**, and the DM made it canon on the spot.

Red poured from the jug. Leif drank out of his new glass goblet. Nobody had to buy anything.

### The Sailor

Úlfr found a sailor at the bar hauling a shipment up to **the Citadel**, bound afterward for **[[Bayhaven]]** in Artmegia. On the local craftsmen:

> *"A little too artsy fartsy for my taste, but they make good stuff. Quality. Especially anything made by a WitchCrafter herself — it'll last your lifetime, if not more."*

Asked the real question — *how does a town inside Empire borders not belong to the Empire?* — he gave **three competing rumors**, and the party has no way yet to tell which is true:

1. **A truce.** The woman who founded the town and **the Emperor go way back**; they made an agreement before she died, and he has honored it ever since.
2. **A facade.** The town is **secretly working with the Empire**, and its independence is theater.
3. **Fear.** The WitchCrafters are powerful enough to **hold their own**, and the Empire won't risk provoking a fight it isn't sure it wins.

He also confirmed the borders are hard: get caught outside the town doing **unauthorized magic without [[Keystones|keystone]] authority** and the army will make it your problem.

### Word from Tobin

**[[Tobin]]'s Satyr roll succeeded** (**[[Simone]]'s failed** — no word from her). His [[Sending Stones|sending stone]] message, in 24 words:

> *"Tobin here. Checked Academy. Knows of four Sinful Spoils. Spear, book, chalice, dress. They think Seeker already has one. Will continue to investigate."*

**Four items — and they match [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s dream exactly** (lance/spear, book, chalice, white gown/dress). The new and unwelcome part: **the Academy believes [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|the Seeker]] already holds one of them.** Which one is unknown.

The party's reply, after some negotiation with the 25-word limit:

> *"Tobin, what is the Academy's plan to get the chalice back from us?"*

### What Asha Hadn't Told Them

Prompted by the conversation, Asha finally shared a vision from her exhausted rest two sessions back:

> A figure matching **the Seeker's poster — white hair with red tips** — **throwing magic at a man**: long **blond-gray beard**, heavily built, in a **whitish-yellow robe**. He may or may not have been fighting back.

She reminded the party of her own limitation: **she cannot tell whether what she sees is past, present, or future.**

That fed into the other item the party wants to ask the Satyrs: on Simone's map, **the yellow (divine) speck that appeared at the Academy was assumed to be the [[Golden Chalice]]** — so **did that yellow dot move when the party left with it?** If the chalice's magic travels with the chalice, the map is a tracking device pointed at them. If it stayed put, the yellow was never the chalice at all.

### The Night

Rooms were a gold piece each. **Leif**, freshly poor from buying dinnerware, asked to sleep on the deck and was refused by the barkeep; **Aren** offered to sponsor him out of party funds. Everyone got their own room. *"There's more money to be spent on lodging when we don't have to spend it on beer."*

**Dream rolls.** Several PCs have now rolled through their entire bank of dreams and get nothing but the consequences. **Red, Úlfr, and Asha failed** and took **exhaustion** — Úlfr, already carrying a level out of Morgana's tower, now sits at **two.** *(The tally got a little muddled at the table; worth confirming with Tommy.)* **Aren and Leif rested well** — Leif *"slept like a baby. It felt like I was being rocked to sleep."*

**Only Red still had a dream left to receive**, and it was two images:

> Something grand and good — a **great city, or a temple**. And then **the same thing in ruins.**

No context. A bad feeling attached. Over breakfast the party threw out guesses — a floating city, Drolta's temple — and got nowhere.

## The WitchCrafters Guild

![[Pasted image 20260826211841.png]]

In the morning the party went to the **guild hall** — walled off from the rest of the city, flying the same banner as the gates. Inside: an entry hall busy with people carrying paint, smoke, and half-finished work between doors, and a receptionist named **Dolly.**

They asked to see **Madame Verre.** Dolly laughed, kindly.

> *"A lot of people want to talk to Madame Verre."*

What she could offer was **Genni** — the guild's knowledge-seeker, its bookworm, the one who'd know. Dolly offered to carry the chalice to her. **Asha's insight said absolutely not**, and a successful persuasion got the whole party walked back instead.

On the way, Dolly explained the guild's structure: **open classes for the townsfolk**, advancement to study with a master witch once you show sufficient skill, and very few who ever reach that level.

### Genni

![[Pasted image 20260826212307.png]]

They found her in her study, which doubles as **the guild library.**

Genni cast **Identify** on the [[Golden Chalice]] — and hit **the same wall the Academy hit, and the same wall the party hit themselves.** Three attempts now, three failures.

> *"I'm sorry. There's some non-detection magic. I can't quite figure out what exactly it is."*
> *"How often does this happen?"*
> *"Not often, to be quite honest."*

The party laid out the whole chain for her: found by [[Lucian Cugg|Lucian]] in **[[Caelwyrm]]**, stolen, sold to goblins, recovered, submitted to the **[[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]]** at the Academy, signed away under a disclosure form, and now on a clock before a **repo man** comes to collect it. And the name they'd attached to it: **the Sinful Spoils.**

**Her ears perked at that.** She'd heard it. She went through her books and came back with **"the Seeker of the Sinful Spoils"** — the same title the Academy uses — and nothing else. Beyond the name, she had nothing.

On the **[[White Forest]]**: she knows the curse (it threatened Pentamarone before it receded) but little of the forest's history, except one thing the party has now heard from three independent sources — **a coven of witches lived in there.**

On the town's founder, she confirmed what the biography implied and what the party had been hoping was false:

> The town **was** founded by one of the [[Magistus]]. **She is no longer with us.**

**Rellyona is dead.** The Magistus the party came here half-hoping to consult has been gone for years.

**Asha cast Detect Thoughts** on her — surface only, no deeper probe (Genni would have felt it, and Asha wanted to stay trustworthy). What came back was confirmation rather than betrayal: **Genni genuinely does not know**, and she was **already planning to take it to Madame Verre.** Asha's follow-up persuasion for an audience **failed** — Genni would go ask, and report back, but the party was not coming with her.

### The Safe

While they waited, **Asha walked the perimeter of the room** and looked past Genni's desk.

> There is **a safe under the desk**, and on its face is **the symbol of the magic the Endymion Empire uses** — the same symbol Asha then noticed **on Genni's own staff.**

The party's read: this is where the guild stores its **[[Keystones]]** — the Empire-issued gems required to cast legally anywhere outside the town's borders. A brief, immediately-vetoed suggestion to steal them followed. The more uncomfortable reading also got raised: *is the guild's independence a front?*

### Genni's Verdict

She came back twice for clarification, then delivered the answer: **Madame Verre couldn't help either.** *"Best of luck to you all."*

But pressed on what *she* would do with it, Genni gave the party the single most valuable thing they learned all day:

> The nondetection **is not a spell laid on the chalice.** **Dispel Magic would do nothing.** It appears to come **from the chalice itself** — *"almost like the chalice is casting non-detection constantly, on itself. Which I don't really know how that works."*

Nobody put a ward on this thing. **It wards itself.**

One last exchange, easy to miss. Asked about the guild's relationship with the Academy, Genni was diplomatic — scholars, mutual respect, magic as academic study versus creative expression, *"some say they have radical overreach, but they've also held out against the Endymion Empire."* When [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] said the Empire was worse, Genni answered:

> *"Yes. But I would know."*

And left before anyone could ask what she meant.

## The Chalice, Finally

Five months of carrying it. Three failed Identifies. A bounty, a deadline, and a repo man.

And then, sitting in a library with a magic jug that makes beer on command, **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] asked the question nobody had thought to ask:**

> *"A chalice is technically a cup. What would happen if we tried to drink from it?"*

Red rinsed it out, filled it, and handed it over.

### What Happened

**Aren drank first.** She lowered the cup, and — *poof* —

> **Cats.** Cats appearing all over the guild library. Solid. Warm. Real to the touch. Held up by the tail, a cat just... **lets it happen.**

**Asha drank.** Behind her appeared **her missing mother** — exactly as she looked **the last time Asha saw her in person.** She feels real. She **doesn't react**, doesn't recognize her daughter, just looks blankly. She carries nothing of value, and **anything taken from her disintegrates** the moment it leaves her.

Asha hugged her anyway, said *"I will find you,"* and walked out of the room.

**Leif drank**, and conjured **a second Golden Chalice.**

**Úlfr drank**, and — with the inevitability of a man who has spent this entire campaign trying to acquire a horse — conjured **a horse.** It would not be ridden. It would not move on command. So he **punched it**, on a genuinely terrible attack roll, and

> **the horse exploded.** Anatomically. All over the guild library.

Aren was done. Dolly was allergic. Genni had gone very quiet.

### Rules of the Cup, As Observed

- **You drink, you think of a thing, the thing appears.** No incantation, no components.
- **It has to be something you know.** Úlfr tried to conjure **the repo man** — to see the face of who's coming for them — and was **refused.**
- **Living things are copies, not creatures.** Physical, touchable, and completely inert — no will, no reaction, no use in a fight. *"Physical, but not exactly the thing. Not spiritually."*
- **Objects may be another matter.** The duplicate chalice is sitting right there. The party immediately started doing math on **duplicate gold** and **decoy chalices left in every town they pass through.**

### The Catch

The DM's closing note on the subject, offered without elaboration:

> **Everyone who drank from the chalice feels a little weird afterward.**

Weird how? *"Hard to tell."*

> *"You know what they say. If something's too good to be true, it probably is."*
