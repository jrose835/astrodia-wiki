**Dolly** is the **receptionist at the [[WitchCrafters]] guild hall** in [[Pentamarone]] — a commoner rather than a witch, cheerful, and the first person [[The Fallen]] spoke to inside the guild.

## Session 10 (August 26, 2026)

- Met the party's request to see **[[Madame Verre]]** with a kind laugh: *"A lot of people want to talk to Madame Verre."* Offered **[[Genni]]** instead.
- Volunteered to carry the [[Golden Chalice]] back to Genni herself. **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s insight said not to let it out of their hands**, and a successful persuasion got the whole party escorted back instead.
- Explained the guild's structure on the walk: **open classes for the townsfolk**, and advancement to study under a master witch once you show sufficient skill — though few ever reach that level.
- Stayed in the library while the party experimented with the chalice, mostly telling [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] not to touch the books — and discovered she is **allergic to cats** at roughly the same moment several appeared out of thin air.
