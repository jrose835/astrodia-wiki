**Edel** is the **[[WitchCrafters]]' gemcutter and stone-worker** — the guild's expert on jewels, ornament, and magic stones. Of all the masters, she is the one who took the most straightforward road to the guild.

> *"I always just liked pretty things."*

## Background

Gems and magic stones fascinated her early. She came from **a comfortable background with good teachers**, studied whatever she wanted, got very good at it, and **[[Madame Verre]] found her.** She has worked for the guild ever since — the only master whose story contains no war, no curse, and nothing she'd rather not discuss.

## Session 11 (September 9, 2026)

**Assigned to the materials team with [[Schmietta]]**: determine what the [[Golden Chalice]] is physically **made of** — the metal, the jewels set into it — and whether either can be **dated to a particular era or place in [[Astrodia]].** Of the four lines of inquiry [[Madame Verre]] opened, hers is the one most likely to tell the party where the chalice actually came from.

She joined the guild's night out at a [[Pentamarone]] dive bar with [[Seed]], [[Schmietta]], and [[Marka]], and was the one who explained the **fireworks festival** to the party: every guild in the city competes, no limits on materials or spells, and **the winner runs the city.** The WitchCrafters have won every year since its inception.

> *"Is that due to your magic, or to actual effort?"*
> *"Magic is effort, as I'm sure you know."*
