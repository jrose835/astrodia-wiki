A professor at [[Witherbloom College]], a satellite campus of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] located to the south of the Acropolis.

A potion seller in [[Thornvale]] (a former Academy student) recommended Professor Lang to [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] as someone who may be able to help diagnose and cure the mysterious illness affecting Leif's sister.
