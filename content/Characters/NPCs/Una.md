**Una** was the beloved wife of [[Leith Chantos]], the historical orc figure whose final library the party explored. She was **poisoned and died** — a loss Leith mourned deeply, as evidenced by the black robes of mourning the party found in his bedchamber.

## Her Grave

Beyond the **spider pit** in the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], across a chasm, lies a far ledge bearing her **headstone**. The inscription reads, in Orcish:

> Una, beloved wife

A **platinum ring** rested on the headstone. Recognizing it as a tribute to Leith's poisoned wife, the party respectfully agreed **not** to take it and left it undisturbed.

Ulfr later took it secretly
