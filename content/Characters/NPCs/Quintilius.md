A **[[Prismari]]** student at [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] and a member of the [[S.A.T.O.]] — an old friend from [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s Academy cohort. A 2nd-year with a flair for performance and a love of old words, he's the Satyr who **struck up a conversation with [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** in the [[Biblioplex]] back in Session 3.

## How the Party Knows Him

In the [[Biblioplex]] (Session 3), Quintilius was recruiting for the **Play Actors Drama Guild** and the **Dead Languages Society** when he approached [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]. Úlfr made his preference for oral tradition over written plays clear:

> *"I have all sorts of stories, and they're all in here"* — (pointing to his head).

Neither knew then that they'd meet again in the [[Lathen]] safehouse, where Quintilius was revealed as one of the Satyrs.

## Role in the S.A.T.O.

As a [[Prismari]] student steeped in drama and dead languages, Quintilius brings performance, presentation, and a scholar's ear for old texts to the group — useful skills for a cohort trying to decode forbidden history and keep its activities quiet. His specific contributions to the group's work are still emerging.
