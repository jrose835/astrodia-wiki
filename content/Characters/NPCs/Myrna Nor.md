A 4th-year [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]] student at [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] and a **leader of the [[S.A.T.O.]]** (the "Satyrs"). Myrna is researching a thesis on [[Leith Chantos]], a historical orc figure with ties to the [[War Rock Mountains]]. Beneath the eager academic is a sharp, careful operator — the one who planned the party's covert extraction to the [[Lathen]] safehouse.

She believes she has located Leith Chantos's **final library** — about a 4-day trek northeast of the Academy in the mountains — and hired the party to explore it, recover the [[Nath Loom]], and return it to her.

## Role in the S.A.T.O.

Myrna is the group's organizer and public face. Her family's wealth and beach house gave the Satyrs their safehouse, and she handles the logistics — teleport fees, runes, gifts, and the cover stories that keep the group beneath the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]]'s notice. The [[Nath Loom]] commission was both a genuine research need and a **test** of whether [[The Fallen]] were the outside adventuring arm the Satyrs needed. They passed.

## The Quest (Lost Library)

Myrna had a research partner named **[[Powell]]**, a skilled wizard. They parted ways, and Powell hired mercenaries (including goblins and hobgoblins) to claim the [[Nath Loom]] for herself. Powell had about a one-day head start on the party.

### Payment Offered

- 100 gold per person
- Acknowledgment in her Lorehold thesis
- Access to look through the Nath Loom
- Any dungeon loot found is the party's to keep
- A **Scroll of Teleportation** as a down payment (includes main campus teleportation circle glyphs)

## Background

- Her parents own a **vineyard south of [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]]**; the family is wealthy.
- The family's **vacation beach house in [[Lathen]]** now serves as the S.A.T.O. safehouse, reachable by teleport rune.
- Noticed [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] and asked if he was a Goliath or descendant of giants. He deflected. She mentioned hearing that giants have some connection to the university — an early thread toward the Academy's hidden nature (see [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]]).
- Can usually be found at the **[[Fire Jolt Café]]** most afternoons, or in her dorm in [[Pillardrop]].

## Session 6 Developments

- The party returned the **[[Nath Loom]]** and **[[Leith Chantos]]'s personal journal** to Myrna at the [[Fire Jolt Café]]. She was overjoyed — the journal fills the gaps in her research and will **vindicate her thesis** completely. She confirmed the party retains **access to read the Nath Loom anytime**.
- Unable to hand over 600 gold in public, she invited the party to her [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]] dorm in [[Pillardrop]] that evening, covering the teleport fee.
- In her dorm she cracked open an "expensive celebratory bottle of wine" that was secretly a **sleeping draught**. She insisted *"You have to trust me — this is for Asha's sake,"* and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s insight confirmed she was **not malicious**. The wine let her transport the group **undetected** to the safehouse, not to harm them. (Asha didn't drink and stayed awake the whole way.)
- The party woke unrestrained, gear intact, in the basement of her **family's beach house in [[Lathen]]**.
- **Revealed as a leader of the [[S.A.T.O.]]**, the secret cohort working to clear [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s name and uncover the truth behind the [[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]] and the forbidden [[White Forest]] research.

### Gifts to [[The Fallen]]

- **100 gp per member**
- A pair of **Sending Stones**
- **Teleport runes** to return to the Lathen safehouse anytime (she covers all teleport fees going forward)
- A bottle of the (untampered) celebratory wine for a real toast

## Session 7 Developments

- Hosted [[The Fallen]] at the family **beach house** in [[Lathen]], where the party met the Nor family butler, **[[Archibald]]** — an improbably powerful spellcaster who teleports the [[S.A.T.O.]] between campuses.
- Was **hurt and annoyed** when [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]], [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]], and [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] tried to break into a **locked upstairs room** — her father's private study. She refused to open it (*"you could have just asked me"*), but forgave the party after a dinner-table apology.
- Confirmed her family runs a **cross-continental shipping / supply-chain business** through the port; may be able to arrange **passage by sea/river** toward [[Pentamarone]].
- **S.A.T.O. tasks going forward:** [[Simone]] will trace **who donated the star-tablet [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] translated** (the artifact that got her expelled); [[Tobin]] will dig into the **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]]** (how many, what they are). Contact via the [[Sending Stones]] (once/day).

## Session 9 Developments

**Her first Satyr task, and she nailed it.** With four horses at 75 gp threatening to drain the party's entire purse, [[The Fallen]] used the [[Sending Stones]] to send Myrna the **teleportation runes they had copied from the roof of [[Morgana's Tower]]** and a request for a lift — squeezed into the stones' 25-word limit, with 10 of those words spent on the runes themselves.

**She succeeded**, and sent **[[Archibald]]** through the circle, who teleported the party the last leg to the [[Nephthys]] temple and turned a two-day walk into a morning. His parting terms make her an ongoing logistics line: *"Should you need me again, just ask Myrna"* — wherever there's a circle to post to.

She is now **free for a new assignment.**
