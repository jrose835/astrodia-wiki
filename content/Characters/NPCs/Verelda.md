**Verelda** — "V" — is a scholar at [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]], based at the **[[Biblioplex]]** and connected to the **[[Quandrix College|Quandrix]]** faculty. She was **[[Morgana]]'s longtime correspondent, collaborator, and friend**, and the two of them jointly designed the **construct guardians** that now stand watch across the Academy. She has never been met in person — [[The Fallen]] know her entirely through two letters recovered from Morgana's tower in Session 9.

## The Collaboration

Verelda supplied the **theory**; Morgana supplied the **forge**. Verelda's notes on an **"animating lattice"** corrected the temperament of their **third guardian**, which had previously *"nearly cleav[ed] the poor delivery boy in half over a mispronounced password."*

The result is not a curiosity. The guardians standing at the [[Biblioplex]] — and, per [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s history check, **posted throughout the Academy** — descend directly from this work. Both women deliberately kept the origin quiet:

> *"Tell your colleagues at the Biblioplex their new watchmen owe more to a witch in the hills than they'll ever know, and let's keep it that way — I'd rather not explain myself to a room full of deans."* — Morgana

Neither of them ever worked up the nerve to present it to the Quandrix faculty.

## Her Letter (Session 9)

Verelda's reply to Morgana's final letter was found **still in the tower's foyer**, dusty and undelivered, bearing **the seal of the Academy**. It is the more revealing of the two documents:

- She **read Morgana's letter three times** and *"did not much like what I keep finding between the lines of it."*
- She did not try to forbid the trip — *"You are, as ever, allowed to go and look at things"* — but pressed hard: as *"someone who has watched you talk yourself into 'safe enough distances' before,"* she asked Morgana to **write from wherever she ended up before doing anything past looking**, threatening to send someone after her **on a very fast horse**, *"and you know I have the connections at [[Quandrix College|Quandrix]] to make that inconvenient for you."*
- She reported the **third guardian holding steady**, having asked one of her students *"quite politely, to state their business"* before letting them pass — *"which I choose to take as evidence your correction worked rather than evidence it has developed opinions."*
- She told the story to **Dean Lassette** over dinner, who **laughed until she had to leave the table.**
- Her closing: *"Go carefully. Go loudly, if you can manage it, so that whatever it is knows you were never planning on being quiet about any of it. Write back the moment you're able. I mean that."*

**Morgana never wrote back.** The letter sat unopened in the foyer for roughly twenty years while her body lay in the basement.

## Why She Matters

Verelda is the party's most concrete unfollowed lead out of Freedale:

1. **She holds a copy of Morgana's research.** Morgana's journal: *"I've sent what I know to Verelda, in case my instruments say more than I do."*
2. **She is reachable** — an Academy scholar, at a place the party can get to.
3. **As far as anyone knows, she does not know Morgana is dead.**
4. **She understands the guardians** better than anyone alive — relevant now that [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] carries a [[Guardian Command Amulet]] and the [[S.A.T.O.]] may eventually need to move against Academy security.
5. She names **Dean Lassette**, a figure the party has not otherwise encountered.

Both letters are undated but roughly two decades old; whether Verelda is still at the Academy — or still alive — is unknown.
