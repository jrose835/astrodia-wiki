**Marka** is the **[[WitchCrafters]]' calligrapher** — the guild's master of penmanship, cartography, and documentation of every kind. *"Anything the guild needs written, I'm the go-to for that."* She drafted and amended the party's contract with [[Madame Verre]] on the spot, using a self-writing quill.

She is the guild member who wrote down, in enforceable language, the only protection [[The Fallen]] have over the [[Golden Chalice]].

## Her Craft

Marka's thesis is that **a spell is not only what it says but how it is written.** Wizards use spellbooks; she believes the **calligraphy itself imbues a specific type of magic**, independent of the words.

> *"How the spell is written, and the calligraphy of that in itself, can imbue a specific type of magic."*

## Background

She studied at the **Grand Spellbook Tower in Marcell** *(name as heard at the table in Session 11 — unconfirmed)* and left because it didn't suit her: *"I felt it a bit too — I don't know. Just didn't really vibe with me."* She went looking, ended up in [[Pentamarone]], and met [[Madame Verre]].

## Session 11 — The Contract and the Notebook (September 9, 2026)

- **Drafted the guild contract** binding [[Madame Verre]] and [[The Fallen]] — the guild's best effort at identifying the [[Golden Chalice]] in exchange for Verre's undisclosed use of it, plus a written acknowledgment that the party warned her of the danger, and **no harm to the party.**
- Deadpan negotiator. When [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] demanded food and lodging, she turned to her boss and asked whether to add a clause *"that we have to provide you housing, or else you don't agree to the contract."* Verre rolled her eyes and agreed.
- **Assigned to the records team with [[Genni]]** — searching every book, history, and account in the guild for the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]] or any chalice able to manifest physical copies. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] worked alongside them.
- On the fireworks festival, she was cagey and pleased with herself: the guild's entry is already handled, and whatever Verre is planning with the chalice is *"something else."* — *"If it goes how we want it to go, it'll be a beautiful unveiling."*

### She Read Snorri's Notebook

At the tavern, [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] handed her **[[Snorri's notebook]]** — never letting it out of his sight — and asked what she made of it. She flipped through it and immediately placed the language:

> *"I'm picking up some words here that originate from the **[[War Rock Mountains]]**."*

Her reading of the object itself:

> *"The words are particularly powerful. It almost feels like someone really put their heart and soul into this book. Whether that's benevolent or malevolent power, I'm not one to say — but you seem to be able to wield it how you see best."*

Her theory: **power can be imparted into an object subconsciously**, by whoever wrote the right words in it. She also noted, drily, *"a couple words that are not quite appropriate to say at this table."*

Úlfr and Marka closed the exchange in complete agreement on the subject of words as weapons:

> *"Sticks and stones may break your bones, but words can never hurt me."*
> *"I strongly disagree."*
