A **Bullywug** (frog-like humanoid) who was **expelled from [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]]** years ago.

[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] recalled Murgaxor after rolling a natural 20 on a history/arcana check during the giant frog incident at the [[Fire Jolt Café]]. After being expelled herself, Asha had researched other expelled students and remembered this name.

## Suspected Activity

Murgaxor is the prime suspect behind at least two incidents at the Academy involving a **black, oily substance** with **transmutation magic**:

1. A **tampered mimic** found in the [[Biblioplex]] (Session 3)
2. **Enchanted frogs** that grew into Giant Frogs at the [[Fire Jolt Café]] (Session 4)

In both cases, the same black oily substance was found. The frog connection (Bullywug = frog-like race) strengthens the suspicion.

## First Appeared

Session 4 — identified by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] after the giant frog incident
