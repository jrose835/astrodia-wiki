**Howl** is **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s owl** — his constant companion since he found her injured in the **[[Purodetapo Forest]]** with a hurt wing, and, since the Session 6 reveal, understood to be his **Pact of the Chain familiar** rather than an ordinary animal friend.

She is also, on the evidence of Sessions 8 and 11, **something more than that.** *(Revealed in Session 12: Howl is **a servant of [[The Whispering One|Sorath]]**, Leif's patron. She told Leif so herself. What else she is remains unknown.)*

## What Howl Has Brought Him

- **The [[Leif's Glass Orb|glass orb]]** on top of Leif's staff, his **spellcasting focus** — Howl brought it to him in the [[Purodetapo Forest]] at some point before the campaign began. [[Madame Verre]] took one look at it in Session 11 and abruptly gave the party everything they were asking for.

## The Two Incidents

### Session 8 — The Whispering One Spoke Through Her

In a dream on the road north, [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] had his usual nightmare — everything turning yellow, [[The Whispering One]]'s untranslatable voice — except this time **the voice came out of Howl**, whose **eyes went completely yellow.** Leif's fear since: that the entity is trying to **reach through, speak as, or take** his familiar.

### Session 11 — Aleister Knew Her on Sight

Walking to the guild hall on an ordinary morning in [[Pentamarone]], Howl **snapped her head around, started screaming, and bolted** down a back alley — leading half the party to **[[Aleister the Invoker]]**, a man she had no reason to know.

[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s animal handling read the reaction precisely: **not fear. Rage.** She is furious at him.

Then Aleister looked at her, and she **collapsed in on herself** — feathers hunched, eyes on the ground, making herself as small as possible.

> *"Oh. **I do know what this is, then.**"*

He would not say what. He asked Leif's name instead, learned where she was found, and announced he would be **visiting the Purodetapo Forest.**

## Session 12 — "Bad Man" (September 23, 2026)

After the fight, Howl came out of her shock and landed back on Leif's shoulder. Asked how she knew Aleister, she **whispered two words aloud**, ***"Bad man,"*** and told Leif the rest privately *(delivered to Emily in a DM)*.

What Leif later shared with the party, in a private room with the water running:

- **Howl is a servant of [[The Whispering One|Sorath]]**, and she is the one who told Leif the patron's name.
- **[[Aleister the Invoker]] has fought Sorath before.** That explains her reaction in the alley: **rage, then submission**, and why he recognized her at once.
- **She can help Leif contact Sorath, but not yet.** If he *opens his mind*, he'll be able to reach it at another time.
- **What Sorath wants with him**, Howl is *not at liberty to say.*

Leif had chosen to hold his private audience with [[Madame Verre]] **with Howl present**: *"I don't know enough about you yet, and I think it's time that we knew each other more."*

## The Open Question

Three separate threads now point at this owl:

1. **[[The Whispering One]]** used her as a voice.
2. **[[Aleister the Invoker]]**, who summons beings from outside the world, **recognized her instantly** — and she recognized him.
3. She **brought Leif the glass orb** that [[Madame Verre]] found so interesting she stopped negotiating.

Leif's own reaction, in the guild hall, was the right one: *"You been holding back on us?"*

*(Partly answered in Session 12: she serves Sorath, and Aleister has fought Sorath. Still open: what she actually is, where the glass orb came from, and what Sorath wants.)*
