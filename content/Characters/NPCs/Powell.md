A skilled wizard and former research partner of [[Myrna Nor]]. They parted ways over disagreements, and Powell hired **mercenaries** — including goblins and hobgoblins — to claim the [[Nath Loom]] from the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]] for herself.

Powell had about a **one-day head start** on the party. Her mercenaries were found already inside the library when the party arrived — the door was open and goblinoid boot prints led inside.

## First Appeared

Session 4 — referenced by [[Myrna Nor]] at the [[Fire Jolt Café]]; her mercenaries encountered in the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]]
