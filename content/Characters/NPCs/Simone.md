The **brains** of the [[S.A.T.O.]] and the most advanced mage in the group — an old friend from [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] cohort. Where [[Myrna Nor]] organizes the Satyrs, Simone is their researcher and theoretician; the group's central discovery about [[The Fourth Magic|the fourth magic]] is her work.

> **The one the party hadn't met.** Of all the Satyrs, Simone is the only member the party had never spoken to before Lathen. They had merely **glimpsed** her studying in the [[Biblioplex]] on their first day at the Academy; the formal introduction came in the safehouse.

## Role in the S.A.T.O.

Simone supplies the magical theory and the evidence behind the group's mission. She frames the questions the Satyrs are trying to answer — why the [[White Forest]] research was forbidden, and what the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] is actually running on — and built the tool that turned suspicion into something demonstrable.

## The Map of Magic

Simone authored the group's great discovery: an **enchanted-sand map of [[Astrodia]]** that visualizes the [[Types of Magic|types of magic]] flowing across the world. Pouring colored sand onto the map revealed four currents:

- **White — arcane** magic, spread uniformly everywhere, yet oddly **does not spike at the Academy** despite the Snarl and the [[Biblioplex]].
- **Red — higher-being** magic, concentrated where expected (Southern Kingdoms, Oceanic Alliance, Fire King Islands, [[War Rock Mountains]]) — but again **nothing at the Academy**, strange for a school reputedly founded by [[Dragons]] and giants.
- **Yellow — divine** magic of the Solemn Pantheon, with a **huge spike in the [[White Forest]]** and a tiny new speck at the Academy that the party recognized as the **[[Golden Chalice]]** they'd brought back.
- **Green — the [[The Fourth Magic|fourth magic]]**, newer than arcane, tracing a line that matches the path of the [[Great Cataclysm]] and **secretly powering every major civilization** — and no one is talking about it.

She also exposed [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s hidden warlock nature: the green sand crawled toward him, and she proved the point by **commanding him to cast Eldritch Blast**, which worked.

## Hypothesis

Simone believes **arcane magic may not be native to Astrodia**. Before the [[Severance]], there are no records of mortal wizards casting spells — all magic flowed from divine or higher sources. Only *after* the Severance could mortals suddenly harness arcane magic on their own. See [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] for the related "what powers the Academy?" mystery.

## Session 10 — Still Nothing, and a Question Waiting for Her

Simone's standing task — tracing **who donated the star-tablet** [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] translated — **failed its roll again** (August 26, 2026). No word from her. It has yet to succeed once, which the party is beginning to notice; per the Satyr Roll rules, some assignments are **secretly impossible.**

Meanwhile the party has a question for her that only her map can answer. On the [[Types of Magic|map of magic]], the **tiny yellow (divine) speck at the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]]** was assumed to be the [[Golden Chalice]] the party had just delivered. Now that they have carried the chalice hundreds of miles north:

> **Did the yellow speck move with them — or is it still sitting at the Academy?**

If it moved, Simone's map is effectively a **tracking device pointed at the party.** If it didn't, the yellow was **never the chalice**, and something else divine and newly-arrived is sitting inside the Academy. Neither answer is comfortable, and the question had not yet been sent as of the end of Session 10.
