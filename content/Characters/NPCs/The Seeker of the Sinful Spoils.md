A figure the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] is secretly hunting, revealed by **Tobin** from Academy records during the [[S.A.T.O.]] meeting (Session 6, May 31, 2026). Depicted as a **woman dressed in black and red**.

## Identity

- Matches the **woman in black with red** who appears in [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s dream — kneeling in the [[White Forest]], reaching out toward something.
- The Academy is actively trying to keep something from her — the so-called **"Sinful Spoils."**

## The Sinful Spoils

The party theorizes that the **artifacts themselves are the "Sinful Spoils"** the Seeker pursues — the [[Golden Chalice]] among them. The Academy's interest in collecting such artifacts (see [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]]) may be an effort to keep them out of the Seeker's reach.

It remains unknown whether the Seeker is a **threat or an ally** — and why she appears in Asha's dream.

## Connections

- [[Golden Chalice]] — likely one of the Sinful Spoils.
- [[White Forest]] — where Asha's dream placed her; the party's next destination.
- [[S.A.T.O.]] — first surfaced this figure via Tobin's Academy records.
- [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] — the Academy program that may exist to deny the Seeker her Spoils.

## First Appeared

Session 6 (Academy records, via Tobin).
