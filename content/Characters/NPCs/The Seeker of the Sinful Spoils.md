A figure the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] is secretly hunting, revealed by **Tobin** from Academy records during the [[S.A.T.O.]] meeting (Session 6, May 31, 2026). Depicted as a **woman dressed in black and red**.

## Identity

- Matches the **woman in black with red** who appears in [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s dream — kneeling in the [[White Forest]], reaching out toward something.
- The Academy is actively trying to keep something from her — the so-called **"Sinful Spoils."**

## The Sinful Spoils

The party theorizes that the **artifacts themselves are the "Sinful Spoils"** the Seeker pursues — the [[Golden Chalice]] among them. The Academy's interest in collecting such artifacts (see [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]]) may be an effort to keep them out of the Seeker's reach.

It remains unknown whether the Seeker is a **threat or an ally** — and why she appears in Asha's dream.

### Session 8 — Four Items, Two Women

[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s recurring dreams, told in full on the road north, gave the party a concrete list of what the Spoils may be — **four objects**, wielded by **two distinct women, neither matching the wanted poster** above:

| Object | Notes |
|---|---|
| **[[Golden Chalice|Chalice]]** | The one the party already carries. Appears in *both* women's hands. |
| **Lance** | Held by the white figure. |
| **Book** | Held by the white figure — possibly the tome the party recovered from the [[Biblioplex]]. |
| **White gown / dress** | Worn/held by the white figure. |

- **The black-and-red woman** — **winged, with talons for hands and wings of talons** — holds the chalice. (An evolution of the earlier "woman in black and red kneeling in the [[White Forest]]" dream.)
- **The white woman** — robed **all in white, "godly,"** — **drinks from the chalice** while bearing the book, lance, and gown.

The party relayed the four-item list to **[[Tobin]]** via the [[Sending Stones]] to cross-check against Academy records (see [[S.A.T.O.]]). That **neither dream-woman matches the Academy's poster** of the Seeker is itself a mystery — are they the Seeker at different times, rivals for the Spoils, or something else?
