A gnome member of the [[S.A.T.O.]] based at [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]], and an old friend from [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] cohort. Her full name is **Rosimyffenbip** — "Rosie" for short. An enthusiastic LARPer, she's the student **the party rescued from the tampered mimic** in the [[Biblioplex]] back in Session 3.

## How the Party Knows Her

In the [[Biblioplex]] (Session 3), Rosie — a gnome LARPer hunting for costumes — opened a chest that turned out to be a **mimic**, and the party fought it off to save her. Afterward, [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] found a **black, oily substance** on the creature: it had been **tampered with**. [[Mavinda Sharpbeak|Professor Sharpbeak]] dismissed it as "leather conditioner," but the party saw through the lie. Rosie was the grateful student at the center of that still-unexplained incident — and, unbeknownst to the party, a future ally.

## Role in the S.A.T.O.

Rosie is one of the group's **researchers**, stationed at [[Lorehold College|Lorehold]] — the college of history, archaeology, and anthropology, and the same college [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] attended before her expulsion. She dug through **history books** to uncover *why* the [[White Forest]] topic was forbidden, likely working alongside fellow Lorehold member [[Myrna Nor]].
