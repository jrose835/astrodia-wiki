**Schmietta** is the **[[WitchCrafters]]' smith** — the guild's weapon-maker, and the only master with a forge. She is also the guild's second refugee from the **[[Endymion Empire]]**, alongside [[Genni]].

## Background

She grew up in a **small crafting village inside the [[Endymion Empire]]**, known for its forges. She learned the trade there and was good at it.

> *"But their army came. I wanted to get away."*

She reached [[Pentamarone]] with nothing, started at the bottom — internships, apprenticeships — and worked her way up over years. *"And now I make very good weapons."*

Asked whether she has had any trouble with the Empire since: **none.** Whatever agreement holds between [[Pentamarone]] and the Empire, *"it stands strong."* She has never had an issue since the day she arrived.

## Session 11 (September 9, 2026)

**Assigned to the materials team with [[Edel]]** — establishing what the [[Golden Chalice]] is physically made of and whether the metal or the set jewels can be dated.

When [[Madame Verre]] opened the floor to the party, [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] asked whether any of the work needed **brute force.** Schmietta spoke up immediately — *"I could always use help in my forge"* — and Úlfr spent the montage swinging a hammer for her, on **Athletics with advantage.**

She joined the guild's night out with [[Seed]], [[Edel]], and [[Marka]].
