**Professor Astellar** *(spelled A-S-T-E-L-L-A-R by Tommy)* is a **[[Lorehold College|Lorehold]] professor** at [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] who **donated the star tablet**: the [[White Forest]] artifact [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] was translating when she was expelled. This semester she is **on sabbatical at [[Quandrix College]]**, the Academy campus closest to the White Forest.

Nothing else about her is known yet: not where she got the tablet, what she knows about the forest, or how she might react to the expelled student who studied it.

## How the Party Learned Her Name

Tracing the tablet's donor was **[[Simone]]'s standing [[S.A.T.O.]] assignment** from Session 7 on. It failed every roll through Sessions 10–12, and the party had begun to suspect it was one of the **secretly impossible** tasks. In Session 13 the party put their single high Satyr roll (**98**) on it, and a [[Sending Stones|sending stone]] message arrived over breakfast:

> ***"Professor Astellar, Lorehold Professor, taking a sabbatical at Quandrix College this semester. She donated the tablet."***

**The party decided not to go to her yet.** Quandrix would mean going back toward the Academy with the [[Golden Chalice]]'s repossession clock running (about ten days). They chose the [[Temple of Nephthys]] and then the White Forest. The reasoning for her as a lead: whoever owned the tablet may know a good deal about the White Forest.
