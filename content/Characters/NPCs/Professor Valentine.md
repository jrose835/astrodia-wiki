**Professor Valentine** is a **Dean of [[Witherbloom College]]** and a **full vampire** — a sangromancer who receives visions from the blood he drinks. Uniquely among his kind, he has **publicly denounced the higher vampires**, which has made him a standing target for vampire assassins. He mentors the born-dhampir student **[[Aurora Luna Wynterstarr]]**.

## A Potential Source on the Severance

As one of the few active vampires the party can plausibly approach, Valentine represents a rare **living witness to deep history**. As [[Rosie]] pointed out in Session 7, vampires are the **only higher beings still active** in the world and likely among the **oldest living beings** — which may make them the only ones who truly remember the **[[Severance]]**, an event mortals do not understand. If any safe source exists for that knowledge, it is a vampire who has already turned against his own — like Valentine.

**[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** intends to visit him at some point; the party chose to prioritize the [[Nephthys]] temple and [[White Forest]] first, so the meeting is deferred. **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]**, a cleric sworn against the undead, floated simply destroying him — an unresolved tension for whenever the party does reach [[Witherbloom College]].
