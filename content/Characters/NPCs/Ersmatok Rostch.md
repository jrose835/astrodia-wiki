An orc **[[Witherbloom College|Witherbloom]]** student at [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] and a member of the [[S.A.T.O.]] — an old friend from [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] cohort. He works at the campus **magic labs** and is in the **Fantastical Horticulture Club**, a dedicated plant-nerd. He is the Satyr **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] befriended** in the [[Biblioplex]] back in Session 3, before either knew the other's secrets.

## How the Party Knows Him

In the [[Biblioplex]] (Session 3), [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] and Ersmatok hit it off over a shared love of plants — a genuine bond between two herbology-minded students. He told Leif he could usually be found on the **[[Witherbloom College|Witherbloom]]** campus, working the magic labs. Neither realized at the time that they'd meet again across the table in the [[Lathen]] safehouse, where Ersmatok was revealed as one of the Satyrs.

## Role in the S.A.T.O.

Ersmatok's posting in Witherbloom's magic labs makes him the group's hands-on **labs-and-botany** contact. At the safehouse reveal he showed an unexpected theatrical streak, summing himself up:

> *"I am my own fan... but I believe in telling a good story."*
