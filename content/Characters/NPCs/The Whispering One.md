**The Whispering One** is the working name for **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s warlock patron**, an entity that reached out to him while he meditated in the [[Purodetapo Forest]], within the path of the [[Great Cataclysm]]. The name was **coined by [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** in Session 7, drawn from a Norse-flavored belief among his people in a *time before names*, when older, vast powers "spoke the world" before the gods carved it into things that could be named.

**Its true name is *Sorath*** *(spelling as heard — unconfirmed)*, learned by Leif from [[Howl]] in Session 12. Per [[Madame Verre]], it is **not of this world**: a being from another plane, world, or dimension that most likely came into [[Astrodia]] through the [[Great Cataclysm]]'s rift, and that is **probably living in the Purodetapo Forest.** What it wants with Leif is unknown. See [[The Whispering One#Session 12 — It Has a Name (September 23, 2026)|Session 12]] below.

## What Is Known

- It contacted Leif **during meditation**, in the [[Great Cataclysm]]'s path. Leif never knowingly agreed to a pact. *(Revealed in Session 12: Verre's reading is that Leif **unknowingly made a pact with something not of this world**.)*
- Leif experiences it only as **whispers in a language he cannot understand**, heard occasionally in his head.
- Its language **matches the creature imprisoned in [[The Orb]]** — directly tying it to the mystery of [[The Fourth Magic]] (the green, undisclosed magic that traces the Cataclysm's path).
- Naming it was framed as a first step toward **understanding** the entity rather than merely being spoken *to* by it.

## Leads Being Pursued

- **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** (a fellow warlock) will ask her own patron, **[[Nephthys]]**, for information.
- **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** prayed to his goddess for the entity's **true name**.
- **[[The Orb]]** and its imprisoned creature remain the clearest physical link.

## Session 8 — It May Be Reaching Through Howl

In a dream on the road north, **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** experienced his usual nightmare — everything **turning yellow**, the Whispering One's untranslatable voice in his head — but this time **the voice issued from his familiar, the owl Howl**, whose **eyes went completely yellow.** Leif now fears the entity is trying to **reach through, speak as, or take** his familiar (his [[Leif Kalanchoe|Pact of the Chain]] companion). [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] urged him to make consulting **[[Nephthys]]** a priority. This is the first sign the Whispering One may act through a **vector other than direct whispers.**

## Session 11 — Madame Verre Is Looking Into It (September 9, 2026)

**[[Madame Verre]]**, head of the [[WitchCrafters]], **agreed to investigate the Whispering One** — for free, unrequested, and having asked nothing in return. She summoned [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] to her office alone, cleared the room, asked him whether he had told her *"everything ever,"* asked a great many further questions, and answered none of them.

Leif volunteered a great deal in that conversation, including **[[The Fourth Magic]]**.

What she took as her working material was **[[Leif's Glass Orb|his staff and its glass orb]]** — the object **[[Howl]]** brought him in the [[Purodetapo Forest]], the same forest where the Whispering One first reached him. Verre's reaction to it: *"I think there's something very special about that."*

The party's reaction to the arrangement was near-unanimous alarm. [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]: *"Be wary of what she might request of you."*

### And Then Someone Else Recognized Howl

The same week, **[[Aleister the Invoker]]** — a man who summons beings from outside [[Astrodia]] — looked at **[[Howl]]** in a [[Pentamarone]] alley and said *"I do know what this is, then."* He would not say what.

**Three separate powers have now taken an unusual interest in that owl**: the Whispering One (Session 8, speaking through her), Madame Verre (via the orb Howl brought), and Aleister. See [[Howl]]. *(Revealed in Session 12: Howl is **Sorath's servant**, and Aleister recognized her because **he has fought Sorath before**.)*

## Session 12 — It Has a Name (September 23, 2026)

After a private audience with [[Madame Verre]] (held **with Howl present**, at Leif's choice), [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] gathered [[The Fallen]] in a private room, ran the water to cover their voices, and told them what he had learned. **Howl told him the name.**

> **The Whispering One's name is *Sorath*.** *(Spelling as heard — unconfirmed; confirm with Emily/Tommy.)*

- **[[Howl]] is a servant of Sorath.**
- **[[Aleister the Invoker]] has fought Sorath before.** That is why Howl reacted to him with rage and then submission, and why he recognized her immediately. Leif: *"I don't know if he's also my enemy, but now he's really my enemy."*
- **Per Verre**, Leif **unknowingly made a pact with something not of this world**, a being from another plane, world, or dimension, which somehow reached [[Astrodia]] and is **probably living in his home forest**, the [[Purodetapo Forest]]. That is the same forest Leif told Aleister about.
- **How it got here:** the [[Great Cataclysm]] was caused by mortals opening **a portal to another world**, and other things came through with the otherworldly magic (**[[The Fourth Magic]]**). Sorath is **probably one of them.** *(This part Verre asked Leif not to share; it is kept within the party at Verre's request.)*
- **Can Howl help him contact it?** Yes, **but not yet.** If Leif *opens his mind*, he will be able to reach it "at another time." He admitted that in most of his dreams he has been trying to run away.
- **What does it want with him?** Howl is *not at liberty to say.* Still unknown.

[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] had never heard of an accidental, non-consensual pact; the party wondered whether that is a trait of the entity itself.
