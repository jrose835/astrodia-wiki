**The Whispering One** is the working name for **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s warlock patron** — an unknown entity that reached out to him while he meditated in the [[Terget Forest|Purodetapo Forest]], within the path of the [[Great Cataclysm]]. Its true name is not yet known. The name was **coined by [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** in Session 7, drawn from a Norse-flavored belief among his people in a *time before names*, when older, vast powers "spoke the world" before the gods carved it into things that could be named.

## What Is Known

- It contacted Leif **during meditation**, in the [[Great Cataclysm]]'s path. Leif never knowingly agreed to a pact.
- Leif experiences it only as **whispers in a language he cannot understand**, heard occasionally in his head.
- Its language **matches the creature imprisoned in [[The Orb]]** — directly tying it to the mystery of [[The Fourth Magic]] (the green, undisclosed magic that traces the Cataclysm's path).
- Naming it was framed as a first step toward **understanding** the entity rather than merely being spoken *to* by it.

## Leads Being Pursued

- **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** (a fellow warlock) will ask her own patron, **[[Nephthys]]**, for information.
- **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** prayed to his goddess for the entity's **true name**.
- **[[The Orb]]** and its imprisoned creature remain the clearest physical link.

## Session 8 — It May Be Reaching Through Howl

In a dream on the road north, **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** experienced his usual nightmare — everything **turning yellow**, the Whispering One's untranslatable voice in his head — but this time **the voice issued from his familiar, the owl Howl**, whose **eyes went completely yellow.** Leif now fears the entity is trying to **reach through, speak as, or take** his familiar (his [[Leif Kalanchoe|Pact of the Chain]] companion). [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] urged him to make consulting **[[Nephthys]]** a priority. This is the first sign the Whispering One may act through a **vector other than direct whispers.**
