**Morgana** was a **witch** who lived in the **wizard's tower** at the edge of **[[Freedale]]** — see [[Morgana's Tower]]. When the [[White Forest]] curse began creeping toward the town, Morgana **went into the forest herself to fight whatever was causing it** — and **never returned.** [[The Fallen]] found her body in her own basement in Session 9.

She was also, it turns out, a constructs engineer of real consequence: with her Academy colleague **[[Verelda]]**, she designed the guardian line that now stands watch across [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]].

## The Rumor — Confirmed

Locals said that when the cursed vines swallowed Freedale, they **carried Morgana's body back and left it at her tower** — as if in mockery. Since then most townsfolk avoided the place, believing it **haunted.**

Approaching in Session 8, **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s** pendant registered a **strong undead presence beneath the tower.** In Session 9 the party found out what both signs meant, and the rumor was essentially right — her body was in the basement, behind a door **barred from the outside.** The undead were **ghouls** that had gotten in and were **feeding on her.**

## Her Death (Session 9)

The body in the basement is a woman, dead roughly **two decades** — skin and bones — though her remaining flesh had been **eaten within the last few hours or days** by the ghouls the party killed.

- Her face was covered by a **mask** over the eyes and brow. [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] hooked a claw under it and pulled it free: **hollowed-out eye sockets.** The eyes had been **gouged out before she died** — not eaten afterward.
- **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s medicine check** read the rest of the corpse: the remnants of a **prolonged magical battle**, spells hurled at her that wounded her over time. **She died fighting**, not ambushed.
- In her pocket, radiating faintly: the **[[Guardian Command Amulet]]** — her guardian's "charm." She was carrying it when she died, and it came home on her body.
- Aren spoke **last rites** over her so she might rest.

So: something in the [[White Forest]] fights with spells, and **takes eyes.** Whether the mask was placed on her by whatever killed her, or something she wore herself, is unknown.

## Her Papers (Session 9)

Inside the tower [[The Fallen]] recovered Morgana's own writings — a **journal** spanning several years and a **letter** to a colleague at the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]]. Together they are the closest thing yet to a firsthand account of the [[White Forest]] curse from someone who studied it deliberately.

### The Journal

An **undated entry near the back** of the book — almost certainly among the last things she wrote before leaving.

- She had **watched the forest through a scrying font in the tower for "the better part of a decade."**
- Her word for the trees is **"wrong"** — *"not sick, not dying, just wrong, the way a face is wrong when it's smiling and shouldn't be."* **Whiter every season**, and **the birds no longer land in them.**
- She **grew up in that forest** — *"before it was anything anyone in [[Freedale]] would recognize now, before the whitening, before the silence where the deer used to be."* She **left when she was young**; her childhood friend **[[Yssa]]** stayed, as she always said she would. Morgana never asked her why, and the guilt over it runs through the whole entry.
- She independently confirms Freedale's omens: **children vanishing and returning "as though no time passed for them at all."** Her conclusion: *"Something in that forest is not just decaying. **Something in that forest is reaching.**"*
- **The fate of the coven is unknown to her** — she doesn't even know whether Yssa is still alive.
- **[[Dia]]** used to tell her an old story when she was small: **someone who fell before anyone else ever fell — someone the forest itself is supposed to remember even if no one else does.** She had always dismissed it as a tale to keep children away from the tree line. *"I'm no longer certain of that."*
- She closes by naming what she did before leaving: **sent what she knew to [[Verelda]]**, **asked the guardian to hold the tower**, and went to look for herself — *"because I don't think anyone else is coming, and I am tired — genuinely, finally tired — of being the one who left."*

### The Letter to Verelda

Written before the first frost, to **[[Verelda]]** — a colleague at the [[Biblioplex]] with ties to the **[[Quandrix College|Quandrix]] faculty**. Warm, wry, and clearly a long correspondence between equals.

- The two of them **collaborated on constructs**: Verelda supplied the theory (an **"animating lattice"**), Morgana **built them at her forge.** The **watchmen of the [[Biblioplex]] owe their design to this work** — a fact both women deliberately kept from the Academy's deans. *(A quiet new wrinkle in the Session 3 Biblioplex incident.)*
- On the curse: **whitening trees, animals "behaving as though they no longer recognize this as home," and now children.** She had **seen the edge of it herself**, *"from a safe enough distance to still call it 'study.'"* She judged that it **would not stay at a distance much longer.**
- Her instruction if she didn't return: ***"If I am gone longer than a season, do not send anyone after me — send them after whatever I find instead."***
- She asked Verelda to **keep an eye on the tower**, noting that **the guardian "answers only to its charm."**

### Verelda's Reply

A third document turned up in the tower's **foyer** at the end of Session 9: **[[Verelda]]'s answer**, under the **seal of the Academy**, dusty and **never collected.** She argued with Morgana about going, asked her to write before doing anything past looking, threatened to send someone after her *"on a very fast horse,"* reported the third guardian still *"holding steady,"* and mentioned a **Dean Lassette** who laughed herself out of a dinner table over it.

Her closing line — *"Write back the moment you're able. I mean that"* — sat unopened in the foyer for twenty years while Morgana lay in the basement. Full text in `Sessions/Session 9.md`.

## The Tower's Guardian

Morgana's letter establishes that the tower is held by a **construct guardian — the third of its kind** she and [[Verelda]] built together.

- Early versions had an unstable temperament: one **"nearly cleaved the poor delivery boy in half over a mispronounced password."** Verelda's lattice correction fixed this; it now *"holds a much steadier temperament."*
- She **left it standing watch on purpose** — *"I've asked the guardian to hold the tower as it always has"* — and expected it to still be there for *"whoever finally comes to dust the place."*
- **The "charm" is an object, not a word.** The party spent Session 9 hunting a spoken password before finding the **[[Guardian Command Amulet]]** in her pocket. It is a **shield guardian command amulet**; [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] held it out, the construct scanned it, flashed green, and stood down.
- **The guardian line is now a campaign-level lead.** The tower's construct is an **older model of the same guardians standing watch at the [[Biblioplex]]** — and per Asha's history check, those guardians are posted **throughout the Academy.** Since each amulet binds one guardian, the Academy likely keeps a **control room holding all of them.**

## Session 10 — Her Network Outlived Her (August 26, 2026)

Hundreds of miles north, in [[Pentamarone]], [[The Fallen]] met a young **glassblower in the market who fled [[Freedale]] as a child during the curse** — and who knew Morgana's name.

- **Morgana told her family where to go.** She was *"relatively prolific"* and had **connections with many different covens and guilds**, and she kept a list of places she considered **safe havens** for people fleeing the curse. Pentamarone was on it. The glassblower's family survived because of it.
- **She had worked with [[Madame Verre]]** — head of the [[WitchCrafters]] — *"in the past,"* on something the glassblower couldn't name.
- Told that Morgana was dead, the woman's response was simply: ***"A great witch."***

This adds a third dimension to a witch previously known as a recluse who studied a forest and built constructs with an Academy colleague: **she was networked across the witch world**, and she used that network to **evacuate people** ahead of the curse. It also gives the party a live lead — **Madame Verre knew her**, and may know what she was working on.

*(She was also, per the glassblower, the reason Freedale's survivors ended up in an Empire border city at all — the refugee population of Pentamarone is partly Morgana's doing.)*

## Significance

Morgana is the first known individual to have **deliberately gone into the [[White Forest]] to confront the curse** — a lead on both the curse's nature and what became of those who tried to stop it.

Her papers gave the party a **decade of firsthand observation** of the whitening, the claim that the curse is **"reaching"** rather than merely spreading, and the existence of a **coven contemporary** ([[Yssa]]) who may still be inside the forest. Her **body** gave them something sharper: proof that whatever is in there **fights back with spells, and wins.**

Her legacy is also unexpectedly practical. She left [[The Fallen]] a **working scrying pool**, a **teleportation circle**, **500 gp of spell components**, and a **war construct they can command** — and, through the guardian line she built with [[Verelda]], a structural weakness in the Academy's own security.

## Open Questions

1. **Who gouged out her eyes?** Taken **before death**, and her corpse shows a prolonged **magical battle**. Something in that forest takes eyes.
2. **The one "who fell before anyone else ever fell"** — the forest is supposed to remember them. Party speculation ties this to [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]] (the winged, taloned woman of [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s dreams), but **nothing in Morgana's papers names her** — this is theory, not canon. See [[Dia]].
3. **[[Verelda]] holds a copy of Morgana's research** — *"I've sent what I know to Verelda, in case my instruments say more than I do."* She is at the [[Biblioplex]], reachable, and **does not know Morgana is dead.**
4. **[[Yssa]] and the coven** — Morgana couldn't say whether any of them survived the whitening.
5. **The abjuration infusion circle** in her workroom — she was warding something in her final months. Against what?
6. **The mask over her eyes**, and how the ghouls got into a basement **barred from the outside.**
7. **The password was never tested.** The party's best guess remained **[[Yssa]]**; the amulet made the question moot at the tower, but the word may still matter.

*(Resolved in Session 9: the "charm" was an object, not a password; the undead beneath the tower were ghouls feeding on her body; her **font** was found intact on the second floor.)*
