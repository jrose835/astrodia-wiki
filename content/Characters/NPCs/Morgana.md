**Morgana** was a **witch** who lived in the **wizard's tower** at the edge of **[[Freedale]]**. When the [[White Forest]] curse began creeping toward the town, Morgana **went into the forest herself to fight whatever was causing it** — and **never returned.**

## The Rumor

Locals say that when the cursed vines later swallowed Freedale, they **carried Morgana's body back and left it at her tower** — as if in mockery. Whether that's true, no one knows; it's told as a grim rumor. Since then most townsfolk avoid the tower, believing it **haunted.**

The party has reason to take the haunting seriously: approaching the tower in Session 8, **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s** undead-detecting pendant registered a **strong undead presence buried in the ground beneath it.** Whether that presence is Morgana herself, what the vines brought back, or something else entirely remains to be seen.

## Significance

Morgana is the first known individual to have **deliberately gone into the [[White Forest]] to confront the curse** — a lead on both the curse's nature and what became of those who tried to stop it. Her tower (and whatever lies beneath) is the party's active objective as of the end of Session 8.
