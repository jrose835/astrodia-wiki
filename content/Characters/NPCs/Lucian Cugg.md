An explorer and former student of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] who left because he didn't like academia. He's interested in **higher beings** and ancient history.

Lucian explored the **Great Ravine** in [[Caelwyrm]] and found the [[Golden Chalice]] in an excavated temple. He hired [[Vonet]] (orc) and [[Dilly]] (human) to escort him to the Academy to sell it through the Academy's magical item buyback program, but they double-crossed him outside [[Thornvale]], stole the chalice, and fled north.

The party tracked down the thieves and recovered the chalice. Lucian is now traveling with the party to the Acropolis, where he hopes to sell the chalice and split the payment with the group.

## Notes

- [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] suspects he is understating how valuable the chalice actually is (insight check)
- [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] intimidated him when Lucian questioned whether Úlfr was really a fighter: *"I said I don't like to fight. Doesn't mean I'm not a fighter."*
