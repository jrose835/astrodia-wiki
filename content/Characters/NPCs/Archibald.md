**Archibald** is the butler of the **[[Myrna Nor|Nor]]** family, serving at their vacation beach house in [[Lathen]]. Impeccably composed and endlessly capable, he is — on the surface — the perfect household servant. Beneath that, the party suspects he is something closer to a **fixer and bodyguard**: an unusually powerful spellcaster quietly providing "additional services" to a wealthy merchant family.

## More Than a Butler

- **Casts Teleport.** Archibald is the one who ferries the [[S.A.T.O.]] between their campuses and teleported [[The Fallen]] to [[Quandrix College]]. Teleport is a high-level wizard spell (confirmed by the party's Arcana check) — extraordinary for a "butler," and he deflects questions about where he learned it (*"butler school"*).
- **Uses Unseen Servant.** He served an elaborate twelve-person dinner nearly single-handed, with plates and courses floating themselves to and from the kitchen ([[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] recognized the spell).
- **Reads as a fixer.** [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s Insight put him as genuinely competent at *everything* — the kind of retainer who doubles as protection.
- **Knows what happens under the roof.** He eavesdrops as a matter of professional duty — *"It's my job to know what takes place under this roof"* — but stays discreet about the Nor family's private business. He vouches for Myrna as *"a good soul, with a strong will and a good head on her shoulders."*

## What He Told the Party

On the **[[White Forest]]**, Archibald offered the session's most useful lead: there was **research into the forest's history before [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]] banned the subject**, and the party should seek out a **scholar or professor who studied it prior to the restriction**. On reaching [[Pentamarone]], he advised **traveling by ship up the river** that runs the [[Endymion Empire]]–[[Artmegía|Artmegia]] border.
