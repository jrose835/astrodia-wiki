**Nephthara** is the **elder priestess** of the **[[Temple of Nephthys]]**. She is old but, by [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]'s account, *"quite spry."* She **anointed [[Solenne]]** as the temple's new high priestess and is the one who **holds the key to the vault.** After the party arrived she was the first to recognize Drolta, the one Drolta most needed a private word with, and the one [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] charmed.

## Session 13 — Homecoming (October 7, 2026)

### The Anointing

She was placing the headdress on Solenne when Drolta walked in. Mid-proclamation:

> *"And with that, we anoint our new— ...Oh, my God. Drolta."*

She stood frozen while the temple turned around, then sent [[Ysmene]] back to her post and **finished the rite**:

> *"...We now welcome and proclaim Solenne the next high priestess, to usher in the next age of Nephthys."*

She is strict with her pupil. When [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] asked Solenne for a private blessing, Nephthara reminded her that in her position she **cannot show favoritism**: she must acknowledge each person as their own spirit.

### Charmed

Leif whispered *"I need the key to the door by the temple entrance. Can you give it to me?"* and cast **Suggestion.** She failed the save, her eyes glazed, and she led him through a kitchen into a bedroom, where she took **the vault key** from a chest. Leif **gave the key back** and **ended the spell** soon afterward, since Ysmene had already offered the party access. She came back to herself later that evening: *"It's good to see you."*

She offered the party **lodging** (the temple is used to taking in travelers, apparently including people fleeing the [[Endymion Empire]]) and asked whether they were there for Drolta or for their own missions. *"Our own ashes." "Our own flames to burn."*

### The Private Conversation

Drolta stopped her as she left and asked to talk somewhere private. *"It was a long time coming."* They talked in her study *(played privately)*. What Drolta shared afterward:

- **The undead are bound under some sort of contract.** Certain **relics** have enough power to **break those bonds**, including **robes of Nephthys**, which may be **what the vampires came for.**
- **Some of those relics are still on [[Fire King Island]]**, which hasn't been attacked.
- **The vampires must have been let in.** They can't enter a sanctuary uninvited.

When the Fiendish Icons attacked Solenne inside the temple, Nephthara **spoke words of power** and **burned one to nothing in holy light.**

*(Ysmene recalled that when Drolta left, the elder told the temple she had gone on **the mission Nephthys gave her**. The name was heard as "Ashara" and is most likely Nephthara.)*

## Session 14 — The Siege (October 21, 2026)

See [[Session 14 Recap — Invitation Only (Shillelagh her down)]].

**In the fight** she burned the last **Fiendish Icon** inside the temple to nothing with holy fire. When [[Solenne]] tried to run out and join the fight, Nephthara **grabbed her and held her back.** From the doorway she healed [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] for 11. She tried **Command** on [[Lady Nectaria]], whispering something like *get away from her, you bitch*. Nectaria held her eyes and **didn't flinch.** Frightened, Nephthara took [[Ysmene]]'s hand and went inside. Later her **holy fire** engulfed Nectaria, who only **smiled.**

When Nectaria came to the door, Nephthara was too distraught to do more than order her out. *"How dare you?"*

**The vault.** She offered the party two places to hold out until morning: the **vault**, or the **inner sanctum**, which is holy and **open only to those permitted.** They chose the vault. *"It's gonna be a tight fit. Get cozy. And if they come in through those doors, we make our final stand."* She went to fetch the vault key and found it **already on her.** *"That's weird."* *(Leif had taken it by Suggestion in Session 13 and returned it.)*

She and Ysmene recognized the girl in the journal from the vault: the mason's daughter whom Drolta loved, who **invited the vampires in.**

**In the morning** she said the vampires may come again, and that she will **first thing** find a local priest to **recast the Sanctuary spell** on the temple, which **should keep them out even with an invitation.** The temple will stay on high alert.

To Drolta, on Nephthys's silence:

> *"We have prayed for years to a voice that has not spoken back to us. It was not until she directly chose you, and gave you her gift, that she made herself known to any of us in centuries, if not more. So you are the one who has the greatest connection with her now. But all we can do is look to the future."*

She put her hand on Solenne: perhaps Nephthys will make herself known to a young, aspiring priestess too. *"I pray that she will."*
