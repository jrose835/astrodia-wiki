**Ceramia** is a **master [[WitchCrafters|WitchCrafter]]** of [[Pentamarone]] — and, like her guild head [[Madame Verre]], she **appears to be a small girl.**

Unlike Verre, she **acts like one.**

That contrast is the most useful thing the party learned about her. Where Verre is smug, bored, and *"wise beyond her years,"* Ceramia conducts herself as an actual child of the age she appears. When the guild took the party out drinking, she declined:

> *"I don't think I can drink yet."*

Whether the two share a condition, a bloodline, or nothing at all is completely unexplained. **She is the second child-shaped master witch in a guild the party is already suspicious of.**
