Lady Nectaria is a vampire lord who led the attack on [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta's]] temple, killing the children in her care.

## Connection to Drolta

After the attack that transformed Drolta into a dhampir, Lady Nectaria may still be attempting to reach Drolta's mind through psychic "tapping." [[Nephthys|Nephthys']] protection appears to be blocking this connection.

Drolta now hunts the vampires who destroyed her temple, with Lady Nectaria as a primary target.
