Lady Nectaria is a vampire lord who led the attack on [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta's]] temple, the [[Temple of Nephthys]], killing the children in her care. **She is the vampire who turned Drolta** (confirmed in Session 13). She is pale-haired, winged, and red-eyed, and she dodges attacks by simply tilting her head. **She got into the temple the first time by befriending a five-year-old girl until the child invited her in** (revealed in Session 14).

## Connection to Drolta

After the attack that transformed Drolta into a dhampir, Lady Nectaria may still be attempting to reach Drolta's mind through psychic "tapping." [[Nephthys|Nephthys']] protection appears to be blocking this connection. *(Session 13: that door has been **thrown open**. Session 14: it is a **channel of control**. Nectaria can **take Drolta over** the way she controls her spawn, and was expected to use Drolta to open the temple door.)*

Drolta now hunts the vampires who destroyed her temple, with Lady Nectaria as a primary target.

## What She Wanted *(Session 13)*

Per [[Nephthara]], as relayed by Drolta: the undead are **bound under some sort of contract**, and certain **relics** are powerful enough to **break those bonds**. These include **robes of [[Nephthys]]**, which may be **what Nectaria's vampires were searching for** in the attack. Some such relics are still on **[[Fire King Island]]**, which has not been attacked. Nephthara also believes the vampires **must have been let in**, since they can't enter a sanctuary uninvited.

**Session 14 sharpened this.** The relic is **the dress Nephthys wore in her human form**, said to **break chains and destroy unjust hierarchies**, including a vampire's bond to its **sire**. It is in the **Fire Kings' temple** on Fire King Island, and **Nectaria knows it isn't at the Temple of Nephthys.** Her demand for [[Solenne]] was, by Drolta's account, a **"test of love"** to see whether the temple would give someone up.

## Session 13 — At the Temple Again (October 7, 2026)

At sunset on the day [[The Fallen]] arrived at the temple, [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s pendant pinged outside. Nectaria was standing about **a hundred feet off, red eyes glowing.** **The locked door in Drolta's mind**, where she has felt knocking and tapping for years, was **thrown open** with a pain in her head, and Drolta saw *the woman that turned her*. Nectaria's whisper carried across the distance:

> *"Brave of you to return to where your Phoenix Queen once failed you."*
> *"She never fails me. I still stand here before you."*
> *"Not for long."*

*(Earlier that day Drolta had failed a Wisdom save during the anointing, which caused a lapse in her mental fortification, and a voice spoke in her head. That was delivered privately. Whose voice it was hasn't been stated.)*

### Her Forces

- **Six Fiendish Icons** pulled themselves out of the ground: gargoyle-like figures that chip and crumble like stone or clay. [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s Arcana check says they **aren't undead** and are more like **constructs**. They attack with two slams, can dash, and set off a **fiery eruption** (a Dexterity save within 5 feet) when surrounded or nearly destroyed. Three got inside the temple.
- When the icons failed: *"I knew those fiendish icons weren't going to be able to get the job done. I need to send in a bit more of the artillery. **Now is the time.**"* Undead came out of the woods behind her, **most likely vampire spawn** *(to be confirmed)*.

### How She Fought

- **She dodges with a head tilt.** Eldritch Blast, a thrown hand axe, and Guiding Bolt all missed that way.
- **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s pact magic got through.** She shrugged off his Dissonant Whispers (*"You can't touch me. You are nothing."*), but an **echo in his patron's language** behind his words hurt her anyway, and did it again with Mind Spike. She was furious, and **intrigued**: no one should be able to do that to her.
- **She answered by taking [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]].** Holding Leif's eyes the whole time, she walked to Úlfr and **whispered in his ear**. He failed the Wisdom save and was **charmed.** To Leif: *"That is how you fuck with someone's mind."*
- **She bit Úlfr** as he walked away (an opportunity attack), but **couldn't pierce his Goliath hide.** She **spat out the blood.**
- Red's lion raked her for 9 (*"that's nothing"*), and Drolta's plume landed with Hex damage.

The fight continued into Session 14. See [[Session 13 Recap — Homecoming]].

## Session 14 — The Siege (October 21, 2026)

See [[Session 14 Recap — Invitation Only (Shillelagh her down)]].

### The Fight

She brought **two vampire spawn**, and **won the fight.**

- **Her bite drains maximum HP.** She used **Grave Strike** (bludgeoning plus necrotic) and grappled [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s lion, which she was strong enough to hold, then bit it: **−17 maximum HP**. A spawn's bite took **−12** from [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]].
- **She nearly killed [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]].** Two Grave Strikes dropped her, and Nectaria **bit her unconscious body** for **−18 maximum HP**, leaving Aren at 13. Her own wounds from Aren's **Guiding Bolt** (21, the biggest hit anyone landed on her) **closed as she fed.** She dropped the body and whispered: ***"See you soon."*** Black veins spread across Aren before [[Ysmene]]'s Healing Word brought her back.
- When a spawn moved to finish Aren off, she stopped it: ***"No. Let them go."***
- She walked up to Úlfr and whispered a **Command** in his ear: ***"Run."*** He failed the save. [[Nephthara]]'s own Command bounced off her, and when Nephthara's holy fire engulfed her she just **smiled.**
- She **swatted [[Howl]] out of the air** and killed her.
- Her spawn **never once tried to bite Drolta.**

### The Parley

With the party inside, she stood mockingly at the open door.

> *"You didn't even give me a chance to say what I was here for."*
> ***"All we want is the girl. Solenne, if that's her name."***

She called Drolta an **"abomination"** and a **"brave little half-blood,"** and blamed **[[Ysmene]]** for Drolta's state: *"Aren't you the one who **kept her from succumbing**?"* She mocked the dead: *"I'm sure the people that didn't survive the last time thought they were safe, too."* Asked why she wanted Solenne, she said she was trying to **"right the wrongs of the past."**

Her spawn **Damien** had died in the fight. She barely remembered his name. When the party tried to turn the surviving spawn against her, he admitted he had been turned years ago and **had no free will**, strained against her, and **froze** as she took control: *"I live to serve my lady."*

She gave them **until midnight** to hand Solenne over.

### Midnight

She whispered privately to Drolta: the relic she wants is **Nephthys's dress**, on [[Fire King Island]]; this was **a test of love**; **Nephthys failed the test the first time**; and **the Phoenix Queen knows more than she has shared.**

At midnight she **rapped on the temple door** while the bells rang. She had already **seeded [[Petra]]'s mind**. Petra thinks it happened on a night she can't remember, on her way to the temple years ago. Petra walked to the door in a trance to invite her in. The party stopped Petra in time.

> *"Don't think you've won just yet. I'll be back. And **I'll always find my way in.**"*
> *"Just know: **we'll always be with you.**"*
> ***"And once again, your goddess did nothing."***

Her surviving spawn turned into a bat and flew off, and she left.

### The First Time

A journal [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] found in the temple vault showed how she got in five years ago. A woman with white hair befriended **a five-year-old girl** who lived at the temple, the daughter of a mason and a child **Drolta loved like her own.** She learned the girl's name, gave her a flower, asked about the prettiest parts of the temple, and mentioned that she could only come in **if somebody invited her.** One night she asked to see the windows from the inside. *"Of course you can. Come on in."* **The girl died in the attack.**
