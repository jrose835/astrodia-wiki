Lady Nectaria is a vampire lord who led the attack on [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta's]] temple, the [[Temple of Nephthys]], killing the children in her care. **She is the vampire who turned Drolta** (confirmed in Session 13). She is pale-haired, winged, and red-eyed, and she dodges attacks by simply tilting her head.

## Connection to Drolta

After the attack that transformed Drolta into a dhampir, Lady Nectaria may still be attempting to reach Drolta's mind through psychic "tapping." [[Nephthys|Nephthys']] protection appears to be blocking this connection. *(Session 13: that door has been **thrown open**. See below.)*

Drolta now hunts the vampires who destroyed her temple, with Lady Nectaria as a primary target.

## What She Wanted *(Session 13)*

Per [[Nephthara]], as relayed by Drolta: the undead are **bound under some sort of contract**, and certain **relics** are powerful enough to **break those bonds**. These include **robes of [[Nephthys]]**, which may be **what Nectaria's vampires were searching for** in the attack. Some such relics are still on **[[Fire King Island]]**, which has not been attacked. Nephthara also believes the vampires **must have been let in**, since they can't enter a sanctuary uninvited.

## Session 13 — At the Temple Again (October 7, 2026)

At sunset on the day [[The Fallen]] arrived at the temple, [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s pendant pinged outside. Nectaria was standing about **a hundred feet off, red eyes glowing.** **The locked door in Drolta's mind**, where she has felt knocking and tapping for years, was **thrown open** with a pain in her head, and Drolta saw *the woman that turned her*. Nectaria's whisper carried across the distance:

> *"Brave of you to return to where your Phoenix Queen once failed you."*
> *"She never fails me. I still stand here before you."*
> *"Not for long."*

*(Earlier that day Drolta had failed a Wisdom save during the anointing, which caused a lapse in her mental fortification, and a voice spoke in her head. That was delivered privately. Whose voice it was hasn't been stated.)*

### Her Forces

- **Six Fiendish Icons** pulled themselves out of the ground: gargoyle-like figures that chip and crumble like stone or clay. [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s Arcana check says they **aren't undead** and are more like **constructs**. They attack with two slams, can dash, and set off a **fiery eruption** (a Dexterity save within 5 feet) when surrounded or nearly destroyed. Three got inside the temple.
- When the icons failed: *"I knew those fiendish icons weren't going to be able to get the job done. I need to send in a bit more of the artillery. **Now is the time.**"* Undead came out of the woods behind her, **most likely vampire spawn** *(to be confirmed)*.

### How She Fought

- **She dodges with a head tilt.** Eldritch Blast, a thrown hand axe, and Guiding Bolt all missed that way.
- **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s pact magic got through.** She shrugged off his Dissonant Whispers (*"You can't touch me. You are nothing."*), but an **echo in his patron's language** behind his words hurt her anyway, and did it again with Mind Spike. She was furious, and **intrigued**: no one should be able to do that to her.
- **She answered by taking [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]].** Holding Leif's eyes the whole time, she walked to Úlfr and **whispered in his ear**. He failed the Wisdom save and was **charmed.** To Leif: *"That is how you fuck with someone's mind."*
- **She bit Úlfr** as he walked away (an opportunity attack), but **couldn't pierce his Goliath hide.** She **spat out the blood.**
- Red's lion raked her for 9 (*"that's nothing"*), and Drolta's plume landed with Hex damage.

**The fight is unresolved.** It continues next session. See [[Session 13 Recap — Homecoming]].
