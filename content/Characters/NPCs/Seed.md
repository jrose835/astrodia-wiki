**Seed** is the **only [[WitchCrafters|WitchCrafter]] who studies living things** — botany and horticulture — in a guild otherwise built entirely around making objects. She keeps the guild's **courtyard garden** in [[Pentamarone]], and describes herself, cheerfully, as the odd one out.

## Background

She came from **a line of druids in a forest up north.** She left after *"some bad things happened in the forest"* — and stopped there.

> *"I'd rather not. I really..."*

She didn't expect to fit in anywhere, least of all a guild of crafters, since her work isn't a craft in the usual sense. **[[Madame Verre]] carved out a spot for her anyway** — one of several stories the party heard that night in which Verre's interest in an unusual person amounted to a rescue.

*(A druidic bloodline, a forest up north, and something that happened there she won't name — worth watching alongside the [[White Forest]] coven and [[Yssa]].)*

## Session 11 (September 9, 2026)

**Assigned, with [[Ceramia]], to the hardest question the [[Golden Chalice]] poses**: the **living copies.** What are they made of, are they **truly alive**, and what functions or powers them if they are. Their test subjects are the **cats** [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] conjured in Session 10, which are still in the guild hall.

[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] worked with them through the montage, on **Investigation**.

Seed joined the guild's night out at a [[Pentamarone]] dive bar with [[Edel]], [[Schmietta]], and [[Marka]], where the party's two plant enthusiasts found each other: [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] pointed at [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] — *"this big green guy here likes plants too. We thought he was a druid for a while, but I guess not."*
