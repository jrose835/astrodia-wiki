**She Who Fell Before the First Fall** is the pseudonym of a being — in most tellings **a female angel** — whom a **fringe sect** of the [[Solemn Pantheon]]'s faithful believe was **exiled from the [[Sanctuary in the Sky]]** before the war with the [[Darklords|Dark Lords]], and whose exile **spurred the Morning Star to rebel.** Nobody knows her name, what she did, or whether she existed at all. The party wonders whether she is **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]**.

## The Sect's Belief

As explained by the priest of the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] (Session 12):

- A **sect within the church** holds that **a being was exiled from the Sanctuary in the Sky** before the rebellion, and that this exile is **what caused the Morning Star's rebellion** — the cause of which scripture otherwise leaves **disputed.**
- Members of the sect **slip that belief into artworks**, representing her as a **small flaming meteor.**
- The **Morning Star's fall was the first fall**; hers came *before* it — hence the name.

The priest **does not personally believe it.** The temple library holds **no books on her** — *"It's a bit of a fringe theory."*

## Aren's Prior Knowledge

[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] **had heard of her before.** One or two believers turn up in many of the churches she's visited; it's a fringe belief with a quiet following. In their version she was **an angel, a woman**, who did something that **angered the gods so much she was exiled to earth**, and the Morning Star's rebellion came *in response.* **What she did and what became of her are unknown.**

## The Mural's Meteor

The Temple of Venus's basement mural (above) shows **the Solemn and his host against the Morning Star and his armies** at the moment the rebels were cast down. [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] sensed that something in it didn't belong, and [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] found it: a small **flaming meteor falling in the middle of the scene**, clearly a **deliberate addition.** [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] dated the mural to **a couple of centuries** old, brought from **the South**, unsigned.

Asked through Drolta's communion, [[Nephthys]] said the battle *"was even before my time,"* and that *"there are still many mysteries about it that the higher beings are not aware of."*

## Session 13 — The Forest Is Supposed to Remember (October 7, 2026)

- At the [[Temple of Nephthys]], [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] asked [[Ysmene]] about the meteor in the Venus mural. **Nothing came to mind.**
- Back with the party, a player noted the phrase had sounded familiar, and that it matches the story **[[Dia]]** told the young **[[Morgana]]**, recorded in Morgana's papers (Session 9): *"someone who fell before anyone else ever fell, someone the forest itself is supposed to remember even if no one else does."* **The forest is the [[White Forest]].**
- There was also some table confusion over whether she is the Morning Star. She isn't: his was the *first* fall, and hers came before it.

**The link between the sect's figure and Dia's story is the table's, not established.** It would put her in the White Forest, which the party is heading for.
