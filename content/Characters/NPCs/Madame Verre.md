**Madame Verre** is the **head of the [[WitchCrafters]]** and, by every account [[The Fallen]] heard, the person who effectively **runs [[Pentamarone]]**. She is described by the city's own artisans with a mix of reverence and resignation: the standard against which all their work is measured, and almost impossible to actually meet.

She appears as **a small girl seated on a floating throne of glass.**

## What Was Known Before Meeting Her

Gathered in the market and the guild hall before she ever appeared:

- **All of her work is magical**, and her **staff is made of glass** — she uses it as her **arcane focus**. This is the party's first confirmation that **glass can serve as a spellcasting focus**, which matters to [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s **glass orb** and to [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s plans for the [[Nithing Pole]].
- **She is a genuinely powerful witch**, distinct from the guild's junior artisans, whose market work is entirely mundane.
- **Audiences are effectively unavailable.** Commoners deal with other guild members; [[Genni]] said flatly that she could go ask Verre but the party could not come. [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s persuasion attempt failed.
- **She worked with [[Morgana]] in the past.** A [[Freedale]] refugee in the market remembered it, though not on what. Morgana was well-connected among covens and guilds and steered fleeing families toward Pentamarone as a **safe haven** — implying a real relationship between the Freedale witch and the guild.

## Session 10 — The Glass Throne (August 26, 2026)

Sent [[Genni]] away with the [[Golden Chalice]] and returned word that she **could not help either** — and then [[The Fallen]] discovered what the chalice does by drinking from it, filling her guild's library with **cats**, a **duplicate chalice**, a **copy of Asha's missing mother**, and a **horse that [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] punched hard enough to detonate.**

As the party hurried for the exit, she caught them in the guild's **central courtyard**:

![[Pasted image 20260826221207.png]]

**Every member of the party failed a Wisdom save** (Úlfr on a natural 1), was **paralyzed**, and was **turned bodily to face the garden.** Genni stepped aside, wide-eyed and frightened, and Madame Verre spoke:

> *"Well, well. I haven't had something like this happen in quite a while."*

Read as **amused rather than angry**. The session ended there.

## Session 11 — The Deal (September 9, 2026)

She dropped the paralysis almost at once.

> *"I apologize. I never do anything like this, but I couldn't let the first interesting thing that's happened here in so long get out of here without me investigating a little bit."*
> *"Are we your prisoners here?"*
> *"Oh, God, no, never. It was the only way to get your attention."*
> *"Efficient, I'd say."*

### What She Revealed About Herself

- **She succeeded [[Rellyona]] personally.** The guild and the city *"were handed down to me from Madame Rellyona"* — the [[Magistus]] who founded [[Pentamarone]] and has been dead for years. She has run both ever since, *"very efficiently, I'd say."*
- **She has looked like a child that entire time.** Her own masters shrug it off: *"we kind of just don't question it at this point."* Their read — **not the mind of a child, but the smugness of one**, and *"wise beyond her years."* [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] asked for the anti-aging secret outright and was refused: *"I like to be a girl's girl, but it's too good. I gotta keep it a secret."* *(Later explained to [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] in Session 12: an accident of her own **eternal-youth research** — not undead, and still aging. See [[#Why She Looks Like a Child|below]].)*
- **She is chronically bored** by meetings and guild business, and **magnetically drawn to anything interesting.** None of her masters had ever seen her paralyze anyone before.
- **She keeps her word.** Unanimous, from people with no reason to flatter her to strangers.
- **Her magic is "grassroots."** Her contempt for divination is genuine: *"as soon as your Identify doesn't work, everyone just assumes it's impossible. This is grassroots magic — getting to the essence of things without relying on a pre-existing spell."* She does not solve artifacts. She **takes them apart.**

### The Contract

She proposed a trade, and [[The Fallen]] took it:

| She gives | She gets |
|---|---|
| The guild's **best effort at identifying the [[Golden Chalice]]** — origin, function, meaning | **Use of the chalice** for one specific, **undisclosed** goal |
| The chalice **returned** once that goal is met; no claim of ownership | The party's **participation** in the research, which she called *"imperative"* |
| **Housing and meals** at an inn of the party's choosing | |
| A **guarantee of no harm** to any of them | |
| Her estimate: **one week**, less with help | |

At [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s insistence, the contract also records **in writing that the party warned her** what carrying the chalice means. Verre was delighted by this — *"that's fair, and it makes it quite interesting"* — and unimpressed by the danger itself: *"I am not worried about such people."* **[[Marka]]** drafted it; **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** read it before anyone signed.

The clause she initially refused was **lodging** — she laughed at [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] outright — and then reversed herself completely the moment she noticed **[[Leif's Glass Orb|the glass orb]]** on [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s staff.

> *"The [[Purodetapo Forest]], you say."*
> *"Marka. Give them whatever they want."*

### The Research

She convened **every master in the guild** and assigned four lines of inquiry in under a minute — materials ([[Edel]] & [[Schmietta]]), conjured objects ([[Haine]] & [[Pittore]]), living copies ([[Seed]] & [[Ceramia]]), records ([[Genni]] & [[Marka]]) — then invited the party to join whichever they liked. See [[WitchCrafters]].

### What She Wants From Leif

Late in the week she **sent for [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] alone** and cleared her office. He came out confused: she asked whether he had told her *"everything ever,"* asked many questions, and answered none of them. What he got was an agreement that **she will look into [[The Whispering One]]** — for free, unrequested compensation, which alarmed everyone in the party except Leif. *(She followed through in Session 12: an **unknowing pact with something not of this world** — see [[#Private Audiences|below]].)*

What she got was **[[Leif's Glass Orb|his staff and the orb]]**, left in her study for study. He had no arcane focus when [[Aleister the Invoker]] turned up two mornings later. *(She tossed the staff back to him in Session 12, mid-teleport.)*

### She Knows About the Fourth Magic

**[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] told her**, in that private conversation. When [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] came separately to ask what kind of magic the guild actually uses, she opened with *"you know, I just had this conversation with Leif"* — and then gave a beautifully empty answer:

> *"We don't use any higher-being magic. We have great honor for the [[Solemn Pantheon]], but we just happen to use the natural magic that flows around us. Whether that be arcane, whatever it may be."*

**Úlfr's insight:** she knows he is holding something back, and she is **meeting him exactly where he stands and no further.** Not lying. Not forthcoming. See [[The Fourth Magic]].

### The Whisper

**[[Haine]]**, alone with her after the deal, asked whether she was sure about signing contracts with strangers.

> *"I have a good feeling about this group. And this chalice. **It's the best lead we've got.**"*

**A lead toward what remains the guild's biggest unanswered question.** *(Still unanswered after Session 12 — and she has since dropped the chalice from it. See below.)*

## Session 12 — Something from Nothing (September 23, 2026)

### Woken by a Description

When [[Genni]] slipped off to report [[Aleister the Invoker|Aleister]]'s monster, [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] and [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] followed her in. **Madame Verre was fast asleep on her glass throne.**

> *"You like interesting things, right, Madame Verre? We've got something interesting for you."*

She had **heard of Aleister** but *"didn't think he was real,"* and was unimpressed at first (*"a lot of wizards can fly"*). Then the party described him — green hair, white robe with red, monocle, a book, a **staff drawn out of the summoned creature's chest**, a sigil, and **glowing red symbols on his hands: a diamond with an eye.** **With each detail she sat up a little straighter.**

> *"Yes. I very much do need to check out this monster. Is it still there? You left the corpse untouched?"*

She **teleported away to examine [[Cocytus]]**, reappeared just long enough to **toss [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] his staff** (and [[Leif's Glass Orb|the orb]]), and vanished again. What she found on the body, she hasn't said.

### She Gave the Chalice Back

At the report, [[Genni]] argued that given its effects on its users, the [[Golden Chalice]] **shouldn't be used for what Verre wanted it for.** Verre agreed:

> *"I think the risk is too great. And I don't want what we're working on to be tainted by whatever magic this thing has."*

**She returned the chalice** and honored the contract — simply declining to exercise her right to use it. **She still won't say what the goal was**, *"until I'm certain of a few other things."* When the party asked how to track the other Spoils and Genni proposed contacting [[Simone]] about her sand-map spell, Verre cut in: ***"That won't be necessary. I know the spell."***

### Private Audiences

The next morning she received **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** — with [[Howl]] present, at his choice — and then **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** alone. *"Not many people can just walk into my office, but I guess y'all have made enough of an impression to earn that privilege."* Both conversations were played privately; what follows is what each chose to share.

**From Leif (to the party, behind a closed door):**
- She told him he **unknowingly made a pact with something not of this world** — a being from another plane, world, or dimension, that somehow reached Astrodia and is **probably living in his home forest**, the [[Purodetapo Forest]]. See [[The Whispering One|Sorath]].
- She told him **the [[Great Cataclysm]] was caused by a group of people opening a portal to another world**, letting in magic — and other things — that aren't of this world. **Who opened it is lost to time.** She told him *not* to share this; he did, and the party agreed it stays among them.

### Why She Looks Like a Child

As Aren stepped out of the first audience, she **quietly grasped her pendant.** ***No undead.***

What Aren came out with after her own audience:

- **She is not undead**, in the necromantic sense. Her condition is **the result of an experiment**: she was **researching eternal youth**, and the result wasn't what she was aiming for. *"A simple accident. Which we'll see how long it remains an accident."*
- It isn't frozen time. It's more like **the hands of the clock were wound back.** **She is still aging**, and still moving toward her end.

*(This supersedes the Session 11 guesswork. It says nothing about how long ago the accident happened, or how old she actually is.)*

### The Festival

At the fireworks festival Leif found her **in a very good mood** and asked the questions he'd forgotten:

- **Where did the Cataclysm begin?** In the **northern mountain range of what is now the [[Endymion Empire]]**, travelling **southeast** across Astrodia — matching [[The Fourth Magic|the green line]] on Simone's map.
- **Was the rift ever healed?** *"Oh, yes, the [[Magistus]] did what they had to do to seal the **thergy**."* *(Corrected by Tommy in Session 13: the word was "thergy", as heard, not "rift", and it's an important word.)* Then, pursing her lips: ***"Ooh. I don't think I was supposed to say that."***
- **Is the leftover green magic from the original leak?** Yes; to her knowledge it is still present across Astrodia.

Her farewell:

> *"You and your group have found yourself in the crosshairs of a number of very interesting happenings in Astrodia at this point, I will say."*
> *"Keep that chalice safe. Very powerful artifact. If word of its power were ever to get out, more powerful people would probably be after you."*

She expects them to leave soon — for the [[White Forest]], *"by way of a temple or two"* — and **invited them to come back later**, when *"maybe we'll be done with our project."* She asked how long they had before the Academy comes for the chalice: **about 10 days.**

### Party Theories *(speculation, not established)*

- **How does she know all this?** [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] suspects she was **a disciple of one of the [[Magistus]]**, most likely [[Rellyona]].
- **A Spoil against [[Pluto]]?** If each [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoil]] mocks an Agent, one mocking Death might look like *unnatural, eternal life* — and Verre is *suspiciously* alive and **wears a white dress** (one of the Spoils is a gown). The party wondered whether she carries a Spoil, knowingly or not. Her own account to Aren — an accident, still aging — cuts somewhat against it.

## Session 13 — Too Busy (October 7, 2026)

She didn't see the party off. Her office was locked, and [[Dolly]] said business was booming after the festival. Her masters gave the gifts without her (see [[WitchCrafters' Parting Gifts]]).

- **Her project has a workshop.** Behind the guild hall is a **secret lab** with a station for every master. [[Genni]] reminded the others that Verre had **specifically told them not to tell the party** what they're working on. **[[Haine]] is sewing enormous clothing cut for a woman**, too big even for [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]. It's the first physical clue to the goal she won't name.
- **A message is on its way to her.** [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] asked Genni to tell her that the creature in **[[The Orb]]** speaks **the same language as his patron**. If she wants to reach the party, *"she has her ways."*
- **The word was "thergy."** At the start of Session 13 Tommy corrected her Session 12 line: the Magistus sealed **the *thergy*** *(as heard; spelling not given)*, not "the rift," and he flagged the word as important.

## Why She Matters

She is the one person everyone — the glassblower, the blacksmith, Genni, the party — agrees **might actually be able to identify the [[Golden Chalice]]**, after three failed Identifies. The party spent a day failing to get an audience with her and then got one by accident, in the worst possible way. *(She delivered, in Session 12: the guild's full report on the chalice — and then declined to use it.)*
