A historical orc figure, originally named **Naf**, who lived approximately 500 years ago. His story is the subject of [[Myrna Nor]]'s Lorehold thesis.

## History

Naf was an orc wizard living in a kingdom established under the [[War Rock Mountains]], led by an orc priest named **Wound** with a figurehead ruler named **Uruth**. Unhappy with the warmongering direction of the kingdom, Naf fled to [[Artmegía]] and settled in a small village with his wife and children, farming and studying arcane magic.

Eventually, Naf felt guilty for abandoning his people. He had skilled crafters forge a **sentient axe** capable of communicating with its wielder, hoping to use it to reconcile differences with the kingdom's rulers. He returned to the [[War Rock Mountains]], leading to a years-long power struggle between himself, Uruth, and Wound.

When communications from his family ceased, Naf fled home to find **his family had been poisoned** — killed by a poison only Wound knew how to brew. Wound had sent spies to identify and murder them.

Devastated, Naf donned **black robes of mourning**, forsook his birth name, and became known as **Leith Chantos**.

## After the Tragedy

Leith Chantos abandoned the power struggle and spent years researching poisons and their antidotes, compiling them into a book called the **[[Nath Loom]]** — a spellbook containing hundreds of poisons and their antidotes.

He traveled around [[Astrodia]] **building libraries**, and within these libraries, he **buried his murdered family members**. He built a final library, after which he departed to parts unknown.

## Legacy

- Well-known historical figure — considered somewhere between a criminal and a vigilante
- [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] recognized the name as connected to the [[War Rock Mountains]]
- [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] recalled him as an infamous figure who killed people
- [[Myrna Nor]] advocates that he was misunderstood and trying to protect his family

## The Lost Library

Myrna believes the **final library** is located about 4 days northeast of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]], in the mountains. The party was hired to explore it and recover the [[Nath Loom]].

## Session 6 Findings

The party — now **[[The Fallen]]** — finished exploring Leith's final library and uncovered much of his personal history:

- **Personal journal** — recovered from his black-on-black **bedchamber**, telling his life story in his own words. The party gave it to **[[Myrna Nor]]**, completely **vindicating her thesis**.
- **Sentient axe** — **[[Leith Chantos's Legacy]]**, a good-aligned +1 greataxe sworn to **protect the family of its bearer**, was found resting on the anvil in his **forge**. It had **not been touched since Leith himself** and **does not know where he is**. [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] claimed it. *(This is one of his magical creations — not the famous double-headed axe from the fresco that he forged to stop the warlord.)*
- **[[Una]]'s grave** — his wife's headstone lay beyond the **spider pit**, reading in Orcish, *"Una, beloved wife."* A **platinum ring** rested on it; the party left it untouched (Úlfr left a platinum ring there in respect).
- **Black robes of mourning** — his garments for his dead wife were found in the bedchamber and kept by **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]**.

**His fate remains unknown.** After building this final library and burying Una, Leith departed for parts unknown. There is no consensus on what became of him — whether he **vanished into the ether**, **ascended**, or is **still wandering** Astrodia.
