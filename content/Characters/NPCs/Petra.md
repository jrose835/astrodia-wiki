**Petra** is a **priestess of [[Nephthys]]**, formerly of the **[[Temple of Nephthys]]**. Originally from **[[Fire King Island]]**, she is the priestess the **Fire Kings** sent to help rebuild after [[Lady Nectaria]]'s attack. She **fell in love with the place and stayed**, and went home only for short visits. She is the party's only direct source on the island. **In Session 14 she turned out to be a sleeper:** Nectaria had **seeded her mind**, and at midnight she tried to let the vampires in. She has since **left to wander**, and is now an honorary **staff bro.**

## Session 13 — Homecoming (October 7, 2026)

When word of the attack reached the island, a number of the faithful wanted to help, and she came to rebuild the temple physically and spiritually and to recruit worshippers. [[Ysmene]] pointed her out to [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] as the Fire Kings' one envoy, and the temple has had **little contact with the island** since.

Drolta asked her about anomalies on the island:

- **Relics of high importance belonging to both the [[Phoenix King]] and the Phoenix Queen** are housed on the island, **for when the Phoenixes are reborn**, so the items will be ready and waiting for them. **She doesn't know what they are.**
- She doesn't think the **[[Great Cataclysm]]** affected the island's temple.
- **After the attack here, security on the island was increased.** It has been years, and she isn't sure it's still as strong.
- **As a woman she isn't privy to the higher rites or secrets** on the island, because of the division between the orders of Nephthys and the Phoenix King *(a name heard as "Garunix", possibly the Phoenix King's; unconfirmed)*. That division is part of why she feels better at the temple than on the island.

*"It's part of the territory."*

## Session 14 — The Sleeper (October 21, 2026)

See [[Session 14 Recap — Invitation Only (Shillelagh her down)]].

**She was the obvious suspect.** When the party asked who could have let the vampires in five years ago, Petra was **the only priestess who arrived after the attack**. The others (Doria, Ophira, and Renn) had all been there. A Perception read on them found nothing.

**Drolta asked her to protect what the vampires really want**, which is kept at the Fire Kings' temple: **[[Nephthys]]'s dress** from her human form, said to **break chains and destroy unjust hierarchies.**

**At midnight**, while [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] read the journal from the vault aloud and [[Lady Nectaria]] rapped on the door, Petra **got up in a trance** and walked toward the door, faster and faster. [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] grabbed her. She was **much stronger than she looked**, and she was **taking a breath to issue an invitation** when he covered her mouth. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s **Suggestion** found **something already occupying her mind**. She had become a kind of **vampire familiar.** [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s **Hold Person** froze her mid-word, and the party beat her unconscious. **The rapping stopped.**

**In the morning** she thanked them:

> *"You did the right thing. I did not have any control over my faculties, and who knows what I would have done if I was able to let her in."*

- She doesn't know whether she's **susceptible**, or whether **a seed had been planted** in her mind earlier.
- **On her way to the temple years ago**, there was **a night she couldn't remember.** She took it for sleepwalking or a bad nightmare. She now believes that's when they **got their claws into her mind**, and it lay **dormant until that night.**
- She **will not go home** to Fire King Island. She'll **travel**, going nowhere of note, on coin from the priestesses, possibly **north to Exod's Cross** to **disappear into a crowd.**
- She told the party there may be **others who have been touched by them.**

**Red gave her his spare quarterstaff**: *"You can always just be a staff bro with us."* *"I don't really know what that means, but... I think I like it. I'll be happy to be a staff bro."* She hugged the priestesses and Drolta, and left.
