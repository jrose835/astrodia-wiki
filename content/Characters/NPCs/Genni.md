**Genni** is the **[[WitchCrafters]]' knowledge-seeker** — the guild's scholar and bookworm, described by her own colleagues as *"the smartest of us here."* Her study in the [[Pentamarone]] guild hall doubles as the **guild library**. She is helpful, curious, a little overwhelmed, and — as far as the party could tell — honest.

## Session 10 — The Chalice (August 26, 2026)

[[Dolly]] brought [[The Fallen]] to her when they came to the guild hall asking for an appraisal of the [[Golden Chalice]].

### What She Couldn't Do

She cast **Identify** and hit the same wall as everyone else — the **third failure**, after the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] and the party's own attempt.

> *"I'm sorry. There's some non-detection magic. I can't quite figure out what exactly it is."*
> *"How often does this happen?"*
> *"Not often, to be quite honest."*

### What She Worked Out

Pressed on what *she* would do with it, Genni produced the single most valuable finding of the session:

> The nondetection is **not a spell cast on the chalice.** **Dispel Magic would do nothing.** It appears to come **from the chalice itself** — *"almost like the chalice is casting non-detection constantly, on itself. Which I don't really know how that works."*

### What She Knew

- **"The Sinful Spoils"** — her ears visibly perked at the phrase. She went through her books and produced the title **"the Seeker of the Sinful Spoils"** on her own, independent of the Academy. Beyond the name, nothing. *(Confirms the name circulates outside Academy records — see [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]].)*
- **The [[White Forest]]** — she knows the curse, which threatened Pentamarone before it receded, but little of the forest's history. One thing she did offer: **rumor of a coven of witches who lived inside it** — the third independent source to say so.
- **The city's founder** — confirmed the town was founded by one of the [[Magistus]], and that **[[Rellyona]] is dead**: *"She's no longer with us."*
- **On the Academy** — diplomatic. Fellow scholars, mutual respect, magic as academic study versus creative expression; *"some say they have radical overreach, but they've also held out against the Endymion Empire."*
- **On the [[Endymion Empire]]** — when [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] said the Empire was worse, Genni answered ***"Yes. But I would know"*** — and left before anyone could ask what she meant. Likely a refugee, like much of the city. **Unresolved.**

### Two Things the Party Found Out About Her

1. **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] cast Detect Thoughts on her.** Surface thoughts only — Asha declined to probe deeper, since Genni would have detected it. The read came back **clean**: Genni **genuinely does not know** what the chalice is, and was already intending to take the question to **[[Madame Verre]].** She was not concealing anything.
2. **There is a safe under her desk**, and on it — as on **her own staff** — is **the symbol of the magic the [[Endymion Empire]] uses.** The party's benign reading is that this is where the guild stores its **[[Keystones]]**, needed to cast legally outside the city's borders. The uncomfortable reading is that the guild's independence is a front. Unresolved either way.

### How It Ended

Genni went to Madame Verre, came back with *"she doesn't know either — best of luck to you all,"* and then watched the party **drink from the chalice** and fill her library with cats, a duplicate chalice, a stranger's mother, and the exploded remains of a horse.

She was **standing wide-eyed and frightened** in the courtyard a few minutes later when Madame Verre paralyzed everyone — and stepped aside to let her through.
