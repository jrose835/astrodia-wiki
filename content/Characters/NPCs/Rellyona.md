**Rellyona** is one of the **[[Magistus]]** — the legendary spellcasters who stopped the **[[Great Cataclysm]]** roughly a century ago — and the **founder of [[Pentamarone]]**, the one city inside [[Endymion Empire]] borders that the Empire does not rule.

**She is dead.**

*(Name recorded as heard at the table in Session 10; spelling unconfirmed.)*

## What Is Known

- **She founded [[Pentamarone]]**, and the city's independence from the Empire is generally traced back to her.
- **She was a great sorceress and a great witch**, per [[Genni]] of the [[WitchCrafters]] — the guild she left behind in her city.
- **She is no longer alive**, confirmed in Session 10. This closes off the party's hope that the surviving Magistus might be found here; **Emperor Endymion remains the only Magistus known to live.**
- **Rumor: she and the Emperor "went way back."** A trader at the Pentamarone docks reported that the two made a **truce before she died**, and that the Emperor has honored it ever since — one of three competing explanations for why the Empire tolerates the city (see [[Pentamarone]]).

If the truce rumor is true, it complicates the standing theory that the Magistus **fell out** with one another after the Cataclysm. They may have parted on terms.

## The Biography

**[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]** found ***The Biography of Rellyona*** at a market bookstall in [[Pentamarone]] and bought it, along with a history of the Greater [[Endymion Empire]], for 15 silver.

**She has not read it yet.** It is the party's only sustained written source on any Magistus other than the Emperor.
