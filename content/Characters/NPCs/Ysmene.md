**Ysmene** *(heard at the table as "Yasmin"; spelling from the map token)* is a **priestess of [[Nephthys]]** at the **[[Temple of Nephthys]]** and one of **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]'s sisters** from before the attack. She was there that night and **saved a lot of people.** She was the first to run to Drolta when Drolta came home, and **she knows what Drolta is.** She wears **blue** ceremonial dress.

## Session 13 — Homecoming (October 7, 2026)

### The Reunion

When Nephthara recognized Drolta in the middle of the anointing, Ysmene **abandoned her post** and ran to hug her:

> *"Oh, Drolta, it's been so long. I can't believe you're here."*
> *"Have you been well? Are you safe? Unharmed?"*
> *"I have been protected by Nephthys. And that's all I can ask for."*

Nephthara sent her back to her post to finish the rite.

### What She Told Drolta

- She has tried to push that night out of her memory.
- **Rebuilding:** locals helped. A visiting priest was paid out of temple funds for a **protective spell** *(heard as "hallow")*, and **they haven't had the money to cast it again, so they don't know if it still holds.** There have been no attacks since. They never got to the **root cause.**
- **The congregation:** many longtime worshippers left. **[[Solenne]]** came about **two years after Drolta left**, *"much like you did,"* and Ysmene was quick to put her on Drolta's path.
- **The ashes** Drolta guarded were **untouched.** *"We think they were looking for something."*
- **The Fire Kings:** little contact. They sent **[[Petra]]** to help rebuild.
- **[[Venus]]:** there's some good faith between Venus and the Phoenixes, since the Phoenix re-creates itself every cycle. She knew nothing of the **meteor**.
- **The vault** holds the belongings of those who died, and she offered to let Drolta in.

### What She Knows

> *"I did what I could to save a lot of people that night. And you put up a good fight, and it's a miracle that you're still here. But I know I wasn't able to stop everything... I never knew if that was the truth, or if you felt shame or embarrassment for... what you are. I hope you know that we hold no ill will. At least, I don't. And **I'm sorry if what I did pushed you into this uncomfortable spot between life and death.**"*

Ysmene **knows Drolta is a dhampir**, and believes **something she did that night** helped put Drolta in that state. What she did hasn't been said.

> *"You did the best you could, as we all did. I appreciate you."*

She can't speak for the temple now that it has a new high priestess, but she told Drolta that **she and her comrades are welcome.** She met the party, including [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] disguised as "Gertrude." [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] praised the rebuilding: *"It will not be done in vain."*

When [[Lady Nectaria]] attacked at sunset, Ysmene fought the **Fiendish Icons** that got inside.
