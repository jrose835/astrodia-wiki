**Dia** is a figure from **[[Morgana]]'s childhood** — the person who told her stories when she was small, near the forest that would become the [[White Forest]]. She is mentioned exactly once, in a single line of Morgana's journal, and that line carries what may be the campaign's most consequential piece of folklore.

Nothing else about her is established: not her relationship to Morgana (mother, grandmother, elder of the coven), not whether she is alive, not whether she is remembered by anyone else.

## The Story She Told

> *"There's an old story that Dia used to tell when I was small, about someone who fell before anyone else ever fell — someone the forest itself is supposed to remember even if no one else does. I never believed it was more than a story to keep children from wandering too far past the tree line. **I'm no longer certain of that.**"*

Morgana dismissed it as a children's cautionary tale for her entire life. After a decade of watching the trees whiten through her scrying font, she stopped dismissing it — and shortly afterward walked into the forest and died there.

## Why the Story Matters

*"Someone who fell before anyone else ever fell"* points at a **first fallen** — which sits uncomfortably close to established Astrodia canon:

- The [[Agents]] page records a class of **angels below the Agents, some of whom rebelled and were cast down** — the **[[Darklords|Dark Lords]]**. A "first" among them would predate the rest.
- The [[White Forest]] holds the **largest concentration of divine magic in [[Astrodia]]**.
- [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s recurring dreams feature a **winged woman in black and red with talons for hands and wings of talons** — [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]].

The party has connected these dots at the table, but **nothing in Morgana's papers names the figure or confirms any link.** Treat the connection as theory until something establishes it.

Notably, the phrasing is not *"the forest remembers"* as metaphor — Morgana's own conclusion about the forest was that *"something in that forest is **reaching**."*
