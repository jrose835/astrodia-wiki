**Cocytus** is the being **[[Aleister the Invoker]]** summoned into a back alley of [[Pentamarone]] in Session 11 — a vast, draconic elemental of **ice and running water**, with blue light moving through its body like blood.

**Nobody present had ever seen anything like it**, including the adventurers who had come to [[Pentamarone]] hunting Aleister specifically because of what he summoned in *their* dungeon. Whatever he called up in those ruins, it wasn't this.

![[Pasted image 20260909223053.png]]

## What the Party Learned Fighting It

- **Cold damage slows it.** The single most important discovery of the fight, and it came from the enemy: the bandit **mage's Ray of Frost** landed and part of the creature's body **froze solid**, halving its movement. Every frost effect on the board after that was a tempo win — which is why **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s Ice Knife** carried the encounter.
- **Slam attacks, two per turn.** One landed on [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] for **28** and put her **down** — the first death saves of the campaign.
- **It engulfs.** It swallowed **[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]** in brown-bear form: 20+ damage, knocked out of Wild Shape, then **restrained and suffocating** inside its body until he escaped on a Strength check.
- **Large pool of hit points**, ground down slowly by the party and the adventurers together.
- **It is not loyal.** Consistent with the adventurers' account of the last one, it stayed and fought after **Aleister flew away and abandoned it.**

## How It Died

The **swashbuckler** of the adventuring party *(named in Session 12 as [[The Sundered Watch]])* finished it: she ran up the back of Red's lion form, **launched off it, and drove her rapier through the beast's eye.**

## Aftermath (Session 12, September 23, 2026)

**Nobody searched the corpse.** When the party described [[Aleister the Invoker]] to [[Madame Verre]] at the [[WitchCrafters]] guild hall, with details down to the staff he drew from the creature's chest, she asked:

> *"Yes. I very much do need to check out this monster. Is it still there? You left the corpse untouched?"*

Then she **teleported away to examine it**, reappearing only long enough to toss [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] his staff. **What she found has not been disclosed.**

[[Genni]] had perked up at the word "monster" when [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] described it as a water creature, something like an elemental but "scarier looking," that could be slowed by freezing parts of it, and went straight to tell Verre.

## Why It Matters

Cocytus is the party's first direct look at what [[Aleister the Invoker]] actually does. It is **not native to [[Astrodia]]** by any account available — which places it in the same uncomfortable category as [[The Orb]]'s prisoner and whatever speaks to [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] as [[The Whispering One]]: *things from outside, that people in this world can nonetheless reach.* *(Session 12 context: Verre's account, via Leif, is that the [[Great Cataclysm]] opened a portal to another world and that beings came through it; whether Cocytus has any connection to that is not established.)*
