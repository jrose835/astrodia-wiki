**Pittore** is a **master [[WitchCrafters|WitchCrafter]]** of [[Pentamarone]], one of the "rainbow of women" [[The Fallen]] met when [[Madame Verre]] convened the guild's masters to study the [[Golden Chalice]].

She was **assigned to the generated-materials team with [[Haine]]**: determining what the objects the chalice conjures are physically **constructed of**, and whether a conjured copy **fully replicates** the original. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] joined them, since the duplicate chalice on the table was his — and opened the work with a **natural 20 on Perception** comparing the two cups side by side.

She did not join the guild's night out and has not yet told the party her story. *(Session 12 says every master except [[Genni]] was open about why she came to [[Pentamarone]] — but Pittore's specific story isn't recorded.)*

## Session 12 (September 23, 2026)

- **Fireworks:** she explained **the chemistry behind the colors** to [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]].
- **Her team's findings** (with [[Haine]] and Leif) went into the report on the [[Golden Chalice]]: object copies are **100% physical duplicates, down to the imperfections**, carry **no magic**, and can reproduce details the drinker never noticed — provided the drinker has shared space with the original. A copy would pass inspection, and **can be enchanted afterward.**
