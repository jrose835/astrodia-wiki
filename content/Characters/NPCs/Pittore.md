**Pittore** is a **master [[WitchCrafters|WitchCrafter]]** of [[Pentamarone]], one of the "rainbow of women" [[The Fallen]] met when [[Madame Verre]] convened the guild's masters to study the [[Golden Chalice]].

She was **assigned to the generated-materials team with [[Haine]]**: determining what the objects the chalice conjures are physically **constructed of**, and whether a conjured copy **fully replicates** the original. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] joined them, since the duplicate chalice on the table was his — and opened the work with a **natural 20 on Perception** comparing the two cups side by side.

She did not join the guild's night out and has not yet told the party her story.
