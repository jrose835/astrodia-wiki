**Yssa** was **[[Morgana]]'s childhood friend** — the two of them *"girls together in that forest"* that is now the [[White Forest]], back before the whitening. When Morgana left, **Yssa stayed.** She is presumed to have been part of the **coven** rumored to live within the forest. Her fate is unknown, and was unknown to Morgana herself.

She has never appeared in play. Everything known about her comes from a single passage in Morgana's journal, recovered in Session 9.

## What Morgana Wrote

> *"I keep thinking of Yssa. Of all of them, if I'm honest, but mostly her. We were girls together in that forest before it was anything anyone in [[Freedale]] would recognize now — before the whitening, before the silence where the deer used to be. I left when I was young and foolish enough to think leaving meant I'd outgrown it. She stayed, the way she always said she would. I never once asked her why, because I was young and foolish and thought there would always be time to ask her later."*

And later in the same entry:

> *"I don't know what became of the coven. Up until now I don't know if Yssa is alive to be angry with me for staying away as long as I did."*

The journal closes on her: *"I hope she remembers me kindly. I hope there's a she left to remember anything at all."*

## Significance

- **She is a coven contemporary who was inside the forest when the whitening began** — potentially the only living witness to how the curse actually started, if she survived it.
- **She is the emotional center of Morgana's decision to go.** Morgana watched the forest through her scrying font for a decade largely because Yssa was in it, and her guilt over leaving — *"tired of being the one who left"* — is what finally drove her out the door and to her death.
- **The party believed her name was the guardian's password.** *"The guardian answers only to its charm"* had [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] convinced the word was **"Yssa."** The theory was never tested — the party found the guardian's [[Guardian Command Amulet|command amulet]] on Morgana's body instead, and the "charm" turned out to be a physical object. The guess may still matter elsewhere.

## Open Questions

- **Did she survive the whitening?** Morgana couldn't say, and Morgana was watching the forest daily for ten years.
- **Is she connected to the bound woman in white** seen in [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s scrying vision, tied to a tree in the White Forest alongside Asha's mother? **Nothing establishes this** — the vision gives only "all in white, long blonde hair." Pure speculation at this stage.
- **What became of the coven** as a whole.
