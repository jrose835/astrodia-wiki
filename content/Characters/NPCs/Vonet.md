An orc woman hired by [[Lucian Cugg]] as an escort. She and [[Dilly]] double-crossed Lucian outside [[Thornvale]], stealing the [[Golden Chalice]] and fleeing north. They sold the chalice to goblins in a cave for 50 gold and were planning to steal it back to sell again at [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]].

The party tracked them down and defeated them in combat. Both were knocked unconscious (non-lethally) and left in the cave. They were gone by morning.

## First Appeared

Session 1 — bandit cave north of [[Thornvale]]
