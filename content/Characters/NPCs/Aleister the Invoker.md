**Aleister the Invoker** is a name told across [[Astrodia]] as half folk tale and half warning: **a man who summons beings from outside the world** — things that cannot be explained and are found nowhere in nature. Most people who have heard of him assume he isn't real.

[[The Fallen]] met him in a back alley of [[Pentamarone]] in Session 11, cornered by the adventuring party he had abandoned in a dungeon *(named in Session 12 as [[The Sundered Watch]])*.

**Appearance** (pooled from the party's memories in Session 12): **green hair**, a **white robe with red** detailing, a **monocle**, a **book**, and **glowing red symbols on his hands**: a **diamond with an eye** inside it.

**His reputation is genuinely split.** [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s **natural 20 History check** returned both versions: some tell his story as a hero's, some as a villain's, and nobody the party could name agrees on which.

> *"His reputation precedes him. This is a well-known man. I'm not sure if he's good or bad — I guess it depends on the circumstance."* — [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]

## What He Can Do

Observed in one short fight:

- **Summons beings from outside Astrodia.** He called **[[Cocytus]]** — an elemental of ice and running water — out of a sigil in mid-air, onto a rooftop.
- **Draws a staff out of his summons.** Per the party's Session 12 description, he pulled **a staff that seemed to emerge from the summoned creature's chest** and then cast with it.
- **He cannot fully control what he summons.** By his own admission: *"I'm still getting used to working with magic in this way. I can't control everything that I summon."*
- **Teleportation.** He stepped straight out of [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s grapple onto a second-story roof.
- **Flight.** He left the fight by casting **Fly** on himself and going 120 feet up and away in two turns. Nobody could reach him.

He never once attacked the party. He summoned, watched, and left.

## What the Adventurers Say He Did

The group cornering him — a captain with a **pistol**, a mage, a swashbuckler, and a druid — had hired him to help explore **a set of ruins**. Their account:

- At the end of the dungeon he **took the loot they were after.**
- They were ambushed by whatever haunted the temple. He **summoned something** to fight it off, and then **left.**
- The summon killed the haunts, then **turned on them.** It killed **two of their friends** and only lost interest before killing the rest.

His response was an apology, sincere and useless, followed by an attempt to walk away. **The ruins have not been identified, and neither has the loot he took.** *(Revealed in Session 12: the loot was **a sword**, and he threw it away. See below.)*

### The Sundered Watch's Full Account (Session 12)

After the fight, the adventurers introduced themselves as **[[The Sundered Watch]]** *(name as heard — unconfirmed)*, resistance fighters against the [[Endymion Empire]], and told the whole story:

- They met Aleister in a town **far to the north**. He offered to help them explore a set of **ruins** rumored to hold a magical artifact.
- Underground they were **overrun by undead**. Aleister summoned one of his creatures to fight them off.
- While the fighting went on, he **walked straight to the artifact and took it**. It was **a sword**. He decided it *"wasn't going to be able to help him,"* **tossed it aside, and fled.**
- His summon killed the undead and then turned on them. **Two of their friends died.** They have been hunting him **for weeks**, and plan to get back on his trail.

A sword isn't on the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]] list (lance, book, chalice, gown). The party's read: **Aleister may be hunting the Spoils too**, and rejected this sword because it wasn't one of them.

## He Knows What Howl Is

This is the part that matters.

**[[Howl]]**, [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s owl, sensed him from across the city, screamed, and flew to the alley on her own. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s animal handling read her reaction not as fear but as **rage** — she is *furious* at this man.

Then Aleister looked at her properly, and **Howl shrank**: feathers hunched, eyes down, making herself as small as she could.

> *"Oh. **I do know what this is, then.**"*
> *"What is it?"*
> *"...What's your name?"*

He refused to answer. Told that Leif found her injured in the **[[Purodetapo Forest]]**, he said:

> *"How interesting. The Purodetapo Forest, you say. **I'll have to make a visit there.**"*

That is Leif's home, and where his family lives. Leif tried to stop him with **Eldritch Blast** and discovered his arcane focus was in [[Madame Verre]]'s study. The fight that followed cost the party [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]'s first death saves and killed nobody who mattered to Aleister, who **escaped clean.**

> *"I just seem to make a mess of everything I do. I guess I'm still not ready."*

**Not ready for what is unstated.**

## Session 12 — He Has Fought Sorath (September 23, 2026)

**Why he knew Howl.** [[Howl]] called him a ***"Bad man"*** and told [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] the rest privately. What Leif shared with the party: Howl is a servant of **[[The Whispering One|Sorath]]**, Leif's patron, and **Aleister has fought Sorath before.** That is why she met him with rage and then submission, and why he recognized her at once. Sorath is probably living in the Purodetapo Forest, **the same forest Leif told Aleister about.** Leif: *"I don't know if he's also my enemy, but now he's really my enemy."*

**Madame Verre had heard of him.** When the party described him at the [[WitchCrafters]] guild hall, [[Madame Verre]] said she *"didn't think he was real"* and was unimpressed at first (*"a lot of wizards can fly"*). **With each detail** (the hair, robe, monocle, book, the staff drawn from the summon, the sigil, the eye-in-diamond marks) **she sat up a little straighter.**

> *"Yes. I very much do need to check out this monster. Is it still there? You left the corpse untouched?"*

Nobody had searched [[Cocytus]]'s body. She **teleported away to examine it**, reappearing only to toss Leif his staff.

**Is he the Seeker?** Back at the inn the party floated the theory that Aleister is **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]**: he's looking for a Spoil, he carries **a book**, the Seeker supposedly already holds one Spoil, and his hood resembles the wanted poster. Against it: the poster shows **a woman in black and red**, and Aleister has **green hair**. Unresolved.

## Open Questions

1. **What is [[Howl]]?** He recognized it instantly and would not say. Howl's reaction to him — rage, then total submission — suggests recognition running both ways. *(Partly answered in Session 12: she serves Sorath, whom he has fought.)*
2. **Is he going to the [[Purodetapo Forest]]?** He said so out loud, to the one person in the world who would object. As of Session 12, that is **probably where Sorath lives.**
3. **Where do the things he summons come from?** Nothing in [[Astrodia]] explains [[Cocytus]] — which is the same problem [[The Fourth Magic]] poses, from a different direction. *(Session 12 context: the fourth magic is now understood as otherworldly magic that leaked through the [[Great Cataclysm]]'s rift.)*
4. **Whose side is he on?** Both, per the stories.
5. **What is he looking for?** He rejected a sword as unable *"to help him."* Is he hunting the Sinful Spoils, and is he the Seeker?
6. **What did Verre find on Cocytus's corpse**, and why did the eye-in-diamond marks rouse her?
