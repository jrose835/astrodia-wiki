**Aleister the Invoker** is a name told across [[Astrodia]] as half folk tale and half warning: **a man who summons beings from outside the world** — things that cannot be explained and are found nowhere in nature. Most people who have heard of him assume he isn't real.

[[The Fallen]] met him in a back alley of [[Pentamarone]] in Session 11, cornered by the adventuring party he had abandoned in a dungeon.

**His reputation is genuinely split.** [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s **natural 20 History check** returned both versions: some tell his story as a hero's, some as a villain's, and nobody the party could name agrees on which.

> *"His reputation precedes him. This is a well-known man. I'm not sure if he's good or bad — I guess it depends on the circumstance."* — [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]

## What He Can Do

Observed in one short fight:

- **Summons beings from outside Astrodia.** He called **[[Cocytus]]** — an elemental of ice and running water — out of a sigil in mid-air, onto a rooftop.
- **He cannot fully control what he summons.** By his own admission: *"I'm still getting used to working with magic in this way. I can't control everything that I summon."*
- **Teleportation.** He stepped straight out of [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s grapple onto a second-story roof.
- **Flight.** He left the fight by casting **Fly** on himself and going 120 feet up and away in two turns. Nobody could reach him.

He never once attacked the party. He summoned, watched, and left.

## What the Adventurers Say He Did

The group cornering him — a captain with a **pistol**, a mage, a swashbuckler, and a druid — had hired him to help explore **a set of ruins**. Their account:

- At the end of the dungeon he **took the loot they were after.**
- They were ambushed by whatever haunted the temple. He **summoned something** to fight it off, and then **left.**
- The summon killed the haunts, then **turned on them.** It killed **two of their friends** and only lost interest before killing the rest.

His response was an apology, sincere and useless, followed by an attempt to walk away. **The ruins have not been identified, and neither has the loot he took.**

## He Knows What Howl Is

This is the part that matters.

**[[Howl]]**, [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s owl, sensed him from across the city, screamed, and flew to the alley on her own. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s animal handling read her reaction not as fear but as **rage** — she is *furious* at this man.

Then Aleister looked at her properly, and **Howl shrank**: feathers hunched, eyes down, making herself as small as she could.

> *"Oh. **I do know what this is, then.**"*
> *"What is it?"*
> *"...What's your name?"*

He refused to answer. Told that Leif found her injured in the **[[Terget Forest|Purodetapo Forest]]**, he said:

> *"How interesting. The Purodetapo Forest, you say. **I'll have to make a visit there.**"*

That is Leif's home, and where his family lives. Leif tried to stop him with **Eldritch Blast** and discovered his arcane focus was in [[Madame Verre]]'s study. The fight that followed cost the party [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]'s first death saves and killed nobody who mattered to Aleister, who **escaped clean.**

> *"I just seem to make a mess of everything I do. I guess I'm still not ready."*

**Not ready for what is unstated.**

## Open Questions

1. **What is [[Howl]]?** He recognized it instantly and would not say. Howl's reaction to him — rage, then total submission — suggests recognition running both ways.
2. **Is he going to the [[Terget Forest|Purodetapo Forest]]?** He said so out loud, to the one person in the world who would object.
3. **Where do the things he summons come from?** Nothing in [[Astrodia]] explains [[Cocytus]] — which is the same problem [[The Fourth Magic]] poses, from a different direction.
4. **Whose side is he on?** Both, per the stories.
