**The Prophecy Destroyer** *(name as heard — unconfirmed)* is a figure thought to have been **an enemy of [[Pluto]]**, in some unknown capacity. Nothing else about it is known.

## Source

In the library beneath the **[[Temple of Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]], [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] read about Pluto's enemies. Most of what she found was **necromancers** and other things she already knew; the Prophecy Destroyer was the one exception. Who or what it was, when it lived, and why it opposed Pluto are **not established.** It's carried as an open thread.
