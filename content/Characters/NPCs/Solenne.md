**Solenne** *(heard at the table as "Celine" or "Selin"; spelling from the map token)* is the **new high priestess of the [[Temple of Nephthys]]**, anointed on the day [[The Fallen]] arrived. She is **a child**, *"just out of childhood,"* and was the second orphan the temple raised to its highest office. **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** was the first.

## Background

- She **arrived at the temple as an orphan** about **two years after Drolta left**, *"much like you did"* ([[Ysmene]]).
- **The temple raised her.** Ysmene was quick to put her on Drolta's path.
- *"I had nowhere else to go."*

## Session 13 — Homecoming (October 7, 2026)

**Anointed by [[Nephthara]]** in a public ceremony and then a private one. Drolta walked in during the private part. The worshippers outside thought she'd make a great high priestess: *"so young, but she really looks ready for the task."*

Her first hours in office: [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] tried to pull her aside for a blessing so the party could separate her from Nephthara. She declined politely after Nephthara reminded her **not to show favoritism**, and was then mobbed by worshippers. [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]], walking past with her charmed mentor: *"Just be firm with them."* *"Uh, okay. Who are you?"*

That evening she **shook Drolta's hand**:

> *"I've heard so many things about you. So many good things. It's good to finally meet you. I really hope I can do for the temple what you did. I'm gonna strive to make Nephthys proud, and I want to see her rise again from the ashes, and I will do what I can to make that happen."*
> *"I'm wishing you the best. It is an honor, and also a heavy load to bear."*

[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s pendant found **no undead** in her.

**When [[Lady Nectaria]] attacked**, Solenne **ran straight at the Fiendish Icons** that had gotten inside, with Nephthara shouting *"Solenne, no, don't!"*, and fought them with radiant flame and **Fire Bolt.** When one hit back, Nephthara burned it to nothing. *"Thank you."*
