Asha is a half-elf scholar from the Kingdom of [[Artmegía]] who was raised among oral historians and star-readers in the long shadow of [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]]. Gifted with an unsettling ability to remember fragments of knowledge she was never taught, she ignored her mother's warnings not to dig into pre-[[Severance]] lore and pursued forbidden cosmology at the Academy during a time of growing political scrutiny. While translating a damaged star-tablet tied to the [[White Forest]], Asha spoke a name meant to be erased, and something answered—not with power, but with recognition. Her research was quietly denounced, her mentor recanted, and her academic record was expunged to protect the institution. When she returned home, her mother had vanished, leaving behind only a note that read, "I told you the truth would cost you." Now exiled from both Academy and home, Asha wanders [[Astrodia]] bound to an obsolete cosmic truth that remembers her, seeking to understand what was buried after the Severance—and whether some truths should remain forgotten.

## Session Events

### Session 0
Met [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] on the road about a day west of [[Bayhaven]] — she screamed upon seeing him but he calmed her with his unexpectedly rich voice. She offered to guide him to [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]] in exchange for a travel companion. At a small village, Asha was recognized by an old acquaintance despite trying to hide — a villager who knew her as a child. She successfully deceived (DC 15) the woman into thinking they were taking "the scenic route." The woman mentioned she'd lost touch with Asha's mother.

### Session 1
At the village, Asha searched for signs of her mother (investigation 18)—no leads, but she noticed the town was preparing for a festival or marketplace.

Asha explained to the group that in the [[Solemn Pantheon|Solemn and Beyond]] cosmology, the [[Agents]] are like "the hands of God"—each managing a domain. Believing in one Agent essentially means believing in all of them.

Before bed, Asha searched her **mother's notebook** prompted by [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red's]] dream about three spheres. She rolled a **Nat 20** and found a **triangle of orbs** drawn in the notebook, colored red, blue, and purple, with the word **"[[Venus]]"** written next to it. She has not shared this discovery with the group.

Asha rolled **insight on [[Lucian Cugg]]**—he seems genuine about being robbed, but she suspects he's understating how valuable the [[Golden Chalice]] actually is.

Asha overheard [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif's]] conversation with the potion seller about [[Professor Lang]] at [[Witherbloom College]].

In combat, Asha landed a max-damage **Firebolt** that charred a goblin to death instantly, and contributed quarterstaff attacks against the bandits.

### Session 3
At [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]], Asha tried to sign a fake name on the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] disclosure form, but the enchanted pen and paper revealed her **true name**. She rolled poorly on deception; fortunately [[Ms. Maris]] didn't recognize her, though someone in the party noticed (DM private message).

Smoothed things over with [[Tobin]] after [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s failed intimidation/grapple. Got Tobin talking — he revealed the [[Golden Chalice|chalice]] matches one of several specific items the Initiative was told to watch for, called **"the Something Spoils"** (full name unknown). It's the first such item to come through, and there are 3 other item types on their watch list. Tobin also recognized Asha and knew about her academic trouble.

Asha mentioned the **Oracle of the Academy** — supposedly the most powerful being at the school. She noted the **3rd floor of the [[Biblioplex]]** is where she was caught previously.

### Session 4
Asha rolled a **nat 20 on history/arcana** (25 total) during the Giant Frog incident at the [[Fire Jolt Café]] and identified **[[Murgaxor]]** — an expelled Bullywug student — as the likely culprit behind both the mimic and frog incidents.

In the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], she used **Mage Hand** to raise an iron portcullis and used her **mother's notebook** reaction ability to subtract a d6 from a warg's escape roll. She killed two hobgoblins with **Fire Bolt** in critical moments — including the last one as it tried to alert goblins in the circular library.

**Mystery**: Asha had a **private supernatural event** during the night on the road to the library (DM private channel — unknown to other players). She also received a private DM message about something unusual with [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s Starry Wisp.

### Session 6
This was Asha's session. In the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], she cast **Detect Magic** as a ritual on the sentient axe ([[Leith Chantos's Legacy]]) and later **spotted the concealed wall vault** that the armoire key opened — revealing **2 Potions of Healing** and jars of **jade, gold, and ruby dust** (spell components). On returning to [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]] via teleportation circle, the party reached **Level 3**, and Asha **learned the Identify spell**.

That night was the session's single dream check; **everyone passed except Asha**, who was pulled aside privately by the DM. *(Asha's **private night event** remains unknown to the other players — no details shared.)*

**Central revelation:** at [[Myrna Nor|Myrna's]] safehouse in [[Lathen]], the **[[S.A.T.O.]] (Students Against Tyrannical Overreach)** revealed that the group **formed around Asha's own expulsion** — she had been expelled for her forbidden research into a [[White Forest]] artifact, which they consider an abuse of academic freedom. Their mission is to discover *why* the topic is forbidden and **clear Asha's name**. Asha alone **refused the drugged wine** in [[Pillardrop]], staying awake through the journey, and **vouched that the students were trustworthy** via her insight read on Myrna.

Simone's [[The Fourth Magic|map of magic]] showed a huge **divine spike in the [[White Forest]]**. **Tobin** revealed the Academy is hunting the **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]** — a woman in **black and red** matching **Asha's dream figure** (and [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s) — who appears connected to her. On her **missing mother**, Tobin and the S.A.T.O. agreed to **watch for leads**. The party named themselves **[[The Fallen]]**, with their next destination the [[White Forest]] via the Quandrix campus and a [[Nephthys]] temple.

*(Kenyaita was absent for Session 7; Asha traveled with the party but stayed out of the spotlight.)*

### Session 8
Traveling north from [[Quandrix College]], Asha fought the **bandit ambush** in the woods — raining **Fire Bolt** and commanding her hawk familiar, **Astra**, to dive on the enemy — and used one of her **Portent** rolls to turn one of [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s misses into a hit (earning a **Bardic Inspiration** die in thanks).

**Her dreams took center stage.** Asha connected several recurring visions into a theory about the **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]]**: a **winged, taloned woman in black and red** holding the **[[Golden Chalice|chalice]]**, and a *second*, **godly woman robed all in white** who **drinks from the chalice** while holding a **book, a lance, and a white gown**. Tallying the objects — **chalice, lance, book, dress** — the party now suspects these *are* the Sinful Spoils. Crucially, **neither woman matches the Academy's wanted poster** of the Seeker. The party relayed the item list to [[Tobin]] via the [[Sending Stones]].

At **[[Freedale]]**, Asha investigated the town's buildings (spotting the private residence, the stable, and the tavern) and joined the break-in at **[[Morgana]]'s** wizard tower — though she and [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] were **left stranded below** when the climbing rope snapped. The party reached the town **17 days** out from the chalice's repossession.

### Session 9
**Asha's session, and the biggest personal development of the campaign so far.**

Inside **[[Morgana's Tower]]** she was the party's interpreter for everything magical. Running **Detect Magic** continuously, she found the **evocation cloak** in the third-floor chest that everyone else had dismissed as laundry (she checked whether it matched the robed figure from her dreams — it didn't), identified the fourth-floor storage room as a witch's **spell-component stock** worth up to **500 gp of material components**, and turned up **three spell scrolls** in the library: **Leomund's Tiny Hut**, **Sleet Storm**, and **Erupting Earth** (all 3rd level — held to transcribe once she can cast them at level 5). She also identified and **copied the runes of the roof's teleportation circle.**

**The orb corridor.** Wearing the cloak, Asha walked into the second-floor ring alone, **pacing herself with the patrolling orbs**, and forced open the inner door. An orb caught her with a **lightning bolt** — and the cloak revealed itself: **resistance to lightning damage**, halving the hit. Behind the door was the **scrying pool** from Morgana's journal, built so anyone Arcana-proficient may use it **once per day, free.**

**She found her mother.** Between how well she knows her and the **mother's notebook** she has carried since Session 1, the save was at **-9**, and it failed:

> Her mother is **alive**, in the **[[White Forest]]**, **tied to a tree** in a clearing near the forest's edge — beside a second captive, a **woman all in white with long blonde hair**, also bound.

The thread the [[S.A.T.O.]] promised to "watch for leads" on in Session 6 is now a **rescue with a location.** The party's answer was immediate: *"We're definitely going to save your mom, Asha."*

**The amulet.** In the basement, her Detect Magic caught a faint emanation from the dead witch's pocket — the **[[Guardian Command Amulet]]**. She **cast Identify on it for free** using the components found upstairs, walked up to the shield guardian holding it out, and **shut the construct down**. Her follow-up history check produced the session's other major revelation: the guardian is an **older model of the constructs posted throughout [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]]**, and since each amulet binds one guardian, the Academy must keep a **control room holding all of them.** She wears the amulet now.

She also **spotted the barred basement door** while the rest of the party was busy congratulating itself on sneaking past the guardian, and rounded out the night with a bad dream roll and a **library in Freedale she never got to enter.** Her hawk **Astra** landed the killing blow on the ghast.

*(Asha, like [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]], came out of the night with something she hasn't yet told the party.)*

### Session 10
**Asha was the party's eyes all session.** She **identified the [[Endymion Empire]]'s banner** — a sword crossed with an axe — off [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s eagle-scouted description when nobody else recognized it, placing the party inside Empire territory.

**At the market bookstall** her investigation turned up ***The Biography of [[Rellyona]]***, and a history check named her: **one of the [[Magistus]], and the woman who founded [[Pentamarone]].** Asha bought it together with a history of the Greater Endymion Empire, **haggling 30 silver down to 15.** She has **not read either yet.** She also priced armor at the blacksmith before being gently told that wizards can't cast in it, and bought an **ornate curved dagger for 2 gold** instead. She cast **Detect Magic** over [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s new glassware to be sure nothing had followed them; it was clean.

**At the [[WitchCrafters]] guild hall she ran the interrogation.** Her insight said not to let the [[Golden Chalice]] out of the party's hands, and her persuasion got them escorted back to [[Genni]] rather than waiting at the desk. She then cast **Detect Thoughts** on Genni — **surface only**, deliberately declining to probe deeper because Genni would have felt it and Asha wanted to keep her trust. The read came back clean: Genni genuinely didn't know, and was already planning to take it to **[[Madame Verre]].**

**She found the safe.** Walking the perimeter of the study, Asha spotted a **safe under Genni's desk bearing the symbol of the magic the [[Endymion Empire]] uses** — the same symbol on Genni's own staff. The party read it as guild-held [[Keystones]]; nobody has ruled out worse.

**She finally told the party about her vision** from the Session 9 rest: a figure matching **the Seeker's poster — white hair with red tips — hurling magic at a heavily built man with a long blond-grey beard in a whitish-yellow robe.** She reminded them she cannot tell past from present from future.

**And she drank from the chalice.** What appeared behind her was **her missing mother**, exactly as Asha last saw her in person — real to the touch, **blank, unrecognizing**, carrying nothing, everything taken from her disintegrating on removal. Asha **hugged her**, said ***"I will find you,"*** and walked out of the room.

She **failed her dream roll** and took **exhaustion**, with no dreams left in the bank to receive.

### Session 11
**Asha read the contract.** Before anyone signed the [[WitchCrafters]] agreement over the [[Golden Chalice]], she went through **[[Marka]]'s** draft line by line — *"we got burned with the fine print at the Academy"* — and her investigation came back clean. Given that the party's last signed document handed the chalice to the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]], this was hard-won caution.

She spent the research montage **in the archives with [[Genni]] and [[Marka]]**, hunting records of the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]] on **History** — with a **Portent** die behind it. *(She rolled a 20 and a 5; the 20 replaced a natural 1, and the 5 went unspent.)*

**She got Genni to talk about her past.** Asha raised the [[Endymion Empire]] symbol she'd spotted in Session 10 — on Genni's staff and on the safe under her desk — first with the other witches at the tavern, then with Genni directly. What came back: the triangle is the mark of the Empire's **[[Keystones|power stones]]**; Genni **grew up close to the Citadel** and learned magic on them; she **still has family there**; she **hasn't been back in a long time**; and she is **not related to the Emperor.** Then she changed the subject. Asha, who is herself in exile from an institution that erased her, let her.

She also tried to get [[Madame Verre]]'s anti-aging secret out of her and **failed the persuasion** — *"I like to be a girl's girl, but it's too good."*

**She has now read both of her books**, delivered privately by the DM and **not yet shared with the party**: the history of the Greater [[Endymion Empire]] on the first night, and ***The Biography of [[Rellyona]]*** on the third.

When [[Howl]] bolted, Asha **stayed at the guild hall with [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** to keep eyes on the chalice, and missed the fight with [[Cocytus]].
