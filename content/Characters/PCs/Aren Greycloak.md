Aren Greycloak is a human cleric of [[Pluto]], the [[Agents|Agent]] of Death. A former refugee who wandered the [[Endymion Empire]], she found faith after hearing a sermon about Pluto and was granted divine power—including the ability to detect undead via her pendant. She travels the land to rid the world of those who seek to extend life past their mortal time.

Uninterested in making friends, Greycloak understands the safety in numbers and is willing to team up for a time. She prays to her god every night, seeking answers to questions she refuses to ask aloud. For now, her holy rage is focused on cleansing the lands from necromancers and undead, following orders from her potential salvation.

She learned about [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy's]] College of Witherbloom, which teaches necromancy, and set out to investigate and possibly destroy it.

## Allies

**[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** - Aren's pendant detected a faint undead signature leading her to Drolta in a bustling city. After a tense confrontation, Drolta revealed her condition was involuntary and that she shares Aren's mission to destroy undead. They formed a cautious alliance. Both Aren and [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] now know Drolta's true nature as a dhampir.

## Session Events

### Session 0
In a bustling city, Aren's pendant detected a faint undead signature leading her to [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]. After a tense confrontation, Drolta revealed her condition was involuntary and that she shares Aren's mission to destroy undead. They formed a cautious alliance. Aren's religion check (16) confirmed that [[Nephthys]] and the Phoenix faith are compatible with [[Pluto]]'s doctrine. At the village inn, Aren physically restrained Drolta when her fangs became visible after [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] showed his neck scars.

### Session 1
Aren apologized privately to Drolta for dragging her into the group, but acknowledged the safety in numbers and connection to the Academy. Drolta offered to kill Aren if her curse ever takes over; Aren responded: *"You can try."* Aren expressed understanding of being burdened by unwanted curses and promised to help.

At camp, Aren shared her belief that the gods exist and hold sway over life, and it's important to recognize when they act through others.

In the spider den, Aren impulsively cast **Sacred Flame** on two wolf spiders on the ceiling—both [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] and Leif tried to restrain her but she rolled a Nat 20 and broke free. The spiders turned out to be already dead, riddled with crossbow bolts.

In combat, Aren healed [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] with Healing Word after he took a goblin arrow, and contributed Sacred Flame damage throughout. She cast **Light** on her mace for the cave exploration.

### Session 3
Followed [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] outside the [[Bow's End Tavern]] when her pendant detected a dhampir signature from [[Aurora Luna Wynterstarr|Aurora]]. Assumed Drolta was in danger, but the situation was explained and resolved peacefully.

Asked [[Lorehold]] apprentices about [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s dream of 3 orbs (red, blue, purple). They identified the figure as **[[Venus]]**, the Agent of Creation — one of the Hands of the [[Solemn Pantheon]]. Showed Red a textbook image.

### Session 4
Aren finished off a Giant Frog with **Poison Spray** at the [[Fire Jolt Café]]. In the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], she declined to attack a trapped warg, reasoning it was just an animal, not undead. She healed [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] with **Cure Wounds** for 7 HP during the hobgoblin fight.

### Session 6
In the spider-pit room of the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], **Aren's spider phobia triggered violently**. She **broke free of an attempted grapple on a Nat 20** and **opened with Sacred Flame before initiative was even rolled**. As a cleric of [[Pluto]], she healed allies through the fight against the giant spiders and the swarm. Afterward the party crossed the chasm to reach [[Una]]'s grave.

The party reached **Level 3** on returning to [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]]. Aren accompanied the group to [[Myrna Nor|Myrna's]] safehouse in [[Lathen]], where the [[S.A.T.O.]] and the mystery of [[The Fourth Magic|the fourth magic]] were revealed, and the party took the name **[[The Fallen]]**.

### Session 7
- On [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s warlock reveal: doubted **any cleric could heal a warlock pact** (*"a divine deal"*), but held **no objection to Leif himself** — *"he's fully living... he's not broken."* Her enmity is reserved for the undead, who pervert the boundary between life and death.
- Stayed **cryptic about her own deity** when [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] guessed she serves [[Pluto]] — *"no real need to concern yourself with that."* (Her devotion to [[Pluto]], Agent of Death, remains known to the reader but not openly confirmed to the party.)
- A native of the **[[Endymion Empire]]**, she supplied the party's knowledge of **[[Pentamarone]]** and the **[[WitchCrafters]]**, speculating they harness the fourth magic to resist the Empire.
- Recognized the winged statue of **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth, the Agent of Mysteries]]** at the [[Lathen]] temple and paid respects.
- Openly floated **destroying the vampire dean [[Professor Valentine]]** at [[Witherbloom College]]; the party deferred the trip. Dropped ominous hints of personal experience with **eternal life** (*"it's really not \[all you think]... from what I've seen"*), fueling the party's running "portrait in a basement" joke.

### Session 8
- **Her spider phobia collided with [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s new form.** When Red (now **Circle of the Moon**) wild-shaped into a **giant spider** during the bandit ambush, Aren repeatedly hurled **Sacred Flame at her own ally** — *"abominations should not be tolerated"* — even after being told, repeatedly, that the spider was Red. She refused every reassurance, and on the road afterward gave Red nothing but *"Spiders are abominations. They must be dealt with,"* declining to explain the root of it. (See Sessions 0 & 6 for the phobia's history.)
- Fought the ambush otherwise as the party's healer, keeping **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** up (*"sisters in arms"*) and cutting down the fleeing **Cutthroat** with Sacred Flame. She **noticed the bandit Boss fled with purpose** — straight toward someone waiting in the woods.
- At **[[Freedale]]**, she used her pendant to **Detect Undead** at the wizard's tower and sensed a **strong undead presence buried beneath it** — *"there's something unnatural beneath the earth here."* She and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] were **left stranded below** when the climbing rope snapped.
### Session 9
**Aren ran the undead half of the session, and it was the closest thing she's had to a holy mission.**

Stranded at the tower's front door with [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]], she failed the rope climb twice before [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] hauled her up in a one-man tug of war. Once inside, she was the one who **sensed necromancy** through the barred basement door and volunteered to open it: *"I'm opening it, because I will not regret killing whatever's in there."*

In the basement she opened with **Sacred Flame**, summoned a **Spiritual Weapon** for the first time, and took the ghast's **leg off at the knee** with it. She **barred doors behind the party** as they advanced, and re-barred the basement on the way out — nobody knows how the ghouls got in. When her amulet finally went quiet she called it: *"We have cleansed this place, as is our holy duty."*

**Her religion check identified the roof shrine** — nine winged statues, nine [[Agents]] — picking out [[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]], Saturn, [[Venus]], and [[Pluto]] (she spent her inspiration to get there). She then stopped mid-dungeon-crawl to **pray at Pluto's statue**, and later paused again at a door to **ask Pluto for strength** before opening it.

Her most characteristic beat came last. On finding **[[Morgana]]'s corpse**, she ran a **medicine check** that read the remnants of a **prolonged magical battle** across the body — establishing the witch **died fighting** — and then **spoke last rites over her** so she might rest.

### Session 10
**Aren asked the question that cracked the campaign's longest-running mystery.** After five months of failed Identifies on the [[Golden Chalice]] — and while sitting in the [[WitchCrafters]] library with [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s new [[Alchemy Jug]] pouring free beer — she pointed out that *a chalice is technically a cup*, and asked what would happen if someone drank from it. Red filled it and handed it over.

**She drank first.** What appeared, all over the guild library, was **cats.** Solid, warm, real to the touch — and completely inert; held up by the tail, one simply lets it happen. She was delighted, and immediately protective of them (she blocked [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s attempt to bowl one cat into another).

She also **called time on the experiment** — after Úlfr punched a conjured horse hard enough to explode it across the room, Aren announced *"I think I'm done here"* and made for the door, which is how the party ended up paralyzed in the courtyard by **[[Madame Verre]]**.

Elsewhere in the session: she spent the market **listening rather than shopping**, and overheard a passerby praising a service at a **Temple of [[Venus]]** in [[Pentamarone]] — she intends to pay her respects, and hasn't yet. *(The party visited the [[Temple of Venus]] together in Session 12.)* She was the one who suggested asking the guild for a paid **detect magic / identify** service in the first place, offered to sponsor [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s room out of party funds when he tried to sleep on the boat deck, and vetoed the idea of robbing [[Genni]]'s keystone safe (*"before we start getting violent and stealing things..."*). She **rested well** and took no exhaustion.

### Session 11
**Aren invented the party's first legal department.** When [[Madame Verre]] proposed borrowing the [[Golden Chalice]] for an undisclosed purpose, Aren's condition was that the party's warning about the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] go **in writing** — an acknowledgment of disclosed danger, and no liability. Verre loved it (*"that's fair, and it makes it quite interesting"*), [[Marka]] drafted it, and the table's verdict was *"a cleric and a lawyer."* Aren also pushed for an explicit **guarantee of no harm**, which is in the contract.

She spent the research montage **in the books with [[Genni]] and [[Marka]]**, using **Insight** to sort genuinely useful sources from noise.

**She asked after a temple to [[Pluto]]** in [[Pentamarone]]. There isn't one — the city worships [[Venus]] almost exclusively. Two cities running, Aren has found a Venus temple and no Pluto.

**And she was right about [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]].** When he came out of Verre's office having handed over his spellcasting focus for nothing more than a promise, Aren's reaction was immediate:

> *"Be wary of what she might request of you."*
> *"In the future, young one, you should be clear when your weapon is no longer with you."*

**She dragged him out and bought him a dagger**, dismissing his magic stones as *"just holding some rocks"* — the most she has ever spoken to him, which visibly rattled him. **Two mornings later that dagger was the only weapon he had** when [[Aleister the Invoker]] appeared. He thanked her for it in front of the whole party; she promised to pick him up in a hug next time, and that he would hate it.

She also, in the party huddle, floated using her **undead-detection amulet on Madame Verre** — a child who has been a child for a very long time. **She has not done it yet.** *(She did in Session 12: **no undead.** See below.)*

Aren was among those who **stayed at the guild hall** to guard the chalice when [[Howl]] bolted, on the theory that a bird running is exactly what a diversion looks like, and so **missed the fight with [[Cocytus]] entirely.** Tommy has promised her and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] their own scene to open the next session.

*(Running gag of record: Aren is not reasonable about spiders. If [[Madame Verre]] turns out to be one, Aren has committed to divine smiting her on sight.)*

### Session 12
**Aren finally got her own scene, and came out of it having said more than in the rest of the campaign combined.** See [[Session 12 Recap — Something from Nothing]].

**She told the [[WitchCrafters]] her mission**, or as much of it as she ever tells anyone. When [[Marka]] asked what had brought her to the group:

> *"I'm on a mission to cleanse this world of the abominations that are undead."*
> *"A mission from who?"*
> *"You needn't concern yourself with that... It is a holy mission."*

*"We love a girl with a mission."* The city has never had undead trouble, but the guild has heard tall tales of **haunted ruins or a haunted mansion** nearby and promised to keep an ear out.

**She asked what keeps the Empire off [[Pentamarone]].** [[Genni]] gave Verre's version: [[Rellyona]] and the Emperor parted with deep mutual respect after the [[Great Cataclysm]], and he has never encroached, to honor her legacy. Aren wasn't persuaded:

> *"We shall see how long such respect lasts. You never know what's in the hearts of men."*

**She noticed Genni perk up at the word "monster"** when the party described **[[Cocytus]]**, and **followed her** when she slipped off to tell [[Madame Verre]]. Her **Guidance** helped [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] talk his way in behind them. Later, Úlfr passed her a lead from [[The Sundered Watch]]: **undead-infested ruins far to the north.**

**She wouldn't take the dagger back.** When [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] tried to return it:

> *"Keep it. It's always best to have at least a secondary weapon. More is preferable, though... Never leave yourself defenseless."*
> *"Safety is an illusion."*
> *"As long as you do not continuously repeat mistakes, it's okay."*

Leif admitted that when his Eldritch Blast fizzled in the alley, *"I felt the spirit of Aren telling me, I told you so."*

**She kept working the archives on Insight** through the last days of the montage, and sat on the **history** team for the report.

**The cats were put down.** The guild explained, apologetically, that the copies aren't technically alive. **Aren was visibly upset** and got even quieter than usual.

**She checked Madame Verre at last.** When she went with Leif to Verre's office the next morning and stepped out for his private conversation, she **quietly grasped her pendant** on the way out:

> **No undead.**

She waited by the door anyway, since she doesn't trust Leif alone. Then **Verre asked for her**, and she went in alone *(played out privately)*. What she shared afterward:

- **Madame Verre is not undead**, in the necromantic sense. Her condition is **the result of an experiment in eternal youth** that didn't turn out as intended. *"A simple accident. Which we'll see how long it remains an accident."*
- It isn't frozen time. It's more like **the hands of the clock were wound back.** She is still aging.
- Aren now wants to visit **[[Marseille]]** *(heard at the table as "Marcio"; possibly where [[Marka]] studied)*: *"I know interesting ways of magic there."* It may hold something useful against her undead problem, though she wouldn't say more.

Leif was delighted. *"The shell is cracking."*

**Her fear about the Spoils.** When [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s religion checks produced the theory that each of the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoils]] mocks one Agent, **Aren became very concerned** that one might rebuke **her god**, [[Pluto]], and about what a rebuke of death would look like: *unnatural, eternal life.* That pointed the party straight back at Verre.

**At the [[Temple of Venus]]** she read the entry-hall scriptures with Red, and when the priest described **[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]**, **Aren had heard of her before.** One or two believers turn up in many of the churches she's visited: an **angel, a woman**, exiled to earth for something that angered the gods, whose exile provoked the Morning Star's rebellion. When the temple library had nothing on her: *"You don't need to have evidence to have faith."*

In the library she **read about Pluto's enemies**. It was mostly necromancers and things she already knew, with one exception: **[[The Prophecy Destroyer]]** *(as heard)*, an enemy of Pluto in some unknown capacity.

### Session 13
**Aren went to a temple of the Phoenix Queen and got her undead.** See [[Session 13 Recap — Homecoming]].

**She stopped the party pushing [[Genni]].** When the table kept prying after [[Haine]] revealed Genni had been the Emperor's right-hand assistant: *"Not all stories are meant to be told at this time."* Genni thanked her, and then told them a little herself.

**At the [[Temple of Nephthys]]** she studied the architecture against temples she knows and quietly **checked every entrance** for how vampires got in. On a strong Investigation she found **two doors leading into the mountainside** (unlikely entry points through rock), **no sign of forced entry**, and **graves** through a window. Her prayer for them: *"I hope that they are at peace within the afterlife."* She does want them to stay dead. To [[Ysmene]]: *"I admire the work you've done to recover from such devastation... It will not be done in vain."*

**Her pendant, twice.** When [[Solenne]] and [[Nephthara]] returned, she checked: **no undead but Drolta.** At sunset, on a bad feeling, she checked again, and it **pinged outside**. **[[Lady Nectaria]]** was standing about a hundred feet away. *"There's an undead out there."* She went out to meet her, careful not to invite anything in.

**[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] was looking at Aren** when she relayed what [[Nephthara]] had told her: the undead are **bound by some sort of contract**, and certain relics, including **robes of [[Nephthys]]**, can **break those bonds.**

**In the fight** she summoned **Spiritual Weapon** (a sword) and rolled max damage on her first strike. It destroyed two **Fiendish Icons**. She healed herself with Cure Wounds and spent her **Human inspiration** on a Guiding Bolt at Nectaria that missed.

**A bell rang out of her.** When an icon's fiery eruption caught her, she used her reaction on a **new feature tied to her backstory**, a d20 roll on a table of effects *(name unclear on the recording)*. It came up 11: a sound like a **church bell tolling** and a flash of light, and **she and the icon were both stunned.** She had never seen it happen before.

At session's end, undead were coming out of the woods. *"Y'all wanted undead."* She did not want *these*.
