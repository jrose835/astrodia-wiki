Aren Greycloak is a human cleric of [[Pluto]], the [[Agents|Agent]] of Death. A former refugee who wandered the [[Endymion Empire]], she found faith after hearing a sermon about Pluto and was granted divine power—including the ability to detect undead via her pendant. She travels the land to rid the world of those who seek to extend life past their mortal time.

Uninterested in making friends, Greycloak understands the safety in numbers and is willing to team up for a time. She prays to her god every night, seeking answers to questions she refuses to ask aloud. For now, her holy rage is focused on cleansing the lands from necromancers and undead, following orders from her potential salvation.

She learned about [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy's]] College of Witherbloom, which teaches necromancy, and set out to investigate and possibly destroy it.

Both Aren and [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] now know Drolta's true nature as a dhampir.

## Session Events

### Session 0
In a bustling city, Aren's pendant detected a faint undead signature leading her to [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]. After a tense confrontation, Drolta revealed her condition was involuntary and that she shares Aren's mission to destroy undead. They formed a cautious alliance. Aren's religion check (16) confirmed that [[Nephthys]] and the Phoenix faith are compatible with [[Pluto]]'s doctrine. At the village inn, Aren physically restrained Drolta when her fangs became visible after [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] showed his neck scars.

### Session 1
Aren apologized privately to Drolta for dragging her into the group, but acknowledged the safety in numbers and connection to the Academy. Drolta offered to kill Aren if her curse ever takes over; Aren responded: *"You can try."* Aren expressed understanding of being burdened by unwanted curses and promised to help.

At camp, Aren shared her belief that the gods exist and hold sway over life, and it's important to recognize when they act through others.

In the spider den, Aren impulsively cast **Sacred Flame** on two wolf spiders on the ceiling—both [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] and Leif tried to restrain her but she rolled a Nat 20 and broke free. The spiders turned out to be already dead, riddled with crossbow bolts.

In combat, Aren healed [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] with Healing Word after he took a goblin arrow, and contributed Sacred Flame damage throughout. She cast **Light** on her mace for the cave exploration.

### Session 3
Followed [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] outside the [[Bow's End Tavern]] when her pendant detected a dhampir signature from [[Aurora Luna Wynterstarr|Aurora]]. Assumed Drolta was in danger, but the situation was explained and resolved peacefully.

Asked [[Lorehold]] apprentices about [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]]'s dream of 3 orbs (red, blue, purple). They identified the figure as **[[Venus]]**, the Agent of Creation — one of the Hands of the [[Solemn Pantheon]]. Showed Red a textbook image.

### Session 4
Aren finished off a Giant Frog with **Poison Spray** at the [[Fire Jolt Café]]. In the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], she declined to attack a trapped warg, reasoning it was just an animal, not undead. She healed [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] with **Cure Wounds** for 7 HP during the hobgoblin fight.

### Session 6
In the spider-pit room of the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], **Aren's spider phobia triggered violently**. She **broke free of an attempted grapple on a Nat 20** and **opened with Sacred Flame before initiative was even rolled**. As a cleric of [[Pluto]], she healed allies through the fight against the giant spiders and the swarm. Afterward the party crossed the chasm to reach [[Una]]'s grave.

The party reached **Level 3** on returning to [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]]. Aren accompanied the group to [[Myrna Nor|Myrna's]] safehouse in [[Lathen]], where the [[S.A.T.O.]] and the mystery of [[The Fourth Magic|the fourth magic]] were revealed, and the party took the name **[[The Fallen]]**.

### Session 7
- On [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s warlock reveal: doubted **any cleric could heal a warlock pact** (*"a divine deal"*), but held **no objection to Leif himself** — *"he's fully living... he's not broken."* Her enmity is reserved for the undead, who pervert the boundary between life and death.
- Stayed **cryptic about her own deity** when [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] guessed she serves [[Pluto]] — *"no real need to concern yourself with that."* (Her devotion to [[Pluto]], Agent of Death, remains known to the reader but not openly confirmed to the party.)
- A native of the **[[Endymion Empire]]**, she supplied the party's knowledge of **[[Pentamarone]]** and the **[[WitchCrafters]]**, speculating they harness the fourth magic to resist the Empire.
- Recognized the winged statue of **[[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth, the Agent of Mysteries]]** at the [[Lathen]] temple and paid respects.
- Openly floated **destroying the vampire dean [[Professor Valentine]]** at [[Witherbloom College]]; the party deferred the trip. Dropped ominous hints of personal experience with **eternal life** (*"it's really not \[all you think]... from what I've seen"*), fueling the party's running "portrait in a basement" joke.

### Session 8
- **Her spider phobia collided with [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]]'s new form.** When Red (now **Circle of the Moon**) wild-shaped into a **giant spider** during the bandit ambush, Aren repeatedly hurled **Sacred Flame at her own ally** — *"abominations should not be tolerated"* — even after being told, repeatedly, that the spider was Red. She refused every reassurance, and on the road afterward gave Red nothing but *"Spiders are abominations. They must be dealt with,"* declining to explain the root of it. (See Sessions 0 & 6 for the phobia's history.)
- Fought the ambush otherwise as the party's healer, keeping **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]]** up (*"sisters in arms"*) and cutting down the fleeing **Cutthroat** with Sacred Flame. She **noticed the bandit Boss fled with purpose** — straight toward someone waiting in the woods.
- At **[[Freedale]]**, she used her pendant to **Detect Undead** at the wizard's tower and sensed a **strong undead presence buried beneath it** — *"there's something unnatural beneath the earth here."* She and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] were **left stranded below** when the climbing rope snapped.
### Session 9
**Aren ran the undead half of the session, and it was the closest thing she's had to a holy mission.**

Stranded at the tower's front door with [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]], she failed the rope climb twice before [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] hauled her up in a one-man tug of war. Once inside, she was the one who **sensed necromancy** through the barred basement door and volunteered to open it: *"I'm opening it, because I will not regret killing whatever's in there."*

In the basement she opened with **Sacred Flame**, summoned a **Spiritual Weapon** for the first time, and took the ghast's **leg off at the knee** with it. She **barred doors behind the party** as they advanced, and re-barred the basement on the way out — nobody knows how the ghouls got in. When her amulet finally went quiet she called it: *"We have cleansed this place, as is our holy duty."*

**Her religion check identified the roof shrine** — nine winged statues, nine [[Agents]] — picking out [[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Earth]], Saturn, [[Venus]], and [[Pluto]] (she spent her inspiration to get there). She then stopped mid-dungeon-crawl to **pray at Pluto's statue**, and later paused again at a door to **ask Pluto for strength** before opening it.

Her most characteristic beat came last. On finding **[[Morgana]]'s corpse**, she ran a **medicine check** that read the remnants of a **prolonged magical battle** across the body — establishing the witch **died fighting** — and then **spoke last rites over her** so she might rest.
