Drolta Amara Tzuentes—the Crimson Priestess—is a dhampir warlock whose amber-gold eyes burn with divine fire and dangerous secrets. She was a High Priestess of [[Nephthys]] the Phoenix, reciting the prayers for her Goddess, healing the broken until ancient vampires led by **[[Lady Nectaria]]** attacked her temple seeking something still unknown, killing the children in her care—including a little girl she loved like a daughter. Drolta destroyed them with holy flame and nearly died herself, but Nephthys transformed her into something between living and dead, binding her to a mission, her Pact of the Blade.

Now she wanders [[Astrodia]] hunting vampires, wielding the power bestowed onto her by the Phoenix Queen. She carries a scar on her neck from the attack and occasionally feels a psychic "tapping"—possibly Lady Nectaria trying to reach her mind. Nephthys' protection may be blocking this connection.

She says she's protecting the world, but late at night, while she quietly prays the evening liturgies, she wonders if some truths are too heavy to hold, and if the Phoenix Queen chose the right warrior.

## Allies

**[[Aren Greycloak]]** - A cleric of [[Pluto]] whose pendant detected Drolta's undead nature. After a tense confrontation, they formed a cautious alliance based on their shared mission to destroy undead abominations.

**[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** - Leif confronted Drolta about her fangs. She revealed her dhampir nature to him, and they agreed to keep it between them for now.

## Session Events

### Session 0
At the village inn, Drolta nearly lost control when [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] showed his neck scars from a carnivorous plant attack. Her fangs became momentarily visible before Aren physically restrained her. Leif clearly saw the fangs. That night, Drolta snuck out to feed on a farm sheep, successfully making it look like a wolf attack.

### Session 1
At the campfire, Drolta shared the story of the Phoenix Queen rising from the ashes, revealing to the group that she was a high priestess at [[Nephthys|the Phoenix Queen's]] temple and that she and the Queen still have a relationship.

Drolta privately told [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] she would kill Aren if Aren's curse ever takes over. Aren responded: *"You can try."*

Leif confronted Drolta about her fangs. She teased him—circling him and pinching his cheeks—before revealing she is a **dhampir**, not a full vampire. She asked him to keep it secret and they agreed to let the others maintain their wrong assumptions. She called him "little one."

In combat, Drolta opened against the bandits with Eldritch Blast and demanded the chalice's location. Against the goblins, she used her Hexblade pact weapon (Phoenix Queen's feather—bladed claws) and Hex. She cast **Detect Magic** on the [[Golden Chalice]], discovering it radiates all schools of magic simultaneously—highly unusual and powerful.

That night in the cave, Drolta snuck out to commune privately with the Phoenix Queen. She also let the last fleeing bandit on the road escape, saying: *"Mercy."*

### Session 3
At the [[Bow's End Tavern]], Drolta spotted a woman playing lute in the corner — **[[Aurora Luna Wynterstarr|Aurora]]**, a born dhampir wearing [[Witherbloom College|Witherbloom]] robes. This was the first time either had met a dhampir of the other type (born vs. turned). Aurora shared that **[[Professor Valentine]]** is a full vampire and one of the deans of Witherbloom — a sangromancer who denounced the higher vampires and serves as Aurora's mentor. Aurora confirmed vampire assassins still occasionally target Valentine for defecting.

[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] followed them outside, sensing the dhampir presence with her pendant and assuming Drolta was in danger. Tense moment, but Drolta explained and it resolved peacefully. The group exchanged contact info with Aurora (she can use Sending).

Drolta also **failed a Wisdom saving throw** during the night at the [[University Inn]], though the specific effects were not revealed.

### Session 4
During the Giant Frog fight at the [[Fire Jolt Café]], Drolta was **swallowed whole** by a frog before [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] freed her with Ice Knife. While grappled by another frog, she used a **vampiric bite on its tongue** to heal herself for 4 HP — **[[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] saw her do it** and reacted with shock: *"What the fuck? What tarnation?"*

In the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], she dealt the killing blow to a brown bear and killed a hobgoblin with Chill Touch. She summoned her pact weapon — "The Broom of the Phoenix Queen."

**Mystery**: Drolta received a private DM message after [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s Starry Wisp flew past her head during combat (Arcana check: 17). Something about his magic is unusual.

### Session 6
In the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], Drolta missed a platform jump and fell into the webs of the spider pit — then went on to **eradicate the entire spider swarm in a single flaming-claw strike** ("a thousand blades"). At [[Myrna Nor|Myrna's]] safehouse in [[Lathen]], Simone confirmed (by commanding her) that **Drolta is a Warlock**. She also recognized that **[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** is a fellow warlock when the green [[The Fourth Magic|fourth-magic]] sand crawled toward him; she **offered to help him**, but warned she **can't come between him and his patron** if one exists.

Drolta joined the newly-named party, **[[The Fallen]]**, and allied with the **[[S.A.T.O.]]**. The party's next move is to carry the [[Golden Chalice]] toward the [[White Forest]], stopping en route at a **temple to [[Nephthys]]** — a higher-being hotspot of deep personal significance to Drolta, near the site where [[Lady Nectaria]]'s vampires attacked her temple. **If those attackers remain in the area, the party means to hunt them.**

The party reached **Level 3**.

### Session 7
- As a fellow warlock, **reassured [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]** about his patron — *"it depends on your relationship with them... it's not always bad."*
- Joined [[Harlan "Red" Reed|Red]] and Leif in **trying to break into a locked upstairs room** at Myrna's beach house (her lockpicking failed); later **apologized to [[Myrna Nor|Myrna]]** at dinner (Persuasion with advantage, backed by Red and [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]), smoothing things over.
- Offered to **ask her patron, [[Nephthys]], for information about [[The Whispering One]]** — the name [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] gave Leif's patron.
- Shared more of her past en route planning: she was a **high priestess** at the [[Nephthys]] temple until [[Lady Nectaria]]'s vampires attacked it searching for *something*; she's been seeking the truth ever since. The party set the temple as their next destination (~6 days from [[Quandrix College]]).
- Still intends to seek out the vampire dean **[[Professor Valentine]]** at [[Witherbloom College]] as a source on vampires and the [[Severance]] — deferred for now.

### Session 8
- At the Level 3 reintroductions, Drolta re-told her origin — orphan, **High Priestess of [[Nephthys]]**, the vampire massacre, her rebirth as a **dhampir** — and confirmed she now wields **celestial-patron warlock** healing. **Only [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] and [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] know her true nature**; [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] still does not.
- Her **[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|history roll]]** identified the midway town of **[[Freedale]]** and its devastation by the [[White Forest]] curse.
- In the **bandit ambush**, she worked **Inflict Wounds, Hex, and Mind Sliver**, taking heavy dagger damage but finishing the **Muscle** alongside Úlfr; [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] repeatedly healed her (*"sisters in arms"*).
- **A haunting dream:** someone she **deeply loved at the [[Nephthys]] temple**, now with **blood-red eyes and fangs.** She confided to [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] her fear that **vampires may still hold the temple** they're approaching, and that the homecoming will be *"a little rough."* She resolved to warn the group, and to ask [[Nephthys]] about Leif's [[The Whispering One|patron]].
- Her **dhampir agility** let her free-climb to the tower's third-floor balcony and drop the rope for the others at **[[Morgana]]'s** tower.
### Session 9
**Drolta's dhampir mobility solved the tower's hardest obstacle.** When the fourth-floor statues incinerated a rolled barrel with crossing cones of fire, and Bama proved the trap was floor-triggered, Drolta **walked up the wall and across the ceiling** over it — *"vampire shit"* — and opened the far door into [[Morgana]]'s workroom, where she found the **potions** (still unidentified) and the abjuration glyphs chalked on the floor. She made the crossing repeatedly as the party worked the floor.

In the basement she was one half of the warlock pair that ended the first fight almost immediately — her **Eldritch Blast killed the bloodied ghoul** outright, and later the second ghoul as well — followed by a triple fist bump with [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]].

She also, unknowingly, provided the party's exit. Her **plume** — the Phoenix Queen's feather from the [[Nephthys]] temple — served as **[[Archibald]]'s associated object** for the teleport, letting him target a temple he had never seen and land the party **a full day early.**

Which means Drolta reaches her homecoming **sooner than she expected**, with her vampire dream still hanging over it. *"I just want to get to the temple and get traumatized."*

### Session 10
*(Yemko was out; the DM ran Drolta.)*

Drolta's contribution to the misfired teleport was to confirm the obvious as soon as the party stepped out of the circle: ***"This is definitely not where my temple is."*** She had been the one whose **plume** [[Archibald]] used as the associated object, which makes the miss faintly personal.

Otherwise she travelled quietly with the party through [[Pentamarone]]. **Her homecoming at the [[Nephthys]] temple is deferred a second time**, and the party is now further from it than they were at the end of Session 9.

### Session 11
*(Yemko back at the table after missing Session 10.)*

Drolta worked the chalice research with **[[Seed]] and [[Ceramia]]** on the hardest of the four questions — **are the conjured living copies actually alive** — after a spirited table argument that **Deception** was the correct skill for the job (*"aren't undead things deceptive in nature?"*). She settled for **Investigation.**

**Then she took the campaign's first death saves.**

In the alley fight against **[[Cocytus]]**, she used her **spider climb** to scale a wall for line of sight and landed a **Guiding Bolt** on **[[Aleister the Invoker]]** through three-quarters cover — one of only two hits anyone managed on him. The elemental came down off the roof and hit her with a slam for **28 damage.**

She went down. **She failed her first death save** — rolled privately to the DM, at her request — before **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** burned his last spell slot on **Healing Word** and brought her back up for 8. She spent her next turn standing and going straight back at it: *"it fucked me up, boy."*

*(Her [[Nephthys]] temple homecoming is deferred for a third session running.)*
