Harlan Reed was born on the edge of the [[Terget Forest]], down beneath them tall pines that
never quit whisperin’. Most folks knew the place as Longleaf Holler, but those that lived there,
really lived there, called it Awen. Home.
His parents, Silas and Annabelle Reed, were farmers, same as near everyone else. Steady,
patient people. The kind that believed the land gives back what you put into it… eventually.
Harlan grew up barefoot more often than not. Learned the woods long before he ever learned
his letters. Neighbors used to joke he’d wander off for hours and come back with stories nobody
else had heard yet. The name “Red” stuck with him early on, same as that red clay that was
always caked onto his feet.
Even as a boy, animals trusted him.
Once, a skittish mule wouldn’t settle for nobody, till Red came along. Chickens followed him
round without so much as a handful of feed. Another time, his folks found him sittin’ on the stoop
one mornin’, a bear restin’ her head on his left leg and a stag laid up on his right while he read
’em a story like it was the most natural thing in the world.
At first, folks said he just had farmin’ in his blood. When people asked him how he did it, he’d
only shrug and say, “They listen better when you don’t rush ’em.”
As he got older, it got harder to pretend nothin’ strange was goin’ on. Crops leaned toward him
when he walked the fields. Storms seemed to break softer near his land. Animals that had no
business hangin’ round farms started showin’ up on his.
Eventually, somebody said the word everyone’d been thinkin’. Druid.
Nobody made a fuss over it. Awen had druids before. They come from the land, take care of it
while they’re here, and then they go back to it when their time’s done.
Silas and Anna Reed lived long, full lives. Long enough to watch Harlan take on more and more
of the farm without ever havin’ to be told. Long enough to see him sittin’ on the porch at dusk,
talkin’ low to the goats like they was old friends. When they passed, it was peaceful, same as
most things are under them pines.
Harlan marked their resting place at the edge of the property with stones, laid alongside the
stones of their parents, and theirs before ’em. Took over the farm without ceremony. Life in
Awen kept movin’, just like it always had.
Red lived alone, but he was never by hisself.
Neighbors stopped by regular-like, for food or company or both. Animals wandered through his
fields same as they always had. Kids from the holler came to him with questions and advice,
even when they didn’t rightly know which they were askin’ for. When bandits drifted too close,
Red handled it quiet. When storms came, he was already there. When animals or crops took
sick, folks came to him first.
Red was generous to a fault. Believed a body was only as good as their word. Gave what he
had and never kept count.


## Session Events

### Session 0
Boarded a ship heading to [[Artmegía]] where he met [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]], bonding over their shared druidic practice. Offered Leif some homemade whiskey — Leif failed his Constitution check spectacularly. At the group meetup on the road, Red recognized [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] from working together on the farm — a joyful reunion that helped integrate the full party. At the village inn, Red participated in the "Staff Brothers" moment — four party members produced their staves and did a staff fist bump.

### Session 1
Found a passable campsite (decent nature check). Went hunting with [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]], [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]], and [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] — successfully brought back deer and vegetables.

At the campfire, Red told the story of an Academy student who years ago tried to enter the "old forest" near his village. Red got the student drunk at a party so he'd forget about going in, potentially saving his life. He hopes to find the student at the Academy. Shared his **recurring dream**: running with a pack of animals under a full moon, seeing **three spheres — purple, blue, and red** — making strange twists around the moon.

At [[Thornvale]], Red tried to fix a leaky roof (carpenter job) — rolled poorly and refused to accept payment for a job not done. Approached [[Lucian Cugg]] at the Silver Tankard: *"I don't like thievery. People work hard enough as it is."* Spotted a trip wire in the cave that would have hit Leif. Rolled a **Nat 20 on initiative** in the goblin lair and went first. Hit a bandit with Poison Spray. Stole one of [[Dilly]]'s remaining boots after combat.

### Session 3
Overheard [[Tobin]] whisper to [[Ms. Maris]]: *"Wasn't that a chalice? Wasn't that one of the—"* before she cut him off. Red immediately confronted them. Ms. Maris deflected, but the party's Insight checks confirmed she was hiding something.

At the [[Biblioplex]], asked [[Lorehold]] apprentices about his dream of 3 orbs. They identified it as connected to **[[Venus]]**, the Agent of Creation — one of the Hands of the [[Solemn Pantheon]]. They showed him a textbook image. After the mimic fight, received 1 platinum piece from [[Mavinda Sharpbeak|Professor Sharpbeak]] (possibly a bribe).

### Session 4
At the [[Fire Jolt Café]], heard frogs croaking in the back lounge, leading to the frog racing discovery. Cast **Speak with Animals** to coach the suspenders frog to victory (30 gold winnings). When the frogs transformed into Giant Frogs, cast **Animal Friendship** on the suspenders frog, making it docile: *"Don't hit this one!"* **Adopted the friendly frog as a pet** after the fight.

**Witnessed [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] bite a frog's tongue and drink its blood** during combat. Reacted with shock: *"What the fuck? What tarnation?"*

Gained **Wild Shape** at Level 2 (Druid). Available forms: giant frog, wolf, constrictor snake, panther. In the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]], scouted as a **panther** (+7 Stealth, Darkvision) — rolled a 23 on Stealth. Spotted the circular library with its glass-cased book through a cracked door. Dealt 9 damage to a goblin with Rend. Crit a hobgoblin for 10 damage.

After the bear fight, noticed something **unusual about how [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] casts Starry Wisp** — it looked different from other druids he'd seen. Asked Leif to cast it again to confirm. Received a private DM message about it.

### Session 6
At [[Myrna Nor|Myrna's]] dorm in [[Pillardrop]], Red's **survival check flagged that the celebratory wine had been tampered with** (the DM secretly warned him). Despite the warning, Red — true to his trusting nature — **drank anyway**. The wine was a sleeping draught used to travel the party undetected to Myrna's safehouse in [[Lathen]], where the **[[S.A.T.O.]]** and the mystery of [[The Fourth Magic|the fourth magic]] were revealed.

The party reached **Level 3**, and Red joined the others in adopting the name **[[The Fallen]]**.

### Session 7
- **Bound a raven familiar named Bama** via ritual — she and [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s owl **Howl** get along well.
- Held no grudge over [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s hidden warlock nature — *"still staff bros."*
- Curiosity got the better of him: he tried to **dismantle a locked door** in Myrna's beach house with his **carpenter's tools**, striking it so hard it rang a **deafening TANG through the whole house** and got the party caught by [[Myrna Nor|Myrna]] and the [[S.A.T.O.]]. His cover story (Deception at disadvantage) flopped.
- Floated a side-hustle: offered [[Myrna Nor|Myrna]]'s family samples of his home-brewed **whiskey, beer, and smoking leaf** to distribute through their shipping business.
- **The Moon thread deepened.** At the [[Lathen]] temple of the [[Earth (Agent of Mysteries)|Agent of Mysteries]], Red told the priest of his old dream of **[[Venus]]'s three orbs** (purple/blue/red) — but revealed the orbs hang **before the moon**, and that he's been having **recurring dreams of the moon itself**, which is partly why "things started happening" and he set out. The priest's read: **[[The Moon]]** — the disputed tenth Agent, protector of the pantheon — **may be reaching out to Red** as one of her chosen few. Red dropped a silver in the collection basket; the priest promised to research the Moon's worship.

### Session 8
- Reintroduced at Level 3 as a **Circle of the Moon** druid — a lot more Wild Shape incoming.
- **Sniffed out the ambush:** traveling with his raven **Bama** (plus Leif's owl Howl and Asha's hawk **Astra**) and having cast **Speak with Animals**, he caught a **faked bird-call** — a signal to hidden attackers — and sent Bama to scout, exposing the bandits before they struck.
- **Wild-shaped into a giant spider** and dominated the fight — his **poison bites finished both the Eyes and the Slicer** (*"That was for Bama"*) — but the form triggered **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s spider phobia**, and she spent much of the battle (and days afterward) treating him as a monster to be burned. Red tried repeatedly to reassure her (*"it's me, I'm your friend"*) and got nothing back but *"spiders are abominations."*
- Rolled a **Nat 20** on the night's dream check, gaining **Inspiration**. Chose horses over horse-theft at **[[Freedale]]** and climbed into **[[Morgana]]'s** tower with the others. Afterward he **swapped one of his Wild Shape forms** (dropping the giant toad option due to its swimming speed).

### Session 9
**Red's investigation roll opened the whole session.** On the tower's third floor he picked up the book on Morgana's writing table, rolled a **natural 20**, and recognized it as **[[Morgana]]'s journal** — plus the detail that mattered: *everything else in the room had been untouched for decades, but this book had been opened recently.* (*"Someone's here."*)

**Bama earned his keep repeatedly.** The raven scouted the ground floor and came back with the session's best line — *"Big metal guy, big metal guy"* — giving the party warning before the shield guardian rounded the corner. Later Bama proved the fourth-floor fire trap was **floor-triggered** by flying over it along the ceiling, then **ferried Morgana's papers out of the trapped workroom in his talons**, trip after trip, until [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] had the whole pile to read.

Red spent most of the session **wild shaped as a tiger** (with a [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] Bardic Inspiration behind the stealth retreat up the stairs), knocked a ghoul prone in the basement, and — unable to work a doorknob with paws — had to fetch Úlfr to open the roof door for him.

He also examined **[[Morgana]]'s body**, reading its ~two-decade decomposition against flesh eaten *within the last few days*, and **pulled the mask off her face with a retractable claw** to reveal the hollowed-out eye sockets beneath.

*(Notably, [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] tolerated the tiger without comment — "cool, there's a cat" — a marked thaw after the giant spider.)*

### Session 10
**Red's eagle ran reconnaissance for the whole first act.** **Bama** found the **[[Endymion Empire]] column** marching south under a sword-and-axe banner roughly 30–45 minutes behind the party, then went up a second time and caught the detail that mattered: at the crossroads, the column **split east and west** and stopped following the road south. His **survival roll then carried the entire party** cross-country through the woods, keeping the slower members on pace.

**He talked them through the gate.** After [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s attempt to lie to the guards collapsed, Red stepped in with the truth — armed forces on the road behind them, and all anyone wanted was a night off the hard ground — and got the party into [[Pentamarone]]. His read afterward was pure Red: *"These guards don't seem too concerned with safety... I don't think we'll be safe here if that army comes. We protect ourselves."*

**Then he bought the [[Alchemy Jug]].** He went to a pottery stall asking after brewing supplies; the potter liked the cut of his jib and produced a WitchCrafter-assisted jug that pours **four gallons of beer** on command. **50 gold**, no hesitation. He said *"beer"* at the dockside tavern and has not paid for a drink since — and the jug is, indirectly, the reason the party solved the [[Golden Chalice]], since it put a magic cup on the table next to a mundane one and prompted [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s question. Red **rinsed the chalice, filled it, and handed it over.**

He **failed his dream roll** — exhaustion, and hungover besides — but was the **only party member with a dream left in the bank.** What he saw was two images: **something grand and good, a great city or temple**, and then **the same thing in ruins.** No context, and a bad feeling attached. He told the party over breakfast; nobody could place it.

### Session 11
*(Roy joined partway through; Red was quietly along for the ride until he did — a state the party noted approvingly as *"hanging around, observing."*)*

**Red's fight, once it started, was almost entirely Wild Shape.** When [[Howl]] bolted down a back alley, Red went with [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] — *"I don't know, I guess I am chasing a bird"* — and ended up in the middle of the encounter with **[[Aleister the Invoker]]** and **[[Cocytus]].**

- **Moonbeam on Aleister** for 8 radiant, the only sustained damage anyone got on him before he flew away.
- **Wild Shaped into a brown bear** — large enough to shrug off the knockdown rider on the elemental's slam.
- **Engulfed.** [[Cocytus]] swallowed him whole: 20+ damage, knocked out of bear form, **restrained and suffocating** inside the creature's body. He broke the grapple on a Strength check, described as a brown bear reading a river the way it reads a salmon run.
- **Wild Shaped again, into a lion** — and the swashbuckler of the rival adventuring party finished the fight by **launching off his back** and driving a rapier through the beast's eye.

He also caught **5 damage from [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s Ice Knife** on the way through, and offered the [[Alchemy Jug]] at the tavern before being reminded the drinks were on the guild.

### Session 12
**Red set the direction of the night's theorizing and then took the party to church.** See [[Session 12 Recap — Something from Nothing]].

**He helped describe [[Aleister the Invoker]] to [[Madame Verre]]**, on a **natural-20 perception** as helper. The pooled description: **green hair**, a **white robe with red** detailing, a **monocle**, a **book**, a **staff drawn out of the summoned creature's chest**, a summoning **sigil**, and **glowing red symbols on his hands**, a **diamond with an eye** inside it. With each detail Verre sat up straighter.

**The [[Alchemy Jug]] got explained.** In the chalice report, [[Genni]] used it as the counterexample. It only *seems* to make liquid from nothing. It actually **siphons microscopic amounts of that liquid from all across the world**, so each cup of beer is less than a drop taken from a thousand cans elsewhere. The [[Golden Chalice]] doesn't siphon from anywhere, and that is what makes it **sinful**.

**His religion checks produced the one-Spoil-per-Agent theory.** Back at the inn, Red led a round of religion checks, and the pattern clicked: if the chalice mocks [[Venus]]'s domain of creation, perhaps **each [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoil]] mocks an Agent**. His own contribution was the **gown**: *a white gown as a mockery of a burial shroud*, which would make it a Spoil against [[Pluto]]. That set [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] worrying and pointed the party back at Verre's white dress.

**The [[Temple of Venus]] was his idea.** *"Who feels like going to get religion today?"* The statue out front shows Venus with her **three orbs**, the orbs he has been seeing in his dreams. He read the entry-hall scriptures with Aren, and in the basement **he was the one who found the meteor**: a small **flaming meteor** deliberately added to the mural, which the priest tied to **[[She Who Fell Before the First Fall]]**.

**He asked the priest about his dream** of the three spheres (**purple, blue, and red**) orbiting **the moon** while animals sat staring at it:

- **[[The Moon]]** is thought to be **a tenth Agent**, though that's disputed. She may instead be **the protector of the Agents**, the one who *"makes sure that everything stays as it should,"* including protection from the Dark Lords, *"ideally."* *(This matches the Session 7 reading at the [[Lathen]] temple.)*
- Perhaps the divine is **inspiring Red to create something with animals.** *"That certainly is [something that resonates]. I love animals."*

**And he asked about home.** Red described the people back home carrying banners showing no Agent he recognized. The priest identified them as the **Church of [[Dogmatika]]** in the [[Closed Land]]: a new pantheon and deity separate from the Solemn, saints who perform miracles, exponential growth over the last couple of decades, and a founding about **a century or a bit more ago**, possibly around the [[Great Cataclysm]]. *"In times of great peril, people are always looking for hope."* The party's whispered follow-up: **what if Dogmatika's god and saints came through the rift too?**

At the fireworks festival that night, Red showed up in the crowd **in lion form**.

### Session 13
See [[Session 13 Recap — Homecoming]].

**He caught the WitchCrafters hiding something.** A **natural 20 on Perception** spotted the masters trading looks over a faster way to the temple. *"We're tired of walking."* With **[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s Bardic Inspiration** (*"You got this, Red"*), his Persuasion just got there, and [[Ceramia]] gave up the lab.

**At the [[Temple of Nephthys]] he was the distraction.** He tried to pull [[Solenne]] away for a private blessing (*"High priestess! Will you come bless me?"*). It didn't work, so he kept at it: *"I need a blessing! I need a blessing!"* Introduced to [[Ysmene]]: *"Howdy. Name's Red."*

**In the fight** he opened with **Moonbeam**, wild shaped into a **lion**, and **roared** to warn the temple. His **Arcana** read on the **Fiendish Icons**: **not undead, more like constructs**, possibly clay. *(Leif: "I'm a geologist.")* Caught in an icon's fiery eruption, he spent his reaction on *"what in tarnation"* as Aren's bell went off. He leaped across the field and raked **[[Lady Nectaria]]** twice for 9. She scoffed.

### Session 14
See [[Session 14 Recap — Invitation Only (Shillelagh her down)]].

**Nectaria fed on his lion.** She picked out the lion as *"the only thing that has damaged her,"* hit it with a **Grave Strike**, **grappled** it, and **bit.** He failed the save: **17 necrotic**, and **his maximum HP dropped by 17.** He tore free and ran, and the opportunity attack **knocked him out of lion form.** [[Nephthara]] healed him from the doorway.

**He killed Damien.** He put **Moonbeam** on Nectaria (half damage, plus his Battle Cry die), gave Leif a **Healing Word**, and then **moved the beam onto the prone spawn** with its claws in Leif's ankle. It made the save, but it had about 3 hit points left, and **it burned to dust.** Nectaria later named it: **Damien.** He also healed Drolta for 6.

**He broke the news to Drolta.** The party picked him as the most sincere, honest fellow they had. *"Remember how we couldn't figure out how they got in last time? Turns out there's some connection between you and Nectaria, and she's gonna take over you. And you're gonna open the door."* With her agreement he **Shillelagh'd her down** (hence the **Shillelagh Bros**: *"Shillelagh her down"*), cast his new cantrip **Spare the Dying**, and **tied her up with his rope.** His backup offer was to **turn into a giant toad and swallow her.** *(Pan's Labyrinth was invoked.)* He left his raven **Bama** in the vault as a **security camera.**

**He fortified the temple.** His **[[Alchemy Jug]]** siphoned **real holy water** to douse every door, and he carved a **stake.** When Nectaria rapped on the door: *"Can I help you? We're closed. Come back in the morning."* Every instinct of his Southern hospitality said to open it. He didn't.

**He caught Petra.** When [[Petra]] walked to the door in a trance, he **grabbed her** in a contested Athletics check (**23**, with advantage and Guidance) and got a **hand over her mouth** before she could issue the invitation. *"You're going in time out."* Once [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] paralyzed her, he went **lion** and helped knock her out.

**He made her a staff bro.** In the morning he gave Petra **his spare quarterstaff**: *"Whenever you find yourself losing control of yourself again, you just need something to remember yourself by. You can always just be a staff bro with us."* *"I'll be happy to be a staff bro."* *"Sorry for what I had to do." "No. You did the right thing."*
