A ceramic **Alchemy Jug** bought by **[[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]** in the [[Pentamarone]] market for **50 gold** — the single most consequential purchase of Session 10, for reasons nobody could have predicted.

## Properties

- Appears to hold about **a gallon**, but functions like a bag of holding for liquids — it can produce **far more than it looks like it should.** *(Not quite: in Session 12 the [[WitchCrafters]] explained that it siphons the liquid from elsewhere — see [[#How It Actually Works (Session 12, September 23, 2026)|below]].)*
- **Use an action and name one liquid** from its list; the jug produces it. **Beer is on the list** — **four gallons of it.**
- Made by the seller **with help from one of the [[WitchCrafters]]**, and therefore one of the only genuinely magical items sold outside the guild's monthly market.

## How He Got It

Red went to a pottery stall asking after **brewing supplies** — he's a brewer with no farm, no equipment, and no beer since he left home. The potter liked the cut of his jib, reached under the stall, and set the jug down:

> *"There's not much magic that goes around here, it's pretty novice — but I did get help from one of the WitchCrafters with this. I've been saving it for somebody I think is gonna appreciate it."*

Red's Arcana identified it. He paid the asking price without hesitating, sat down at the **SS Nap Time**, said **"beer,"** and has not paid for a drink since.

## Why It Actually Matters

The jug is the reason the party finally solved the **[[Golden Chalice]]**. With a magic cup pouring free beer at the table, **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]** thought to ask the question nobody had asked in five months — *a chalice is technically a cup; what happens if we drink from it?* Red filled the chalice from the jug and handed it over.

*(A table proposal to test the word "mayonnaise" remains, as of Session 10, untested.)*

## How It Actually Works (Session 12, September 23, 2026)

It doesn't make anything. Per [[Genni]] of the [[WitchCrafters]], in the guild's report on the chalice, the jug only *seems* to produce liquid from nothing: it actually **siphons microscopic amounts of that liquid from all across the world.** Each cup of Red's beer is less than a drop taken from a thousand cans elsewhere.

That makes it the guild's example of **ordinary magic obeying the rules** — and the contrast that exposed the [[Golden Chalice]], which **doesn't siphon from anywhere.** It makes **something from nothing**, and that is what the guild believes makes it *sinful.*

## Holy Water (Session 14, October 21, 2026)

Besieged by vampires at the [[Temple of Nephthys]], Red used the jug to siphon **real holy water**, since it pulls genuine liquid from elsewhere, and **doused every door and entryway** before midnight. *"Any liquid?"* The table floated blood transfusions. Red uses it primarily for beer. *(Mayonnaise came up again.)* See [[Session 14 Recap — Invitation Only (Shillelagh her down)]].
