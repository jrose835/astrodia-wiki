A golden chalice found by [[Lucian Cugg]] in an excavated temple in the Great Ravine of [[Caelwyrm]].

## Properties

- **Conjures what the drinker thinks of** — the artifact's actual function, discovered in Session 10. **Creates something from nothing** — no components, no ambient arcane magic drawn, no source siphoned (Session 12). See below.
- **Indestructible** — Goblins attempted to smelt it down in a white-hot forge. It was completely unmelted. The [[WitchCrafters]] confirmed it in Session 12: **no physical or magical damage** left a dent or a crack.
- **Too-pure gold** — no imperfections or impurities at all; *"too pure to be made of this earth"* ([[Schmietta]], Session 12). **Age undeterminable.**
- **All-school magic** — [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] cast Detect Magic and found it radiates all schools of magic simultaneously, which is highly unusual. **Undispellable** — every dispel the WitchCrafters tried failed (Session 12).
- **Self-concealing** — the nondetection blocking every Identify is **not a spell cast on it**; it emanates from the chalice itself, and cannot be dispelled ([[Genni]], Session 10). **It extends to whoever holds it**: the holder cannot be scried (Session 12).
- **Corrupting** — repeated use shapes the drinker's psyche toward grandeur, superiority, and a god complex (Session 12). **The party has sworn off using it except in extreme emergencies.**

## History

Lucian found it in the Great Ravine, thought to have been carved by [[Dragons]] in ancient times. *(Session 12: the [[WitchCrafters]]' best match for its earlier history is a **white-dragon-worshipping clan's ritual object**, fought over by rival clans in the Great Ravine and lost **a few centuries ago** — see [[#II. History|below]].)* He hired [[Vonet]] and [[Dilly]] to escort him to [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] to sell it through their magical item buyback program, but they stole it and sold it to goblins in a cave north of [[Thornvale]] for 50 gold. The party recovered it from the goblin forge.

## Session 10 (August 26, 2026) — What It Actually Does

Five months of carrying it, three failed Identifies, a bounty and a deadline — and the answer was that **it is a cup, and nobody had tried drinking from it.** [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] asked the question in the [[WitchCrafters]] guild library in [[Pentamarone]]; [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] rinsed it, filled it from his new **[[Alchemy Jug]]**, and handed it over.

### The Function

> **Drink from the chalice, think of something, and it appears.**

No incantation, no components, no apparent limit on uses that the party found in one afternoon. What they observed:

| Drinker | Conjured | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **[[Aren Greycloak\|Aren]]** | **Cats** | Numerous. Solid, warm, real to the touch — but inert; held up by the tail, a cat simply lets it happen |
| **[[Asha Nyvani Korré\|Asha]]** | **Her missing mother** | Appears exactly as Asha last saw her **in person**. Feels real. **No recognition, no reaction** — she just stares. Carries nothing; **anything removed from her disintegrates** |
| **[[Leif Kalanchoe\|Leif]]** | **A second Golden Chalice** | Physical. Sitting in the party's possession. Whether it is genuinely *real* is untested |
| **[[Úlfr Kveld\|Úlfr]]** | **A horse** | Would not be ridden, would not move on command. Úlfr punched it and **it exploded**, anatomically, across the guild library |

### Rules, As Observed

- **It has to be something the drinker knows.** Úlfr tried to conjure the Academy's **repo man** — to see the face of whoever is coming — and was **refused.**
- **Living things are copies, not creatures.** Physical and touchable, but with no will and no use in a fight. *"Physical, but not exactly the thing. Not spiritually."* *(Confirmed in Session 12: hollow, bloodless, painless husks.)*
- **Objects may be a different matter.** The duplicate chalice raised immediate party plans for **conjured gold** and **decoy chalices** left in towns behind them. *(Session 12: object copies are **100% physical duplicates, down to the imperfections, with no magic** — a decoy would pass physical inspection.)*
- **There is a catch.** The DM's only comment: **everyone who drank feels "a little weird" afterward.** Weird how is unspecified. **Aren, Asha, Leif, Úlfr, and Red all drank.** *(Explained in Session 12: repeated use **shapes the drinker's psyche** — grandeur, superiority, impulses not their own. See [[#V. The Cost|The Cost]].)*

### The Nondetection Is the Chalice's Own

**[[Genni]]** of the [[WitchCrafters]] cast **Identify** and failed — the **third** failure, after the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] and the party's own attempt. She said it almost never happens to her. Pressed further, she gave the party the most useful thing anyone has said about the chalice:

> The nondetection is **not a spell laid on it.** **Dispel Magic would do nothing.** It appears to come **from the chalice itself** — *"almost like the chalice is casting non-detection constantly, on itself. Which I don't really know how that works."*

Nobody warded this thing. **It wards itself.** *(This also undercuts the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]]'s stated plan of having the party strip the concealment for them.)*

### The Spoils, Narrowed

**[[Tobin]]** reported from Academy records via the [[Sending Stones]]: the Academy knows of **four Sinful Spoils — spear, book, chalice, dress** — matching [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s dream exactly, and believes **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|the Seeker]] already has one of them.** Which one is unknown.

### Consequences

The demonstration was loud enough — cats, a stranger's mother, and a horse detonating in a guild library — that **[[Madame Verre]]** herself came down and **paralyzed the whole party** in the courtyard. She is also, by everyone's reckoning, the person most likely to be able to read the chalice.

**Repossession clock: roughly 15 days.**

## Session 12 (September 23, 2026) — Something from Nothing

On the fourth night of research, **[[Genni]]** presented the [[WitchCrafters]]' findings in [[Madame Verre]]'s office, the guild on one side, [[The Fallen]] on the other, and **the chalice sitting on Verre's desk.**

### I. The Chalice Itself

*Team: [[Edel]] & [[Schmietta]], with Úlfr.*

- **Indestructible.** No physical or magical damage left a dent or a crack.
- **Pure gold — *too* pure.** Schmietta: it is as if someone **crafted it out of the *idea* of the pure essence of gold**, not mined gold. No imperfections, no impurities. *"Too pure to be made of this earth."*
- **Age undeterminable.** It shows no wear. It could be new, or **prehistoric.**

### II. History

*Team: [[Genni]] & [[Marka]], with Aren and Asha.*

Searching for chalice references tied to **[[Caelwyrm]]**, the team found stories from **dragon-worshipping clans**:

- One clan **worshipped a white dragon** and used a ritual object that could **conjure whatever the dragon required.**
- The object was **fought over by rival dragon-worshipping clans** in **the Great Ravine** — where [[Lucian Cugg]] found the chalice.
- The last record of it **disappeared a few centuries ago.**

**Unconfirmed** that this is the same object, but it's the best match they have. See [[Dragons]].

### III. The Magic On It

*Team: the whole guild.*

- **Every dispel failed.** The magic can't be separated from the object.
- **The nondetection extends to whoever holds it.** Scrying on the holder returns nothing, *as if they were not on this plane of existence.* Scry someone standing near the holder, and the image shows **empty space** where the holder should be. **Carrying the chalice makes you invisible to divination** — so if the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] is trying to track the party by scrying, **it can't.**
- **The magic is every school at once.** When they dispelled it, they couldn't tell whether the dispel failed or whether the magic was **replaced so fast it was imperceptible.** *(Tommy's analogy: you can't tell whether you're failing to flip the light switch or it's flipping back on instantly — and the light switch is all of magic.)*

### IV. The Copies

*Team: [[Haine]] & [[Pittore]] with Leif; [[Seed]] & [[Ceramia]] with Drolta.*

**Objects:**
- **100% physical copies**, same material, **down to the imperfections.**
- **No magical properties carry over.** [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]]'s duplicate chalice is only gold.
- A copy **can reproduce details the drinker doesn't know about**, provided the drinker has **shared physical space or interaction with the original.** Genni's example: tear a page out of a book you've seen, hand it back without telling you, and your copy will be missing that exact page.
- **Things the drinker has never encountered can't be made** — why [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] couldn't conjure the repo man.
- A copy **would pass physical inspection** as the real chalice.
- **A copy can be enchanted afterward.** [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s idea — a mundane duplicate imbued with magic of your choosing. Genni confirmed it would work.

**Living things:**
- **Physically real, but hollow.** No pain, **no blood.** They can be injured, and **injuries that would kill or maim the original destroy them.**
- **No self-preservation, emotion, or social response** — inert husks shaped like the creature.
- **Pieces separated from the body evaporate**; **a dead copy leaves no trace.** The horse had evaporated by the time anyone reached the library.
- **The cats were put down.** The guild explained, apologetically, that they aren't technically alive. **[[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] was visibly upset.**
- To **Asha**, directly: *the copy is not your mother, has no connection to your mother, and feels nothing.*

> *"So you're telling me, if I punch a real horse, it would explode like that?"*
> *"You're a strong man, Úlfr."*

### V. The Cost

This is what "you feel a little weird" meant.

> **Repeated use of the chalice shapes the drinker's psyche.**

- **Unnatural feelings of grandeur and a drive for power** — a growing **god complex** and an **unnatural sense of superiority.**
- **Thoughts and impulses the user would not normally have.** WitchCrafters who drank during testing documented these themselves.
- **Cumulative**, and scaled to **the size of the request** — asking for more, or for something bigger, costs more.
- **Varies by person.** The same request can affect two drinkers at different speeds and intensities.
- **Reversible?** Maybe. Best guess: it fades with time **if the chalice goes unused.** No guarantee.
- **Overuse** could lead to **involuntary actions, possibly a full psychotic break.**

**Five PCs have drunk** (Aren, Asha, Leif, Úlfr, Red). *(Tommy confirmed that the players he messaged privately about how their characters feel still feel that way.)*

### VI. Something from Nothing

Genni's summary, and the most important finding of the night:

> **The chalice generates something from nothing.**

Ordinary spells **transmute material components** or **channel ambient arcane magic** through verbal and somatic components. **The chalice does neither.** It draws on no arcane magic and needs no components — impossible under every known rule of magic. *(The table called it a violation of **equivalent exchange**.)*

The contrast is [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s **[[Alchemy Jug]]**, which only *seems* to make liquid from nothing: it **siphons microscopic amounts from all across the world.** The chalice doesn't siphon from anywhere.

> *"And I think that is what makes it **sinful**. From our best ability, we believe that this object violates the laws of nature — and specifically, quite appropriately, the domain of **the Agent of Creation.**"*

**[[Venus]].** Who could make such a thing? A god, possibly — or, given the history, **the [[Dragons]].**

### Returned Unused

Genni argued it **shouldn't be used for what Verre wanted it for.** Verre agreed:

> *"I think the risk is too great. And I don't want what we're working on to be tainted by whatever magic this thing has."*

**She returned the chalice** and honored the contract, simply not exercising her right to use it. **Her goal is still undisclosed.** Her parting advice at the festival:

> *"Keep that chalice safe. Very powerful artifact. If word of its power were ever to get out, more powerful people would probably be after you."*

### The Party's Ruling

- **No one uses the chalice except in extreme emergencies.** (*"Like needing horses?"*)
- **Keep it close.** Destroying the Spoils was floated; the consensus was that they must stay out of the wrong hands — and **the Academy counts as the wrong hands.**
- **One Spoil per Agent?** *(Speculation.)* If the chalice is sinful because it **mocks [[Venus]]'s domain**, perhaps each [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|Sinful Spoil]] mocks an Agent. See [[Session 12 Recap — Something from Nothing#One Spoil Per Agent?|the Session 12 recap]].
- **Tracking the other Spoils:** Verre knows [[Simone]]'s sand-map spell and the guild will look for hotspots — though if the other Spoils share this nondetection, it will be hard.
- **Did [[Lucian Cugg]] set them up**, to watch how it's used? *(Speculation, raised at the inn.)*
