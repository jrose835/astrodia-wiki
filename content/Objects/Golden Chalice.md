A golden chalice found by [[Lucian Cugg]] in an excavated temple in the Great Ravine of [[Caelwyrm]].

## Properties

- **Conjures what the drinker thinks of** — the artifact's actual function, discovered in Session 10. See below.
- **Indestructible** — Goblins attempted to smelt it down in a white-hot forge. It was completely unmelted.
- **All-school magic** — [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] cast Detect Magic and found it radiates all schools of magic simultaneously, which is highly unusual.
- **Self-concealing** — the nondetection blocking every Identify is **not a spell cast on it**; it emanates from the chalice itself, and cannot be dispelled ([[Genni]], Session 10).

## History

Lucian found it in the Great Ravine, thought to have been carved by [[Dragons]] in ancient times. He hired [[Vonet]] and [[Dilly]] to escort him to [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] to sell it through their magical item buyback program, but they stole it and sold it to goblins in a cave north of [[Thornvale]] for 50 gold. The party recovered it from the goblin forge.

## Current Status

Submitted to the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] at the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy of Arcane Arts]]. [[Ms. Maris]] offered 50 gold, which the party declined. The chalice was found to have a **non-detection spell** blocking all divination — identity unknown. The party signed a disclosure form and has **30 days** before a "repo man" is sent to collect it.

[[Tobin]], a junior apprentice, reacted with recognition when he saw it, whispering *"Wasn't that one of the—"* before [[Ms. Maris]] cut him off. It appears to be one of several specific items the Initiative has been told to watch for — referred to as **"the Something Spoils"** (full name unknown). Three other item types are also on their watch list; the chalice is the first to come through.

[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] suspects [[Lucian Cugg|Lucian]] is understating its true value.

## Session 6 (May 31, 2026)

- **Identified as DIVINE magic** of the [[Solemn Pantheon]], and **extremely powerful** — revealed by Simone's enchanted-sand map of [[Astrodia]], where divine (yellow) grains crawled toward the chalice. Its **heavy nondetection magic** is why Identify still fails to read it directly.
- Its arrival explains the **new tiny divine-magic speck at [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]]** that only appeared recently on the map — the party realized it was the chalice they had brought back two days earlier.

- The party plans to take the chalice to the **[[White Forest]]** — away from the Academy and toward answers about the forbidden research and [[The Fourth Magic]].
- It may be one of the **"Sinful Spoils"** (see [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils]]) — the artifacts the Academy is trying to keep from a woman in black and red. **This also reveals the full name of "the Something Spoils"** that [[Tobin]] could only half-remember in Session 3: **the Sinful Spoils.**

## Session 10 (August 26, 2026) — What It Actually Does

Five months of carrying it, three failed Identifies, a bounty and a deadline — and the answer was that **it is a cup, and nobody had tried drinking from it.** [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]] asked the question in the [[WitchCrafters]] guild library in [[Pentamarone]]; [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]] rinsed it, filled it from his new **[[Alchemy Jug]]**, and handed it over.

### The Function

> **Drink from the chalice, think of something, and it appears.**

No incantation, no components, no apparent limit on uses that the party found in one afternoon. What they observed:

| Drinker | Conjured | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **[[Aren Greycloak\|Aren]]** | **Cats** | Numerous. Solid, warm, real to the touch — but inert; held up by the tail, a cat simply lets it happen |
| **[[Asha Nyvani Korré\|Asha]]** | **Her missing mother** | Appears exactly as Asha last saw her **in person**. Feels real. **No recognition, no reaction** — she just stares. Carries nothing; **anything removed from her disintegrates** |
| **[[Leif Kalanchoe\|Leif]]** | **A second Golden Chalice** | Physical. Sitting in the party's possession. Whether it is genuinely *real* is untested |
| **[[Úlfr Kveld\|Úlfr]]** | **A horse** | Would not be ridden, would not move on command. Úlfr punched it and **it exploded**, anatomically, across the guild library |

### Rules, As Observed

- **It has to be something the drinker knows.** Úlfr tried to conjure the Academy's **repo man** — to see the face of whoever is coming — and was **refused.**
- **Living things are copies, not creatures.** Physical and touchable, but with no will and no use in a fight. *"Physical, but not exactly the thing. Not spiritually."*
- **Objects may be a different matter.** The duplicate chalice raised immediate party plans for **conjured gold** and **decoy chalices** left in towns behind them.
- **There is a catch.** The DM's only comment: **everyone who drank feels "a little weird" afterward.** Weird how is unspecified. **Aren, Asha, Leif, Úlfr, and Red all drank.**

### The Nondetection Is the Chalice's Own

**[[Genni]]** of the [[WitchCrafters]] cast **Identify** and failed — the **third** failure, after the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy]] and the party's own attempt. She said it almost never happens to her. Pressed further, she gave the party the most useful thing anyone has said about the chalice:

> The nondetection is **not a spell laid on it.** **Dispel Magic would do nothing.** It appears to come **from the chalice itself** — *"almost like the chalice is casting non-detection constantly, on itself. Which I don't really know how that works."*

Nobody warded this thing. **It wards itself.** *(This also undercuts the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]]'s stated plan of having the party strip the concealment for them.)*

### The Spoils, Narrowed

**[[Tobin]]** reported from Academy records via the [[Sending Stones]]: the Academy knows of **four Sinful Spoils — spear, book, chalice, dress** — matching [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]'s dream exactly, and believes **[[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils|the Seeker]] already has one of them.** Which one is unknown.

### Consequences

The demonstration was loud enough — cats, a stranger's mother, and a horse detonating in a guild library — that **[[Madame Verre]]** herself came down and **paralyzed the whole party** in the courtyard. She is also, by everyone's reckoning, the person most likely to be able to read the chalice.

**Repossession clock: roughly 15 days.**

## Session 11 (September 9, 2026) — Under Contract

Instead of confiscating it, **[[Madame Verre]] asked to borrow it.**

### The Terms

| The [[WitchCrafters]] give | [[Madame Verre]] gets |
|---|---|
| Their **best effort at identifying the chalice** — origin, function, and why it matters | **Use of the chalice** for **one specific, undisclosed goal** |
| The chalice **returned** once that goal is met; **no claim of ownership** | The party's **participation**, which she called *"imperative"* |
| **Housing and meals** for the party at guild expense | |
| A **guarantee that no harm comes** to any of them | |

Written by **[[Marka]]**, amended at [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]'s insistence to include lodging, amended at [[Aren Greycloak|Aren]]'s insistence to record **in writing that the party warned her** about the Academy — and **read by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] before anyone signed.** Verre's estimate: **one week.**

**What her goal is, she would not say.** To [[Haine]], privately: *"I have a good feeling about this group. And this chalice. **It's the best lead we've got.**"*

### The Four Lines of Inquiry

Where three Identifies failed, Verre stopped casting spells at it and started **taking it apart** — *"grassroots magic... getting to the essence of things without relying on a pre-existing spell."*

| Team | Question | Party member embedded |
|---|---|---|
| **[[Edel]] & [[Schmietta]]** | The **materials** — metal, set jewels, and whether either can be **dated** to an era or place in [[Astrodia]] | [[Úlfr Kveld\|Úlfr]] (Athletics, at the forge) |
| **[[Haine]] & [[Pittore]]** | The **conjured objects** — what they're made of, and whether a copy **fully replicates** the original | [[Leif Kalanchoe\|Leif]] (Perception, **nat 20**, comparing the two chalices) |
| **[[Seed]] & [[Ceramia]]** | The **living copies** — are they alive, and what animates them. *(Test subjects: the cats.)* | [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes\|Drolta]] (Investigation) |
| **[[Genni]] & [[Marka]]** | **Records** — any account of the [[The Seeker of the Sinful Spoils\|Sinful Spoils]], or of a chalice that manifests copies | [[Aren Greycloak\|Aren]] (Insight) and [[Asha Nyvani Korré\|Asha]] (History) |

**Progress: two working days cut the seven-day estimate to three days remaining.** No findings have been released — the guild is holding everything for a single report at the end.

### Housekeeping

- **Magic still works after drinking.** Confirmed by a test cast of **Message** in the guild hall.
- **The "you feel a little weird" effect is still unexplained** and still being tracked. One party member's introspection returned a single word: **still.**
- The **second chalice** [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] conjured is now a research subject in its own right, sitting next to the original on [[Haine]] and [[Pittore]]'s bench.

**Repossession clock: roughly 13 days, and the chalice is now several hundred miles outside Academy jurisdiction and legally spoken for.**
