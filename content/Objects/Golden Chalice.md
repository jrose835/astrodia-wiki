A golden chalice found by [[Lucian Cugg]] in an excavated temple in the Great Ravine of [[Caelwyrm]].

## Properties

- **Indestructible** — Goblins attempted to smelt it down in a white-hot forge. It was completely unmelted.
- **All-school magic** — [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] cast Detect Magic and found it radiates all schools of magic simultaneously, which is highly unusual.

## History

Lucian found it in the Great Ravine, thought to have been carved by [[Dragons]] in ancient times. He hired [[Vonet]] and [[Dilly]] to escort him to [[The Academy of Arcane Arts]] to sell it through their magical item buyback program, but they stole it and sold it to goblins in a cave north of [[Thornvale]] for 50 gold. The party recovered it from the goblin forge.

## Current Status

Submitted to the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] at the [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|Academy of Arcane Arts]]. [[Ms. Maris]] offered 50 gold, which the party declined. The chalice was found to have a **non-detection spell** blocking all divination — identity unknown. The party signed a disclosure form and has **30 days** before a "repo man" is sent to collect it.

[[Tobin]], a junior apprentice, reacted with recognition when he saw it, whispering *"Wasn't that one of the—"* before [[Ms. Maris]] cut him off. It appears to be one of several specific items the Initiative has been told to watch for — referred to as **"the Something Spoils"** (full name unknown). Three other item types are also on their watch list; the chalice is the first to come through.

[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] suspects [[Lucian Cugg|Lucian]] is understating its true value.
