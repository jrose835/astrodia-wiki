The **command amulet** of the **shield guardian** that holds [[Morgana's Tower]] — recovered from **[[Morgana]]'s own pocket** in the tower basement in Session 9, and the object she meant when she wrote that *"the guardian answers only to its charm."* Currently worn by **[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]]**.

The party had spent the session hunting for a spoken password. The "charm" was never a word.

## What It Does

Identified by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] in Session 9 — cast free of charge using the **spell components** found on the tower's fourth floor rather than a 100 gp pearl. It is a standard **Shield Guardian command amulet**:

- **Bound.** While amulet and guardian are on the same plane, the wearer can **telepathically call the guardian to come to them**, and always knows its **distance and direction.** Within 60 feet, **half of any damage the wearer takes is transferred to the guardian.**
- **Protection (reaction).** When an attack hits the wearer within 5 feet of the guardian, the wearer gains **+5 AC** against that attack and until the guardian's next turn — often turning the hit into a miss.
- **Spell Storing.** A spellcaster wearing the amulet can store **one spell of level 4 or lower** in the guardian by casting it on the construct; the guardian can later release it with the original caster's parameters, needing no components.
- The guardian's overriding priority is **protecting the amulet's bearer** — it has no self-preservation instinct and will destroy itself to do so.

**It commands only this one guardian.** Asha's study of the amulet confirmed the binding is specific, not general.

## How the Party Got Past the Guardian

[[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] simply walked up to the construct with the amulet **held out in front of her.** It scanned her, **flashed green**, and stood down out of attack posture. The party then filed out the front door behind her, staying close so it read them as one group.

## The Bigger Implication

The **history check** that followed is the reason this amulet matters beyond one tower door. The guardian is an **older model of the same constructs standing watch at the [[Biblioplex]]** — and per Asha, those guardians are stationed **throughout [[The Academy of Arcane Arts|the Academy]]**, not just at the library.

Since each amulet binds one guardian, the party inferred that the Academy must keep a **control room holding every corresponding amulet.** For a [[S.A.T.O.]] operation against Academy security, that is a single point of failure worth knowing about.

The party also raised the darker reading: **the Academy's guardians may be the muscle the [[Arcane Reclamation Initiative]] sends to seize the [[Golden Chalice]].** This amulet will not command those — but it proves how they work, and who built them.

## Provenance

Designed jointly by **[[Morgana]]** (the forge) and **[[Verelda]]** (the animating lattice theory) — see [[Verelda]] for the correspondence. This is the **third** guardian of their collaboration, the one whose temperament they finally got right after an earlier model *"nearly cleav[ed] the poor delivery boy in half over a mispronounced password."* Verelda's own letter confirms it was still *"holding steady"* and politely asking students to state their business years after Morgana built it.

It was in Morgana's pocket when she died in the [[White Forest]], and came home on her corpse.
