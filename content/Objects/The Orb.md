A glass sphere that is an **enchanted prison**, holding an unknown **living creature** within. Recovered from the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]] and carried by the party.

## Properties

- **Enchanted prison** — a glass sphere imprisoning whatever lives inside it.
- **1 HP** — it **breaks if dropped or thrown**, freeing the creature within.
- **Cannot be identified** — the thing inside is **alive, not a magic object**, so Identify reveals nothing about it.
- **Communicates** — it tries to speak in a **language no one understood**. Once [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] used Comprehend Languages, it was understood to be **asking to be let out**.

## A Dangerous Connection

The creature's language **matches the voices that [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] secretly hears** — the same tongue heard after he meditated in the Purodetapo Forest, within the path of the [[Great Cataclysm]]. This ties the orb's prisoner directly to the mystery of [[The Fourth Magic]] and Leif's patron, named in Session 7 as **[[The Whispering One]]**. The orb creature is the clearest physical link to whatever reached out to Leif.

[[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] determined the orb creature and the sentient greataxe ([[Leith Chantos's Legacy]]) are **different entities** despite both communicating through emotion.

## Current Status

Identified in Session 6 (May 31, 2026) and still carried — carefully — by the party. The creature remains imprisoned; the glass prison's 1 HP makes it fragile cargo.

## Session 13 — Shown to Genni (October 7, 2026)

[[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] showed the creature to **[[Genni]]** of the [[WitchCrafters]]. She had never seen anything like it (*"It even speaks"*), and it started talking in **the language only Leif recognizes.** She couldn't say what it is. Leif asked her to tell **[[Madame Verre]]** that it **speaks the same language as his patron**, [[The Whispering One|Sorath]]. Verre had never been asked about it directly. *(Leif had planned to ask her himself and forgot, twice.)*
