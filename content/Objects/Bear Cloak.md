A cloak made from the hide of the **brown bear** [[The Fallen]] fought at the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]] — **with the head left intact**, resting on the wearer's shoulder.

**Worn by [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]].**

## Properties

> **While wearing this cloak, you have advantage on Intimidation checks.**

## History

[[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] carried the hide from the library all the way north and finally **commissioned a tailor in the [[Pentamarone]] market circle** in Session 10 to make it into a cloak, for **2 gold 5 silver**, specifying that the head stay on. It was ready the next day, and he **collected it during the chalice-research montage in Session 11.**

Fitting equipment for a Goliath whose combat style is largely a matter of what he says to people before he hits them — and a straightforward upgrade for a character whose intimidation attempts have, to date, mostly failed.
