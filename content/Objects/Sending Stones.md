A pair of **Sending Stones** gifted to the party by [[Myrna Nor]] on behalf of the [[S.A.T.O.]], so the party and the students-on-the-inside can stay in contact.

## Properties

- A standard pair of attuned Sending Stones — holding one allows the bearer to send a short telepathic message to whoever holds the other, once per day per the usual rules.

## History

Given to the party in Session 6 (May 31, 2026) as part of the [[S.A.T.O.]]'s gifts when they recruited the party as their outside adventuring arm, alongside teleportation runes to the Lathen safehouse and gold.

## Current Status

Held by the party (recently named **The Fallen**) to maintain contact with [[Myrna Nor]] and the rest of the [[S.A.T.O.]].

## Notable Traffic

- **Session 9** — used to reach [[Myrna Nor|Myrna]] with [[Morgana's Tower]]'s teleportation runes, which brought [[Archibald]].
- **Session 10** — carried **[[Tobin]]'s** report on the **four Sinful Spoils** into [[Pentamarone]], and carried the party's reply back out. The 25-word limit remains the table's least favorite mechanic and produced several minutes of negotiation over whether *"how's your mom"* could be afforded. It could not.
