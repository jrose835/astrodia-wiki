A minor magical amulet that sat **unclaimed in [[The Fallen]]'s party inventory** for several sessions before finally finding an owner.

## Properties

- When the wearer makes an **attack roll**, they may **forgo rolling the d20** and **treat the result as a 10.**
- Identified previously by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]].

## Current Status

**Claimed by [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]]** in [[Pentamarone]] during Session 10 (August 26, 2026), on the grounds that a guaranteed 10 beats whatever the table's dice were doing that night. *(He then rolled a natural 1 on the session's final saving throw, which the amulet does not help with.)*
