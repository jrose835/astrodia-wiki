A **copper amulet** of tiny interlocking gears that ticks and whirs, **powered by the magic of Saturn**.

## Properties

- **Saturn-powered clockwork** — built from tiny interlocking gears that make a constant ticking/whirring sound.
- **Once per dawn**, the wearer may **forgo rolling the d20** on an attack and instead **treat the die as a flat 10** (ability and other modifiers still apply).

## History

Found earlier on a **skeleton in the bear's cave**, where it lay unidentified until Session 6.

## Current Status

Identified in Session 6 (May 31, 2026), once [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] learned the Identify spell. Currently worn/carried by [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]].

## First Referenced

Recovered from the skeleton in the bear's cave.
