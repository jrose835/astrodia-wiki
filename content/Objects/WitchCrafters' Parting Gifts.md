On the morning after the [[Pentamarone]] fireworks festival, the masters of the **[[WitchCrafters]]** gathered [[The Fallen]] in the guild courtyard and gave them **one made item each**, to make the road (and probably the [[White Forest]]) a little easier. *"Who knows what's going on there?"* Tommy added them all to the party inventory. Who carries what is mostly unassigned, though [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] used the lens in Session 13.

## The Gifts

| Gift | Maker | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Unbreakable Flask** | [[Ceramia]] (ceramics) | **Indestructible.** **Purifies anything poured into it**, as *Purify Food and Drink*. Meant as a companion to [[Harlan “Red” Reed|Red]]'s [[Alchemy Jug]] |
| **Seed Pouch of Plenty** | [[Seed]] (botany) | **Once per day** produces a handful of berries that works as a full casting of **Goodberry** |
| **Pocket Canvas** | [[Pittore]] (painting) | Paint what you want to become: **Disguise Self**, once per day |
| **Smithy's Whetstone** | [[Schmietta]] (smithing) | **Once per day**, a **non-magical slashing weapon** sharpened with it is treated as **magical** for the day, as *Magic Weapon* |
| **Cloak of Protection** | [[Haine]] (textiles and weaving) | **+1 AC** and **+1 to saving throws** |
| **Clear-Sight Lens** | [[Edel]] (gems) | A reskinned **Wand of Secrets**: when used, it **pulses and points to the nearest hidden door or trap within 30 feet** |
| **Sealed Letter of Introduction** | [[Marka]] (calligraphy) | Carries **Illusory Script**: make the page look like **any document** you like, or **hide a secret message** that only chosen readers can see |
| **Power stone** | [[Genni]] | An **empty [[Keystones\|Empire power stone]]**, a black disc bearing the triangle symbol. See below |

## The Power Stone

- **Any spell can be cast into it.** Loading takes the spell's normal casting time.
- It **holds one spell at a time**, and **anyone** can use the stored spell later, including someone who can't cast it. It works like a reusable spell scroll.
- After use it can be loaded again.
- In [[Endymion Empire]] territory it works **like a passport**: carrying one shows you're allowed to be there and have the means to cast legally.

> *"These things have kind of a terrifying or uncomfortable history for me, but they are extremely useful. And you'll need one if you're getting into the Empire's territory at all."*

It is the first answer the party has had to the problem [[Leif Kalanchoe|Leif]] and [[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] face as **warlocks** under Empire law. Genni's own history with the stones (she was the **Emperor's right-hand assistant**) came out the same morning. See [[Genni]].

## In Use

- **Session 13:** [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] used the **Clear-Sight Lens** to find the hidden **vault door** at the [[Temple of Nephthys]], after his own Investigation check failed.
