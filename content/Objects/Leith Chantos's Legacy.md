A sentient magic greataxe found resting on the anvil in the forge room of the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]]. It is a **double-bladed axe** with arrowhead-shaped blades and a leather-loop haft. Its given name is **"Leith Chantos's Legacy,"** though [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] intends to rename it.

## Properties

- **+1 greataxe** (double-bladed, arrowhead blades, leather loop haft).
- **Sentient** — possesses Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores.
- **Communicates by emotion, not words** — it transmits feelings to its wielder rather than speaking. ([[Drolta Amara Tzuentes|Drolta]] confirmed it is a *different entity* from the creature in [[The Orb]], despite both communicating through emotion.)
- **Good-aligned.**
- **Special purpose:** to **protect the family of its bearer** — "family" explicitly includes chosen and found family.

## History

Forged by [[Leith Chantos]] himself and left on the forge anvil in his final library. It had **not been touched since Leith, centuries ago**, and it **does not know where he is**.

*Note: this is **NOT** the famous double-headed axe from the fresco that Leith forged to stop the warlord — it is a separate creation of his.*

## Current Status

Claimed by [[Úlfr Kveld|Úlfr]] in Session 6 (May 31, 2026). [[Asha Nyvani Korré|Asha]] detected its enchantment magic via a ritual Detect Magic in the forge; it was later fully identified once Asha learned the Identify spell.

**As of Session 8 (July 22, 2026), Úlfr has begun wielding it in combat** — swinging it during the bandit ambush on the road north (and using it to smash into [[Morgana]]'s tower) — a step toward retiring his old greataxe **[[Úlfr Kveld#Objects & Weapons|Last Word]]**. He still means to give the sentient axe a new name.

## First Referenced

Session 6 — found in the forge room of the [[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]].
