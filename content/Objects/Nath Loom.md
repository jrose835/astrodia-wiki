A spellbook created by [[Leith Chantos]] (originally known as Naf) containing **hundreds of poisons and their antidotes**. Leith Chantos compiled it over years of research after his family was murdered by a poison brewed by the orc priest Wound.

## Current Status

Believed to be located in the **[[Lost Library of Leith Chantos]]** — the final library he built before departing to parts unknown. [[Myrna Nor]] hired the party to recover it for her Lorehold thesis.

[[Powell]], Myrna's former research partner, is also after the book and hired mercenaries to claim it first.

## Session 6 (May 31, 2026)

- **Returned to [[Myrna Nor]]** this session, along with [[Leith Chantos]]'s newly-found **personal journal** (recovered from his bedchamber). The journal vindicates Myrna's Lorehold thesis.
- The party **retains permission to read the Nath Loom anytime**.
- Beyond its hundreds of poisons and antidotes, it also contains **transcribable wizard spells** (e.g., **Glyph of Warding**) that are above the party's current level — set aside for the future.
